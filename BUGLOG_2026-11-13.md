  the decision session's own RTH 1Min bars (09:30-16:00 ET).

  the first cycle after the session date rolled — live artifact:

  (09:00 ET, 30 min before the open) with daily_rows 10794 and
  intraday_rows 0. That scan cannot succeed: no RTH bar for the
  session exists yet. It writes an errored proposals file, which
  QullaFileProducer correctly fail-closes on
  (QULLA-EVIDENCE-ERROR-20261111), and the attempt marker stamped
  by the doomed launch suppresses retries for the full 3600 s
  retry window. (3) A second timing component, measured live:
  the door's SIP 1Min bars carry a 15-minute serve lag (fetched
  2026-11-13T14:39:29Z, newest bar served 14:24:00Z), so the
  first RTH bar is not servable until ~09:46 ET; a scan at
  09:38 ET still landed intraday_rows 0 (file written
  14:39:05Z). The prior session reads clean at the night roll
  only because by then all of its RTH bars exist and are
  servable; midnight re-arms the defect. The standing hypothesis

  therefore PROVEN, with the serve lag as its second half.
- Fix (minimum sufficient, at the source): one shared rule,
  desk3.qulla_scan.scan_deferred(session, now_et): a scan for

  serve lag + buffer). desk3.qulla_scan.run_scan refuses with a
  named deferral BEFORE the engine is invoked, so no caller can
  write an errored file in the window; desk3/producers_g4.
  QullaScanWarmer launches nothing and stamps no marker inside
  the window (its first post-window call launches into a session
  whose bars exist). Rolled-back sessions (weekend/holiday) are
  never deferred: their RTH bars all exist. The engine, the
  file producer's fail-closed posture, and the sleeve are
  untouched. The qulla-wire blocker fixture clock moved from
  06:00 ET to 10:00 ET: the old fixture encoded the defective

- Blocker: tests/test_qulla_preopen_deferral_20261113_blockers.py
