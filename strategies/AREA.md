# strategies/: what lives here

Strategy cards: one card per catalogued strategy (269 cards), grouped by family folder. The family folders are enumerated in docs/CATALOG.json and in INDEX.json.

Exact file count, byte total and captured revision: [the generated repository index](../INDEX.md). The matching entries in [INDEX.json](../INDEX.json) retain every path and blob hash.

Nothing sits at the top of strategies/ besides this entry doc: the 269 cards live in 15 family folders beneath it. Family names and counts are in docs/CATALOG.json (the catalog data) and INDEX.json.

## Where its index lives

- Every file in this area is listed, with size and blob sha, in the root INDEX.json (search it by the path prefix `strategies/`).
- The prose map is the root INDEX.md; the mandatory first read is the root START-HERE.md; routing by topic is the root ROADMAP.md.
