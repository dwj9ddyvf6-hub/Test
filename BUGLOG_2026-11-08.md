## JOURNAL-ROTATION-LEDGER-20261108 — journal rotation destroyed the epoch's first generation; reconcile truth lost 2026-10-26..11-02
- Severity: critical (the desk's ledger of record silently truncated;
  every journal derivation understated by the destroyed span).
- Files: desk3/journal.py (rotation), desk3/reconcile.py,
  desk3/dispatcher.py, desk3/producers3.py (readers).
- What broke: JournalWriter rotation did os.replace(path, path + ".1"),
  overwriting the previous rotated generation on every rotation. The
  dispatcher journal rotates at 5,000,000 bytes, roughly weekly at the
  current rate. The second rotation of this epoch landed
  2026-11-08T13:35:38Z and destroyed the generation covering the epoch
  start (2026-10-26T20:14:30Z) through 2026-11-02: 2,227 desk orders
  and their fills left every derivation. Reconcile's unexplained cash
  jumped +5,122.82 -> +40,835.40 in one cycle with no broker event,
  and per-symbol expected positions swung to journal_sleeves
  attribution system-wide. Readers compounded it: reconcile and the
  session-fills producer hardcoded the two-generation form, and the
  dispatcher's duplicate index read only the current file.
- Fix: rotation now SHIFTS generations (path.N -> path.N+1, then
  path -> path.1) and never deletes; one shared reader,
  journal.journal_generations(path), enumerates every generation
  oldest-first, and reconcile.journal_rows, Dispatcher._index and
  SessionFillsProducer all consume it. The destroyed generation's
  order_submitted rows were recovered from the broker's own order
  record (the only surviving source) into state/dispatcher.jsonl.2:
  2,226 rows, each carrying recovered: true and
  recovery: ORDER-SUBMITTED-RECOVERY-20261108 provenance; the census
  was cross-proven against the broker FILL activity ledger (2,222
  distinct filled order ids in activities, 0 missed by the census).
  The desk's own sweep then re-derives the fills through the proven
  path; no fill rows were hand-written.
- Regression blocker: tests/test_curelane_20261108_blockers.py
  (TestJournalRotationPreservesGenerations, 2 tests). Fail-first
  verified on a pre-cure shadow tree: both fail (journal_generations
  absent; two-file readers lose rows), pass after.
- Completion (same lane, after the first offline proof): a full
  epoch census (6,793 broker orders) against every surviving
  journal generation found 19 further orders with no
  order_submitted row anywhere: the UNJOURNALED-FLATTEN-BURST-
  20261102 set below. Their submission rows were appended to the
  same recovery generation with the same provenance, so the
  desk's own sweep imports their 7 still-missing fills (net
  -$186,257.19 of uncredited sells) through the proven path.
- Live proof: offline proof on a copy of live state with live broker
  reads (2026-11-08): see the cure lane report
  (goals/muse-desk-v3/hidden_files/cure-lane-2026-11-08.md) for the
  counted sweep + reconcile result. Deployment rides the runner's
  next restart onto the cured tree; the first post-restart
  reconcile_last.json is the live proof event.

## ORDER-HOLD-CASH-20261108 — reconcile compared expected cash to raw broker cash; the desk's own resting order manufactured the desk-wide cash halt
- Severity: high (desk-wide entry halt from a phantom discrepancy).
- Files: desk3/reconcile.py, desk3/cycle.py, desk3/broker.py.
- What broke: the broker `cash` field excludes funds encumbered by
  open orders while `equity` still counts them; the reconcile
  expected-cash derivation has no order book and compared against
  raw cash. On 2026-11-07 the aster native sleeve's BTC buy
  (client_order_id v3-aster_native_btc_eth_sma200-20261107T122300-1-
  BTC-USD, submitted 13:22:25Z) went pending_new with a 5,087.72
  hold; broker cash stepped 930,471.71 -> 925,383.99 in the first
  reconcile after submission (13:23:48Z) with no fill, no fee and no
  cash activity at that minute, and unexplained cash flipped
  +35.10 -> +5,122.82 with cash_halt TRUE (materiality 930.51).
  Mechanism proven three ways: the step equals the hold to the
  cent; the account identity equity - cash - position_market_value
  equals 5,087.72 on repeated live reads (2026-11-08) while the BTC
  price moved; the activity ledger shows no cash event at 13:23Z.
  The 35.10 pre-halt residue is the Saturday residual (Saturday's
  18 fill-sweep TimeoutError read failures on Nov 6 order ids are
  its named suspect; it sits below materiality either way).
- Fix: PaperDoor and FixtureBroker get_account now also return
  position_market_value (plus long/short market value); cycle.py
  derives the hold from the broker's own identity
  (equity - cash - position_market_value, None when any leg is
  missing, leaving behavior exactly as before) and passes
  broker_order_holds_usd into reconcile; reconcile compares
  expected cash against cash + holds, clamps negative holds to
  zero, and NAMES the hold on the report (broker_order_holds_usd)
  and on the cash discrepancy row (with the comparable figure)
  while the raw broker cash stays on the row. No threshold,
  tolerance or materiality value changed.
- Regression blocker: tests/test_curelane_20261108_blockers.py
  (TestReconcileOrderHold, 4 tests: raw gap halts without holds;

  pre-cure shadow tree (TypeError on the new kwarg).
- Live proof: the 2026-11-08 offline proof reconciles live broker
  state with holds derived live at 5,087.72 (see cure lane report).

## POSITION-DIALECT-READBACK-20261108 — aster native sleeve read its BTC position in the wrong dialect and re-bought its full target every cycle
- Severity: high (one sleeve emitted 41 filled BTC buys on
  2026-11-07 totalling 2.697191085 BTC / $204,863.15 against a
  $10,000 slice; a stuck pending_new twin of the same intent now
  blocks BTC sells desk-wide through wash-trade 403s).
- File: desk3/aster_batch1.py (_positions).
- What broke: _positions keyed the snapshot's position rows by
  their raw symbol. The snapshot serves the broker positions
  endpoint's BARE form (BTCUSD); the native engine looks up the
  dashed internal form (BTC-USD) and then the slash broker form
  (BTC/USD). All missed, held qty read 0 forever, and the pair
  rebalance emitted its full ~$4,995 BTC delta every evaluation:
  one buy per cycle on 2026-11-07, two of them the identical qty
  0.065804301 because the price input is the last daily close.
  Note what did NOT cause it: runner restarts. The dispatcher is
  rebuilt per cycle by construction, so its slice book is empty
  every cycle in steady state too; the evaluator's own read-back
  was the only position truth it ever had, and the dialect
  mismatch blinded it.
- Fix: _positions re-keys ONLY the bare broker form (no dash, no
  slash, not the USDT internal form) through the ONE canonical
  translator, desk3/broker_dialect.normalize_broker_symbol (the
  same function reconcile already compares through). The bare
  form is the one form the broker emits that no sleeve's internal
  book uses, so the re-key cannot disturb internal-dialect
  contracts; rows are re-keyed, never edited. The first cure
  draft re-keyed every row and the gate caught it breaking the
  absorption family's USDT-keyed contract (2 failures in
  test_aster_batch1_blockers / test_aster_cure3_blockers); the
  gated form is the landed cure. With holdings visible the pair
  delta shrinks geometrically (target is 50% of cash +
  holdings), so the repeater self-extinguishes instead of
  compounding.
- Regression blocker: tests/test_curelane_20261108_blockers.py
  (TestNativePositionsDialect, 4 tests: bare-form keys normalized
  with equities untouched; internal-dialect keys untouched;
  held-at-weight emits nothing; held position shrinks the buy to
  the delta). Fail-first verified on the pre-cure shadow tree
  (the 3 cure tests fail behaviorally; the preservation test
  passes on both trees, as a control must).
- Named limit (not part of this cure): the evaluator still reads
  the AGGREGATE broker book, not the sleeve's own virtual book,
  and _cash returns the static slice cash. Both belong to the
  virtual-book serving build (see the cure lane report's write-up);
  this cure removes the blindness, not the aggregation.

- What broke: both consumers required proposals.json session ==
  today's ET CALENDAR date. The scan's own rule

  build_calendar use), consumed by both the producer and the
  warmer. Trading-day behavior is byte-identical; a wrong-session
  file is still rejected by name on any day.
