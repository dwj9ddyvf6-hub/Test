# The Lineup, Slate Four: 10 More Builds on Unbacked Teardowns (61 to 70)

Designed 2026-10-28 at the owner's direction. This is the final design slate: with it, the lineup stands at 70 tools designed, and design work closes. Building proceeds in the established order.

Every tool here follows the Slate Three Part B discipline: designed together with the backing it must create (fixtures from the vendor's own published material, a calculation or replica proof, a forward-grading cohort on the ledger), published only under the Proving Ground's rules (tool 60), in a visibly marked proving state until its backing matures.

### 61. Holly Ideas (Trade Ideas class)
- **Job:** an AI idea scanner in the Trade Ideas style: a stream of machine-generated trade ideas, each with its trigger and its own graded record attached.
- **Backing to create:** the idea types defined in writing from the teardown, fixture cases from the vendor's published examples, and a per-idea-type cohort. An idea type whose cohort grades dead leaves the stream and joins the Graveyard.

### 62. Agent Debate (TradingAgents class)
- **Job:** for one ticker, a structured bull case and bear case argued side by side from the same evidence pack, ending in a stated lean and the evidence that would change it.
- **Backing to create:** the debate is a presentation, so its backing is the lean's record: every published lean goes to the ledger and grades forward. The evidence pack is fixed in composition (price action, flow, positioning, filings) so two debates are comparable. Ships labelled as structured reading, never as a forecast, until its lean record matures, at which point its grade decides what it may call itself.

### 63. AI Chart Read (AInvest class)
- **Job:** an automated chart read in the AInvest style: trend, key levels, pattern labels drawn on the chart, in plain language.
- **Backing status:** the family's card work is partial (two of its semantics items still open from the recreation checklist); the chart read itself is unbacked.
- **Backing to create:** a labelled-chart fixture set: charts where the correct read is known by construction, scored mechanically. Below the fixture bar, it does not publish.

### 64. Reversal Wire (GainzAlgo class)
- **Job:** reversal buy and sell signals as a timestamped wire, every signal kept and graded, in the style of the reversal-signal vendors.
- **Backing to create:** the signal definition frozen from the recreation work, then a 30-session minimum cohort per market regime before any signal may appear without its proving banner. This family has attracted replicas before (including a peer's); ours publishes only its own graded record.

### 65. Alert Tape (Unusual Whales class)
- **Job:** the permanent archive behind the flow boards: every flow alert ever fired on a ticker, searchable, each with what happened afterward at 1, 5, and 21 sessions.
- **Relation to tools 4 and 56:** the Flow Tape is the live stream and the Flow Board is a daily ranking; the Alert Tape is the memory. Different moment: looking up a name's alert history before acting on a new one.
- **Backing to create:** capture integrity (alerts hash-timestamped at capture, no backfill) and per-alert-type outcome cohorts.

### 66. Breakout Scanner (Qullamaggie method class)
- **Job:** the torn-down momentum method as a scanner face, not a sleeve: episodic-pivot and breakout candidates near highs with tightness measures, published as candidates with their follow-through graded.
- **Note:** this is a reading tool over the method's published rules. The sleeve freeze is untouched; nothing here trades.
- **Backing to create:** the method's setup definitions frozen from the teardown with worked examples as fixtures, and a candidates cohort graded on follow-through, the same standard the desk applied when it buried a peer's flag setup on evidence.

### 67. One-Page Report (Stock Rover class)
- **Job:** one page per ticker that answers "what kind of company is this, in numbers": factor profile, growth, value, dividends, and peer comparison, all from named public data.
- **Backing status:** the factor-profile candidate was graded partial in the recreation checklist; this design completes it as a reader tool.
- **Backing to create:** every figure on the page reproducible from a named source field (the Receipts standard, tool 19), with a parity check against the source data on each refresh.

### 68. Pattern Draw (TradingKit class)
- **Job:** automatic drawing: trendlines, channels, and ranges drawn on any chart by rule, the way the charting toolkits do it, with the rule that drew each line visible on tap.
- **Backing to create:** a drawn-line fixture set on synthetic charts where the true lines are known by construction, plus a stability rule (a line may not repaint its history; revisions append, never rewrite).

### 69. Echo Lens (EchoAnalysis method class)
- **Job:** one analyst's published newsletter method as a chart lens: the method's own levels, reads, and conditions overlaid on any ticker.
- **Backing status:** the method was coded as an algorithm in desk work in October and used as a lens in a retired tool; as a public tool it is unbacked.
- **Backing to create:** the method's rules restated from the teardown with the author's own published examples as fixtures, and the lens's calls graded forward like any other source on the Echo Board's standard.

### 70. Momentum Clock (SystemTheory class)
- **Job:** long-only time-series momentum as a dashboard: for the major indexes and sectors, trend state by the torn-down method's rules, with the clock showing how long each state has held.
- **Backing status:** the desk's PhD-level replication of this backtest parked it over data ghosts; as a descriptive dashboard it needs no return claim, but its state definitions still need proof.
- **Backing to create:** state calculations fixture-checked on clean, de-ghosted data (the re-run already in motion in desk research is the source), and the dashboard graded as description only: it may report state durations, never performance, until a clean replication exists.

## Close of design

The lineup is complete at 70 designed tools: Slate One (1 to 20), Slate Two (21 to 40), Slate Three (41 to 60), Slate Four (61 to 70). No further design slates. Build order stands: Wave 1 first, one tool at a time, completely, with the honesty surfaces (The Record, Replication Wire, The Graveyard, The Proving Ground) early because everything else publishes through them.
