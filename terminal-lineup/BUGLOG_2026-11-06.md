# BUGLOG 2026-11-06

## GRADING-STREAM-20261106: live grading held the whole parsed ledger and died at exit -9 (CURED)

- Severity: lane-down (the forecast engine daily refresh failed
  three straight nights: 2026-11-04 runner kills across host
  restarts, 2026-11-05 SIGTERM about 85 minutes in, 2026-11-06
  the forecast-grading step killed at exit -9 about 44 minutes
  in; the lane's whole week carries no clean full run).
- What broke: forecast.run_grading held the entire parsed
  ledger in its process for the run's whole length, beside the
  full price panel, through the report aggregations as well.
  Measured on the live ledger (500,551,085 bytes, 253,163
  rows): one Ledger.rows() load peaks at 1,832.9 MB RSS and
  28.9 s. The runner verified through rows(), and
  Ledger.append_grade additionally scanned rows() on every
  grade (the classic grading of one 6,000-row prefix took
  54.9 s against the streaming form's 1.3 s), so one process
  sat at multiple GB resident for 44-plus minutes with other
  lanes on the host; the kernel took the largest process
  (exit -9 on the grading child; the runner survived to report
  it). Side damage: the killed run's grade appends landed
  without the closing attestation regeneration, leaving the
  two attestation recount tests red until the next completed
  grading run regenerates (verified pre-existing on the
  pre-cure tree, not a cure regression).
- Fix at the source (all inside forecast/, classic paths kept
  for every other caller):
  - ledger.Ledger.iter_rows(): streaming row reader, same
    parse and same LedgerError contract as rows(), nothing
    retained, cache untouched.
  - ledger.Ledger.grading_stream_state(): one chain-verifying
    streaming pass (a broken link or unparseable line raises,
    the append path's refusal semantics) collecting only the
    tail hash, the graded-id set, the void map, the grade
    count and the context rows grade_forecast scans for
    (GRADING_CONTEXT_KINDS: first_failure_side,
    league_interval, league_ranking; 28 rows in the live
    ledger).
  - ledger.Ledger.append_grade(stream_state=...): the same
    frozen-label validation, refusal checks and messages
    against the state's sets, the same appended body, written
    through _append_streaming; a file that moved under the
    state is rebuilt once and rechecked.
  - grading.run_grading_streaming(): run_grading's predicates,
    order and skipped accounting over iter_rows(), with
    grade_forecast served by a shim whose rows() returns only
    the context rows and whose append_grade routes through
    the stream state.
  - proof_gate.grade_forward_record(stream_state=...)
    pass-through (default unchanged).
  - forecast.run_grading: verifies and grades by streaming;
    its proof-gate pass walks iter_rows() against the state's
    graded ids; every printed line unchanged.
  - forecast.run_grading_reports (new): the overlap, paired
    baseline, HAR control and cell family aggregations run in
    a child process that loads the rows exactly once and
    exits, so the one remaining full parse is isolated and
    freed with the child; a child failure is the step's
    failure, reported by the runner.
- Proof: equivalence on twin 6,000-row prefixes of the live
  ledger: classic 3,997 new grades / 604 skipped in 54.9 s;
  streaming 3,997 / 604 with byte-identical appended lines and
  equal skipped lists, chain verifying after. Proof-gate
  equivalence on a forged fixture (one resolvable, one
  waiting, one off-panel record): counts [1, 1, 1] on both
  routes, files byte-identical. (No proof-gate grade exists in
  the ledger's history yet: 79,365 forward records, first
  horizons completing about 2026-11-11, so the live run
  exercises the waiting and off-panel counts only.)
- Regression blocker: forecast/tests/
  test_grading_stream_20261106.py (6 tests): the streaming
  path completes with Ledger.rows() rigged to raise while the
  same rig stops the classic driver at once; byte-equivalence
  of appended grades on twin prefixes and on the proof-gate
  fixture; streaming refusal messages for double-grade and
  voided forecasts; broken-chain refusal; the reports child
  loads rows exactly once. Fail direction verified by
  temporary revert: on the pre-cure tree the file errors at
  collection (the streaming surface does not exist); on the
  cured tree, 6 passed. Adjacent suites re-run on the cured
  tree: proofgate append-stream, proofgate streaming, proof
  gate, chain content and grading construction all green
  (the proofgate streaming and proof gate files first showed
  failures that were an artifact of the host's 512 MB /tmp
  tmpfs filled by pytest temp dirs, ENOSPC on fixture writes;
  after clearing the temp dirs both files pass whole, 18
  passed); the two attestation recount failures reproduce
  identically on the pre-cure tree (the killed run's
  unregenerated appends, above) and are expected to clear
  when a completed grading run regenerates attestations.
