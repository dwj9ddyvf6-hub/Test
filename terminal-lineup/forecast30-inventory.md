# Forecast-30: Invention Inventory (refresh phase)

Date: 2026-10-28. Purpose: the grounded inventory the Forecast-30 design phase must draw from, and nothing else. **No engines are designed in this document.**

Verdict vocabulary, used exactly:
- **validated**: held out of sample or forward, replicated in a second study or dataset (evidence grade A, or a distributional construction that recomputes exactly and is calibration-checked). Note: in this corpus "validated" never means a validated directional edge at tradeable strength; see the kill list and section 2 caveats.
- **killed**: tested against a proper control (random entries, shuffle, holdout, or pre-registered replication) and failed, or graded X in the graded evidence registry.
- **untested**: mechanism exists (built, fixtured, or described) but no forward or holdout directional test of it exists in the record.
- **vendor-claim-only**: the only support in the corpus is the vendor's own claim, marketing, or an unbacked teardown (no test or evidence entries behind it).

Principal sources, by short name used in the tables:
- **01** = `analysis/01-CORPUS-MAP.md`; **02** = `analysis/02-FIDELITY-AND-EVIDENCE.md`; **03** = `analysis/03-CONFLICTS-AND-NOVELTY.md` (conflicts C1-C18, novelty N1-N12).
- **EV** = `terminal-lineup/records/forecast-search-2026-10-27/EVIDENCE.md` (graded registry: T timing, S selection, A avoid, V size/vol, E exit, X execution, D data, AD added findings; grades A/B/C/V/X/P).
- **PICKER** = `terminal-lineup/records/picker-replay-2026-10-27/picker-replay-2026-10-27.md` (3,446 names, 296,093 cases, 2019-12-20 to 2026-10-15).
- **FACTORY** = Forecast Factory rerun record (03 C12; grade-a-board data): first search 15,135 cells over 6,385 names, window 2016-01-04 to 2026-10-19; 1,217 promoted; pre-registered rerun kept 63 grade A, 337 confirmed, 564 not replicated, 253 untestable.
- **SITE** = terminal site `data.json` receipts spot-read 2026-10-28 (named per row in section 4).
- **70** = `terminal-lineup/THE-70-TOOLS.md` (tools already built; an inventory item being already tooled does not make it validated).

Standing fact that frames everything below (REA-ANALYSIS-SUMMARY, 03 C11-C13): **nothing in the corpus has a validated directional edge.** The Board score does not rank returns, the picker replay passed nothing, and the textbook pullback died against random entries. What has positive evidence is narrower: timing lifts that random entries share, a set of cross-sectional selection and avoid effects with B/A grades on specific universes, volatility (not direction) forecasting, and distributional constructions that are exactly computable and calibration-checked.
