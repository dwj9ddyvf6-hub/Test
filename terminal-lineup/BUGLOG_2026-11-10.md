- Severity: capture-fatal for the last scheduled snapshot of
  the session for two of three symbols (the chain-snapshot
  collector's 2026-11-09T20:57:21Z run stored SPY, then landed
  manifest status=error rows for QQQ and IWM:
  "RuntimeError: spot quote for QQQ is stale
  (quote_ts=2026-11-09T20:56:17+00:00); refusing to store a
  snapshot on a stale live quote", and the same for IWM at
  20:56:18Z). The canonical daily files for QQQ and IWM stayed
  at the 20:27Z capture; no 20:57Z capture exists for them.
- File: terminal-lineup/site/collect_chains.py
  (fetch_spot_fresh added; the main loop's spot fetch routed
  through it; the store README's gate sentence updated in both
  the code constant and the stored copy).
- What broke and root cause: the freshness gate is
  terminal_common.quote_freshness, 90 seconds, fail-closed,
  anchored at the spot fetch moment (CHAIN-SPOT-ANCHOR-20261031).
  The collector fetched the spot exactly once per symbol and
  refused on that single verdict. The symbols are processed in
  sequence and each symbol's Alpaca content leg runs between
  the previous symbol's spot fetch and this one's, so by the
  time QQQ and IWM were reached their fetch returned a Yahoo
  regularMarketTime that had lagged at the source (20:56:17Z /
  20:56:18Z, the same minute SPY's passing quote carried) and
  its age had crossed 90 seconds. That the source's timestamp
  advances between fetches is proven by the surrounding
  captures: on 2026-11-04, 2026-11-06 and the 2026-11-09 20:27Z
  run, QQQ's and IWM's stored spot_quote_ts values land roughly
  a minute after SPY's in the same run. A single stale verdict
  was therefore a curable fetch state, not a market fact, and
  the collector treated it as final. The gate itself is
  correct and was not widened: a threshold change that
  tolerates stale data is a fence, not a cure.
- Fix: fetch_spot_fresh(symbol, require_fresh) re-fetches the
  quote when the verdict is not fresh, inside a bounded spaced
  budget (SPOT_ATTEMPTS = 3 fetches in total, sleeps 10s and
  30s, the FINRA lanes' retry shape), and returns the first
  fresh result. A quote still stale after the last attempt is
  returned stale, so the existing fail-closed refusal fires on
  the last quote actually seen, with its timestamp. When
  freshness is not required (outside the market window, the
  --force seed path, where a closed market's quote can never
  turn fresh) exactly one fetch is made. No other behaviour
  changed: the gate, the anchor rule, the manifest error shape
  and the one-spot IV ledger rule all stand.
- Blocker: forecast/tests/test_chain_spot_retry_20261109.py
  (4 checks: stale-then-fresh stores on the fresh quote and
  waits before re-fetching; persistent stale returns the last
  stale after exactly SPOT_ATTEMPTS fetches; fresh first fetch
  is never re-fetched; no retry when freshness is not
  required). Fail-first verified: 4 failed with AttributeError
  (no fetch_spot_fresh) on the pre-cure code, 4 passed on the
  cured code; the neighbouring test_chain_collector.py still
  passes (14 passed together).
- Live proof, bounded by the clock: the cure landed at about
  05:10Z on 2026-11-10, outside the market window, and a
  --force run tonight would mint a mis-dated pre-market
  snapshot, so no live capture is claimed for the retry leg;
  its proof is the blocker plus the next scheduled in-window
