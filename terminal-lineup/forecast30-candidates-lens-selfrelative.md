# Forecast-30 Candidates — Lens: SELF-RELATIVE AND IDIOSYNCRATIC (ascend N10)

Date: 2026-10-28. Lens rule: every input is a name measured against its OWN history only. No cross-sectional rank, no SPY-relative level except as the grading benchmark, no vendor score. Cross-name information enters only at grading time, never at forecast time.

Grounding read for this pass: `forecast30-inventory.md` (mechanisms, survivors §2.1, kills §3, data-in-hand §4), `03-CONFLICTS-AND-NOVELTY.md` (N10, N5, N3, N1), `REA-ANALYSIS-SUMMARY.md`, `THE-70-TOOLS.md` (non-duplication check against all 70).

Why this lens has the corpus's best prior: the single strongest surviving directional idea in the record is self-relative. F3 pullback depth vs the stock's own norm: IC +0.026 / +0.031 (t 4.3 / 4.3) at 20d across 2016-22 and 2023+, 60d 2023+ IC +0.062 (t 9.8), inventory §2.1 grade B, novelty N10. Its textbook sibling (fixed pullback to the 20/50-day) was killed against random entries in the same names (+0.05R vs random +0.08R, picker replay 296,093 cases passed nothing). The difference between the killed and surviving versions is exactly the self-normalisation. This lens generalises that one difference to every measurable family.

Honesty law applied to every candidate below: (a) falsifiable forecast stated as quantity + horizon + direction/distribution; (b) ledgered in the append-only ledger (N1) before the horizon opens; (c) graded at fixed horizons, losers kept in the denominator, abstentions counted and published; (d) all inputs here are ONE evidence family (price/bars, own-history transforms of it) under N3, so no candidate may claim multi-family confirmation, and agreement between two candidates in this file counts once; (e) data route named from inventory §4 only; (f) ships in proving state until its own forward record matures. Where a candidate cannot yet meet a clause, that clause is named in its entry. Default grading for all: primary horizon as stated, graded from next open (corpus E3/X: next-open entry is fine, edges decay in 1-5 sessions, best horizon about 60 sessions), excess vs SPY over the identical window, plus calibration grading for every distributional forecast (predicted vs realised coverage in 10 bins). Pre-registered kill rule for all: retire to the Graveyard (tool 50) if, after 200 matured non-abstain forecasts, the primary metric's 95% CI includes zero (directional) or coverage error exceeds 10 points (distributional).

Non-duplication note: Tool 17 Pullback Norm is a descriptive pane whose local study has never run; Tool 20 State Analogue is a MARKET-level analogue with 0 of 126 sessions accrued; Tool 43 Fear-Day Forecaster and Tool 49 Survivors Wire publish survivor cohorts, not per-name calibrated distributions; Tool 26 IV Weather is a percentile reading, not a forecast. Every candidate below is a ledgered, graded forecaster, which none of those is.

## SR-1. Own-Norm Depth Forecaster (F3, promoted from finding to calibrated engine)

- **Falsifiable forecast:** For each uptrend name on each close, publish P(20-session excess return vs SPY > 0) and the predicted median 20-session excess return, as a function of today's pullback depth expressed as a percentile of that name's own pullback-depth distribution over its trailing 750 sessions. Sharp form: names in their own deepest depth quintile are forecast median excess +0.8 pts or more above names in their own shallowest quintile at 20 sessions (the cross-era quintile spread on record is +0.81 pts/20d, 2023+).
- **Mechanism:** Depth = (own 63-session high − close) / own median true range, converted to an own-history percentile (leave-today-out). Depth percentile maps to the forecast through a monotone curve fitted only on DEEP 2016-22, frozen, never refit on holdout or forward data. Fear-day interaction (F3 was measured incl. fear days) is a published sub-forecast, not a separate confirmation (same family, N3).
- **Corpus parts:** xcheck F3 / N10 (the mechanism and its numbers); picker A0-A2 (the killed fixed-band version this replaces); N1 ledger; E3 horizon discipline.
- **Why new:** No vendor in the corpus normalises pullback depth by the name's own distribution (03 N10 vendor check). Tools 17/49 display the idea; neither issues a calibrated per-name probability ledgered before the horizon.
- **Data route:** DEEP daily bars 2016-2026, 6,385 names incl. delisted (inventory §4.1) for fitting/backfill; shipped daily history 1,833 names × 399 sessions for the live forward feed. Exists today.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger before next open; grade at exactly 20 sessions (secondary 60, where the record is strongest, t 9.8); Brier score on the probability, median-error on the point forecast, quintile spread vs the +0.8 pt claim. Meets (a)-(f) in full.

## SR-2. Per-Name Self-Analogue Forecaster (P_NN turned inward)

- **Falsifiable forecast:** For each name, publish the full predicted distribution (10th/50th/90th percentile) of its own +21-session return, taken as the distribution of +21-session returns that followed the nearest one-sixth of that name's OWN past states. Calibration claim: realised returns fall inside the published 10-90 band 80% of the time, ±5 pts.
- **Mechanism:** Ascends N5 / Studies S6: the five normalised coordinates (price position, volume, gap, drawdown, range, all own-norm z-scores using the desk-fixed MAD-to-z constant 1.4826, stamped OURS in S6) are matched only within the same ticker's history (minimum 750 own sessions, else abstain). The market-level P_NN has never rendered (0/126 sessions); the per-name version has no accrual wait because each name's analogue library is its own completed history in DEEP.
- **Corpus parts:** SqueezeMetrics A106-A110 published coordinates via Morgue `studies.py` S6 (N5, recombined); N1 ledger; AD-57's lesson (vendor analogue/touch constructions overstate) enforced as the calibration kill rule.
- **Why new:** Every analogue in the corpus (S6, S21 mined analogs, killed at IC +0.0046) matches a name against OTHER names/days or the market against itself. Self-only matching, where the peer group is the name's own past, appears in no vendor entry and no built tool. S21's kill is the prior to beat, and it was cross-sectional; this is a different construction, stated as such.
- **Data route:** DEEP daily bars (own histories to 2016). IV coordinate of S6 is dropped: the IV ledger (60 names, ≤12 readings, stopped 2026-10-14, §4.2) cannot support it, so the state vector is 4 price coordinates and says so. Exists today.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger before next open; grade at 21 sessions; coverage grading per name pooled and per name; a name whose band coverage misses by >15 pts over 100 forecasts is suspended, not silently kept. Meets (a)-(f); note the dropped IV coordinate is a scope reduction, not an unmet clause.

## SR-3. Personal Seasonality Clock

- **Falsifiable forecast:** For each name with ≥6 years of history, publish its predicted median next-5-session return for the current position in its own calendar fingerprint (day-of-month bucket × days-since-own-last-earnings-report bucket), estimated leave-one-cycle-out from its own past only. Claim graded per name: the fingerprint's top-quartile phases beat its bottom-quartile phases by a stated, per-name spread at 5 sessions.
- **Mechanism:** Calendar effects market-wide are killed (EV T10, grade X: turn-of-month is descriptive only). This engine makes the opposite bet the corpus has never tested: that any calendar regularity is idiosyncratic (a name's own reporting rhythm, its own month-end holder flows), so the fingerprint is estimated per name with shrinkage to zero, and the engine abstains unless the name's own phase spread cleared a within-name shuffle null on its own history.
- **Corpus parts:** T10 (the kill this must not resurrect: no market-wide calendar term is used); D1 lesson (shared synth nulls gave 21-67% false positives, so the null here is per-name permutation of that name's own phase labels); N10 self-relative principle.
- **Why new:** No teardown sells per-name seasonality; every calendar product in the corpus is market-wide, and market-wide is exactly what the record killed.
- **Data route:** DEEP daily bars 2016-2026 plus EDGAR earnings dates as used by Morgue Studies Fix B (inventory §4.3/§4.4), with the D5 caveat on record (4.2% of winter earnings dates were one session late, fixed). Exists today.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger before next open on every trading day the phase is in a published quartile; grade at 5 sessions; per-name and pooled; phases that fail forward are removed per name, publicly. **Partially unmet: (a) sharpness is per-name, so names whose own-history spread is small publish a near-zero forecast that is falsifiable but weak; the engine abstains there rather than padding, and the abstention rate is part of the grade.**

## SR-4. Own-Volatility Normalisation Clock

- **Falsifiable forecast:** When a name's 10-session realised vol exceeds the 90th percentile of its own 3-year vol distribution, publish the predicted number of sessions until its 10-session realised vol returns below its own median, as a distribution (median and 10-90 band). Claim: realised normalisation time falls inside the band 80% ±5 pts of the time, and the median forecast beats the name's own unconditional median normalisation time by ≥20% in mean absolute error.
- **Mechanism:** Volatility forecasting is the corpus's one grade-A family (V1, HAR: long-scale R² gain +0.127/+0.116, t 19/20), but every built version forecasts the market's or a tier's vol LEVEL. This forecasts a per-name first-passage TIME, driven by the name's own vol-of-vol percentile, own vol spike size in own-norm units, and own historical decay rate after its own past spikes.
- **Corpus parts:** EV V1/V4 (vol from vol's own past, validated for size never direction); AD-56 (HAR × 0.88 calibration method, transferred from SPY bands to per-name decay); V6 (snap-back size scales with own jaggedness, grade C, used as a covariate only).
- **Why new:** A time-to-normal forecast per name exists in no vendor teardown and none of the 70 tools (Expected Move Map gives a price band for an index, not a per-name vol clock). Direction is never claimed; this is a size/time engine, which is where the corpus's evidence actually lives.
- **Data route:** DEEP daily bars; shipped daily history live. Exists today.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger at spike close; grade at realised normalisation or censor at 63 sessions (censored cases stay in the denominator as band-misses if the band's 90th percentile is < 63); coverage + MAE vs the own-unconditional baseline. Meets (a)-(f) in full.

## SR-5. Deviation-from-Self Index (the universal own-z input, traded as one forecast)

- **Falsifiable forecast:** Publish, per name per close, a single Self-Deviation Score: the signed sum of own-norm z-scores of (i) close vs own 200-session mean in own-vol units, (ii) range vs own range norm, (iii) volume vs own volume norm, (iv) 5-session return vs own 5-session return distribution. Forecast: at 10 sessions, names with SDS < −2 (unusually depressed vs themselves on all four at once) earn median excess vs SPY at least 1.0 pt above names with SDS > +2, and the relationship is monotone across SDS quintiles. Any non-monotone forward result falsifies it.
- **Mechanism:** Generalises F3's one-variable self-normalisation to a composite, with the corpus's own warnings built in: stacking reversal confirmations was killed (S19: 3+ signs −0.02R, 4+ −0.15R vs random +0.07R), so the four components enter as continuous z-scores (never as sign counts), weights fixed on 2016-22 DEEP only, and the random-entry control in the same names (the picker standard) is republished beside every grade.
- **Corpus parts:** N10/F3 (self-normalisation); S19 (the kill that dictates continuous-not-counted construction); PICKER (control standard); reset2 pass set (f_tight, x_atrpct, x_dd signs as sanity references, not inputs to copy).
- **Why new:** Vendor composites (Leadr Board, Chaikin, Danelfin, all vendor-claim-only as rankers) score a name against fixed cross-sectional transforms. An all-own-history composite with frozen weights and a published random control is in no teardown.
- **Data route:** DEEP daily bars; shipped daily history live. Exists today.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger before next open; grade at 10 sessions (secondary 20); quintile monotonicity test per grading window; family discount: SDS is one price-family signal (N3) and is graded as a rival to SR-1, never averaged with it as confirmation. Meets (a)-(f) in full.

## SR-6. Fear Personality Forecaster (own snap-back, sized by own history)

- **Falsifiable forecast:** On a fear day (tool 16 definition: SPY −1.5%, or VIX +15% closing ≥25, or VIX ≥20 on a SPY/QQQ down day), for each uptrend name publish its predicted 5-session snap-back return = its own median 5-session return after its own past fear-day drops of similar own-norm size, with a 10-90 band from those same own episodes. Claim: pooled realised snap-back falls in-band 80% ±6 pts, and the per-name forecasts rank realised snap-backs with IC > +0.03 (the cross-era fear-personality holdout IC on record is +0.050, t 5.8, but discovery was 0.008, so +0.03 is the honest bar).
- **Mechanism:** Ascends EV S9 (fear personality, grade C, holdout-only, explicitly "forward-score pending" — this engine IS that forward score) and S4/S6 innocent-bystander and gap-absorbed evidence, but the forecast quantity is the name's own recovery distribution, not a cross-sectional rank. T1's lesson is printed on every card: random entries share the fear-day lift (+0.29 to +0.36R), so the grade is forecast-minus-random-in-same-names, never raw snap-back.
- **Corpus parts:** S9, S4, S5, T1, F5/F7 RESET RS evidence (§2.1); tool 16 fear-day definition verbatim.
- **Why new:** Fear-Day Forecaster (tool 43) publishes a supported cohort; no tool forecasts each name's own recovery size from its own fear-day history with a random-entry benchmark.
- **Data route:** DEEP daily bars + VIX daily (SPY/QQQ 2010-2026 band-study series, §4.1). Live constraint on record: the shipped terminal's local fear-day record has only 1 qualifying day (2026-10-16), so forward accrual is slow by nature. Exists today.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger at fear-day close before the 5-session horizon; grade at 5 sessions vs the same-names random control; **(f) is met but the proving state will be long: fear days are rare, and the candidate is marked as slow-to-mature rather than given a shorter, dishonest horizon.**

## SR-7. Own Drawdown Anatomy Forecaster (RESET, per-name)

- **Falsifiable forecast:** For each name currently 35-80% below its own high, publish P(it reclaims its own 50-session average within 60 sessions) and predicted median 60-session return, from three own-history quantities only: current drawdown depth as a percentile of its own past drawdowns, time-under-water as a percentile of its own past underwater spells, and its own median recovery speed from its own past drawdowns of similar depth. Claim: top-tercile predicted-P names reclaim at ≥1.5× the rate of bottom-tercile names.
- **Mechanism:** The deep-reset family has real component evidence (F7 laggard RS IC −0.041 both eras; F6 capitulation volume; N9 RESET composite fear-day IC +0.053/+0.028 holdout-confirmed) but the picker forms failed (B1 +0.327R, CI [−0.205, +0.859], did not pass). The self-relative turn: B1 used fixed thresholds (35-80%, reclaim 50d) for every name; this engine asks whether THIS depth is deep FOR THIS NAME and whether THIS name has historically recovered from depths like this.
- **Corpus parts:** PICKER B0-B3; F5/F6/F7; N9 RESET; reset2 caveat carried on the card (deeper drawdown predicts worse returns pooled, x_dd −): the engine must beat that pooled prior per name or abstain.
- **Why new:** No vendor or tool grades a drawdown against the same name's own drawdown anatomy. Odds Cards (tool 46) gives family-level historical odds, not per-name anatomy forecasts.
- **Data route:** DEEP daily bars (needs the delisted names, which DEEP has and the 1,838 roster does not — survivorship caveat D3 printed on every output). Exists today.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger at first close inside the drawdown band (one forecast per episode, re-entry after exit only, so episodes cannot be double-counted); grade at 60 sessions; reclaim rate ratio and median-return calibration. Meets (a)-(f) in full.

## SR-8. Idiosyncratic-or-Liquidity Splitter (F4 as a conditional forecaster)

- **Falsifiable forecast:** For each name down ≥2 own-residual-vol units today, classify the drop as idiosyncratic (SPY flat/up, name's own residual move dominates) or liquidity-driven (market-coincident), and publish opposite-signed forecasts: idiosyncratic drops forecast 20-session excess ≤ −0.5 pts median (persistence); liquidity-driven drops of the same own-norm size on fear days forecast 20-session excess ≥ +0.5 pts median (reversal). Either leg landing on the wrong side of zero falsifies that leg.
- **Mechanism:** Directly ascends F4 (company-specific declines do worse: IC −0.012, −0.022 at 60d 2023+; filed as avoid A15, grade B) against T2 (short-horizon reversal in stress, grade A, the corpus's only A directional row) and resolves their apparent contradiction the way the corpus itself separates them: information-driven declines persist, liquidity-driven declines reverse. The split variable is the name's own residual share, scaled by its own residual-vol history, never a fixed % threshold.
- **Corpus parts:** F4/A15; T2; N1 innocent-bystander (the liquidity leg's cousin); D2 method law (leave-out centred means wherever an own mean is used, to avoid the mechanical reversal bias the registry names).
- **Why new:** The corpus holds both halves as separate findings and never combines them into one conditional, opposite-signed forecaster. No vendor teardown conditions reversal-vs-persistence on the name's own residual decomposition.
- **Data route:** DEEP daily bars + SPY daily. Exists today.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger at drop close; grade each leg separately at 20 sessions, legs never netted against each other; a leg is killed independently if its median CI includes zero after 150 episodes. Meets (a)-(f) in full.

## SR-9. Own Capitulation Volume Clock (F6, sized and timed)

- **Falsifiable forecast:** When a name in a drawdown prints down-volume above the 95th percentile of its own down-volume distribution AND trade/range activity above its own 90th percentile, publish (i) predicted median 20-session excess vs SPY ≥ +1.0 pt (cross-era F6 quintile spreads: +0.95 / +1.28 pts) and (ii) predicted probability ≥60% that its volume returns below its own median within 10 sessions (capitulation exhaustion). Both quantities graded; either failing falsifies its half.
- **Mechanism:** F6 (high recent down volume in resets is better: IC +0.040/+0.020, both eras) plus new10 N6 volume-confirmed capitulation (IC +0.016 t 4.2 / +0.015 t 3.9 all-days), with both thresholds converted from cross-sectional ranks to own-history percentiles. The avoid-side mirror is respected: trade-count surges are a strong AVOID in A9 for stretched names, so this engine fires only inside a drawdown (own price below own 200-session mean), never on up-moves.
- **Corpus parts:** F6, N6 (§2.1); A9 (the context gate); reset2 f_voldie (−) carried as a named tension: in the reset2 pass set, volume-dying predicted differently, so the card prints that the two studies define the volume state differently and this engine follows F6's definition verbatim.
- **Why new:** Volume products in the corpus (Unusual Volume, tool 38) flag abnormality cross-sectionally or against a short own baseline descriptively; none issues a dual return-plus-exhaustion forecast from own-percentile capitulation inside own drawdowns.
- **Data route:** DEEP daily bars (volume on daily bars only; the AGENTS.md Yahoo-hourly-volume corruption law bars any hourly-volume variant — Alpaca SIP hourly is shallow from 2026-03-17, §4.1, and is not used). Exists today.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger at trigger close; grade return leg at 20 sessions, exhaustion leg at 10; episodes deduplicated per name (10-session lockout). Meets (a)-(f) in full.

## SR-10. Per-Name Gap-Fill Forecaster (Studies S1, made personal)

- **Falsifiable forecast:** For each overnight gap in a name, publish P(gap fills the same session) computed from that name's own past gaps in the same own-norm size band (gap as a multiple of the name's own median gap), plus the predicted fill time distribution when it does fill. Calibration claim: predicted vs realised fill rate within 5 pts in each published size band, pooled after 300 gaps.
- **Mechanism:** Morgue Studies S1 / Gap-Fill Pane (tool 11) publishes market-wide gap-fill distributions split by size and weekday, descriptive only. This engine re-estimates the entire distribution per name: some names are habitual fillers, some are habitual runners, and the corpus has never tested whether that habit is stable within a name (split-half stability on the name's own history is a pre-condition to publish; unstable names abstain).
- **Corpus parts:** Studies S1 fixture-proven distributions; gap_share conditional evidence S15 (gap behaviour carries information in the top VIX third, IC +0.069/+0.074) as plausibility, not as an input; N7 gap-down absorbed as a sub-forecast for down-gaps in own uptrends.
- **Why new:** Every gap product in the corpus is pooled across names. A per-name, stability-gated fill probability is a new object, and it is honestly a probability engine, not a direction call.
- **Data route:** DEEP daily bars give open-vs-prior-close gaps for the history; same-session fill needs intraday lows/highs, available live from the daily bar's own high/low (fill = intraday range reaching prior close), so daily bars suffice for grading. Live intraday Studies figures were no-data in the recorded run (Alpaca route, §4.1), so the live feed uses the shipped daily history plus each completed daily bar. Exists today.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger pre-open after the gap prints (before the fill window opens, satisfying (b) for a same-session horizon); grade at that session's close; per-band calibration, abstentions published. Meets (a)-(f) in full.

## SR-11. Own Trend-Age Hazard Forecaster

- **Falsifiable forecast:** For each name in an uptrend, publish the hazard: P(its uptrend ends, defined as a close below its own 50-session average, within the next 21 sessions), as a function of current trend age divided by that name's own median historical trend length. Claim: names past 1.5× their own median trend age end their trends within 21 sessions at ≥1.4× the rate of names under 0.5× their own median age.
- **Mechanism:** Trend age cross-sectionally is unstable (new10 N8: IC −0.014 discovery vs +0.039 holdout, grade C, "not a survivor"). The self-relative hypothesis for why: a 60-session trend is old for a name whose trends last 30 and young for a name whose trends last 200, so pooling by raw age scrambles the variable. Normalising age by own median trend length is the untested fix, stated as a hypothesis, not a finding.
- **Corpus parts:** N8 (the instability this design answers); T4 leader-tape gate as context printed on the card (leader setups lose when SPY >10% below its high), not as an input; E3 (21 sessions is inside the corpus's useful horizon).
- **Why new:** No vendor sells trend age in units of the name's own trend lifespan; Trade Ideas / Minervini-style age and stage rules in the teardowns use fixed session counts for all names.
- **Data route:** DEEP daily bars; shipped daily history live. Exists today.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger weekly per trending name (one live forecast per name, superseded forecasts stay ledgered and graded, never deleted); grade at 21 sessions; hazard ratio vs the 1.4× claim. Meets (a)-(f) in full, with N8's grade-C status printed on the card as the prior.

## SR-12. Own Mean-Reversion Half-Life Forecaster

- **Falsifiable forecast:** For each name, estimate its own half-life of deviation from its own 200-session mean (from its own deviation series, rolling 3-year window, names whose deviation is non-stationary on their own history abstain). Publish the point forecast: expected deviation in 10 sessions = today's deviation × 2^(−10 / own half-life), converted to a predicted 10-session return. Claim: the point forecast's mean absolute error beats two named baselines, (i) zero-change and (ii) the name's own unconditional 10-session median return, by ≥10% pooled.
- **Mechanism:** A per-name Ornstein-Uhlenbeck read on the deviation series. The corpus kills generic mean-reversion stacking (S19) and analog IC (S21), but never estimates a per-name decay constant and forecasts the decay path itself; the forecast is graded as a point forecast against baselines, not as a trade, which is the grading the construction can honestly support. D2's leave-out rule applies to the 200-session mean (leave-today-out) so the deviation is not mechanically self-referential.
- **Corpus parts:** D2 (method law); S19/S21 (kills respected: no sign-stacking, no cross-name analogues); V1's template (a quantity forecasting itself from its own past is the corpus's validated shape, here applied to deviation instead of vol).
- **Why new:** Half-life is a standard textbook quantity but appears in no vendor teardown in the corpus as a per-name published forecast, and in none of the 70 tools (Momentum Clock, tool 70, ranks raw multi-horizon returns cross-sectionally, explicitly without performance language).
- **Data route:** DEEP daily bars; shipped daily history live. Exists today.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger at close; grade at 10 sessions against both baselines; per-name MAE published for the top universe, pooled MAE is the headline; kill rule as in the header. Meets (a)-(f) in full.

## Explicitly NOT proposed from this lens (and why)

The IV ledger holds 60 names, at most 12 readings each, freshest 2026-10-14 (inventory §4.2/§4.5 gap 7). No candidate in this file uses IV. It becomes proposable only when the ledger resumes accrual and a name holds ≥126 own readings, the same floor S6 set for itself.
- **Own short-interest / short-volume forecaster:** the self-relative version of A11/R1 is attractive but R1 is already data-blocked (D7: FINRA compliant route keeps 12 months; study period unreachable) and the Short Volume Ledger has stored 0 days (§4.5 gap 4). Marked as failing (e) at per-name depth today; the pooled versions already live in tools 15/22.
- **Per-name intraday self-analogues:** hourly history starts 2026-03-17 at the earliest and covers 41 names (AD-09). Fails (e). Daily-bar self-relativity is where the data actually is.
