# The 70 Tools: the full free terminal lineup

All free. No paywalls, no signups, no logins, nothing for sale.
Status as of 2026-10-28: all 70 tools built and independently verified (design closed at 70 by the owner's word).

## Slate One (1 to 20): the proven core

1. **Morning Map** — Built. The pre-open positioning picture for SPY, QQQ, and IWM in one glance: walls, flip levels, dark-pool reading.
2. **The Record** — Built. The public scoreboard: every call the terminal has published, graded at fixed horizons, losers included, nothing edited.
3. **Qualified Board** — Built. The names that passed a strict, explained gate, with the reason every qualifier qualified and every failure failed.
4. **Flow Tape** — Built. The intraday options-print tape at a glance: heat, unusual prints, top positions.
5. **Golden Sweep Wire** — Built. One alert type only: golden sweeps, counted as their 30-session proving record accrues (0 prints so far, decision December 8, 2026).
6. **Payoff Sketch** — Built. Draw any multi-leg options spread's expiration payoff: profit and loss at every price, breakevens, max gain and loss. Runs in the reader's browser.
7. **Expected Move Map** — Built. The priced range for an index, day by day and week by week, from the fear gauge construction on record.
8. **The Screener** — Built. Filter the scored universe by plain conditions (sector, index, score, price, checks in favor) in the browser.
9. **Portfolio Builder** — Built. Turns the day's passing names into an equal-weight portfolio sketch, sector by sector, under stated freshness gates.
10. **Activity Profile** — Built. One index's options activity profile: where its flow concentrated and how complete the record is. Its 20-session baseline is still accruing (1 of 20).
11. **Gap-Fill Pane** — Built. How often opening gaps like today's filled the same day, and how that splits by gap size and weekday.
12. **Opening Range Pane** — Built. What usually follows a break of the first hour's range: one side only, both sides, or neither, and how far breaks extend.
13. **Earnings Drift Pane** — Built. What AAPL, MSFT, and NVDA actually did on their report days across the reports on record.
14. **Wall Check** — Built. Grades the Morning Map's walls and flip levels against the sessions that followed them.
15. **Squeeze Watch** — Built. Short interest by name with days to cover; names above the published 15% bar flagged (CBRL and GO today).
16. **Fear Day List** — Built, unpublished state. On fear days, the names the fear-day evidence supports. Its study has never run, so today it shows the definition and the one qualifying day on record (October 16).
17. **Pullback Norm** — Built, unpublished state. Pullbacks judged deep or shallow against the stock's own history. Its local study has not run yet; the page carries the research record behind the idea, labelled as another study's.
18. **The Second Opinion** — Built. One ticker in, one honest count out: how many independent families of evidence speak for the name, with correlated sources counted once, never stacked.
19. **The Receipts** — Built. Every tool's receipt in plain words: what record its figures come from, when it was made, how complete it is, its grade so far, and what is not recorded.
20. **State Analogue** — Built, counter state. Describes today in five market readings, finds past days most like today, shows what followed. Needs 126 days of readings; 0 accrued so far, and the page counts honestly.
