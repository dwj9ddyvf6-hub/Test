# Forecast-30 Candidates — Lens: META-FORECASTING (forecast the forecasters)

Date: 2026-10-28. Lens rule: every other signal, vendor claim, promoted cell and built tool is a population to be modelled, not a source to be trusted. An engine here forecasts a property OF forecasters (their next-period accuracy, calibration, decay, crowding, or disagreement structure), never re-issues their directional calls as its own.

Grounding read: `forecast30-inventory.md` (all sections), `analysis/03-CONFLICTS-AND-NOVELTY.md` (C11, C12, N1, N3, N9; Part 3), `THE-70-TOOLS.md` (all 70, for non-duplication). Standing facts used: nothing in the corpus has a validated directional edge; Factory run 1 promoted 1,217 cells, the pre-registered rerun kept 63 grade A / 337 confirmed / 564 not replicated / 253 untestable (C12); the Board score does not rank returns (Spearman −0.014/−0.021/−0.010 at 1/5/21d) while its gate shows a mean-only right-tail effect with negative median excess (C11); skill-less best-of-550 selection shows Sharpe ≈3.1 at 1 year and minimum track record for Sharpe 1.0 is 2.7 years (inventory §3 item 10, D10); desk crowding measured at ≈55 sleeves long QQQ, 20 groups / 46 sleeves with identical fills (AD-45).

Honesty law, applied to every candidate below: (a) falsifiable forecast stated; (b) ledgered in the append-only ledger (N1, Morgue `ledger.py`/`scorecards.py`) BEFORE the horizon opens; (c) graded at fixed horizons, losers in the denominator; (d) agreement discounted by evidence family (N3), never counted as independent confirmations; (e) data route named and on record today, or accruing from machinery already running; (f) proving state until its own forward record matures. Any requirement a candidate cannot yet meet is marked UNMET with the exact letter.

Non-duplication note: Tool 47 Decay Watch flags decay descriptively, Tool 48 Horizon Grades reports past per-horizon grades, Tool 44 The Router issues one verdict per name with a BLOCKED replay proof, Tool 18 The Second Opinion counts evidence families for one ticker, Tool 42/45 publish the grade-A cells and promoted book as lists, Tool 41 Replication Wire reports what already replicated or died. None of them issues a ledgered, falsifiable forecast about forecasters. Every candidate below does, and that forecast (not a dashboard) is the product.

## M1 — In-Regime Oracle: forecaster-state estimation

- **Falsifiable forecast:** Each Monday pre-open, for each tracked forecaster population (the 63 grade-A cells grouped by family, Board gate, Tape Heat cohorts, Strat labels, Echo Board posters with ≥10 graded calls): a probability that the forecaster's next-21-session hit rate (direction of its own calls vs realised sign at its native horizon) exceeds its own trailing-126-session hit rate. Falsified per forecaster per window: realised hit rate above/below its trailing rate is a binary outcome scored against the issued probability (Brier score).
- **Mechanism:** A two-state hidden-regime estimate per forecaster built only from its own grading curve: recent Brier trend, hit-rate autocorrelation at lag 1 window, calibration slope, and call-volume change. No price inputs at all; the forecaster's own recent grading history is the state variable. This inverts the corpus lesson of per-name HMM regimes (killed, AD-39/V7): regimes are estimated on *grading series* (low-dimensional, directly observed), not on price series.
- **Corpus parts:** N1 ledger + scorecards (the grading curves), Factory cell families (C12), Horizon Grades' per-family horizon data as input features, D10 (the 2.7-year minimum-track-record warning, printed on the card as the reason no state is ever called proven).
- **Why new:** No vendor and none of the 70 forecasts a forecaster's near-term competence. Decay Watch (47) reports decay after it appears in trailing grades; M1 stakes a probability on the NEXT window before it opens.
- **Data route (e):** Exists today: Morgue append-only ledger (`~/workspace/morgue/output/ledger.jsonl`), published Board rows, Record grades at 1/5/21. Forecaster populations with thin ledgers (e.g. Golden Sweeps, 0 prints in 2 sessions) are excluded until ≥30 graded calls exist, and the exclusion is printed.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger Monday's probabilities before the window; grade at window close (+21 sessions) from the ledger only; Brier and calibration slope published per forecaster and pooled; losers (forecasters whose state was misjudged) stay in the denominator. Kill rule: if pooled Brier after 12 windows is no better than the climatology forecast (each forecaster's own trailing rate, unchanged), the engine is demoted to the Graveyard with its record intact.
- **Honesty:** (a)-(f) all met. (f) proving state is load-bearing: D10 says 12 windows cannot prove skill, and the card says so.

## M2 — Disagreement Barometer: forecast volatility from grading dispersion

- **Falsifiable forecast:** Daily pre-open for SPY and QQQ: a predicted 5-session realised-volatility quintile (and a point forecast of 5-session realised range as % of price), issued only from forecaster disagreement. Falsified against realised volatility computed from daily bars at +5 sessions.
- **Mechanism:** When independent evidence families disagree, the market's information state is unsettled. Dispersion is computed N3-corrected: calls are grouped into the six evidence families (price, options prints, modelled inventory, filings/positioning, events/calendar, cross-sectional), one vote per family, and dispersion = entropy of family votes on the index direction that day, blended with dispersion of family-level hit rates over the trailing 21 sessions. The corpus already validates the target class, not the direction class: vol forecasting from vol's own past is grade A (V1), shapes/internals forecast volatility not direction (V4, 172/384 vol cells vs 2/384 direction cells). M2 tests whether *forecaster* dispersion adds anything over the HAR baseline, and is graded against HAR explicitly.
- **Corpus parts:** N3 family grouping, V1/V4 (the validated vol target and the HAR control), Board/Tape/Map daily outputs as the call population, Expected Move Map's corrected band work (AD-55/56) as the calibration comparator.
- **Why new:** Every vendor treats agreement as bullish/bearish signal. Nobody in the corpus uses disagreement itself as a volatility input, and nobody grades a meta-signal against the HAR control it must beat.
- **Data route (e):** Exists today: daily Map/Board/Tape cards on disk, shipped daily bars (1,833 names × 399 sessions; SPY/QQQ daily 2010-2026 for the HAR control).
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger the quintile + point forecast pre-open; grade at +5 sessions; publish MAE vs HAR-only MAE side by side. Kill rule: if after 60 graded days M2 does not beat HAR-only on MAE, it is killed as an increment and only the HAR baseline remains.
- **Honesty:** (a)-(f) all met. Direction is never forecast; any version of M2 that drifts into direction violates its own registration.

## M3 — The Living Router: Bayesian model-averaging ascended from Tool 44

- **Falsifiable forecast:** For every name where at least two tracked forecasters disagree, a ledgered probability distribution over the name's 21-session SPY-excess return tercile (bottom/middle/top), produced by Bayesian model averaging over forecasters with live weights. Falsified at +21 sessions per name (ranked probability score).
- **Mechanism:** Tool 44's Router emits one verdict (Buy/Wait/Avoid/Abstain) and its replay-book proof is BLOCKED (inventory §1.6). M3 replaces the verdict with a weighted distribution: each forecaster's weight is its exponentially decayed forward Brier/log-score from the ledger (half-life 63 sessions), shrunk toward equal weights by a factor set by D10's track-record math, and capped per evidence family so three price-family forecasters cannot outvote one options-print forecaster (N3 as a weight cap, not a footnote). Abstain is an output with mass, not a default: when total effective weight is below a pre-registered floor, the distribution is published as flat and graded as flat.
- **Corpus parts:** Router logic (44), N1 ledger, N3 family caps, N4 fail-closed eligibility (a forecaster whose lineage/clocks fail gets weight zero, not a guess), Factory P3's blocked proof as the named defect this design cures by construction (the proof IS the forward ledger).
- **Why new:** The Router is a classifier with a blocked proof. M3 is a calibrated probabilistic forecaster whose weights are themselves a public, graded record. No vendor in the corpus publishes live model-averaging weights over its own competitors.
- **Data route (e):** Exists today for Board, Map/Wall, Tape, grade-A cells, Strat labels (all ledgered or carded). Populations not yet ledgered enter at weight zero until they are.
- **Grading protocol:** Every distribution ledgered before the session opens; graded at +21 sessions, ranked probability score vs two controls: (i) equal-weight average, (ii) best single forecaster chosen in-sample (the selection-bias control D10 demands). Kill rule: if M3 does not beat control (i) after 126 graded names-windows, weights freeze and the engine reverts to publishing the equal-weight distribution labelled as such.
- **Honesty:** (a)-(e) met; (f) met with the explicit note that 21-session overlapping windows inflate apparent sample size, so the card prints both name-count and independent-window-count (29 weekly Board dates in the C11 study is the cautionary precedent).

## M4 — Crowd-of-Forecasts Thermometer: crowding forecasts the consensus call's failure

- **Falsifiable forecast:** On any day when ≥70% of *effective* (family-discounted) forecaster weight points the same direction on SPY/QQQ, a ledgered forecast: the consensus direction's 5-session success rate for this crowding episode will fall in the bottom half of all consensus episodes, and specifically a probability ≥0.6 that the 5-session index return sign opposes or is flat vs the consensus sign when crowding exceeds the 90th percentile of its own trailing year. Falsified per episode at +5 sessions.
- **Mechanism:** AD-45 measured desk-internal crowding (≈55 sleeves long QQQ, identical fills in 20 groups) as an execution fact. M4 lifts it to the forecaster population: crowding is measured over published calls, discounted by family (N3) and by call-correlation estimated from the ledger (two forecasters who have agreed 95% of the time count as ~one). The hypothesis is the meta version of A2 (loud attention days fade, grade A): loud *forecaster* unanimity is an attention event about the forecasters, and it should fade like one.
- **Corpus parts:** A2 avoid evidence (the analogy, named as analogy not proof), AD-45 crowding measurement, N3, N1.
- **Why new:** Consensus tools (18, 57) count agreement as information. M4 forecasts agreement's failure rate as a function of its own extremity, a quantity no vendor publishes because it indicts their own consensus products.
- **Data route (e):** Exists today: the same ledgered call population as M2/M3. The internal sleeve-crowding input from AD-45 is NOT used (desk-internal, not a public route); public-call crowding only.
- **Grading protocol:** Episodes defined pre-registered (crowding percentile crossing, one episode per crossing, no re-counting while above threshold); graded at +5 sessions; base rate of all consensus episodes printed beside crowded episodes. Kill rule: if crowded episodes do not underperform plain consensus episodes after 20 episodes, killed.
- **Honesty:** (a)-(f) met, with (e) explicitly narrowed to the public call population as above.

## M5 — Decay Prophet: which of the 63 grade-A cells dies next

- **Falsifiable forecast:** Quarterly, a ranked hazard list over the 63 grade-A Factory cells: for each cell, a probability it fails its next pre-registered replication (same rule as C12's rerun: same direction, passes the false-alarm screen) or, for cells graded continuously, that its forward 63-session effect sign flips vs its grade-A sign. The top-10 hazard names are the headline forecast. Falsified at the next rerun / at +63 sessions per cell.
- **Mechanism:** Cell-level survival features drawn from the corpus's own kill patterns: margin by which the cell passed (thin passes die first, per run 1: 564/1,217 did not replicate), family redundancy (AD-32: weak 6-month momentum is 97% distance-from-high, so near-duplicate cells share one fate), stretch-consistency width, capacity margin, and whether the cell's family has any A/B registry support outside the Factory (cells corroborated by an independent family, e.g. stress reversal T2 grade A, should outlive Factory-only cells).
- **Corpus parts:** Factory run1/rerun full record (C12), the 63-cell survivor set and its printed denominator (Tool 42), AD-32 redundancy, EV registry grades as the cross-family corroboration input, Promoted Book (45) ages/decay states as features (not as verdicts).
- **Why new:** Tool 42 lists the survivors; Tool 47 flags decay in live forecasters generally. Nobody ranks the 63 by predicted time-to-death and stakes the ranking in advance. This is the corpus's most concrete testable meta-claim because the next rerun is a scheduled, pre-registered event with a binary outcome per cell.
- **Data route (e):** Exists today: Factory records, grade-A board data, EV registry. The rerun itself is machinery that has already run once under pre-registration.
- **Grading protocol:** Hazard list ledgered before any rerun data is touched; graded by rank correlation between predicted hazard and realised failure order, plus Brier on per-cell probabilities; the 337 confirmed and 253 untestable cells from run 1 are the training caution printed on the card (untestable is an outcome class, predicted explicitly, not dropped).
- **Honesty:** (a)-(e) met; (f) PARTIAL by construction: grading waits on the next rerun, so the proving period is event-bound, not time-bound, and the card must show "awaiting rerun" rather than a maturing percentage. Marked UNMET-(f)-in-part until the first rerun grades it.

## M6 — Echo Decay: forecast a public caller's accuracy after their best call

- **Falsifiable forecast:** For each tracked public caller on the Echo Board (58) with ≥20 graded calls: after any call that lands in the caller's own top-decile outcome, a ledgered forecast that the caller's next 10 calls' mean SPY-excess will be below their own all-calls mean, with a predicted magnitude band. Falsified per streak event at the 10th subsequent graded call.
- **Mechanism:** Regression to the mean weaponised as a forecast, disciplined by D10 (skill-less best-of selection manufactures apparent Sharpe ≈3.1). The population is callers, the trigger is their own realised extreme, the forecast is shrinkage. A second arm forecasts the opposite for callers whose top-decile call was *typical in size for them* (low variance callers), testing whether the decay is a variance artefact.
- **Corpus parts:** Echo Board's timestamped grading (58), Grade My Call's public ledger (39) as a second caller population (self-selected human calls), D10 selection math, the corpus's Echo verdict (median −4.8% vs SPY for one newsletter's calls, S22/AD-20) as prior, not as conclusion.
- **Why new:** Echo Board grades calls. M6 forecasts the *trajectory of a caller's grading curve conditional on their own extremes*, including for the terminal's own users via Grade My Call. No vendor grades its featured callers' post-peak decay in advance.
- **Data route (e):** Exists today but thin: Echo Board grading has run since Slate Three; callers below 20 graded calls are excluded and counted. Accrual is from machinery already running.
- **Grading protocol:** Trigger events ledgered the session the top-decile outcome matures (never retroactively); graded when the 10th subsequent call matures at its native horizon; callers who stop calling are graded as censored and counted, never dropped (survivorship inside the meta-layer is still survivorship, D3).
- **Honesty:** (a)-(f) met; early record will be small-n and the card prints n per caller-arm. If fewer than 8 trigger events accrue in 6 months, the engine stays proving and says why.

## M7 — Calibration Weather: forecast which forecaster's stated confidence will break next

- **Falsifiable forecast:** Monthly, for each forecaster that expresses graded confidence (Board conviction/data-confidence thresholds, Factory cell grades, Signal Feed confidence language mapped to its graded record): a forecast of its next-month calibration error (mean |stated − realised| over calls maturing that month), ranked across forecasters. Falsified per forecaster per month.
- **Mechanism:** Calibration is a property with its own dynamics: the corpus shows calibration failures arriving in clusters (the walk-forward picker model missed calibration by 16-26 points out of sample, AD-36; Board hit rates of 64%/67% carried Wilson intervals of ~43-80% and ~39-86%, i.e. the honest interval was the story). Features: trailing calibration error trend, confidence-level concentration (a forecaster living at one confidence level cannot be calibrated at others), sample-size shrinkage, and regime change in its input family (e.g. Tape side labels degrade when quote-at-print is unavailable, C16, so Tape-linked confidence should break first when feed conditions change).
- **Corpus parts:** AD-36, C11 live scorecard intervals, C16 declared degradations, N4 lineage (a forecaster whose inputs lose provenance is predicted to miscalibrate), Tool 57's design rule (confidence language replaced by graded record) as the population definition.
- **Why new:** Horizon Grades (48) reports where forecasters graded well. M7 forecasts calibration *failure* as the event, ranked, in advance, which is the actionable version for anyone weighting signals.
- **Data route (e):** Exists today for Board and Factory populations; Signal Feed population accrues from running machinery.
- **Grading protocol:** Monthly forecasts ledgered on the 1st; graded on matured calls only, with immature calls listed; ranking scored by Spearman vs realised calibration-error ranking. Kill rule: two consecutive quarters of rank correlation ≤0 kills the ranking claim (the per-forecaster error forecasts continue to be graded individually in the Graveyard record).
- **Honesty:** (a)-(f) all met.

## M8 — Horizon Migration Forecaster: predict where a forecaster's edge moves

- **Falsifiable forecast:** Quarterly per tracked forecaster family: a predicted best horizon (1, 5, or 21 sessions) for the NEXT quarter, plus a probability that its current best horizon degrades by at least one grade band. Falsified at quarter end from the ledger's fixed-horizon grades.
- **Mechanism:** The corpus's E3 finding (best horizon from next open ≈60 sessions; most edges peak at 1-5 sessions including the overnight move) and X-family decay facts (edges decay in 1-5 sessions; one bar late costs $502/trade on a 1H lane) imply edges do not just die, they *compress* toward shorter horizons as they are arbitraged or as conditions change. M8 models each family's horizon profile (effect by horizon, from Horizon Grades' underlying grades) as a curve and forecasts its shift: peak moving 21→5→1 is the predicted decay signature, tested against the alternative that families die in place.
- **Corpus parts:** E3, X-family decay measurements, Tool 48's per-family horizon grades as the input series, Factory cells (each cell is family × target × context with a native horizon).
- **Why new:** Tool 48 is a rear-view table. M8 stakes next quarter's horizon profile in advance and distinguishes two theories of decay (compression vs in-place death) that the corpus raises but never tests.
- **Data route (e):** Exists today: Record grades at fixed 1/5/21 for Board/Map; Factory horizon data. Families without all three horizons graded are excluded and counted.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledgered quarterly; graded on the full horizon profile (not just the argmax, to avoid argmax noise deciding the verdict); scored by profile distance vs the no-change control. Kill rule: if no-change beats M8 over 4 quarters, killed.
- **Honesty:** (a)-(f) all met.

## M9 — The Abstention Signal: forecast the market from forecasters going quiet

- **Falsifiable forecast:** Daily: when the family-discounted abstention rate across tracked forecasters (Router Abstain verdicts, Board no-data categories, Tape no-print days, Factory cells untestable that window) exceeds its trailing-63-session 80th percentile, a ledgered forecast that SPY 21-session realised volatility will exceed its trailing-63-session median, with an issued probability. Falsified at +21 sessions (and secondarily at +5).
- **Mechanism:** The corpus's fail-closed law (N4) makes absence informative by design: no-data is never a number, it is a state. M9 treats the *rate of honest absence* as a sensor. Forecasters go quiet when their inputs degrade, coverage floors fail (C1: Institutional Positioning no-data at 4/7 leaves), or conditions leave their registered domain. Those are exactly the conditions under which the near future is less ordinary. The 253 untestable Factory cells (C12) are the historical proof that untestability clusters rather than scattering randomly.
- **Corpus parts:** N4 fail-closed contract and its absence states, C1 coverage floor, C14 (the standard's own fail-open holes are a named contamination: abstentions caused by the three uncured Femto functions must be tagged as defect-abstentions and excluded, or M9 learns the desk's bugs, not the market), Router (44) abstain default.
- **Why new:** Every vendor fills gaps silently. A forecast built from the gaps themselves, possible only because this corpus records absence honestly, exists nowhere in the 129 vendor labels.
- **Data route (e):** Exists today: daily cards record no-data states explicitly (Map/Board/Tape/Studies all render explicit no-data per the inventory). Accrues daily from running machinery.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledgered daily pre-open with the abstention rate and its percentile printed; graded against realised vol at fixed horizons; control = VIX level alone (the trivial predictor). Kill rule: if M9's probability adds no Brier improvement over the VIX-only control after 80 graded days, killed.
- **Honesty:** (a)-(f) met, conditional on the C14 tagging above; until defect-abstentions can be tagged, the engine ships proving with that contamination disclosed on the card. Marked PARTIAL-(e): the route exists, one input stream (defect vs genuine abstention) is not yet separable in the record.

## M10 — Family Rotation Forecaster: which evidence family produces next quarter's survivors

- **Falsifiable forecast:** Quarterly: a probability distribution over the six evidence families for the share of next quarter's newly-confirmed forecasts (Factory rerun confirmations + ledgered forecasters crossing from proving to confirmed under pre-registered rules) that each family will supply. Falsified at quarter end against the realised shares (scored by log loss vs the trailing-share baseline).
- **Mechanism:** The corpus's deepest structural fact is family asymmetry: volatility claims survived at ~59% under own-vol controls while direction claims collapsed (172/384 vol cells vs 2/384 direction cells, V4); the avoid stack (filings/price hybrid vetoes) is the most robust layer (A family, grades A/B); price-family textbook mechanisms died in bulk (picker replay passed nothing). Families have epochs. M10 models family-level confirmation rates as a slowly moving composition and forecasts its rotation, using per-family trailing confirmation rate, per-family new-claim inflow, and per-family kill rate from the Graveyard (50) as the state.
- **Corpus parts:** N3 family definitions, V4 asymmetry, EV family grades, Graveyard kill record, Replication Wire (41) grade-change record as the realised-share source.
- **Why new:** Replication Wire reports rotations after they happen. M10 forecasts the rotation, which is the resource-allocation question for the whole programme: which family's mechanisms deserve the next build cycle. No vendor can publish this; it requires owning the graveyard as well as the leaderboard.
- **Data route (e):** Exists today in records (EV registry, Factory reruns, Graveyard) but the *quarterly cadence* of new confirmations is slow; first gradeable quarter is the first full quarter after launch.
- **Grading protocol:** Distribution ledgered quarter-start; graded quarter-end; baseline = trailing 4-quarter shares. Kill rule: log loss no better than baseline over 4 quarters kills it.
- **Honesty:** (a)-(e) met; (f) met but slow: 4 quarters to a kill decision is stated on the card up front, not discovered later.

## M11 — Promoted-Book Mortality Curve: forecast replication before the rerun

- **Falsifiable forecast:** At any future Factory-style promotion event, within 24 hours of promotion and before any rerun data exists: per promoted cell, a probability of replicating under the pre-registered rerun rule, and for the cohort as a whole a predicted grade-A count with a 90% interval. Falsified cell-by-cell and cohort-by-cohort at the rerun. (Backcast registration, labelled as such: the same model form, fitted only on run-1 promotion-time features, must retrodict a grade-A count whose interval covers 63 and a not-replicated count whose interval covers 564, or the form is rejected before any forward use.)
- **Mechanism:** Run 1 → rerun is the corpus's one complete promotion-to-replication life cycle (1,217 → 63 A / 337 confirmed / 564 dead / 253 untestable). Promotion-time features only: pass margin, family, horizon, stretch-consistency, capacity, whether the mechanism had any registry support before the Factory touched it. This is a survival model of *claims*, fitted on the only honest life cycle in the corpus, and its first job is to never let a future "1,217 promoted" headline travel without its predicted mortality attached (the C12 presentational conflict, cured prospectively).
- **Corpus parts:** C12 full record, N9 (the Factory's rigour as method), N1 (the ledger the forecast attaches to), Tool 45 Promoted Book (the cohort surface the forecast rides on, without duplicating it: the Book lists, M11 predicts).
- **Why new:** The Factory grades after the fact by design. M11 is the actuarial layer the Factory lacks: a mortality table for promoted claims, issued at promotion time.
- **Data route (e):** Exists today for the backcast registration (run-1/rerun records complete). Forward route exists conditional on a future promotion event occurring.
- **Grading protocol:** Backcast verdict published first (pass/fail of the interval-cover test). Forward forecasts ledgered at promotion; graded at rerun; untestable is a predicted outcome class with its own probability mass (253/1,217 ≈ 21% prior, printed).
- **Honesty:** (a)-(e) met; (f) PARTIAL: if no new promotion event occurs, the forward arm cannot mature, and the engine remains a backcast-validated instrument in proving state. Marked UNMET-(f)-forward until a promotion event grades it.

## M12 — Streak Trap: forecast the terminal's own headline numbers

- **Falsifiable forecast:** Whenever any terminal surface is about to display a hit rate or success share computed on fewer than 30 matured outcomes (the C11 pattern: 64%/67% on 22 outcomes with Wilson intervals ~43-80% / 39-86%; Wall Check's 6-of-6 flip record on 6 tests), M12 ledgers a forecast of that figure's value after the next 30 outcomes mature: a predicted range, and a probability ≥0.7 that the figure moves toward its family's long-run base rate by at least one third of its current deviation. Falsified when the 30 outcomes mature.
- **Mechanism:** The corpus's recurring failure is not bad mechanisms, it is young numbers quoted at face value (C10's undated test counts, C11's thin scorecard, §2.8's 6-test wall record, Golden Sweep's 0 prints in 2 sessions headed to a 30-session decision). M12 is a shrinkage forecaster aimed at the terminal itself: every young headline figure gets a ledgered, dated prediction of its own future, computed from the relevant family base rate and the Wilson width at issuance. It turns the honesty law inward, on the publisher.
- **Corpus parts:** C10, C11, §2.8 wall record, N1 (figures recomputed from the log; M12's forecasts are log entries like any other), D10.
- **Why new:** The Record (2) grades calls; the Receipts (19) state provenance. Neither forecasts the future value of the terminal's own displayed statistics. A publisher staking dated predictions on its own headlines is in no vendor teardown in the corpus.
- **Data route (e):** Exists today: every published figure with its matured-count is on the site receipts already spot-read for the inventory (wall-check, record, golden-sweep-wire, grade-a-board).
- **Grading protocol:** Trigger and forecast ledgered at first display of a qualifying figure; graded at +30 matured outcomes for that exact figure (figure definition frozen at issuance; if the figure's definition changes, the forecast is graded as void-by-redefinition and counted separately, never silently dropped). Kill rule: if shrinkage forecasts are not better calibrated than a no-change forecast over the first 10 matured triggers, killed.
- **Honesty:** (a)-(f) all met. This is the cheapest engine in the set to grade and the fastest to a first honest verdict.

## Lens summary

12 candidates (M1-M12). All forecast properties of forecasters, claims, or the publisher itself; none re-issues a directional market call. Strongest by the commission's ranking inputs (falsifiability sharpness × data in hand × grading cost): M5 and M11 (binary rerun outcomes, complete historical life cycle), M12 (self-grading, fastest verdict), M2 (validated target class, hard HAR control), M3 (cures the Router's blocked proof by construction). Slowest honest verdicts: M10 (quarterly composition), M6 (event accrual). Marked exceptions: M5 UNMET-(f)-in-part (event-bound grading), M9 PARTIAL-(e) (C14 defect-abstention tagging), M11 UNMET-(f)-forward (needs a future promotion event). No candidate resurrects a kill-list item; M1 explicitly inverts the killed per-name price-HMM by estimating regimes on grading series instead.
