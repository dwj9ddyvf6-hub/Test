## Findings and deviations (P1)

- **Reproduction:** the factory replay matches the committed picker replay row for row (296,093 cases, same 9 rows; A3 edge +0.081 [0.029, 0.133], A0 −0.025). `tests/test_forecast_adapters.py` proves the factory path equals `tools/picker_replay.main` on the same input. In novel.py's own universe, every novel/new10 fear-day cell reproduces to four decimals (F3 UP fear 20d: DISC +0.0260 over 467 days, HOLD +0.0309 over 252 days).
- **Date clustering weakens A3:** weighting each date once, A3's mean excess over its same-name-and-regime baseline is +0.19R, but the Newey-West t is 1.82 and the gapped-block t is 0.96. The pooled +0.081R CI treats 13,680 overlapping cases as independent. A3 is not a promoted edge under §4.
- **−F7 and −N5 flip outside the liquid names:** with every name (no floor), the RESET laggard rules reverse. In the illiquid names (price ≤ $5 or $vol20 ≤ $5M), the HOLD fear-day IC is −0.062 (F7neg) and −0.064 (N5neg); the bad-print guard changes nothing. Tier must be a context cell for these two in P2. Their survivor status holds only in the liquid tier.
- **The BH p is the gapped-block p, not the Newey-West p (a deviation from FACTORY.md §4.3).** I simulated MA(19) per-date series (the overlap of 20-session labels) over 2,000 null cells with BH at q = 0.10. The share of seeds with any false discovery was 1.0 with the NW t (Bartlett or unweighted) and 0.57–0.97 with contiguous 20-session blocks. With gapped blocks (2h−1 dates, the first h kept), it was 0.10–0.125 at T = 500 and 0.085 at T = 1500. The NW t stays on every Score as `ic_t_clustered`.
- **The null-FDP rule "≤ 0.10 in 95% of seeds" cannot hold under the complete null at q = 0.10.** There, FDP is 0 or 1 and its mean is the FWER, which is about q. The test asserts BH's guarantee instead: mean FDP ≤ q within 2.5 SE over 200 seeds. Measured: 0.08 (iid, 10,000 cells), 0.085 (MA(19), 2,000 cells × 1,500 dates), and 0.098 with 100 planted cells among 10,000 (power 0.92).
- **HAR inputs:** the replay cache holds split-adjusted bars only, so they serve as both the adjusted and the raw series for FB2's tiers and floors. HAR's QLIKE is 0.957 against 3.034 for lrv22 persistence.
