Loading the records…

Why families, not products: Many products read the same underlying records, so ten products agreeing can be one fact repeated; this page counts the separate records, called families, instead.

How a direction is read: the price family points positive for a name that passed every check, mixed for a score of 60 or more that did not pass, and cautious below 60. The options prints family follows the sign of its net premium (money in minus money out). The modelled positioning family is cautious when the price sits below its flip level and its modelled pressure is negative, positive when the price sits above the flip and the pressure is positive, and mixed otherwise. The filings family speaks in facts, not a direction, so it is never counted in the agreement count.

Counts, not percentages: a handful of families is too few to turn into a percentage, so this page prints counts only.
