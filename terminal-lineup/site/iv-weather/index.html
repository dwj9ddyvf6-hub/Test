How to read this page

Implied volatility is the yearly move the options market is pricing into a stock, shown as a percentage. Each name below shows its latest recorded reading, the date of that reading, how many readings are on record for it, and the low-to-high range of those readings.

Where a reading sits is said in plain words from that range: high means the latest reading is in the top quarter of its own recorded range, low means the bottom quarter, and middle means between those quarters. High is the rich end for that name and low is the cheap end, as far as its own short recorded history goes. The counts of readings below and above the latest one are printed beside it.
