How to read a direction: a bought call or a sold put points up, a bought put or a sold call points down. Which side of the market a print hit is judged against the latest available quote, not the quote at the exact moment of the print, so on a fast-moving contract the direction read can be wrong. Treat it as a read, not a fact.

The live stream these alerts came from is the Flow Tape; the count of recorded sessions toward a verdict on the rarest prints is on the Golden Sweep Wire.
