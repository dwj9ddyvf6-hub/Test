How to read this page

Large holders must report what they held at the end of each quarter. Each holder below shows the period the report covers and the date it was filed, its total reported value, and its largest holdings with share counts. The change section compares the same holder's report for the period before, where they filed one: holdings started, holdings ended, and how the share count moved on the holdings they kept.

These reports are always weeks old: a figure here is what the holder reported holding on the period date, not what they hold today. This page shows the 20 largest holders and each one's 10 largest holdings, not every holder or every holding. What a company's own officers and directors did with their shares is a different record, and it lives on the Insider Wire.
