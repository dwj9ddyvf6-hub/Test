Opening Range Pane

What counts: the opening range is the highest and lowest price of the first hour of trading (9:30 to 10:30 in the morning, New York time), a break is counted the first time price trades above that high or below that low at any point in the rest of the day (just touching the level does not count), and the outcomes counted are whether the rest of the day broke one side of the range, both sides, or neither, plus how far past the range price travelled on days it broke.

How the count works: every completed trading day in the history on record was checked for each of the three names. A break is counted the moment price trades past the range, even if price later comes back inside it. The count does not record whether a break held, where the day closed, or whether the breaks were up or down, so none of that is shown here. The three names are counted together, the way the count was made.

Why some rows could show no percentage: a row with fewer than 30 events shows its counts only. A percentage built on a handful of events reads as far more certain than it is, so it is left off until the count grows. Every published call and count from these tools is graded in public on The Record.
