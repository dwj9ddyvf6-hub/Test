What counts as a fear day here: a day when SPY fell more than 1.5%, or the fear gauge (the VIX) rose more than 15% in a day, closed at 25 or higher, or closed at 20 or higher on a day when SPY or QQQ fell. This is the same definition the Fear Day List publishes, word for word.

What this page does not do: it makes no forecast for today, names no stocks, and shows no expected move. On a calm day there is nothing to rank, and until the forward record below has entries, there is no live call to show. The day itself, as it happens, is shown on the Fear Day List; the reader's own dip scanner is the Pullback Norm.

Why some counts show no percentage: a count built on fewer than 30 events shows its counts only. A percentage on a handful of events reads as far more certain than it is, so it is left off until the count grows.
