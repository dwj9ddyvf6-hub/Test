Your list stays yours: the names you add are saved only in this browser, on this machine. They are never sent anywhere, and there is no account to sign into. Clear your browser's saved site data and the list is gone.

What a pulse line shows: how many of the six kinds of evidence, called families, speak for the name, and what each speaking family says in plain words. The families are price and trading history, options prints, modelled options positioning, filings and positioning, company facts, and news and events. The filings family speaks in facts, not a direction. For the full read on one name, family by family, use The Second Opinion.
