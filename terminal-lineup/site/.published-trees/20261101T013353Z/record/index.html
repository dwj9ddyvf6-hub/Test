How grading works: a stock call is graded on the change from the closing price on the day it was published to the close 1, 5, and 21 trading days later. It counts as "rose" only if that change is above zero; flat counts with the ones that did not rise. A map card is graded on whether the price stayed inside its walls and on the same side of its flip level during the session the card covers. Grades, once published, are never rewritten, and a call that has not reached a grading date yet says so instead of showing a placeholder number.

Grades are recorded for each publishing day as a group, so a day's result is shown with that day's calls rather than split into a separate figure for each single call.
