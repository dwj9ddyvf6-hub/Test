Distance from the high: a name’s high is the highest closing price over its most recent 252 trading sessions (about a trading year), ending on its own last session on record. The distance is how far its last close sits below that high, as a percent of the high. A name right at its high reads 0%.

Tightness: over the name’s last 10 sessions, the highest close minus the lowest close, as a percent of the last close. A smaller number means the last 10 closes sat in a narrower band. The list is sorted tightest first.

Change over 21 sessions: the last close compared with the close 21 sessions before it, as a percent.
