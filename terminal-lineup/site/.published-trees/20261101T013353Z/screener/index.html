The filters below are a published subset, not the full commercial filter catalog: the commercial screener this style of filtering comes from publishes roughly 82 controls, and this page offers the ones the scored day's data can honestly feed. Matching these conditions says a stock fits the shape you asked for.

What the score means: each name is run through the same eight checks, and the score blends the checks into one number from 0 to 100. Higher means more of the evidence lined up in the name's favor on that day. The score is a summary of the checks, not a forecast, and it does not rank which stock will gain the most.

What passing every check takes: the name trades more than 500,000 shares and more than $5 million a day on average, its typical daily move is at least 2%, its price is not sitting more than 2% below its 50-day or 200-day average line, its trend is in an early stage (stage 1 or 2), at least 5 of the 8 checks line up in its favor, its combined score reaches 65, and at least 70% of the measures behind its score had real data behind them. The full day's board, with each check shown name by name, is on the Qualified Board, and every published call is graded in public on The Record.

Results are always listed highest score first. When two names share a score, they keep the order the scored day gave them.
