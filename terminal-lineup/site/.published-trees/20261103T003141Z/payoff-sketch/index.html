The stock right now

Volatility is how much the stock price usually swings, written as a yearly percent. If you are not sure, 25% is a middle-of-the-road guess for a large stock.

The parts of the position

Buy or sellCall or putStrike price ($) Premium per share ($)How many contracts

Each contract covers 100 shares. The premium is what one share's worth of the option cost: money out if you buy, money in if you sell. A sample position is filled in so you can see how it works; change any number and the picture updates at once.

Profit and loss at expiry

The line shows the whole position's profit or loss on the day the options expire, at each possible stock price. Dashed lines mark the break-even prices, where the position has made and lost nothing. The dotted line marks today's stock price.

What the position is worth today, and how it moves

These four measures describe the whole position right now, at the stock price, days left, and volatility you entered above.

These options are priced as European-style, valued at expiry: they count as settled only on the expiry day itself, with no early exercise. The today figures use the standard textbook pricing formula with a 4.5% interest rate and no dividends. Fees are not included.
