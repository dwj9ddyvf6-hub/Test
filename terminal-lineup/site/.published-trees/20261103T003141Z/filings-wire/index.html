What this page carries

Yearly figures only: sales, earnings, what a company owns and what it owes, and the rest of the headline figures its yearly report carries. Each figure keeps the date its report was filed, and a corrected figure counts only from the date the correction was filed. There is no list of separate filing events on this page, because the record keeps the filed date on each figure itself, not as a separate list. Two things the record does not carry at all: a cash balance, and any borrowing beyond long-term borrowing.
