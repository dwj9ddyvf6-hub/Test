How to read the shading

Darker means more money in that cell, compared with the other cells in the same grid. Callslighter darker, most money. Putslighter darker, most money. An empty cell means no alert print on record there. It does not mean nothing traded there: prints that were not among the session's largest alerts are not on this record. Tap or point at any shaded cell to see its exact figures.
