What counts as a pullback here: a dip in a stock that is in a rising trend, judged by comparing its depth with that same stock's own past dips, not with a fixed band that is the same for every stock.

How this page works: a depth distribution, recovery times, and any list of names appear here only when a study on the record has counted them from the price history on file. Until then the page shows the record as it stands.

Why some counts show no percentage: a count built on fewer than 30 events shows its counts only. A percentage on a handful of events reads as far more certain than it is, so it is left off until the count grows.
