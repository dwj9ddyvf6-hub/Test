How to read a verdict: held up means the re-test found what the claim said. Partly held up means part of it survived and part did not, or the evidence is real but early or narrow. Did not hold up means the re-test found against it. Not yet tested means no finished study stands behind the claim yet, and the entry says why. Cannot be tested on the records on hand means the record that could decide the claim does not exist here, and the entry names that missing record. Where a study reports a strength score (a t value), 2.0 is the usual bar for calling a result real rather than chance.
