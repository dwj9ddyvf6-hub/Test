The option

Volatility is how much the stock price usually swings, written as a yearly percent. If you are not sure, 25% is a middle-of-the-road guess for a large stock. The interest rate and dividend yield are yearly percents too, and they are part of the price: change them and the figures below change. A stock that pays no dividend has a dividend yield of 0.

What this option is worth, and how its price moves

Every figure is for one option on one share. One contract covers 100 shares, so multiply by 100 for a full contract.

These options are priced as European-style, settled only on the expiry day itself, with no early exercise, using the standard option pricing formulas. A year counts as 365 calendar days. The time measure is per day, and the volatility measure is per 1 point of volatility. The interest rate and dividend yield in the price are the yearly percents you set above.
