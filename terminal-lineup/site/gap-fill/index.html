What counts: a gap is counted when a name opens a trading day at a different price from its previous day's close, and it counts as filled if the price trades all the way back to that previous close at any point during that same trading day.

How the count works: every completed trading day in the history on record was checked for each of the three names. A day that opened exactly at the previous close has no gap and is not counted. Fills are counted only within the gap's own trading day: a gap that did not trade back to the previous close that day is counted as not filled, however it moved on later days. Because fills are counted within the same day, every filled gap in this count filled the same day it opened.

Why some rows show no percentage: a row with fewer than 30 events shows its counts only. A percentage built on a handful of events reads as far more certain than it is, so it is left off until the count grows. Every published call and count from these tools is graded in public on The Record.
