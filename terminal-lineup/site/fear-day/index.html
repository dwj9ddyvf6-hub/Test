What counts as a fear day: a day when SPY fell more than 1.5%, or the fear gauge (the VIX) rose more than 15% in a day, closed at 25 or higher, or closed at 20 or higher on a day when SPY or QQQ fell.

How this page is built: the definition above is applied to the daily history actually on file, and a day is listed only when that history can judge it, with a session before it to compare against. What happened after a fear day is shown only when a study on the record has counted it.

Why some rows show no percentage: a count built on fewer than 30 events shows its counts only. A percentage on a handful of events reads as far more certain than it is, so it is left off until the count grows.
