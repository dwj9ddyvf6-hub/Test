What the score means: each name is run through the same eight checks, and the score blends the checks into one number from 0 to 100. Higher means more of the evidence lined up in the name's favor on that day. The score is a summary of the checks, not a forecast, and it does not rank which stock will gain the most.

What passing every check takes: the name trades more than 500,000 shares and more than $5 million a day on average, its typical daily move is at least 2%, its price is not sitting more than 2% below its 50-day or 200-day average line, its trend is in an early stage (stage 1 or 2), at least 5 of the 8 checks line up in its favor, its combined score reaches 65, and at least 70% of the measures behind its score had real data behind them.

Every name below is shown exactly as the published board recorded it: the checks it passed, and the exact requirement it missed when it missed one, in plain words. Nothing is added and nothing is rounded into a pass.

Every name on this board is kept and graded in public at 1, 5 and 21 trading days, losers included: see The Record.
