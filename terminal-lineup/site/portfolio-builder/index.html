The two gates this snapshot had to pass

How the basket is built: start with the names that passed every check in the snapshot. Group them by sector and rank the sectors by the average score of their passing names. Take the top five sectors, and in each one take up to the five highest-scoring passing names. Every member then carries the same weight: the whole basket divided equally. A sector with fewer than five passing names contributes only the names it has; no substitute is pulled in from another sector. Weights are rounded to two decimals, so the printed weights may add up to a hair under or over 100%.

What passing every check takes: the name trades more than 500,000 shares and more than $5 million a day on average, its typical daily move is at least 2%, its price is not sitting more than 2% below its 50-day or 200-day average line, its trend is in an early stage (stage 1 or 2), at least 5 of the 8 checks line up in its favor, its combined score reaches 65, and at least 70% of the measures behind its score had real data behind them. Every name in the snapshot, with each check shown, is on the Qualified Board, and you can filter the whole scored day yourself on The Screener.

This page never invents members. If the freshest snapshot is too old or does not declare that it is complete, the page says so and shows no basket, rather than building one on a snapshot that cannot be trusted.
