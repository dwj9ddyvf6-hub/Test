How to read this page

Large holders must report what they held at the end of each quarter. This page compares each holder's report for the earlier quarter with the same holder's report for the later one, across every holder who reported plain-share holdings in both quarters. A holding a holder kept can have more shares (added to) or fewer shares (trimmed) than the quarter before. A holding that appears only in the later report was started; one that appears only in the earlier report was ended.

The full list of what each of the 20 largest holders holds is on the 13F Tracker. What a company's own officers and directors did with their shares is a different record, and it lives on the Insider Wire; neither of those records is mixed into the changes below.
