# Forecast-30 candidates — Lens: DISTRIBUTIONAL AND CALIBRATION-FIRST

Phase: invent. Date: 2026-10-28. Lens brief: the corpus's one proven strength is distributional honesty (expected move recomputes exactly, gap/ORB/earnings distributions over real sessions, band and POP calibrations graded B in AD-55 to AD-58, vol forecasting graded A in V1). These candidates forecast distributions, ranges, exceedance probabilities and timing densities, never bare direction. A directional by-product is graded as a distribution or not at all.

Honesty law applied to every candidate, labelled (a) falsifiable forecast, (b) ledger-before-horizon (N1), (c) fixed-horizon grading with losers in the denominator, (d) evidence-family discounting (N3), (e) data route that exists today or accrues from machinery already running, (f) proving state until its own forward record matures. Any candidate that cannot yet meet a clause says so on its own line. None of these duplicates a built tool: where a candidate ascends one of the 70, the difference is stated (the 70 publish readings, panes and deterministic sketches; these ledger a forecast distribution forward and are graded on its calibration).

Common grading vocabulary: coverage error (stated % band vs realised %), pinball loss per quantile, CRPS for full distributions, Brier and log loss for probabilities, reliability bins (predicted decile vs realised frequency). All grades computed from the append-only ledger only (N1), all forecasts in the denominator including no-data-abstain rows where the engine chose to publish, horizons fixed at issue.

## D1. Own-Norm Move Atlas

Falsifiable as calibration: the 80% inter-decile band must contain the realised return at its stated rate, per bucket, over the next 60 graded cohorts.
- **Mechanism:** Empirical conditional distributions from the DEEP panel, leave-out centred (D2 fix applied: no full-sample same-name means), shrunk toward the unconditional per-name distribution when a bucket has n < 200. No fitted direction model; the state only selects which historical distribution is quoted.
- **Corpus parts:** xcheck F3 pullback-depth-vs-own-norm (N10, IC +0.026/+0.031, t 4.3/4.3 at 20d; 60d 2023+ t 9.8), T1 fear-day timing lift, Studies distribution panes' n-on-every-cell discipline, D2/D5 data-defect fixes.
- **Why new:** No vendor and none of the 70 publishes a per-name, state-conditioned return distribution. Pullback Norm (tool 17) judges one pullback deep or shallow; Odds Cards (46) gives setup-family odds. Neither issues a ledgered decile forecast per name per week, and no corpus part conditions the whole distribution on F3.
- **(e) Data route:** DEEP daily bars 2016-2026, 6,385 names incl. delisted (training); shipped daily history 1,833 x 399 sessions (live state). Both on record in inventory section 4.1.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger Monday pre-open (b). At +5/+21 sessions: coverage per band, pinball loss per quantile, CRPS, split by bucket and by era; losers and flat cohorts stay in (c). State variables are all price-family, counted as ONE evidence family in any consensus use (d). Proving state (f) until 60 cohorts mature at 21d.

VIX backwardation (V3) widens the 95% band only, by a fixed pre-registered factor.
- **Corpus parts:** AD-55 (VIX/15.87 is an ~81% band, coverage 81.6/80.9% h=1), AD-56 (HAR x 0.88 holds 68% OOS), V1 (HAR grade A), V3 (backwardation precedes bigger moves), Azrael 15.87 construction (vendor-verbatim input, N11, not claimed as ours).
- **Why new:** Expected Move Map (tool 7) publishes the vendor construction as a reading. This engine publishes a corrected multi-quantile band set whose stated percentages are claims it is graded against, and it demotes the vendor band to an input. No corpus part grades band coverage forward as the product.
- **(e) Data route:** SPY/QQQ daily plus VIX, VXN, VIX9D, HV30, HAR, GARCH series 2010 to 2026-10 on record (inventory 4.1, AD-55 study set); daily VIX readings accrue in the shipped terminal record.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger pre-open daily (b); coverage, band width vs the vendor band's width at equal coverage, and pinball loss at +1/+5/+21 (c). All inputs are vol/price family: one family (d). Proving (f) until 120 forecasts per horizon mature. Meets (a)-(f).

## D3. Touch Surface, rebuilt after the kill

Falsifiable per cell as a Brier/log-loss probability and as reliability-bin calibration over 60 forecasts per cell.
- **Mechanism:** Empirical first-passage frequencies from daily high/low bars in the DEEP and index panels, conditioned on distance-in-EM and vol regime, blended with a driftless barrier estimate shrunk hard toward the empirical rate. The vendor reflection formula `2N(.)` is an input candidate only, carrying its measured 13-30 point overstatement (AD-57) as a fixed haircut, never quoted raw.
- **Corpus parts:** AD-57 (reflection touch model KILLED as calibrated: model 63.5% vs realised 32-33% at +/-1 EM on VIX vol, ~50% on HAR vol), AD-55/56 vol inputs, Studies S1/S2 high-low path fixtures, Morgue `strip.p_touch` as the named dead construction being replaced.
- **Why new:** The corpus contains the kill and no replacement. No tool among the 70 forecasts touch probability at all; Payoff Sketch (6) is expiration payoff only. A calibrated touch surface is the missing half of every wall, band and target claim in the corpus.
- **(e) Data route:** Daily OHLC in DEEP (touch is decidable from high/low vs the barrier) and the 2010-2026 index panel; live barriers from D2's ledgered bands and Morning Map chain EM. No intraday feed assumed.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger pre-open (b); Brier + reliability per distance x horizon cell at fixed horizons, touched and untouched both counted (c); price/vol family only (d). Proving (f) until each index cell has 120 graded forecasts; per-name cells stay proving longer and say so. Meets (a)-(f).

## D4. Wall Lifetime Clock

- **(a) Forecast:** For each Morning Map card (SPY/QQQ/IWM, daily): the distribution of sessions until the call wall is first touched, until the put wall is first touched, and until the flip is first crossed and closed beyond, stated as P(event within 1 / 3 / 5 / 10 sessions) plus a median lifetime, conditioned on net-GEX sign and distance-to-level in EM units. Falsifiable as censored survival calibration: of levels forecast at P(touch in 5) near 30%, about 30% must touch within 5, with still-untouched levels carried as censored, never dropped.
- **Mechanism:** Kaplan-Meier style empirical hazard from the accruing map-card record, seeded with a distance/vol prior (hazard rising as distance/EM shrinks); hazards updated only from ledgered cards, one grading definition imported from `scorecards.py` per N8, never forked.
- **Corpus parts:** Morning Map GEX/walls/flip (SpotGamma published construction), wall-validation harness N8, Wall Check's young record (9 cards, 6 graded tests: flip respected 6/6, put wall held 6/6, IWM call wall reached and broke 2/2), D3 touch machinery shared with D3 above (same family, see (d)).
- **Why new:** Wall Check (tool 14) grades respect after the fact, as rates. Nothing in the corpus forecasts a level's lifetime before the fact. Time-to-break is the question a wall reader actually asks, and the corpus has never posed it as a ledgered distribution.
- **(e) Data route:** Map cards accrue daily from machinery already running (`publish_feed.py` / `daily_cycle.py`); chain snapshots accrue via the Chain Explorer lane. **Partial on (e) today, stated plainly:** only 9 cards / 6 graded tests exist, so the hazard prior dominates for months; the engine ships as an accrual counter with its prior labelled, exactly like P_NN's 126-session floor, and issues investable-looking precision to no one.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger at card publication (b); survival calibration and median-lifetime error at 1/3/5/10 sessions, censored rows in the denominator (c). Modelled-inventory family; D3 and D4 share the price/vol family beneath it and must never be counted as two confirmations of each other (d). Proving (f) until 60 cards per index mature. The C2 caveat rides along: grading runs through `wall_study.py`'s own fetch path, not the still-defective `studies_card.main` wall path on main.

## D5. Exceedance Forecaster (tail size, never direction)

- **(a) Forecast:** For SPY/QQQ/IWM and per name in the shipped universe, daily pre-open: P(|next 5-session return| exceeds 1 EM), P(exceeds 2 EM), and P(the 21-session max drawdown exceeds 1.5 EM). Falsifiable per probability: Brier score and reliability bins over rolling 120 forecasts; pre-committed claim: its 2-EM exceedance probabilities beat the unconditional base rate's Brier by a stated margin or the engine reports itself failed.
- **Mechanism:** Logistic-free empirical exceedance rates conditioned on the corpus's downside-only precursors (overnight gap size 1.6-1.9x tail multiplier, down-day trade-count surge, weekly divergence at the low, AD-30), plus vol state (HAR level, backwardation, breadth-washout flags T7). Precursors enter as distribution shifters only; any directional reading is suppressed by construction.
- **Corpus parts:** AD-30 (downside precursors forecast tail size, grade C, "for stops and puts, not direction"), V3, V4 (shapes forecast vol not direction, 172/384 vol cells vs 2/384 direction), T7 extremes, A9 trade-count surge as an avoid-grade fact reused here as a size input.
- **Why new:** The corpus grades these precursors as study rows and avoid vetoes. No engine converts them into a ledgered exceedance probability, and no vendor in the index sells tail-size-without-direction as the headline. It ascends Regime Dial (33) and Breadth Meter (31), which describe today's state and forecast nothing.
- **(e) Data route:** DEEP daily bars and trade counts, index panel 2010-2026, shipped daily history live. All on record.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger pre-open (b); Brier/log loss and reliability at +5/+21, exceedances and non-exceedances both in (c); precursors are price/breadth family, one family (d). Proving (f) until 120 index forecasts and 60 per-name cohorts mature. Meets (a)-(f); precursor evidence is grade C, which is why the claim is a Brier margin against the base rate, not an accuracy boast.

## D6. Earnings Move Distribution Engine

- **(a) Forecast:** For every name on the Earnings Calendar, at the close before its report: the decile distribution of its earnings-day absolute move and its signed 5-session post-report move, plus P(realised earnings-day move exceeds the chain-priced expected move). Falsifiable as coverage of the stated deciles across each earnings season's full cohort (target: the 80% band covers 75-85% of a season's reports) and Brier on the exceedance probability.
- **Mechanism:** The name's own past report-day distribution (DEEP earnings dates, D5 one-session-late defect fixed in the panel) blended with the cross-sectional distribution for its size tier, centred on zero drift (no drift edge is claimed: the record holds none) and scaled by the priced EM ratio for that tier.
- **Corpus parts:** Studies S3 earnings distributions (AAPL/MSFT/NVDA panes), Earnings Calendar's priced EM per name (tool 35), A12 (small-cap earnings gaps fade, grade B, used as a signed-tail conditioner for small caps only), D5 earnings-date fix.
- **Why new:** Earnings Drift Pane (13) shows three names' past report days; Earnings Calendar (35) lists who reports with a priced move. Neither forecasts a per-name move distribution for the coming report, ledgers it, and grades the season. The distribution, not a drift call, is the product.
- **(e) Data route:** Earnings calendar and chain EM on record (tool 35's routes); DEEP panel report-day history. Live route exists today.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger at the pre-report close (b); per-season coverage, pinball and exceedance Brier, every reporting name in the denominator including halts and no-data EM rows marked abstain-at-issue (c). Price + chain families: two families, reported as two (d). Proving (f) for two full seasons. Meets (a)-(f).

## D7. Gap Destiny Distribution

Stated per gap-size band (0.5-1, 1-2, over 2%) x weekday, matching the S1 partition. Falsifiable as Brier on fill/close probabilities and coverage on the range deciles over each 60-forecast block.
- **Mechanism:** The S1 gap-fill empirical distributions re-estimated on DEEP daily OHLC (fill is decidable from the day's low/high vs the prior close), conditioned additionally on fear state (T1) and gap_share's top-VIX-third conditioning (S15) as distribution shifters. No intraday timing claim is made (see (e)).
- **Corpus parts:** Studies S1 gap-fill pane (fixtured descriptive distribution), S15 gap_share conditional on fear (IC +0.069/+0.074, grade B), T1, tool 11 Gap-Fill Pane as the descriptive ancestor.
- **Why new:** Tool 11 shows how often gaps like today's filled, historically, as a pane. This engine takes today's actual gap, issues a ledgered probability set for this session before it unfolds, and is graded on this session. Pane-to-forecast is exactly the ascent the commission asks for.
- **(e) Data route:** DEEP and shipped daily OHLC: full route exists. **Marked limitation:** a time-of-fill variant (fills by 10:30 vs by close) cannot meet (e) today: the live intraday route was no-data in the recorded run (Alpaca 401) and hourly history is shallow (from 2026-03-17, 41 names). - **Grading protocol:** Ledger at the open print (b); graded at that session's close, fills and non-fills both counted (c); price family, one (d). Proving (f) until 120 index gaps and 60 per-size-band cohorts grade. Meets (a)-(f) in the daily form specified.

## D9. Completion Clock (how long a move takes, not whether)

- **(a) Forecast:** Whenever a ledgered cohort event fires (a fear day per tool 16's definition; a deep-reset name entering the RESET cohort), issue the timing density: P(the cohort's median name reaches +1 EM before -1 EM within 5 / 10 / 21 / 60 sessions), and the decile distribution of sessions-to-first-of-those-barriers, with expiry at 60. Falsifiable as survival calibration at each stated session count, expired-unresolved names kept as their own outcome class.
- **Mechanism:** Empirical barrier-race distributions from the picker replay corpus (296,093 graded cases) and DEEP, conditioned on cohort type and vol state; the 60-session cap is the corpus's own best-horizon finding (E3), and the 1-5 session decay fact shapes the early hazard. This is D3's first-passage machinery pointed at cohort barriers instead of fixed levels; the two are one family (see (d)).
- **Corpus parts:** Picker replay corpus and Odds Cards' distributions (tool 46), T1/T2 fear and stress-reversal timing, E3 best horizon ~60 sessions, X-family decay facts, RESET composite N9 (grade B cohort definition).
- **Why new:** Odds Cards publish what a setup family paid on average. Nothing times it: no corpus part forecasts the distribution of when an in-flight move resolves, which is the input position sizing and patience decisions actually consume. Fear-Day Forecaster (43) names names; this clocks them.
- **(e) Data route:** DEEP + picker corpus on record for priors; forward cohorts fire from machinery already running (Fear Day List definition, shipped daily history). Exists today.
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger at cohort entry, before the first barrier session opens (b); survival calibration and barrier-race Brier at 5/10/21/60, unresolved-at-60 in the denominator as its own class (c). Price family; explicitly NOT independent of D3/D1 in any consensus count (d). Proving (f) until 30 cohorts mature. Meets (a)-(f).

## D10. The Calibration League (engines grading engines, with its own forecast on the line)

- **(a) Forecast:** Monthly, for every distributional forecaster publishing to the ledger (D2, D3, D5, D6, vendor-band and vendor-POP constructions included as entrants): a forecast interval for that entrant's coverage error over its next 60 graded forecasts (example form: "the vendor 15.87 band's stated-68% coverage will land 76-86%"). Falsifiable directly: the realised coverage error falls inside the interval or it does not, per entrant per month.
- **Mechanism:** A meta-model over the ledger itself: each entrant's running reliability curve, sample size, regime mix and known structural biases (AD-55's 13-17 point over-cover, AD-57's touch overstatement, AD-58's IV-POP 12-15 point understatement) projected forward with binomial sampling width. Rankings use Brier/CRPS skill vs the climatology baseline, discounted by evidence family (N3) so five price-family entrants agreeing counts once.
- **Corpus parts:** N1 ledger-first grading, N3 family discounting, N4 eligibility gate (an entrant whose lineage fails the five-clock check is listed ineligible, not silently dropped), AD-55 to AD-58 calibration studies as the entrants' priors, The Record (tool 2) and Horizon Grades (48) as display ancestors.
- **Why new:** The Record grades calls; Horizon Grades grades forecaster families by horizon; Decay Watch (47) flags decay. None forecasts the graders' future calibration and stakes its own record on it. A league that must predict its members' honesty, in public, is in no vendor teardown and none of the 70.
- **(e) Data route:** The append-only ledger at `~/workspace/morgue/output/ledger.jsonl` plus the site Record receipts: exists today, young by construction (22 matured 1-day Board outcomes at last snapshot), so early intervals are honestly wide.
- **Grading protocol:** The League's own monthly forecasts are themselves ledgered before the month opens (b, recursively: the grader is graded by the same rule); interval hit rate and width published at each month's close, missed entrants and ineligible entrants both listed (c); the League is meta-family and adds no confirmation to any member's claim (d). Proving (f) until 12 monthly rounds complete. Meets (a)-(f).

## D11. Path Shape Forecaster (one side, both sides, or neither, over 5 sessions)

- **(a) Forecast:** Daily pre-open, for SPY/QQQ/IWM and liquid shipped names: a four-way probability distribution over the next 5 sessions' path class: touches +1 EM only, touches -1 EM only, touches both, touches neither (EM from D2's ledgered band). Plus the decile distribution of the 5-session max extension beyond the first touched barrier. Falsifiable as multinomial log loss and per-class reliability over 60-forecast blocks.
- **Mechanism:** S2 opening-range path logic (one side / both / neither, break extension) lifted from the first hour to the first week: empirical path-class frequencies from daily high/low sequences in DEEP, conditioned on first-day range vs its own norm and on vol regime. Path classes are decidable from daily OHLC alone, which is what makes the forecast honest on today's data.
- **Corpus parts:** Studies S2 ORB distributions (fixtured), D2 EM bands, D3 first-passage estimation shared (same family), tool 12 Opening Range Pane as the intraday descriptive ancestor.
- **Why new:** Tool 12 answers for the first hour, descriptively. A ledgered 5-session path distribution exists nowhere in the corpus, and path (both-sides-touched = churn) is information neither a return distribution (D1) nor a touch probability (D3) carries on its own: D1 cannot distinguish both-touched from one-touched at the same close.
- **(e) Data route:** DEEP and shipped daily OHLC, full route today. The intraday first-hour variant is excluded for the same route reason as D7's timing variant (hourly history shallow, live intraday no-data in the recorded run).
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger pre-open (b); path class is final at +5 sessions, all four classes and all forecasts counted (c); price/vol family, shared with D3/D9, one confirmation among them (d). Proving (f) until 120 index forecasts grade. Meets (a)-(f).

## D12. Honest POP Engine (probability of finishing, calibrated)

- **(a) Forecast:** For SPY/QQQ weekly expiries, at each day's close: for every listed strike within +/-2 EM, P(final close above the strike at expiry), as a number per strike. Falsifiable per strike as Brier/log loss at expiry and as a reliability curve across strikes (of strikes priced near 80%, about 80% must finish in the money).
- **Mechanism:** HV30 driftless N(d2) (AD-58: best-calibrated POP in the corpus, 82.4 predicted vs 83.0 realised at h=1) blended with the name's own empirical finish distribution from the index panel, with the IV-based POP's measured 12-15 point understatement applied as a correction when IV is the input. The output is a probability surface over the chain, recomputed daily, each day's surface ledgered separately.
- **Corpus parts:** AD-58 POP calibration (grade B, calibration only; its own caveat carried on the card: a win rate is not an edge, loss size ignored), Option Alpha teardown (the corpus's most test-backed vendor doc, 20.2% backed), payoff.py deterministic expiration math, Chain Explorer snapshots (tool 25).
- **Why new:** Payoff Sketch (6) draws what a spread pays at each price with no probability attached; Premium Meter (28) compares implied vs realised move size. No corpus part attaches a calibrated, ledgered finish-probability to each strike and grades the surface at expiry. Vendors print POP; none in the index grades theirs.
- **(e) Data route:** Chain snapshots on record via the chain archive lane plus HV30 series; index-first because per-name chain depth is a named accrual gap (inventory 4.5 item 8). Per-name strikes are excluded until that depth exists: **the per-name extension cannot yet meet (e) and is not part of this candidate's claim.**
- **Grading protocol:** Ledger at each close (b); graded at expiry close, every ledgered strike in the denominator including strikes that expire worthless (c); chain/modelled family, distinct from D3's price-family touch probabilities and never merged with them as one confirmation (d). Proving (f) until 26 weekly expiries grade. Meets (a)-(f) for the index form specified.

## Lens notes for the ranking phase

- Sharpest falsifiability per unit of grading cost: D2, D12, D10 (coverage and Brier grade mechanically from data already flowing). - Known corpus traps respected throughout: no reflection touch model (AD-57), no 1-sigma label on VIX/15.87 (AD-55), no directional resurrection of killed setups, no short-book use of avoid facts (A16), no intraday claim on the degraded Alpaca route, no per-name chain claim on a chain history that does not yet exist.
