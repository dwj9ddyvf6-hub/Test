## PANEL-LAGGARD-GAP-20261104: price panel advance never backfilled a lagging symbol's missing session (CURED)

- Severity: lane-blocking for every continuity-gated panel consumer (Momentum Clock refused to publish; the 19:00 tools refresh failed on it 2026-11-03).
- What broke: SUPN was halted on 2026-11-02 (Alpaca SIP carries a zero bar for the session: flat OHLC at the prior close, volume 0, trades 0) and Alpaca published that zero bar late. `site/advance_price_panel.py` fetched only from the day after the panel's NEWEST symbol date and appended only sessions after each symbol's own last date, so once the panel advanced past 2026-11-02 the missing SUPN session could never be fetched, leaving a hole at 2026-11-02 between 2026-10-30 and 2026-11-03.
