# BUGLOG 2026-11-07

The four forecast-refresh legs that failed identically across
four consecutive full runs (abstention-signal, calibration-league,
in-regime-oracle, replication-wire) were root-caused on
2026-11-07 (diagnosis: research_notes/terminal-total-fix-loop-
20261104/leg-diagnosis-20261107.md, read-only, verified against
the code) and cured the same day under the terminal total-fix
loop. No failure traced to a wrong number in a source record:
every source record was internally consistent. The failures
lived in the guards, in two mechanisms: hand-taken census pins
on values that advance by design, and snapshot-versus-live
equality checks across producers with no shared generation
stamp. Seven defects were cured; three of them were stacked
behind the named failures and only became reachable once the
leg in front of them could run again. Each cure carries its
blocker test in site/, each proven to fail on the pre-cure tree
(pre-cure copies kept in the loop home, precure-20261107/) and
pass on the cured tree, and each leg was re-run standalone to
exit 0 on the live records.

## ABSTENTION-SESSION-PIN-20261107: session guards pinned to a hand-censused date (CURED)

- Severity: lane-leg down (abstention-signal failed every full
  refresh run since the 2026-11-04 census; one of the four
  stable failures in the 2026-11-06 full-run accounting).
- Files: site/refresh_abstentionsignal.py;
  site/test_abstention_session_pin_20261107.py.
- What broke: build() required the Qualified Board page's
  session to equal the literal "2026-11-03" and Second
  Opinion's likewise. Both pages keep only their freshest day
  and advance every trading day by design, so the pins broke by
  construction (the Board stood at 2026-11-06, its counts and
  character unchanged: 1838 checked, 50 published, big-investors
  no_data for all 50). The same class sat stacked behind them:
  the intake-bias history was pinned to an exact six-session
  list (the source has since accrued 2026-11-03 to 2026-11-05
  into its history, as designed), its live "today" was pinned
  to the census day (its today is now a later session on which
  it fired), and its live counters were pinned to the census
  values (16 forecasts, 1 fire; the record now stands at 23
  and 2). Two hand re-takes (2026-10-31, 2026-11-04) are
  documented in the file's own comments; re-pinning is the
  treadmill this cure ends.
- Fix at the source: the bindings are computed, not censused.
  board_session_ok: the Board's session must equal the freshest
  session on the SPY panel the same record trusts for its
  outcome history (a Board behind the panel now fails as a true
  staleness signal). sop_session_ok: Second Opinion's freshest
  day must be a panel session at or after the census floor
  (it advances on its own coverage cadence and lags the Board
  legitimately). ib_history_ok: the six inspected intake-bias
  sessions must all survive in the source's retained history,
  their per-day facts still checked individually; accrual past
  the census is expected. The census-day intake-bias facts
  (2026-11-03: no fire, no interaction) are verified from the
  retained history, which still carries that day, and the live
  counters are verified as floors (ib_live_ok). The decision
  window the page assembles is declared CLOSED at the last full
  census day (WINDOW_CLOSE_SESSION, 2026-11-03) in the code:
  the Board and Second Opinion pages keep only their freshest
  day, so later days' character is not recoverable from them,
  and the leg's own guard failures broke the daily evidence
  trail an extension would need. The episode's forecasts stand
  as issued from 2026-11-03 and mature against the advancing
  panel (the ledger dedupes republication and the leg voids
  changed-input re-issues by correction, both by design).
- Proof: blocker 9 tests pass on the cured tree, 9 fail on the
  pre-cure module; the leg ran standalone to exit 0 and
  republished abstention-signal/data.json (decision record
  through 2026-11-03, 9 decision days; 8 recorded forecasts,

  whose grading loop reached a matured row).
- File: site/refresh_abstentionsignal.py (same blocker file as
  ABSTENTION-SESSION-PIN-20261107).

  once in the source (the legitimate use inside outcome_days);
  the leg's standalone green run grades through this loop.

## ABSTENTION-GRADE-VOIDED-20261107: grading loop attempted grades on voided forecasts (CURED)

- Severity: leg crash (exposed by the grade-guard cure, in the
  same run).
- File: site/refresh_abstentionsignal.py (same blocker file).
- What broke: the grading loop never excluded forecasts voided
  by correction rows. Voided duplicate re-issues stay on the
  append-only ledger, and the ledger refuses grades on them by
  design (defect 8 cure, 2026-10-30), so the first matured
  voided row killed the leg with LedgerError. The figures
  section of the same file already excluded voided rows when
  counting valid forecasts; the grading loop did not.
- Fix at the source: the skip decision now lives in a
  gradeable() helper that excludes already-graded, voided, and
  absent rows, and the loop uses it.
- Proof: blocker tests gradeable() on synthetic rows (live,
  voided, graded, absent, wrong type); the leg's standalone
  green run is the live proof.

## CALIBRATION-GENERATION-20261107: count check bound across generations (CURED)

- Severity: lane-leg down (calibration-league reported the
  same 6,606-row gap across four consecutive runs:
  live 70,275 vs its page 63,669).
- Files: site/refresh_calibrationleague.py,
  site/refresh_selfanalogue.py;
  site/test_calibration_generation_20261107.py.
- What broke: the league demanded the live ledger count of
  engine_15 forecasts equal the count the engine's own page
  publishes. The page is written by refresh_selfanalogue at the
  end of the run's longest leg, after its own appends;
  calibration-league reads it early the next run. Two runs
  killed inside self-analogue (after appends landed, before the
  page write) banked 6,606 rows the page never caught up with,
  and every later run inherited the frozen one-sided gap. A
  completed run leaves both sides agreeing to the row
  (72,793 = 72,793 after the 2026-11-06 full run), which
  excludes any filter or semantic difference: the disagreement
  was temporal, not semantic.
- Fix at the source: the producer stamps its generation.
  refresh_selfanalogue now writes live.ledger_rows_at_write,
  the ledger row count its figures were computed from, on the
  same read that produced them. The league reads the stamp
  (ledger_checkpoint_src) and binds the comparison at the page's
  generation via expected_page_count: the page's count plus the
  engine's forecast rows appended after its checkpoint are the
  expected live count; appends after the checkpoint (including
  a killed leg's banked rows) are expected accrual, and a page
  wrong at its own generation still fails, with the failure
  message bucketed (live, page, checkpoint, expected). A page
  written before the cure carries no stamp and gets strict
  equality for one cycle.
- Proof: blocker 6 tests pass on the cured tree, 6 fail on the
  pre-cure modules (banked-appends arithmetic at the live
  figures, a page wrong at its own generation, checkpoint
  scoping to the engine's forecasts only, strict fallback, the
  producer's stamp, the checker's read). The leg ran standalone
  to exit 0 in strict mode (the current page predates the
  stamp and the counts agree); the generation-aware path's
  live proof event is the first scheduled refresh whose
  self-analogue leg writes a stamped page, tonight's run.

## CALIBRATION-INTERVAL-20261107: November stakes demanded to equal a moving recomputation (CURED)

- Severity: leg failure stacked behind CALIBRATION-GENERATION
  (reachable only once the count check passed; latent since
  the panel advanced past the registration sample).
- Files: site/refresh_calibrationleague.py;
  site/test_calibration_interval_20261107.py.
- What broke: build_table demanded the interval recomputed
  from today's measured centre equal the pre-registered
  November will-stake interval within 0.26 points. The measured
  centre is computed over a walk-forward sample that grows as
  the price panel advances (the file's own G2 re-registration
  made the counts floors for exactly this reason), so the
  equality broke on a healthy record: engine_01 computed
  -19.6..4.4 against the registered -19.3..4.6. Worse, the
  recomputed (drifted) interval is what the round-append
  ledgers as the round's stake, so a quietly drifting centre
  would have moved the pre-registered goalposts.
- Fix at the source: the staked interval is the REGISTERED
  one. registered_interval() reproduces it from the register
  itself (a centre scan for the unanchored entrants; the
  anchored entrant's interval is centre-free), and a register
  no centre can reproduce fails closed as a register in
  question. The measured interval is still computed and
  drift-guarded at DRIFT_TOL (2.0 points): beyond it, the
  league fails closed naming the register. What is ledgered
  for a round is now the registered stake, never a drifted
  recomputation.
- Proof: blocker 5 tests pass on the cured tree, 5 fail on the
  pre-cure module (engine_01's register reproduced; the
  anchored entrant's register reproduced at its registered
  stated 82.4; an irreproducible register returns None; the
  live drift shape 0.3 / 0.2 sits inside DRIFT_TOL). The leg's
  standalone green run stakes all seven forecastable entrants
  from the register (0 issued this run, dedupe against the
  rounds already ledgered).
