## CASH-TOL-BOUNDARY-20261031: an exactly-one-cent cash residue halted the desk desk-wide (CURED at source)

- Severity: critical (desk-wide entry halt on a residue the desk's
  own cash cure calls inside tolerance).
- Found live by the 2026-10-31 night inspection. After the
  CASH-RECONCILE-20261030 cure landed (commit 60cf3a0) and trading
  resumed, state/reconcile_last.json at 2026-10-31T04:06Z read

  single cash row (80 reconcile_halt rows on 20261031-dated intents
  in the 03 hour alone, all cash_halt true).
- Root cause: the cash comparison in desk3/reconcile.py was done
  in binary floating point against CASH_TOL_USD = 0.01. At the
  live magnitude, 1077786.01 - 1077786.0 is 0.010000000009313226,
  strictly greater than 0.01, so a decimal difference of exactly
  one cent, the fill-price rounding residue the
  CASH-RECONCILE-20261030 entry records as "inside the existing
  one-cent tolerance exactly as designed", halted anyway. At
  smaller magnitudes the same subtraction lands below 0.01, which
  is why no fixture at toy scale ever caught it.
- Fix (source, minimum sufficient): the comparison now rounds the
  absolute difference to cents before comparing, making it the
  decimal comparison the tolerance denominates. CASH_TOL_USD is
  unchanged (0.01); no tolerance, strategy, sizing, stop, or
  threshold was retuned, and a two-cent difference still halts.

  discover pattern), fixtures at the live amounts. Fail direction

  the live report shape (DISCREPANCY, cash_halt True, the same
