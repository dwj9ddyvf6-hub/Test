## INTENT-LOG-FIELDS-20261115 (Medium): intent and rejection records carried no symbol, side, or reference price

- **Severity:** Medium (no trading behavior affected; the desk's own
  rejection and intent history was permanently ungradable, blocking
  the near-miss shadow portfolio and all per-symbol fleet analysis).
- **File:** desk3/dispatcher.py (`_reject`, the insufficient_slice_bp
  call site, and the inline insufficient_options_bp_csp row);
  desk3/cycle.py (the per-sleeve journal intent record).
- **What broke:** Dispatcher `intent_rejected` rows carried only
  event, intent_id, sleeve and reason; per-sleeve journal `intent`
  rows carried only event, cycle_id, intent_id, result,
  snapshot_hash and ts. Proven 2026-11-15 by the Chaos Lab Lane 3
  census over the estate's own logs (research_notes/
  alpha-hunt-chaos-20261115/LANE3-PRIVATE-DATA.md): 2,354 dispatcher
  rejections (773 of them insufficient_slice_bp entry blocks, the
  true economic near-miss) and 71,495 journal intent rows, none of
  them markable forward, because the records never named what was
  refused, which way, or at what price. A downstream consumer
  (desk3/setups_generate.py) already read `symbol` off journal
  intent rows and silently got nothing. Historical rows stay
  field-less; the cure is at emission, going forward.
- **Fix:** Logging fields only; no strategy, sizing, stop,
  threshold, or engine change. (1) `_reject` stamps `symbol`,
  `side`, `ref_price` on every intent_rejected row: ref_price
  resolves from the caller's authoritative price when one was
  computed, else the intent's limit_price, else its price_hint,
  else None (key present; no reference price existed at that gate).
  (2) The insufficient_slice_bp call site passes the buying-power
  gate's own computed price, so the near-miss class carries the
  exact price it was refused at, including market intents that
  carried no price of their own. (3) The inline
  insufficient_options_bp_csp row gains `side` and `ref_price`
  (symbol was already there). (4) The cycle's journal intent
  record stamps `symbol`, `side`, `ref_price` (limit_price else
  price_hint) at emission.
- **Regression blocker:** tests/test_intent_log_fields_20261115_
  blockers.py (5 tests: gate-price rejection, validation rejection,
  reconcile-halt rejection, filled journal intent record, rejected
  journal intent record plus its dispatcher row). Fail direction
  proven on preserved pre-cure copies of both writers in /tmp:
  all 5 tests error with KeyError 'symbol' on the old record shape;
  all 5 pass on the cured tree. Destination proof beyond the test:
  an independent fixture drive emitted an insufficient_slice_bp
  rejection for a market NVDA entry carrying no price and read the

  gate's lookup price).
