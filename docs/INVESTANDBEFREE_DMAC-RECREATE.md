# investandbefree_dmac: Recreation package

Provenance: InvestAndBeFree method, clean-room extraction from public material only (INVESTANDBEFREE-DMAC-SPEC-2026-10-29) (Source row in strategies/muse/muse-investandbefree-dmac.md in this repo.)

## What this sleeve is
This is a paper-only long-premium options sleeve on SPY and QQQ driven by a double MACD pair (Wilder and exponential, 12/26/9) aligned across a weekly, daily and 4H stack, entering on a daily crossover or curl and managing winners into a runner at +100%. Its edge is unproven: the live paper record is the test, and no backtest is claimed.

## Full algorithm
This sleeve's own parameters (PRIMARY_PARAMS in sleeves/investandbefree_dmac.py): stack_order (weekly, daily, h4), entry_tf daily, min_dte 30, strike_mode atm, flat_by_close False, premium_macd False, premium_warmup 40 (inert while premium_macd is False). Strike atm means, at the chosen nearest expiry at least 30 calendar days out, the strike with the smallest abs(strike - spot), ties taking the lower strike.
4H aggregation (aggregate_4h, for stacks containing h4 and built from 1Hour bars): RTH 1H bars only (bar start 09:30 to 16:00 ET), grouped by ET session date, chunked in consecutive groups of 4 hourly bars (3600 s apart) anchored at the session's first RTH bar; a bucket is emitted only when it holds exactly 4 such bars, carrying the last bar's t, the first open, the extremes and the last close.
Common engine, as applied (sleeves/investandbefree_dmac.py): symbols are SPY and QQQ, evaluated independently, at most one open position per underlying. Closed bars only: closed_bars() keeps a bar only when its period has ended at or before ctx.now (1Hour ends start + 3600 s; 1Day ends at the next ET midnight; 1Week ends at the next Monday 00:00 ET; intraday minutes end start + minutes*60); a bar with no readable timestamp is dropped. The adapter reapplies the same filter at the desk boundary.
Two MACDs on closes on every timeframe in the stack, both 12/26/9: MACD-W uses Wilder smoothing (alpha 1/period) for fast, slow and signal; MACD-E uses exponential smoothing (alpha 2/(period+1)). Each smoother is seeded with the simple mean of its first period values and is None before that; a MACD line exists from index 25 and a signal value from the 9th defined MACD value (index 33); alignment_of returns None when there are fewer than 35 closes.
Alignment on a timeframe: +1 when BOTH MACD lines are above their own signal lines, -1 when BOTH are below, otherwise None (including equality). Dominance: the slowest aligned timeframe in stack_order (first in the tuple whose alignment is not None) gives dominant_tf / dominant_dir.
Entry evaluation per symbol, in order: manage an existing position instead (below) and skip entry for that symbol; skip if there are no entry-timeframe bars; (flat-by-close variants) skip new entries at or after the flat time; require dominant_dir is not None, the entry-timeframe alignment is not None, and dominant_dir == entry-timeframe alignment, otherwise no trade; require a trigger on the last closed entry-timeframe bar: crossover first (MACD-E crosses its signal in the direction: previous diff at or below / at or above zero, last diff strictly into the direction), else curl (MACD-W or MACD-E line's last step is into the direction and at least one of the two preceding steps was against it; needs 4 defined MACD values); spot is the last entry-timeframe close.
Contract selection (select_contract): right is call for direction +1 and put for -1, long premium only. A chain row must have that right, an expiry, a strike and an OCC symbol, DTE (expiry minus session date, calendar days) at or above this sleeve's min_dte, and a quote passing the gate: ask > 0, bid > 0 and (ask - bid) / mid <= 0.10, with bid / ask taken from quotes by OCC when present, else from the row. Among passing rows the nearest expiry (smallest DTE) wins; the strike is then chosen by this sleeve's strike_mode (below). No passing row means no trade.
Sizing (qty_for_position): if ask * 100 exceeds 25% of slice capital (ctx.equity), qty is 0 and there is no trade; otherwise qty = floor(2000 / (ask * 100)), further capped to floor(0.25 * capital / (ask * 100)). The entry Intent is enter, side buy / buy_to_open, qty as computed, price_hint round(spot, 2), reason naming the sleeve, long_call / long_put, symbol, OCC, strike, expiry, ask, qty, trigger and dominant timeframe; a plan {occ, strike, expiry, entry_premium=ask, qty, direction, partial_done=False} is stored in dmac_state for that symbol (premacd adds premium_samples / premium_occ).
Exits in _manage(), one exit Intent at most, first matching reason wins in this order (premacd and flat variants vary the middle steps exactly as their joint states): (1) flat-by-close, if this sleeve sets it, at or after 15:55 ET; (2) premium stop: held-contract mid (bid + ask) / 2 at or below 0.50 * entry_premium, full exit; (3) alignment break: if the position is not yet partial, dominant_dir != direction (a None reading counts as a break), full exit; if it is partial (a runner), the entry-timeframe alignment != direction, full exit (the runner therefore ignores faster-timeframe flips while the entry timeframe holds); (4) partial, only if not yet partial and mid >= 2.0 * entry_premium (+100%): sell ceil(qty * entry / current) contracts, always leaving at least 1 runner (sell is clamped to qty - 1 when qty > 1); if qty == 1 the partial is a full exit; it fires once and sets partial_done; (5) runner exit, only if partial: MACD-E crosses against the position on the entry timeframe, or DTE <= 5, whichever is evaluated first in the code (cross check, then the 5-DTE check sets the reason when no cross reason exists). Exit Intents are exit, side sell / sell_to_close, price_hint round(mid, 2) when a mid exists, reason naming the sleeve, the exit reason, OCC and quantity.

## Data inputs
Inputs are bars (daily bars per symbol), bars:*:1Day, bars:*:1Hour and bars:*:1Week (weekly bars and the 1Hour bars the 4H stack is aggregated from, all from the warm bars product), options (chains per symbol), positions, quotes (bid / ask by OCC) and session_fills. The strategy consumes ctx.extra bars_weekly, bars_1h, chains and quotes, ctx.session, ctx.now, ctx.equity, and ctx.extra sleeve_state ({symbol: {position: {occ, strike, expiry, entry_premium, qty, direction, partial_done}}}) plus ctx.extra dmac_state, which the adapter round-trips to disk. Forming (unclosed) bars never reach the MACDs.

## Install and run
1. Place the sleeve module and adapter at the paths listed under Files in this repo, and place every listed shared engine file at its listed repo path (sleeves/muse/core/sleeve.py is core/sleeve.py on a fresh desk; sleeves/trullas_divergence.py and sleeves/investandbefree_dmac.py are the same paths on a fresh desk).
2. Register the config row for `investandbefree_dmac` from engines/config/sleeves.json (repo copy of config/sleeves.json, also at config/sleeves.json) in the fresh desk's config/sleeves.json, exactly as given in the Configuration section, with no parameter overrides.
3. Wire the producers named in Data inputs so the inputs listed in the Configuration section arrive in the cycle snapshot (view) in the shapes the Full algorithm section describes.
4. Entry point: the adapter module adapters3/investandbefree_dmac.py, whose evaluate(view, ctx) builds a SnapshotSleeveContext and delegates to adapters/investandbefree_dmac.py, which runs sleeves/investandbefree_dmac.py signal(bars, slice_state, context).
5. First evaluation: run one cycle on a fixture snapshot that satisfies the entry conditions in the Full algorithm section and confirm the expected intent (symbol, side, quantity rule and reason string naming this sleeve) is emitted before enabling live paper routing. If the Configuration section says parked is true, keep the sleeve parked exactly as in that row; the waiting behaviour in the Full algorithm section is the expected behaviour until its named input route is proven.

## Verification
- Run integration/test_investandbefree_dmac.py in this repo: MACD seeding / alignment, 4H aggregation, contract selection, sizing, partial arithmetic and the adapter drive must pass; the build record's golden fixture produces exactly one entry Intent (ATM call at the nearest expiry at least 30 days out, qty floor(2000 / (ask * 100)) subject to the 25% cap).
- Fixture with dominant direction differing from the daily direction, no crossover or curl on the last closed daily bar, a spread over 10%, no expiry at least 30 days out, or one contract alone over the 25% cap: no entry.
- Fixture with a held position: mid at or below 50% of entry exits in full before any partial; a dominant-direction break exits in full; mid at or above 2x entry sells ceil(qty * entry / current) contracts leaving at least 1 runner (qty 1 exits in full); a runner then exits on a daily MACD-E cross against it or at 5 DTE.
- Fixture with a forming (unclosed) daily, weekly or hourly bar that would change a MACD reading if counted: the recreation ignores it (closed bars only).
- The config row used by the recreation matches engines/config/sleeves.json for investandbefree_dmac (module, cadence, session, slice, parked state and inputs).
