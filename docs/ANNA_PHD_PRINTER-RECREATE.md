## What this strategy is

A daily printer book over Anna's labeled census of 115 names. It holds a census name while the PhD replay of Anna's printer says buy on daily bars: in on a cyan or green print, out on a red or magenta print or a close back under the 200 day EMA. It began as a recorder and was converted into a funded paper book by ruling D32 (2026-10-26) because its source defines complete entry, exit and size rules. Its first live signal was pending at the strategy card's pin. The census holds the S and P 500 index (^GSPC); with no equity quote it is never bought.

## Full algorithm

Evaluation order, from the code in this repo. The replica logic is the Python port in `sleeves/[family]/anna.py` (build_anna_replica with the gospel variant, which is the variant the PhD replay uses), the location printer in the same module (classify_location and apply_printer), the producer in `sleeves/[family]/monitor/` (the PhD monitor module; its filename carries the blocked name and is not reproduced), and the book in `sleeves/[family]/monitor/printer_books.py` (PhdBook).

1. Producer run. After each close the family job runs the replay under Node over the census names (job schedule and cache in Configuration). Inputs are daily bars over a 2 year range in the replay's fetch shape: fields t (epoch ms), o, h, l, c, v with a missing volume as 0, completed sessions only at the book. Ticker mapping for the census: BTC maps to BTC-USD, ETH to ETH-USD, SPX to ^GSPC, VIX to ^VIX; a ticker longer than 5 characters maps to nothing and is dropped; other tickers pass through. Census names are the events in the census file with a price, a primary side of buy or sell, and a ticker, in first seen order: 115 names.
2. Indicators per name, on the daily bars: EMA 8, 13, 21, 34, 48 and 200; Bollinger and Keltner bands from the squeeze block; ATR 14 (Wilder); RSI 14; MACD; relative volume over 20; session VWAP; the Strat bar classification. Squeeze uses the epoch table for the gospel variant: epochs E0 to E3 read squeeze length 14 and momentum length 9; the gospel E4 epoch, from 2026-05-22, already reads 20 and 10 (the non gospel table switches at E4b, 2026-06-17); E5, from 2026-07-26, reads 20 and 10.
3. Seven votes on each completed daily bar: (a) Stochastic RSI K over D (RSI 14, stochastic 14, K smoothing 3, D smoothing 3; if K equals D the vote follows the 1 bar slope of K); (b) Aroon up over down, length 14; (c) plus DI over minus DI, length 14 Wilder; (d) OBV 5 bar slope positive; (e) squeeze momentum (linear regression endpoint of the squeeze source over the epoch momentum length; the vote is bullish if its 1 bar slope is positive, else if the momentum value is at least 0); (f) EMA 8 over EMA 21; (g) WaveTrend 1 over 2 (channel 10, average 21, smoothing 4). A vote that cannot be read is unavailable, not neutral.
4. Buy gate: all seven votes available, at least 5 bullish, and bullish minus bearish at least 4.
5. Base markers: a UT Bot flip up (ATR length 10, sensitivity 1) through the gate is CYAN_BUY. An EMA 8 over 21 cross up within 6 bars of a UT Bot up flip, through the gate, is GREEN_BUY. A UT Bot flip down is RED_SELL (no gate). An EMA 8 over 21 cross down within 6 bars of a UT Bot down flip is MAGENTA_SELL. On a same bar buy and sell clash the gospel variant lets the buy marker win (the buy is assigned last); the seven vote dashboard variant lets the sell win instead.
6. Location classification per bar (classify_location, options deepRel 0.65, minBull 5, invertLaunchpadSell true, warnUpgrade false, padSequence true, supportBounce true, reclaimAllowRed true, warnConfirm true, holdCyan false, holdLook 22, holdRel 0.94, holdLast true, holdLastBars 2). Position in the 20 bar range is pos20; drawdown dd is (20 bar high minus the lowest close of the last 15 bars) divided by the high; rel is close divided by EMA 200; bbPct is the close's position inside the Bollinger band. Locations: above (close over EMA 200), below, reclaim (above today and not above yesterday), launchpad (deep pad: not above and rel at most 0.65; or washout: dd at least 0.18, days above EMA 200 at most 2, pos20 at most 0.65), failed euphoria (not above, pos20 at least 0.88, bbPct at least 1.2, stochastic K at least 88), extended (days above at least 8, pos20 at least 0.88, bbPct at least 0.85, stochastic K at least 85), fight (close within 0.6 times ATR 14 of EMA 200).
7. Location re-prints, which override or add to the base marker: a RED or MAGENTA print on a deep pad with at least 5 bullish votes and not failed euphoria inverts to a buy. A reclaim with bbPct between 0.35 and 0.92 is an extra buy. A pad sequence buy: not cooled (no buy print in the prior 8 bars), deep pad or washout, not failed euphoria, not above, at least 6 bullish votes, squeeze momentum rising, bbPct between 0.20 and 1.05, and close at or over the open. A support bounce buy: not cooled, not failed euphoria, not an extended failure, at least 5 bullish votes, rel between 0.82 and 1.04, bbPct between 0.20 and 1.05, close at or over the open, pos20 between 0.35 and 0.85, and the bar low within 1.5 times ATR 14 of the lowest low of the last 10 bars. A warn confirm buy: the previous bar was a GOLD_WARNING, not failed euphoria, not an extended failure, at least 5 bullish votes, rel between 0.88 and 1.05, bbPct between 0.20 and 1.05. A failed euphoria, or an extended location whose high fails to beat the highest high of the prior 3 bars, is an extra sell. A sell marker is suppressed (held) when the last flip was a buy at most 2 bars ago and rel is at least 0.94, unless the bar is an extra sell.
8. Signal persistence (the replay lines the book cites, 833 to 845 of the source script): the signal becomes buy on a CYAN or GREEN print and sell on a RED or MAGENTA print; otherwise it carries the previous signal, except that while the previous signal is buy, a close crossing below EMA 200 (previous close at or over EMA 200) turns the signal to sell.
9. Book reads (PhdBook): after the session close, if the producer cache for that session exists and has not yet been printed by the book, the book takes, for each census name whose newest replay read is dated that session, a decision: want long if the signal is buy, want flat otherwise. Each decision lapses at the close of the next trading session.
10. Orders, at the book's session passes (window in Configuration; session passes begin 5 minutes after the open): each decision is sent once. A name wanted long and not held gets a market buy of one slot, but only with a live equity quote over 0; a name wanted flat and held gets a market sell. A name with a working order holds its next decision until that order ends. An unsent decision is dropped when it lapses and the producer's next read decides again. The book takes up the printer's current state when it starts: a name whose signal is already buy is bought, not only a fresh print.
11. Exit: the signal turning sell under steps 7 and 8, which the book executes under step 10. No stop of its own.
