# saty_gpt_v03: Recreation package

Provenance: Sol (ChatGPT), Saty Adaptive Opportunity Engine v0.3.0, from Saty Mahajan's public vocabulary (Source row in strategies/muse/muse-saty-gpt-v03.md in this repo).

## What this sleeve is
This sleeve is a thin desk wrapper over a complete ported Saty research engine: it trades SPY shares long or short on ten Saty-vocabulary setups from completed 10-minute bars, with structural stops, scale-outs and a 1-ATR runner trail. The wrapper itself contains no strategy math; all rules live in the ported core/saty_gpt_v03/ package. Sources: desk sleeves/saty_gpt_v03.py (SYM=SPY, PROVIDER_FEED/PROVIDER=sip, REPLAY_BARS=120, WARMUP_BARS=1000, MAX_QUOTE_AGE_S=5.0; CONFIG cadence_s=60, universe=(SPY,)) and strategies/muse/muse-saty-gpt-v03.md in this repo.

## Full algorithm
Position management runs first in every pass in desk sleeves/saty_gpt_v03.py signal(); exits are never trapped behind warmup or any other entry block.
1. Warmup: 1000 actual completed bars per required clock (10m and 1H) for SPY, no per-clock override. Until both clocks reach 1000 the sleeve stamps blocked_warmup with per-clock counts and emits no entries. The FeatureBook is rebuilt each pass by replaying the door-supplied completed-bar history in availability order (REPLAY_BARS=120 bounded replay when no checkpoint); durable episode, lot and engine state rides the journal in ctx.extra (gpt_v03_journal), restored only when its version and config fingerprint match this build, otherwise rejected, never migrated. Expression is SPY shares, long or short (pinned config main_10m_1h.json, expression equity); options are OFF; VIX and options flow are absent and never invented; daily bars serve only to build the day Reference; 1D/1W streams are not required by the pinned config.
2. The ten setups (core/saty_gpt_v03/strategies.py StrategyEngine; pinned StrategyConfig values read from the wrapper: setup_expiry_bars=6, minimum_trend_bars=3, transition_memory_bars=12, minimum_compression_bars=3, maximum_compression_bars=30, compression_stop=boundary, tolerance_atr=0.1, zone_tolerance_atr=0.1, stop_buffer_atr=0.1, max_chase_atr=0.4, minimum_net_reward_risk=1.5, estimated_round_trip_cost=0.02, minimum_phase_divergence=5.0, minimum_price_divergence_atr=0.05, maximum_pivot_separation_bars=50, maximum_pivot_age_bars=50, phase_exit_threshold=61.8, countertrend_risk_multiplier=0.5, context_timeframe=1H, require_context_alignment=False): three Vomy ribbon transitions (EMA 13, 48 and 21 rejections), Golden Gate, compression breakout and retest, pivot-ribbon pullback, ATR-level retest, and two countertrend reversals (pivot divergence; the Phase Oscillator leaving plus or minus 61.8). The named Golden Gate rule: in an EMA 8 > 21 > 48 stack, a close through plus or minus 38.2 percent of the day's ATR ladder, then a pullback to the gate or EMA 21 that holds EMA 48, then a close beyond that bar's extreme and EMA 8; targets at 50 percent and 61.8 percent.
3. Every signal: a stop 0.1 ATR beyond its structure; the close within 0.4 ATR of the setup's anchor; net reward to the nearest level ahead at least 1.5 times net risk after a 2-cent round trip; good for 6 bars.
4. Selection in v0.3: all ten families advance independently and the OpportunityRouter (core/saty_gpt_v03/routing.py) ranks candidates by net reward/risk to the first obstacle, then fit with the SPY / QQQ / IWM path regime (directional for trend setups, choppy for reversals; the AwarenessBook in core/saty_gpt_v03/awareness.py supplies that context, with event_state stamped event_unknown_coverage every pass because the scheduled-event calendar is absent and require_event_calendar_coverage=false in the pinned config, so it does not block), then chase room left, then age; only the top candidate's direction survives, and an exact opposite tie waits.
5. At the quote (at most MAX_QUOTE_AGE_S=5.0 seconds old): the stop still valid, every target still ahead, chase at most 0.4 ATR, adverse gap at most 0.15 ATR, net R to the nearest target still at least 1.5; entry is a limit at the ask (long) or bid (short). Entries run from the open to 30 minutes before the close, one position at a time.
6. Sizing (core/saty_gpt_v03/risk_lots.py): risk R = 0.25 percent of the session-start $10,000 slice ($25) per trade, half for a countertrend setup; shares = floor(risk / per-share risk), at most 100 percent of the slice in notional and the quoted size; no entries after a 2R session loss or after 3 entries in the session.
7. Exits: Stop: a completed 10-minute close beyond the stop sells everything. Targets in order: the first sells half the shares, each later one a quarter, keeping one share unless the lot is a single share (a 1-share lot closes whole). After a profitable partial / the first scale-out the stop moves to the entry; after a completed target it trails 1 ATR behind the best price since entry; stops never loosen. There is no end-of-day flat in v0.3: a position carries overnight until its stop or a target. Exits are limits at the bid (long) or ask (short).

## Data inputs
Inputs are bars, quotes, options and session_fills (the options input is declared in the roster row but options expression is OFF in the pinned config). The existing doc's Data section names: SPY 5-minute regular-session bars (about 34,000, built into 10-minute and 1-hour bars), SPY daily bars (200), IEX quotes at most 5 seconds old, and the sleeve's own fills. The journal channel (session_fills / the ledger-served journal) carries the durable engine checkpoint between passes.

## Install and run
1. Place the sleeve module and adapter at the paths listed under Files in this repo, and place every listed shared engine file at its listed repo path (an engines/ path maps back to the desk path after engines/, for example engines/desk3/enhance26.py is desk3/enhance26.py on a fresh desk; a sleeves/muse/core/ path is the same path on a fresh desk).
2. Register the config row for `saty_gpt_v03` from engines/config/sleeves.json (repo copy of config/sleeves.json, also at config/sleeves.json) in the fresh desk's config/sleeves.json, exactly as given in the Configuration section, with no parameter overrides: the roster row carries params {} for this sleeve.
3. Wire the producers named in Data inputs so the inputs listed in the Configuration section arrive in the cycle snapshot (view) in the shapes the Full algorithm section describes.
4. Entry point: the adapter module adapters3/saty_gpt_v03.py, whose evaluate(view, ctx) delegates to the sleeve module sleeves/saty_gpt_v03.py signal(bars, slice_state, context), which drives core/saty_gpt_v03/strategies.py StrategyEngine.
5. First evaluation: run one cycle on a fixture snapshot that satisfies the entry conditions in the Full algorithm section and confirm the expected intent (symbol, side, quantity rule and reason string naming this sleeve) is emitted before enabling live paper routing. If the Configuration section says parked is true, keep entries disabled exactly as in that row; exits are still managed where the algorithm says so.

## Verification
- Fixture with fewer than 1000 completed 10-minute or 1-hour bars for SPY: the evaluation stamps blocked_warmup and emits no entry, while a held position still receives its stop and target exits.
- Fixture with a qualifying Golden Gate sequence (EMA 8 > 21 > 48 stack, close through 38.2 percent, pullback holding EMA 48, close beyond the pullback bar's extreme and EMA 8), a fresh quote within 5 seconds and net R at least 1.5: exactly one limit entry at the ask (long), shares = floor($25 / per-share risk) (half risk if countertrend).
- Fixture with a held position whose price reaches its structural stop: a full exit; at the first target: a half exit with the stop moved to the entry.
- Fixture after a 2R session loss or a third session entry: no further entries.
- No entry intent is emitted when a required input is missing or a filter, gate or age check has no evidence: the code fails closed.
- Quantity, price hint, stop price and reason on every emitted intent match the sizing and price rules in the Full algorithm section for the fixture inputs.
- The config row used by the recreation matches engines/config/sleeves.json for this sleeve id (module, cadence, session, slice, parked state and inputs).
