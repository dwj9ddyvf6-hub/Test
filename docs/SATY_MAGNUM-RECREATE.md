# saty_magnum: Recreation package

Provenance: Saty Mahajan's system (ATR Levels, Pivot Ribbon Pro, Phase Oscillator, Volume Stack), from his posts (Source row in strategies/muse/muse-saty-magnum.md in this repo).

## What this sleeve is
This sleeve trades SPY shares long or short, intraday, when the daily and 10-minute Pivot Ribbons agree: Arm A is a close-confirmed Golden Gate with a pullback retest entry, Arm B is a compression release, and Arm C is a quantified oscillator snap-back, arbitrated in that fixed C, B, A order in the code's arm naming (Arm C evaluated as the snap-back arm, then B, then A per the module header's fixed arbitration order). Sources: desk sleeves/saty_magnum.py (constants F_TRIGGER=0.236, F_GATE=0.382, F_MID=0.500, F_TARGET=0.618, F_RUNNER=0.786, F_FULL=1.000, F_EXT=1.236, F_SQUEEZE=2.000, SNAP_M3=-100.0, SNAP_M10=-100.0, SNAP_H1=-80.0, QUOTE_STALE_S=60.0, RISK_PCT=0.005, NOTIONAL_CAP=0.25, SESSION_STOP_PCT=0.01, FRESH_S=1800.0, RETEST_WINDOW_S=3600.0, CHOP_CROSSINGS=3, CHOP_BARS=12, OPEN_WAIT_MIN=10, MIDDAY 11:30-13:30, ENTRY_CUTOFF_MIN=30, ARM_C_TIME_STOP_MIN=60, COIL_MIN_BARS=2, VS_MIN_PCT=55.0) and strategies/muse/muse-saty-magnum.md in this repo.

## Full algorithm
Evaluation is deterministic reconstruction on every 60-second pass in desk sleeves/saty_magnum.py; position facts come from the broker record, and a held position's arm is attributed by replaying the session's entry events (an unattributable position is managed on the Arm A baseline and stamped ownership_mismatch). Intent.stop and Intent.target are never set.
1. Plan (before the open): the day ladder is frozen from the previous completed regular-session daily close (anchor) and that period's ATR(14) (unit): levels at anchor plus or minus unit times 0.236, 0.382, 0.500, 0.618, 0.786, 1.000, with extensions to 1.236 and 2.000; plus the weekly (Multiday) ladder as context, the range gauge, the daily regime, and a VIX key level (the prior session's ^VIX close, recorded, never traded).
2. Regime gate: the Pro ribbon is EMA 8/13/21/48/200 with pullback overlap on (there is no 34 EMA in the Pro). Trend up requires both Pro ribbon clouds (EMA 8 >= 21 and EMA 13 >= 48) up on BOTH the daily and the 10-minute frames; trend down requires all down; repeated crossings (CHOP_CROSSINGS=3 within CHOP_BARS=12) are chop and no arm trades in a sideways market.
3. Schedule and caps: entries from 09:40 ET (OPEN_WAIT_MIN=10 after the 09:30 open) to 30 minutes before the close; Arms A and B stand down 11:30-13:30 ET and once the day's range reaches 90 percent of the unit; caps are 2 Arm A, 1 Arm B and 1 Arm C entries per episode, 3 entries per session, one position at a time; a -1 percent session (SESSION_STOP_PCT=0.01) stops new entries; events older than FRESH_S=1800 seconds are skipped. Orders are limits at the ask (long) or bid (short) from an IEX quote at most 60 seconds old.
4. Arm C, snap-back (half size): the 3-minute Phase Oscillator is strictly below -100, the 10-minute below -100 and the 1-hour below -80 (mirrored above +100/+100/+80 for shorts), AND momentum is already waning (the 3-minute oscillator already turning); one entry per episode.
5. Arm B, compression release: the Phase Oscillator compression predicate is true on at least COIL_MIN_BARS=2 consecutive completed 10-minute bars, then the first bar out closes beyond the coil range with the oscillator colour agreeing; not against both 10-minute clouds or the opposite daily trend.
6. Arm A, Golden Gate continuation: a completed 10-minute bar closes through the plus or minus 38.2 percent gate (a 10-minute close back through the 23.6 percent trigger cancels it); within RETEST_WINDOW_S=3600 seconds a completed 3-minute bar reaches the zone between the gate and the 10-minute EMA 21 and closes back beyond the gate (the pullback retest), confirmed on that bar by the Volume Stack with at least VS_MIN_PCT=55.0 percent of volume split on the trade's side; the regime must be in trend that way; the 10-minute oscillator must not be beyond the opposite 100 and the 1-hour not beyond the opposite 61.8.
7. Sizing: risk 0.5 percent of the $10,000 slice (RISK_PCT=0.005) to the arm's invalidation level (the 23.6 percent trigger for Arm A, the coil midpoint for Arm B, the opposite trigger for Arm C at half size), capped at 25 percent of the slice in notional (NOTIONAL_CAP=0.25); whole shares, none under 1.
8. Exits (underlying close rules and level touches, never premium; limits at the bid for longs / ask for shorts; flat at market 5 minutes before the close; a position from an earlier session is sold at once): Arm A: a 10-minute close back through the 23.6 percent trigger exits all; a third off at +50 percent, a third at +61.8 percent (then a 10-minute close back through the entry exits the rest, the break-even rule), the rest at +100 percent (123.6 percent when the next weekly-ladder level lies beyond it). Arm B: a 10-minute close through the coil midpoint exits; thirds at +50 and +61.8 percent with the same break-even rule, half the runner at +100 percent, the rest at +200 percent. Arm C: out when the 10-minute oscillator returns to -23.6 (+23.6 for a short), on a 10-minute close beyond the opposite 23.6 percent trigger, or after ARM_C_TIME_STOP_MIN=60 minutes.

## Configuration
Module adapters3.saty_magnum; cadence_s 60; session rth; slice capital_usd 10000.0, cash_usd 10000.0; bp_buffer_frac 0.02; flat_by_close false; flatten_time_et null; params {}; inputs [bars, quotes, options, session_fills]; parked false. Universe: SPY shares, long and short; timeframes 3m, 10m, 1h, 1d (plus the weekly ladder as context). Source: muse-desk-v3/config/sleeves.json row id=saty_magnum.

## Data inputs
Inputs are bars, quotes, options and session_fills. The existing doc's Data section names: SPY 5-minute bars (about 52,000, for the daily ribbon warmup) and 1-minute bars (1,200), IEX quotes, ^VIX daily closes, and the sleeve's own fills. Bars are resampled by core/saty_bars into the 3m, 10m, 1h and daily frames the arms read; no bars means no entries, while a held position still receives its time-based exits.

## Install and run
1. Place the sleeve module and adapter at the paths listed under Files in this repo, and place every listed shared engine file at its listed repo path (an engines/ path maps back to the desk path after engines/, for example engines/desk3/enhance26.py is desk3/enhance26.py on a fresh desk; a sleeves/muse/core/ path is the same path on a fresh desk).
2. Register the config row for `saty_magnum` from engines/config/sleeves.json (repo copy of config/sleeves.json, also at config/sleeves.json) in the fresh desk's config/sleeves.json, exactly as given in the Configuration section, with no parameter overrides: the roster row carries params {} for this sleeve.
3. Wire the producers named in Data inputs so the inputs listed in the Configuration section arrive in the cycle snapshot (view) in the shapes the Full algorithm section describes.
4. Entry point: the adapter module adapters3/saty_magnum.py, whose evaluate(view, ctx) delegates to the sleeve module sleeves/saty_magnum.py signal(bars, slice_state, context).
5. First evaluation: run one cycle on a fixture snapshot that satisfies the entry conditions in the Full algorithm section and confirm the expected intent (symbol, side, quantity rule and reason string naming this sleeve) is emitted before enabling live paper routing. If the Configuration section says parked is true, keep entries disabled exactly as in that row; exits are still managed where the algorithm says so.

## Verification
- Fixture with both ribbon frames in trend up, a completed 10-minute close through +38.2 percent, and within 60 minutes a completed 3-minute retest of the gate zone closing back beyond the gate with at least 55 percent volume split on the long side: exactly one limit buy entry at the ask, sized at 0.5 percent risk to the 23.6 percent trigger and capped at 25 percent notional.
- Fixture with ribbon chop (3 crossings in 12 bars) or a midday 11:30-13:30 timestamp for Arm A: no entry.
- Fixture with the 3m, 10m and 1h oscillators at -101, -101 and -81 with the 3-minute already turning: one half-size Arm C entry; the same fixture without the turn: no entry.
- Fixture with a held Arm A position and a 10-minute close back through the 23.6 percent trigger: a full exit at the bid.
- No entry intent is emitted when a required input is missing or a filter, gate or age check has no evidence: the code fails closed.
- Quantity, price hint, stop price and reason on every emitted intent match the sizing and price rules in the Full algorithm section for the fixture inputs.
- The config row used by the recreation matches engines/config/sleeves.json for this sleeve id (module, cadence, session, slice, parked state and inputs).
