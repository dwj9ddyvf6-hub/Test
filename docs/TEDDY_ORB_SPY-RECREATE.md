# teddy_orb_spy: Recreation package

Provenance: X post, strategy from his posted video, installed by owner order 2026-10-28 (provenance statement in sleeves/teddy_orb_spy.py in this repo; the post author's handle is omitted here under the repo PII rule). Spec: docs/TEDDY-ORB-SPY-SPEC-2026-10-28.md in this repo.

## What this sleeve is
This sleeve trades a SPY opening-range breakout: it fixes the 09:30 to 10:00 ET range from six 5-minute bars, requires a volume-qualified breakout, waits for a lower-volume pullback of 1 to 6 bars, and enters on the first continuation close beyond the pullback extreme, with the stop just inside the broken edge and a measured-move or 2R target. Its edge is unproven by design: the live paper record is the test. Sources: desk sleeves/teddy_orb_spy.py and adapters3/teddy_orb_spy.py.

## Full algorithm
Strategy (desk sleeves/teddy_orb_spy.py, frozen spec TEDDY-ORB-SPY-SPEC-2026-10-28), evaluated as TeddyOrbSpySleeve.signal() in this order. Constants: SYMBOL=SPY; OR_BARS=6; BREAKOUT_VOL_MULT=1.5; STOP_INSIDE=0.10; TARGET_R_MULT=2.0; MAX_PULLBACK_BARS=6; DRIFT_BARS=6; BREAKOUT_CUTOFF=12:00 ET; ENTRY_CUTOFF=12:30 ET; FLAT_TIME=14:00 ET; UNIT_RISK_PCT=0.0025; NOTIONAL_CAP_PCT=0.25.
1. Data preparation: current-session RTH SPY 1-minute SIP bars only (zero-volume bars dropped) are aggregated into 5-minute bars in the core (buckets anchored at 09:30 ET, open first, high max, low min, close last, volume sum; a bucket is emitted only when all 5 constituent minutes are present, so a forming bucket never appears).
2. State: ctx.extra teddy_state resets per session (plan None, dead_directions empty). If a plan existed, the position was seen open, and it is now flat, that direction is marked dead and the plan cleared. With a plan and an open position, management applies (step 6). With a plan but no fill yet, wait. With a position but no plan, only the 14:00 ET hard flat applies.
3. Opening range: from the first six 5-minute bars (09:30 to 10:00 ET), OR high is their maximum high, OR low their minimum low, and OR mean volume is their mean volume; the range is fixed at 10:00 ET and never repaints. Fewer than six 5-minute bars means no entry scan.
4. Setup replay (_scan), a deterministic replay of today's post-range bars: a breakout qualifies only at or before 12:00 ET when a 5-minute bar closes above OR high (long) or below OR low (short) with volume at least 1.5 times OR mean volume; a close through the range without that volume does not qualify. After a qualified breakout, a pullback bar is a counter-move close versus the prior bar; every pullback bar's volume must be below the breakout bar's volume (a pullback bar at or above it kills the setup), no bar may close back inside the range (that invalidation kills that direction for the session), and the pullback may run 1 to 6 bars. The entry trigger is the first bar after the pullback that closes beyond the pullback extreme in the breakout direction (above the pullback high for a long, below the pullback low for a short) with volume greater than the last pullback bar. If no trigger fires within the 6-bar pullback window plus 6 further bars (12 bars after the breakout bar), the setup expires and that direction is dead. One qualified breakout per direction per day; the opposite direction may still qualify once.
5. Entry gates, stop, target and sizing: no entry at or after 12:30 ET (the latest 5-minute bar time is checked against ENTRY_CUTOFF); slice equity must be available. Stop for a long is OR high minus 0.10 (just inside the broken edge); for a short, OR low plus 0.10. Target is the measured move (OR height projected from the broken edge) or 2R from entry, whichever is closer. Qty = floor(min(0.25 percent of equity / risk, 25 percent of equity / entry)); qty at or below 0 emits nothing. One open position at a time. The entry Intent carries stop and target exit rules and a reason naming the direction, range, entry, stop and target, and the plan is stored.
6. Management: hard flat first (at or after 14:00 ET, exit in full). Then the invalidation exit: a 5-minute close back inside the opening range after entry exits the trade (the intent emitted on that bar's close fills at the next bar open, per desk convention). Stop and target are carried as exit rules on the entry intent.

## Data inputs
Inputs are bars, the literal bars:SPY:1Min warm product, positions, quotes and session_fills. Intraday bars are Alpaca SIP only (volume signals use SIP only). The core needs today's complete 1-minute bars from 09:30 ET onward so every 5-minute bucket has all 5 minutes. Positions and session state arrive via the snapshot context: the adapter round-trips ctx.extra teddy_state beside the sleeve journal. The day's setup sequence is recomputed deterministically each cycle from today's completed 5-minute bars, so only the open-trade plan and dead directions persist.

## Install and run
1. Place the sleeve module and adapter at the paths listed under Files in this repo, and place every listed shared engine file at its listed repo path (an engines/ path maps back to the desk path after engines/; a desk3/ path is the same path on a fresh desk).
2. Register the config row for `teddy_orb_spy` from engines/config/sleeves.json (repo copy of config/sleeves.json, also at config/sleeves.json) in the fresh desk's config/sleeves.json, exactly as given in the Configuration section, with no parameter overrides.
3. Wire the producers named in Data inputs so the inputs listed in the Configuration section arrive in the cycle snapshot (view) in the shapes the Full algorithm section describes.
4. Entry point: the v3 worker module adapters3/teddy_orb_spy.py, whose evaluate(view, ctx) rebuilds the snapshot context and delegates to adapters/teddy_orb_spy.py, which runs sleeves/teddy_orb_spy.py signal().
5. First evaluation: run one cycle on a fixture snapshot that satisfies the entry conditions in the Full algorithm section and confirm the expected intent (symbol, side, quantity rule and reason string naming this sleeve) is emitted before enabling live paper routing. If the Configuration section says parked is true, keep entries disabled exactly as in that row; exits are still managed where the algorithm says so.

## Verification
- Tests in this repo: integration/test_teddy_orb_spy.py and tests/test_teddy_orb_spy_blockers.py must pass against the recreation.
- Fixture with six opening-range bars, a breakout close beyond the range on volume at least 1.5 times OR mean volume before 12:00 ET, a 1 to 6 bar lower-volume pullback with no close back inside the range, and a continuation close beyond the pullback extreme on volume above the last pullback bar, before 12:30 ET: exactly one entry, stop 0.10 inside the broken edge, target the nearer of the measured move and 2R, qty floor(min(0.0025 times equity / risk, 0.25 times equity / entry)).
- Fixture whose breakout close lacks the 1.5 times volume, or whose pullback bar volume reaches the breakout volume, or with any close back inside the range before entry: no entry, and that direction is dead for the session. Fixture at or after 14:00 ET with a held position: exactly one full exit.
- No entry intent is emitted when a required input is missing or a filter, gate or age check has no evidence: the code fails closed.
- Quantity, price hint, stop price and reason on every emitted intent match the sizing and price rules in the Full algorithm section for the fixture inputs.
- The config row used by the recreation matches engines/config/sleeves.json for this sleeve id (module, cadence, session, slice, parked state and inputs).
