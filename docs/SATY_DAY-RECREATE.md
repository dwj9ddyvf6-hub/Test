# saty_day: Recreation package

Provenance: Milkman Trades' SATY day system (Golden Gate, Bilbo, Trigger Box), on Saty Mahajan's indicators (Source row in strategies/muse/muse-saty-day.md in this repo).

## What this sleeve is
This sleeve trades same-day (0DTE) SPY options intraday on a frozen Saty ATR ladder: Golden Gate touches at the 38.2 percent level, Trigger Box breakouts after an open inside the trigger box, and range-day scalps fading the 38.2 percent level. Everything is flat by the close and a session down $10,000 takes no new entries. Sources: desk sleeves/saty_day.py (constants F_TRIGGER=0.236, F_GATE=0.382, F_MID=0.500, F_TARGET=0.618, F_RUNNER=0.786, F_FULL=1.000, PO_EXTREME=61.8, SPREAD_GATE=0.05, QUOTE_STALE_S=60.0, BUDGET_PCT=0.04, EXPIRY_DTE=0, DAILY_STOP_USD=10000.0, ENTRY_FRESH_S=1800.0, STATION_FRESH_S=600.0, RANGE_LOW_PCT=70.0, RANGE_HIGH_PCT=90.0; CONFIG cadence_s=60, universe=(SPY,), risk_pct=0.04) and strategies/muse/muse-saty-day.md in this repo.

## Full algorithm
Evaluation is a pure reconstruction on every 60-second pass in desk sleeves/saty_day.py signal(): the day plan, touches, gate reads and stations are rebuilt deterministically from completed bars; position facts come from the broker record. No bars means the sleeve stands down for the day.
1. Ladder (frozen before the open): anchor = the previous completed regular-session daily close, unit = that bar's ATR(14); levels at anchor plus or minus unit times 0.236 (trigger), 0.382 (Golden Gate), 0.500 (midpoint), 0.618 (primary target), 0.786 and 1.000 (runner stations). An extended-session ladder is computed and filed as a watch only and never trades.
2. Daily stop first for entries: when this sleeve's session P&L (broker fills for this sleeve, realized plus the open position marked at the current mid) reaches -$10,000, no new entries are taken for the rest of the session; exits, kills and the end-of-day flat continue.
3. Contract rule (all setups): SPY options expiring the same session day only (EXPIRY_DTE=0; no same-day expiry listed is no trade and no other expiry is ever substituted), strike at the money or the nearest listed strike to spot, two-sided quote with spread at or below 5 percent of mid (SPREAD_GATE=0.05) and at most 60 seconds old (QUOTE_STALE_S=60.0). Entry and exit intents carry the OCC in the reason, exact quantity, limit at the quoted mid, buy_to_open / sell_to_close; Intent.stop and Intent.target are deliberately never set (invalidation is an underlying close rule, not a resting premium stop).
4. Golden Gate entry: the first 1-minute touch of the +38.2 percent level (calls) or -38.2 percent level (puts), or a fresh quote beyond it, acted on within ENTRY_FRESH_S=1800 seconds (30 minutes). Gates, all must pass: (a) the last completed 1-hour Phase Oscillator: past +61.8 tags a long Bilbo (runner rights), past -61.8 blocks a long, mirrored for shorts (PO_EXTREME=61.8); (b) the 10-minute Pivot Ribbon Pro: a full opposing stack (EMA 8 < 21 and 13 < 48 against the trade) or a chop read stands the trade down, except on a gap through the level at the open; (c) SPX/NQ confluence with QQQ as the Nasdaq proxy: no Golden Gate entry without QQQ moving the same way (its last 10-minute close beyond its own 23.6 percent level, or its 38.2 percent level touched today). Entries run from 09:30 ET to one hour before the close, one position at a time, no adds.
5. Trigger Box entry: the day opens inside the trigger box (within plus or minus 23.6 percent) and a completed 3-minute bar closes beyond a trigger; acted on within 30 minutes and only until 90 minutes before the close; the same ribbon and chop vetoes apply.
6. Range scalp entry: once a completed hour shows the day's range at or below 70 percent of the unit (RANGE_LOW_PCT) with its close inside the box, and until an hourly range exceeds 90 percent (RANGE_HIGH_PCT) or a 10-minute close goes beyond plus or minus 61.8 percent: buy calls on a touch of -38.2 percent and puts on a touch of +38.2 percent (fade toward the opposite 38.2 percent level).
7. Sizing: premium budget 4 percent of the $10,000 slice equity (BUDGET_PCT=0.04), half for a long Golden Gate from 12:00 ET, whole contracts, at least 1 contract.
8. Exits (keyed to underlying bar closes and level touches, never to option premium; exits are limits at the option mid): Golden Gate: a 10-minute close back through the 23.6 percent trigger exits all; half off at the 50 percent midpoint; all off at 61.8 percent unless Bilbo-tagged or the 10-minute ribbon is fully aligned, in which case half stays on, is halved again at 78.6 percent and exits at 100 percent. Trigger Box: a 3-minute close back through the trigger exits; half at 50 percent; all at 100 percent unless the ribbon is fully aligned, then half rides. Range scalp: out at the opposite 38.2 percent level, or on a 10-minute close beyond the 61.8 percent level past the entry. Flat from 5 minutes before the close (bid from 3 minutes, market from 2 minutes; the roster row also flattens at 15:55 ET); a position from an earlier session is sold at once. Breaks of the 1-hour EMA 21 and the 10-minute EMA 48 are logged, never traded.

## Configuration
Module saty_day; cadence_s 60; session rth; slice capital_usd 10000.0, cash_usd 10000.0; bp_buffer_frac 0.02; flat_by_close true; flatten_time_et 15:55; params {}; inputs [bars_5min, bars_1min, positions, options_chains, options_quotes, session_fills]; parked false; adapter_dir desk_sleeves. Universe: SPY options (QQQ as confluence proxy); timeframes 1m, 3m, 10m, 1h and the prior daily bar. Source: muse-desk-v3/config/sleeves.json row id=saty_day.

## Data inputs
Inputs are bars_5min, bars_1min, positions, options_chains, options_quotes and session_fills. The existing doc's Data section names: SPY and QQQ 5-minute bars (about 22,000) and 1-minute bars (1,200), IEX quotes, today's SPY option chain and quotes, and the sleeve's own fills (session_fills feeds the -$10,000 daily-stop P&L read). Indicator math comes only from core/saty_bars and core/saty_indicators; the sleeve never consults the Volume Stack.

## Install and run
1. Place the sleeve module and adapter at the paths listed under Files in this repo, and place every listed shared engine file at its listed repo path (an engines/ path maps back to the desk path after engines/, for example engines/desk3/enhance26.py is desk3/enhance26.py on a fresh desk; a sleeves/muse/core/ path is the same path on a fresh desk).
2. Register the config row for `saty_day` from engines/config/sleeves.json (repo copy of config/sleeves.json, also at config/sleeves.json) in the fresh desk's config/sleeves.json, exactly as given in the Configuration section, with no parameter overrides: the roster row carries params {} for this sleeve.
3. Wire the producers named in Data inputs so the inputs listed in the Configuration section arrive in the cycle snapshot (view) in the shapes the Full algorithm section describes.
4. Entry point: the sleeve module sleeves/saty_day.py, whose signal(bars, slice_state, context) is the entry point (the v3 roster loads it as module saty_day); the integration/saty_day_adapter.py mirror shows the adapter shape.
5. First evaluation: run one cycle on a fixture snapshot that satisfies the entry conditions in the Full algorithm section and confirm the expected intent (symbol, side, quantity rule and reason string naming this sleeve) is emitted before enabling live paper routing. If the Configuration section says parked is true, keep entries disabled exactly as in that row; exits are still managed where the algorithm says so.

## Verification
- Fixture with a frozen ladder, a first 1-minute touch of +38.2 percent within 30 minutes, the 1-hour Phase Oscillator not past -61.8, no opposing ribbon stack or chop, and QQQ confirming: exactly one buy_to_open SPY call intent for today's expiry, strike nearest spot, limit at the mid, quantity from the 4 percent premium budget (at least 1).
- Fixture with session P&L at or below -$10,000: no entry, while a held position still receives its kill, target and flat-by-close exits.
- Fixture with no same-day SPY expiry in the chain, or a quote spread above 5 percent of mid or older than 60 seconds: no entry.
- Fixture with a held Golden Gate position and a 10-minute close back through the 23.6 percent trigger: a full sell_to_close exit at the mid.
- No entry intent is emitted when a required input is missing or a filter, gate or age check has no evidence: the code fails closed.
- Quantity, price hint, stop price and reason on every emitted intent match the sizing and price rules in the Full algorithm section for the fixture inputs.
- The config row used by the recreation matches engines/config/sleeves.json for this sleeve id (module, cadence, session, slice, parked state and inputs).
