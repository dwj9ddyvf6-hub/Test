# grok_sr_spy: Recreation package

Provenance: X post, algo by Grok, installed by owner order 2026-10-28 (provenance statement in sleeves/grok_sr_spy.py in this repo; the post author's handle is omitted here under the repo PII rule). Spec: docs/GROK-SR-SPY-SPEC-2026-10-28.md in this repo.

## What this sleeve is
This sleeve trades SPY 1-minute horizontal support and resistance: it builds a static zone map from the prior 10 completed sessions, then takes fades on rejection and breakouts on decisive closes through a zone, with a reclaim invalidation in the first 3 bars, a zone-based stop, and a hard flat at 13:00 ET. Its edge is unproven by design: the live paper record is the test and no backtest is claimed. Sources: desk sleeves/grok_sr_spy.py and adapters3/grok_sr_spy.py.

## Full algorithm
Strategy (desk sleeves/grok_sr_spy.py, frozen spec GROK-SR-SPY-SPEC-2026-10-28), evaluated as GrokSrSpySleeve.signal() in this order. Constants: SYMBOL=SPY; PIVOT_K=5; LOOKBACK_SESSIONS=10; BIN_SIZE=0.10; NODE_PERCENTILE=0.90; CLUSTER_GAP=0.40; BAND_MIN_WIDTH=0.40; BAND_MAX_WIDTH=0.80; MAX_BANDS=6; FADE_VOL_MULT=1.5; BREAKOUT_VOL_MULT=1.2; BREAKOUT_MIN_CLOSE_BEYOND=0.10; BREAKOUT_MIN_BODY_FRAC=0.60; FADE_MIN_WICK_FRAC=0.50; TRAILING_VOL_BARS=20; STOP_BEYOND=0.10; TARGET_R_MULT=2.0; RECLAIM_WINDOW_BARS=3; entries 09:30 to before 11:30 ET; FLAT_TIME=13:00 ET; MAX_ENTRIES_PER_DAY=3; UNIT_RISK_PCT=0.0025; NOTIONAL_CAP_PCT=0.25.
1. State: ctx.extra grok_state resets per session (entries_today 0, plan None, dead_bands empty). Bars are SPY RTH 1-minute bars, zero-volume bars dropped.
2. Zone map (zones_for / build_zones), recomputed deterministically each cycle from the last 10 completed RTH sessions strictly before today only (today excluded, no repaint): fractal swing pivots with 5 bars each side inside one session, plus volume-at-price nodes in 0.10 bins at or above the 90th percentile of bin volume (bar volume assigned to the bin of its close); items are single-linkage clustered with max gap 0.40 into 0.40 to 0.80 wide bands (a wider cluster splits at its largest internal gap; a narrower band expands symmetrically to 0.40), scored by pivot touches plus node volume share, top 6 bands kept.
3. Exit bookkeeping first: if a plan existed, the position was seen open, and it is now flat, that band is marked dead for the session and the plan is cleared. With a plan and an open position, management applies (step 7). With a plan but no fill yet, wait (awaiting_fill). With a position but no plan, only the 13:00 ET hard flat applies.
4. Entry gates: today's bars must exist; the latest bar must be 09:30 to before 11:30 ET; fewer than 3 entries today; slice equity above 0; at least 21 intraday bars today (20 trailing volume bars plus the signal bar). Trailing average volume is the mean of the 20 bars before the signal bar.
5. Scan (fades first, across all live bands, then breakouts): a fade short fires when the prior close was below the band low, the bar high exceeds the band high, the upper wick is at least 50 percent of the bar range, the close is at or below the band high, and volume is above 1.5 times the trailing average. A fade long is mirrored at the band low. A breakout long fires when the prior close was at or below the band high, the close exceeds the band high by at least 0.10, the body is at least 60 percent of the range, and volume is above the prior bar's volume and at least 1.2 times the trailing average. A breakout short is mirrored at the band low. Dead bands are skipped.
6. Stop, target and sizing: for a fade, the stop is 0.10 beyond the far edge (band high + 0.10 for a short, band low - 0.10 for a long); for a breakout it is 0.10 beyond the near-side edge in the other direction (band low - 0.10 for a long, band high + 0.10 for a short). The target is the nearest band edge in the trade direction or 2R from entry, whichever is closer (2R when no band lies that way). Entry price is the signal bar close. Qty = floor(min(0.25 percent of equity / risk, 25 percent of equity / entry)); qty at or below 0 emits nothing. One open position at a time. The entry Intent carries stop and target exit rules, buy / buy_to_open for a long and sell / sell_to_open for a short, reason naming the setup, direction, band, entry, stop, target and qty, and the plan (band key, entry index) is stored.
7. Management: hard flat first (at or after 13:00 ET, exit in full and mark the band dead). Then the reclaim invalidation: on bar indices entry_index+1 through entry_index+3, a close back through the band (above the band high for a fade short, below the band low for a fade long, back inside the band for a breakout) exits in full at the next open by desk convention, and the band dies. After 3 bars the reclaim rule lapses and the stop, target and hard flat govern (stop and target are carried as exit rules on the entry intent).

## Data inputs
Inputs are bars, the literal bars:SPY:1Min warm product (added to producers_g4._BARS_TF_KEYS at install), positions, quotes and session_fills. Intraday bars are Alpaca SIP only (volume signals on 1-minute bars use SIP only, enforced by the desk warm and adapter path); the zone map needs the prior 10 completed RTH sessions of SPY 1-minute bars in the same bars payload. Positions and session state arrive via the snapshot context: the adapter round-trips ctx.extra grok_state beside the sleeve journal. Zones need no persisted state because they are recomputed from completed prior sessions each cycle.

## Install and run
1. Place the sleeve module and adapter at the paths listed under Files in this repo, and place every listed shared engine file at its listed repo path (an engines/ path maps back to the desk path after engines/; a desk3/ path is the same path on a fresh desk).
2. Register the config row for `grok_sr_spy` from engines/config/sleeves.json (repo copy of config/sleeves.json, also at config/sleeves.json) in the fresh desk's config/sleeves.json, exactly as given in the Configuration section, with no parameter overrides.
3. Wire the producers named in Data inputs so the inputs listed in the Configuration section arrive in the cycle snapshot (view) in the shapes the Full algorithm section describes.
4. Entry point: the v3 worker module adapters3/grok_sr_spy.py, whose evaluate(view, ctx) rebuilds the snapshot context and delegates to adapters/grok_sr_spy.py, which runs sleeves/grok_sr_spy.py signal().
5. First evaluation: run one cycle on a fixture snapshot that satisfies the entry conditions in the Full algorithm section and confirm the expected intent (symbol, side, quantity rule and reason string naming this sleeve) is emitted before enabling live paper routing. If the Configuration section says parked is true, keep entries disabled exactly as in that row; exits are still managed where the algorithm says so.

## Verification
- Tests in this repo: integration/test_grok_sr_spy.py must pass against the recreation.
- Fixture whose prior 10 sessions produce a known band and whose signal bar is a qualifying fade rejection on volume above 1.5 times the trailing 20-bar average, inside 09:30 to 11:30 ET: exactly one entry at the signal close, qty floor(min(0.0025 times equity / risk, 0.25 times equity / entry)), stop 0.10 beyond the far edge, target the nearer of the nearest band edge in the trade direction and 2R.
- Fixture with a held position and a close back through its band on bar entry_index+1 to entry_index+3: exactly one full exit (reclaim). Fixture at or after 13:00 ET with a held position: exactly one full exit (hard flat), and that band takes no further entry that session.
- No entry intent is emitted when a required input is missing or a filter, gate or age check has no evidence: the code fails closed.
- Quantity, price hint, stop price and reason on every emitted intent match the sizing and price rules in the Full algorithm section for the fixture inputs.
- The config row used by the recreation matches engines/config/sleeves.json for this sleeve id (module, cadence, session, slice, parked state and inputs).
