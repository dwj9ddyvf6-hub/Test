# t_cci_corr: Recreation package

Provenance: GrokBot note 2026-10-25 19:40 ET; reference implementation in the shared research backup, research/cci_correction/ (Source statement in sleeves/t_cci_corr.py in this repo). The existing repo card's Source row names the same GrokBot origin, from an X post's Lowest(CCI(27), 18) below -100 filter; the card's family path contains a blocked token and is omitted here under the repo PII rule.

## What this sleeve is
This is a long-only daily CCI correction pullback sleeve: it buys stocks in an established uptrend whose CCI(27) washed out below -100 in the last 18 sessions and is now turning back up, ranked deepest dip first, in five $200 slots on a $1,000 book. It is a forward test only: the source itself carries a data-mining caveat (a 16.5M-filter search) and no performance is claimed. Sources: desk sleeves/t_cci_corr.py and adapters3/t_cci_corr.py.

## Full algorithm
Strategy (desk sleeves/t_cci_corr.py, TcciCorrSleeve.signal()), in this order. Constants: CCI_LEN=27; LOOK=18; CCI_THR=-100.0; SMA_FAST=50; SMA_SLOW=200; ATR_LEN=14; STOP_ATR=2.0; TRAIL_ATR=3.0; TARGET_R=3.0; MAX_BARS=20; MIN_PRICE=5.0; MIN_DVOL=20,000,000; VIX_MAX=30.0; SLOTS=5; SLOT_USD=200.0; MAX_PRICE=200.0; MIN_BARS=218. CCI is on typical price, (TP - SMA(TP,27)) / (0.015 times mean absolute deviation over 27). ATR(14) uses Wilder smoothing replicating an exponential average with alpha 1/14. Only completed daily bars (bar date before the current ET session date) ever signal; the forming bar never signals.
1. Reconcile first: state records (ctx.extra cci_state: positions, pending, last_scan) are reconciled against the broker read-back (slice_state). A pending symbol that is now held becomes a position record with entry_ref = the signal close, stop = entry_ref - 2 times ATR at the signal, target = entry_ref + 3R where R = 2 times ATR (so target = entry_ref + 6 times ATR), hi_close = entry_ref, bars_held 0. A pending symbol from a prior session that never became a position is re-emitted once in this session at qty int(200 // ref_close).
2. Position management on each new completed bar per held symbol, in bar order: bars_held increments, hi_close tracks the highest close, and the trailing stop ratchets up only to max(stop, hi_close - 3 times ATR at that bar). Exits are then checked on closes, in this order: close at or below the (trailed) stop (stop_on_close); close at or above the target (target_on_close); close below SMA200 at that bar (close_below_sma200); bars_held at least 20 (time_exit_20_bars). An exit is a full-qty sell_to_close Intent at that bar's close, reason naming the exit kind, bars held and the stop, and the record is marked exiting so it fires once.
3. Entry scan, once per new completed bar (only when the newest completed SPY bar date differs from last_scan; last_scan then advances whether or not anything qualifies): the regime must pass first, failing closed on any absence: SPY close above SPY SMA200 at the signal date, and the last VIX close on or before it below 30. Open slots = 5 minus held minus pending; no slots means no scan entries.
4. Per-symbol signal on its last completed bar (at least 218 completed bars required): correction means min(CCI(27) over the last 18 bars) is below -100; trend means close above SMA200 and SMA50 above SMA200; liquidity means close at least 5.00 and 20-day average dollar volume at least 20,000,000; the trigger is CCI crossing back above -100 (current CCI above -100 with the prior bar's CCI at or below -100) OR the close above the prior bar's high while CCI is rising and still below 0. All of correction, trend, liquidity and a trigger must hold, and the signal date must equal the newest completed SPY bar date.
5. Ranking and sizing: candidates are ranked by current CCI ascending (deepest dip first). Symbols priced above 200.00 are skipped. Qty = int(200 // signal close), whole shares; qty below 1 is skipped. The entry Intent is a market buy_to_open at the signal close as price_hint, with stop = close - 2 times ATR, target = close + 3R, exit rules (stop, target, time_bars 20), reason naming the CCI value and the $200 slot, and the symbol is recorded pending. Desk translation (named in the source): entries are emitted in the session after the signal close with stop and target referenced to the signal close and its ATR, and management is evaluated on closes, not intrabar.

## Data inputs
Inputs are daily bars for the screening universe and SPY, the bars:VIX:1Day regime series (also carried as vix_rows in ctx.extra by the adapter), positions and session_fills. Each symbol needs at least 218 completed daily bars for a signal (SMA200 plus lookback slack), SPY needs at least 200 for the regime SMA, and VIX must have a close on or before the signal date. Freshness: only completed bars before the current session date are used, and the entry scan runs once per new completed SPY bar date. Missing regime evidence fails closed: no entries.

## Install and run
1. Place the sleeve module and adapter at the paths listed under Files in this repo, and place every listed shared engine file at its listed repo path (an engines/ path maps back to the desk path after engines/; a desk3/ path is the same path on a fresh desk).
2. Register the config row for `t_cci_corr` from engines/config/sleeves.json (repo copy of config/sleeves.json, also at config/sleeves.json) in the fresh desk's config/sleeves.json, exactly as given in the Configuration section, with no parameter overrides.
3. Wire the producers named in Data inputs so the inputs listed in the Configuration section arrive in the cycle snapshot (view) in the shapes the Full algorithm section describes.
4. Entry point: the v3 worker module adapters3/t_cci_corr.py, whose evaluate(view, ctx) rebuilds the snapshot context and delegates to adapters/t_cci_corr.py, which runs sleeves/t_cci_corr.py signal().
5. First evaluation: run one cycle on a fixture snapshot that satisfies the entry conditions in the Full algorithm section and confirm the expected intent (symbol, side, quantity rule and reason string naming this sleeve) is emitted before enabling live paper routing. If the Configuration section says parked is true, keep entries disabled exactly as in that row; exits are still managed where the algorithm says so.

## Verification
- Tests in this repo: tests/test_t_cci_corr_blockers.py must pass against the recreation.
- Fixture with SPY above its SMA200, VIX below 30, and a symbol with at least 218 completed bars whose last-18 CCI minimum is below -100, close above SMA200 with SMA50 above SMA200, dollar volume at least $20M, and CCI crossing back above -100 on the newest completed date: exactly one buy_to_open entry per open slot, ranked lowest CCI first, qty int(200 // close), stop close - 2 times ATR, target close + 6 times ATR.
- Fixture with VIX at or above 30, SPY at or below its SMA200, or no new completed SPY bar date: no entries. Fixture with a held position whose new completed close is at or below its trailed stop: exactly one full sell_to_close exit, reason containing stop_on_close.
- No entry intent is emitted when a required input is missing or a filter, gate or age check has no evidence: the code fails closed.
- Quantity, price hint, stop price and reason on every emitted intent match the sizing and price rules in the Full algorithm section for the fixture inputs.
- The config row used by the recreation matches engines/config/sleeves.json for this sleeve id (module, cadence, session, slice, parked state and inputs).
