# Psychology Engine: Study of Measurement
## What the market is feeling, how to measure it honestly, and what to build first

Commissioned by the owner, 2026-10-30. Author role: measurement cartographer.
Status: study only. No sleeve, sizing rule, or Tensor engine is changed by this document. The engine described is an information system. Its consumers are Midna's own book (the pre-open read and trade cards) and a terminal page.
House rules applied: InfinityBlade (evidence over claims, honest unverifiable labels, proof from the final state), no paid data or subscriptions, no fabricated or backfilled history, zero em dashes in this deliverable.

## How to read this study

Three kinds of statement appear, and they are not interchangeable.

1. Measured fact about the owner's estate: a count, a date inside a data file, or a ledger state, with the file that proves it.
2. Published evidence about a channel: a study result with a citation. Where the literature is mixed, both sides are given.
3. Judgment: a buildability grade or a ranking. Judgment is labelled as judgment and its reasoning is shown.

A core taxonomy runs through the whole study. Every channel measures one of three different things, and confusing them is the most common error in sentiment work:

- Stated belief: what people say they think (surveys, posts that declare a view).
- Revealed positioning: what people have actually done with money (flows, positioning, options prints, margin, shorts).
- Attention: what people are looking at, regardless of direction (search, news volume, social volume).

Attention is not sentiment. Positioning is not belief. A channel that is excellent at one is often silent on the others. The catalog in Part 2 tags every channel with this taxonomy.

A second rule: freshness means the newest date inside the data, never the file write time. This follows the terminal's own operator standard (Terminal Mastery playbook, 2026-10-29). A page rewritten today carrying October 14 data is October 14 data.

# PART 2: THE MEASUREMENT CATALOG
## Every serious way to measure, monitor and track the psychological pulse of the market

### Rules that govern every entry

1. History ruling (the owner, 2026-10-30): historical INPUT data is backfilled as deep as the real published record honestly goes, from the original source, with provenance per series (source, retrieval date, as published versus later revised values noted where a series is revised). Gauges are calibrated on that backfilled history: percentile bands, extreme zones and regime history come from the backfilled distribution, never from a cold start.
2. Reconstruction label: a historical gauge value computed from historical inputs with a formula written today is a RECONSTRUCTION wherever it appears. It is not a forecast the engine made at the time, and it must never be displayed as one.
3. The line that never moves: the forecast and outcome ledger is never backfilled. A prediction exists only if it was written before its horizon. Backfilled inputs calibrate gauges. They do not create a track record.
4. Verification label: an archive depth below is marked VERIFIED when this study checked it against the source or the source documentation during the 2026-10-30 research pass, and UNVERIFIED when it rests on publisher claims or secondary sources and no download was checked. An unverified depth is a lead, not a fact.
5. Cost classes: FREE (no key), KEYED FREE (free key or free registration, including keys the desk already holds: Alpaca, TastyTrade read only, Coinalyze), PAID (excluded from v1 by house rule; catalogued so the gap is named, never silently filled with a substitute that measures something else).
6. Type tags: SB stated belief, RP revealed positioning, AT attention. Many channels mix types; the dominant type is listed first.

Evidence, family (a). Brown and Cliff (2004, 2005) find survey sentiment (AAII and Investors Intelligence) correlates strongly with each other and with market wide sentiment proxies, but its power to predict near term market returns is weak once past returns are controlled: surveys largely echo what prices already did. Fisher and Statman (2000) find AAII bullishness negatively associated with subsequent S and P 500 returns at short horizons in their sample, and newsletter sentiment positively associated in places, a contradiction that itself warns against single survey trading rules. Lemmon and Portniaguina (2006) show Michigan style consumer confidence predicts the size and value premium pattern Baker and Wurgler predict, which is cross sectional evidence, stronger than market timing evidence. Zhou (2018, reviewing to December 2016) reports the Baker Wurgler index itself explains about 0.3 percent of monthly market return variance (R squared 0.003), its equally weighted version 0.006, and a partial least squares combination 0.019: real, small, and routinely oversold by vendors. Honest summary: surveys are calibration and regime instruments and extreme zone instruments. The published record does not support them as stand alone timing signals.

Traps, family (a). Self selection (AAII voters choose to vote; NAAIM members choose to report). Method breaks (AAII mail to online in 2000; Michigan mode and sampling changes across decades, with the quarterly to monthly break in 1978; Conference Board 2024 revision). Revision and vintage (Baker Wurgler recomputation; Michigan preliminary versus final). Small samples presented as national mood (AAII weekly hundreds, not thousands). Structural bias (NAAIM median far above neutral; AAII long run means near 38.8 percent bullish, 30.5 percent neutral, 30.6 percent bearish per AAII published averages, so a 50 percent bullish print is extreme even though it sounds moderate).

Evidence, family (b). COT: a large practitioner literature claims contrarian value at positioning extremes; the academic record is thinner and mixed, with Wang (2001) and subsequent work finding COT categories predict returns in some commodity markets and weakly in financial futures, and Zhou (2018) noting COT measures are themselves predicted by past returns, which caps their independent content. Treat COT as a crowding and forced flow map first and a return signal second. Fund flows: Warther (1995) and Edelen and Warner (2001) establish flows chase returns contemporaneously; flow based return prediction is weak at market level, stronger as a dumb money versus smart money cross sectional construction (Frazzini and Lamont 2008, where high flow stocks underperform). Margin debt: no peer reviewed timing law survives scaling choices; its value is as a leverage cycle gauge, and any claim of the form margin peaked before every top must be restated with the scaling and the lead distribution shown. Short interest: Asquith, Pathak and Ritter (2005) find heavily shorted stocks underperform, with the effect concentrated in small, low institutional ownership names: crowding cuts both ways, as squeeze episodes show. Insider buys: Lakonishok and Lee (2001) and Jeng, Metrick and Zeckhauser (2003) find insider purchase intensity predicts positive abnormal returns, strongest in small firms; sales are far weaker.

Traps, family (b). COT category breaks (Legacy commercial mixes hedgers and swap driven books; the 2006 to 2009 disaggregation backfill; contract rolls creating false positioning jumps in roll weeks). Flow revisions (ICI weekly estimates are revised in monthly finals: store both vintages). Margin scaling and the February 2010 collection break. Short interest versus short volume confusion (a stock versus a flow; the single most common error in retail sentiment content). Short interest publication lag (a mid month position read at month end is two weeks old). Insider filing amendments and derivative exercises misread as open market conviction: only codes P and S open market transactions count for the classic signal.

Evidence, family (c). Put call: Simon and Wiggins (2001) and Pan and Poteshman (2006) find put call ratios and buyer initiated option volume carry short horizon information about the underlying, with Pan and Poteshman the stronger result (option volume signed by buyer versus seller predicts next day and next week stock returns in 1990s data); later work finds the effect attenuated as option markets electronified, so any estate use must be graded on its own ledger, not imported on 2006 authority. VIX: Whaley (2000, 2009) frames VIX as a fear gauge whose level is dominated by hedging demand; the robust published fact is the negative contemporaneous relation with returns and a weak, horizon dependent predictive relation (high VIX associates with higher subsequent returns on average, with enormous dispersion). Term structure inversion is a stress marker with a cleaner event record than the level. GEX: the academic base is thin (dealer hedging feedback is modelled in Garleanu, Pedersen and Poteshman 2009 and supported in market maker inventory studies); practitioner claims of wall magnetism exceed the published evidence, which is why the estate grades walls as hypotheses (Part 1, Engine 8). SKEW: evidence for incremental return prediction is weak; its honest role is pricing of tail demand.

Traps, family (c). The 2003 VIX methodology break (pre and post 2003 VIX are different constructions; VXO bridges 1986 to 2021, not the present). Volume versus open interest ratios averaged together. Prints misread as directional when they are legs of spreads or stock hedges (the estate condition code and veto discipline exists for this reason). Modelled GEX presented as observed positioning. Survivorship in contract level history: expired contract archives from free sources are incomplete, so any reconstructed long GEX or skew history has a completeness hole that must be stated per period.

Evidence, family (d). Evidence, family (f). Bollen, Mao and Zeng (2011) reported Twitter mood (calm in particular) predicting Dow moves 3 to 4 days ahead with high claimed accuracy; the result has been repeatedly challenged on method and has not replicated robustly out of sample, and honest catalogues cite it with that warning attached. Antweiler and Frank (2004), on 1.5 million stock message board posts, find message volume predicts volatility and disagreement predicts volume, while the directional return effect of bullishness is statistically present and economically tiny: the durable social finding is attention and disagreement, not direction. Sprenger and coauthors (2014) find StockTwits style tweet sentiment associates with contemporaneous returns and volume at message level. Cookson and Niessner (2020) show social media disagreement is a measurable, persistent object distinct from analyst disagreement. WallStreetBets specific studies (2021 meme episode literature) document coordination and attention shocks with real price impact in targeted names and rapid decay: a per name event instrument, not a market mood gauge.

Traps, family (f). Bot contamination (estimates of automated share of finance posts vary wildly and spike exactly during manipulation events). Survivorship (deleted posts, banned subreddits and accounts, edited flairs: the archive a researcher downloads later is the cleaned version of a dirtier live feed). Self selection and demographic drift (Reddit and X finance populations changed radically in 2020 to 2021; a percentile band calibrated on 2018 posters misreads 2026 posters). Irony and slang inversion defeating dictionary tone. Platform API regime changes destroying collection continuity (X 2023, Reddit 2023): any social gauge needs a stated continuity plan and a break marker in the stored series, or its history is fiction.

### Cross family traps that apply to the whole catalog

1. Vintage versus revised values. FRED series, ICI flows, margin statistics and the Baker Wurgler index all revise. The store must keep first published and latest values separately, with retrieval dates, or every backtest is quietly using information nobody had.
2. As of clock discipline. A Tuesday COT position published Friday, a Michigan final print about a month already ended, a 13F holding from last quarter: each gauge enters the daily state with its true as of date, never its download date (the same clock lineage the forecast ledger already enforces, Part 1).
3. Percentile honesty. A percentile band is only as honest as its window. Bands computed on a backfilled reconstruction must say so; bands must never be computed on a window that includes the future relative to the reading being banded.
4. Independence accounting. Channels that share inputs (CNN composite versus its own put call and VIX inputs; Fear and Greed versus its Trends input) cast one vote, not two. The estate Second Opinion rule is the model.
5. The paid wall is a fact, not an embarrassment. Investors Intelligence depth, EPFR flows, RavenPack tone, full transcripts and X firehose are PAID. The engine names them as gaps and builds only on what it can store and prove.

# PART 3: THE BRAINSTORM
## Novel and underexploited ways to read what people are thinking, and to predict what they will think

Grading key. v1: buildable for the first working engine from free data obtainable today, live or as honest history. LATER: data obtainable free today but the build, validation or accrual time puts it after v1. SPECULATIVE: a real mechanism whose data or method is not yet obtainable to audit standard for free; named so it is not forgotten, and never smuggled into v1 under a friendlier label.

Every idea states the mechanism (why it could work, in causal language), the data it needs, whether that data is obtainable free today, and its grade.

### 3.1 Narrative velocity and saturation curves

Mechanism. A narrative (AI capex, rate cuts, a ticker story) has a life cycle: emergence, acceleration, saturation, decay. Position relevant information sits in the derivatives, not the level. Rising mention velocity with rising tone dispersion means a story is spreading and contested; peak velocity with collapsing dispersion means consensus, which historically arrives near position saturation; declining velocity after saturation means the marginal buyer has already heard the pitch. Data needed: daily mention counts and tone for a maintained topic set from GDELT GKG themes and DOC queries, plus Reddit and StockTwits mention counts per topic and ticker. Obtainable free today: yes for GDELT (history in GKG form from 2013 and 2015, live DOC rolling) and for social from collection start. Grade: LATER (the topic taxonomy and saturation curve fitting are a research build; a simple velocity and dispersion panel on GDELT topics is v1 shaped and is folded into shortlist item 10 as its first component).

### 3.2 Positioning convexity: how much forced flow a move would trigger

Mechanism. Crowds matter most when their exit is not optional. Given a positioning map (COT leveraged fund nets, margin debt scaled, funding rates, GEX sign and size, short interest and days to cover, put call and wall geometry), an engine can estimate the forced flow released by a 1, 2 and 5 percent adverse move: margin calls, leveraged fund VaR cuts, dealer gamma hedging in negative GEX, short covering, liquidation cascades in crypto. Two identical sentiment levels are different risks at different convexity. Nobody publishes this as a single daily number for free, and the estate unusually owns most of the inputs. Data needed: families (b), (c) and (h) above, all free or keyed free. Obtainable free today: yes, at weekly resolution for COT and monthly for margin, daily for the rest. Grade: LATER as a computed index with stated elasticities; v1 can ship the qualitative convexity panel (each input in its own extreme zone or not) that the index will later formalise. The elasticities must be published with the index or the number is theatre.

### 3.3 Attention to flow lead times

Mechanism. Attention precedes flow by a measurable lag that varies by channel: search attention leads retail flow by days (Da, Engelberg and Gao 2011 show attention driven price pressure over the following two weeks), social mention spikes lead options prints by hours to a day in targeted names, and fund flow follows news tone by weeks. If the lag structure is estimated per channel on the estate own data, the engine can say not only that attention spiked but when its flow echo is due, and can check whether the echo arrived (an attention spike with no flow echo is distribution into interest, a different fingerprint). Data needed: Google Trends and Wikipedia attention, StockTwits and Reddit mentions, estate OPRA tape, FINRA daily short volume, ICI flows. Obtainable free today: yes, with social and tape history accruing from collection start. Grade: LATER (needs months of joint accrual to estimate lags honestly; the collection that makes it possible is v1).

### 3.4 Dealer exposure as a crowd amplifier gauge

Mechanism. The crowd psychological state is only half the system; the other half is the transmission. The same panic produces a slide in positive GEX (dealers buy dips to hedge) and a cascade in negative GEX (dealers sell into declines). An amplifier gauge multiplies a mood reading by the current transmission state: mood percentile times GEX regime times VIX curve state, published as three separate factors and one labelled composite. The estate Morning Map already computes the transmission leg daily (Part 1). Data needed: Morgue Map GEX, Cboe VIX term structure, any family (a) or (d) mood series. Obtainable free today: yes. Grade: v1 as a displayed factor panel (no new forecast claim until the ledger grades the interaction, which is exactly Engine 8 and Engine 17 territory: join them, do not duplicate them).

### 3.5 Language shift metrics

Mechanism. Before positions change, grammar changes. Rising certainty language (will, definitely, guaranteed) in posts and calls marks conviction hardening; rising first person plural (we are all, everyone knows) marks herding identity; a shift from conditional to future declarative tense marks the moment a scenario becomes an assumption; rising second person challenge language marks polarisation. These shifts are computable with dictionaries and parsers on text the estate can collect free: FOMC statements, earnings call prepared remarks in filings, Reddit and StockTwits samples, GDELT quoted text. Data needed: text corpora above. Obtainable free today: partially (Fed text fully; social from collection start; call text partial on the free tier, which biases the sample toward firms that file remarks, a bias that must be printed with the gauge). Grade: SPECULATIVE for calls, LATER for Fed and social text. The Fed variant is the dark horse: 30 years of statements, a tiny corpus, exact timestamps, and a narrator whose language moves the whole curve.

### 3.6 Capitulation and euphoria fingerprints

Mechanism. Extremes are multivariate events that no single gauge timestamps well, but the joint fingerprint is distinctive. Capitulation fingerprint: breadth thrust setup (percent above 50 day at washout levels), VIX curve inverted for multiple sessions, equity put call extreme with index put call merely elevated (speculation washed out, hedging calm), DIX style short share falling as shorts cover, AAII bears above 55 percent or bulls below 20 percent, FEARS basket spiking, credit spreads wide but no longer widening for five sessions. Euphoria fingerprint: NAAIM above 100, margin debt scaled at highs with cash at lows, IPO and issuance climate hot (the live Baker Wurgler components), call buying extremes in single names, funding rates extreme in crypto, most crowded trade language saturating (idea 3.1). The engine contribution is a fingerprint matcher scored against the backfilled history: how many of the last 40 years of days matched at least k of n conditions, and what followed, printed as a RECONSTRUCTION table until live matches accrue. Data needed: families (a) to (e), all free. Obtainable free today: yes. Grade: v1 as a fingerprint panel with reconstruction history; any forward claim enters through the forecast ledger only.

### 3.7 Calendar, payday and tax season psychology

Mechanism. Retail flow has a pay cheque rhythm: 401(k) contributions land on payroll cycles, tax refunds concentrate in February to April, tax loss selling concentrates in December, and bonus season concentrates in January to March. These are not mood, they are scheduled liquidity wearing moods clothes, and an engine that does not model them will rediscover them as sentiment every year. A calendar layer (payday proximity for the two dominant US pay cycles, refund season progress from IRS published weekly filing statistics, which are free, quarter end window dressing windows, options expiry weeks) lets the mood gauges be read net of the calendar. Data needed: IRS weekly filing statistics (free), a maintained calendar, ICI flows for validation. Obtainable free today: yes. Grade: v1 as an annotation layer (cheap, prevents a known class of false discoveries); LATER as a quantitative adjustment.

### 3.8 Weather and daylight effects

Mechanism. The published claim: sunshine correlates with same day returns across exchanges (Saunders 1993; Hirshleifer and Shumway 2003, who find sunshine significantly correlated with daily index returns in 26 cities, an effect they attribute to mood), and seasonal affective disorder timing correlates with return seasonality (Kamstra, Kramer and Levi 2003, contested in follow up work). Effect sizes are tiny, the literature is replication scarred, and the honest grade follows from that. Data needed: weather for New York and Chicago (NOAA, free, deep history), daylight duration (computable). Obtainable free today: yes, with decades of history. Grade: SPECULATIVE as a return predictor; v1 acceptable only as a clearly labelled curiosity tile if the build is near free, and it must never share a composite with serious gauges. Included because the commission asked, and graded honestly because InfinityBlade requires it.

### 3.9 Sports and news cycle displacement of attention

Mechanism. Attention is a fixed budget. Major sporting events, elections, wars and celebrity news displace market attention measurably (search and social volume for tickers fall, retail order flow thins), and the displacement itself predicts lower volatility and delayed reaction to earnings and news released into the shadow (the inattention literature: DellaVigna and Pollet 2009 show Friday announcements get less immediate response; Edmans, Garcia and Norli 2007 show national team soccer losses predict next day negative market returns in the losing country, a mood effect with a clean identification). An attention displacement gauge (total finance attention today versus its seasonal norm, plus a calendar of known displacement events) tells the engine when silence means apathy and when it means distraction. Data needed: Google Trends and social totals, a maintained event calendar. Obtainable free today: yes. Grade: LATER (the gauge is easy, the event calendar maintenance is the cost, and the payoff is interpretive rather than directional).

### 3.10 Survey dispersion and disagreement as the gauge

Mechanism. Every survey in family (a) publishes a mean and hides the story in the spread. NAAIM publishes respondent dispersion; AAII neutral share is a crude confusion proxy; Michigan publishes cross sectional disagreement in research files. Rising dispersion at a constant mean is a regime change precursor in the disagreement literature (it predicts volume and volatility more reliably than returns, per Antweiler and Frank 2004 and Cookson and Niessner 2020). The engine should store dispersion wherever a source publishes it and headline disagreement next to direction on every survey tile. Data needed: NAAIM published quartiles and extremes (free), AAII neutral share (free), StockTwits bullish bearish split as a live dispersion proxy. Obtainable free today: yes for the named sources. Grade: v1 (a storage and display decision more than a research project).

### 3.11 The quiet gauge: abstention and absence as signal

Mechanism. The estate already owns this idea (Engine 30, Part 1): forecasters, posters and traders going quiet when inputs degrade is itself information. Generalised: social posting volume from normally active accounts falling, options prints thinning in a name before news, insiders filing nothing in a window where the firm historically files. Absence gauges need a baseline per source, which the daily state store provides. Data needed: the daily state store with per source expected versus observed counts. Obtainable free today: yes, once the store exists. Grade: LATER, and it must reuse Engine 30 machinery rather than fork it.

### 3.12 Crypto to equity mood transfer at the open

Mechanism. Crypto trades the hours equities cannot. Weekend funding extremes, weekend Fear and Greed swings and Sunday evening BTC moves are the only live crowd readings available before the Monday pre open read, and Midna book trade cards are written Monday 08:00 like any other day. A transfer gauge simply archives the weekend crypto state next to the Monday equity state and grades the association on the estate ledger instead of assuming it. Data needed: family (h), free. Obtainable free today: yes, history from 2018 for Fear and Greed and from about 2019 for liquid funding. Grade: v1 as a stored panel; its forecast use waits for grades like everything else.

### 3.13 Revision tone: what forecasters change, not what they say

Mechanism. Levels of analyst and forecaster opinion are stale compromises; revisions are decisions. The free world lacks I/B/E/S, but three revision objects are free: Michigan preliminary to final revisions, ICI weekly estimate to monthly final revisions, and Metaculus community prediction updates on standing questions (a revision time series with timestamps). A revision breadth gauge (net direction of revisions across all watched series this week) is a meta sentiment measure no retail dashboard publishes. Data needed: the named series with vintages stored (cross family trap 1 made useful). Obtainable free today: yes. Grade: LATER (it is a byproduct of correct vintage storage; build the storage in v1, read the gauge in v2).

### 3.14 Crowding overlap from 13F plus short interest plus options

Mechanism. A name held by many 13F filers, heavily shorted, and carrying rich call premium is a crowded stage set: any spark produces a two sided stampede. The estate holds all three legs at partial coverage (Part 1) and Engine 26 already formalises a neighbouring triple condition for accumulation. A crowding score per name, refreshed on the slowest leg (quarterly 13F), is a risk map for Midna book position sizing rather than a timing tool. Data needed: EDGAR 13F, FINRA short interest, Alpaca options data. Obtainable free today: yes. Grade: LATER (coverage today is 50 names at partial legs; widen after the daily stores in the shortlist exist).

## The ranked shortlist: the ten highest value measures for a first working engine

Ranking rule, stated before the ranking so it can be argued with: deep honest history weighs heaviest, because the engine first job is calibration (percentile bands, extreme zones, regime history from the backfilled distribution). After history: cost (free only), type diversity (the ten must span stated belief, revealed positioning and attention, or the engine inherits one family blind spots), estate leverage (measures that cure an owned but empty store or unblock an existing engine rank above equally good measures that start a new silo), and falsifiability on the existing forecast ledger.

1. The daily price derived mood spine, rebuilt as a real daily state store. Breadth (percent above 50 and 200 day), advance decline, new highs minus new lows, percent at 52 week highs, thrust flags. Why first: it cures the estate most embarrassing proven defect (the daily state record holding zero sessions, Part 1), it is computable as a labelled RECONSTRUCTION across the full price panel, every other gauge will be timestamped against it, and State Analogue, Fear Day and Family Fracture all consume it. History: panel depth, honestly labelled. Type: RP.
2. The VIX complex. VIX closes (1990, with the 2003 methodology break and the VXO bridge 1986 to 2021 documented in the store), VIX9D and VIX3M term structure, VIX futures curve from 2004, VVIX where obtainable. Why second: 36 years of the single most watched fear price, free from Cboe, daily, and the term structure inversion clock is the cleanest stress regime marker in the catalog. Type: RP priced.
3. The survey triad percentile system. AAII weekly (1987), NAAIM weekly (2006), Investors Intelligence noted as the paid gap (1963, not stored). Stored with dispersion and neutral share per idea 3.10, banded on their own full histories. Why third: 39 years of stated belief, free, weekly, and the retail versus manager contrast (AAII versus NAAIM) is the oldest useful tension in sentiment work. Type: SB, with NAAIM shading to RP.
4. CFTC COT for index futures and VIX futures (TFF form, Legacy as the long bridge). Weekly leveraged fund, asset manager and dealer nets from 1986 in Legacy form and from the 2006 to 2009 TFF era in modern form, with roll weeks and category breaks marked in the store. Why fourth: the deepest true positioning archive available free anywhere, and the raw material of positioning convexity (idea 3.2). Type: RP.
5. Put call and skew from Cboe published ratios, joined to the estate OPRA tape. Total, equity and index volume ratios stored separately, never averaged; Cboe SKEW alongside. Why fifth: daily, free, decades in vendor cited depth (archive start to be verified at download and the store must record what the download actually contained), and it completes the options psychology picture the Morgue Tape began for three tickers. Type: RP.
6. The FINRA pair, cured. Daily short sale volume per name (the never started Short Volume Ledger) plus twice monthly short interest per name (widening Squeeze Watch beyond 20 of 50 names), with the DIX index reading continuing from the Morgue Map and the per name dark values finally stored instead of discarded. Why sixth: three owned but empty stores cured by one collection build, unblocking Engines 9 and 26, on free data with daily files reaching back years (start to be proven at the file index before any depth claim is printed). Type: RP.
7. The slow regime layer. Michigan consumer sentiment (1952, quarterly then monthly, vintage aware) plus the Baker Wurgler index (1965, with download date and hash stored per pull). Why seventh: 61 to 74 years of slow mood and speculation climate that no daily gauge can substitute for; this layer sets the regime bands inside which every faster gauge percentile is interpreted. Type: SB plus behavioural composite.
8. Credit and safe haven confirmation panel. High yield OAS (1996 on FRED), investment grade OAS, Baa Aaa spread, gold versus equity and Treasury versus equity ratios with instrument inception dates printed. Why eighth: the cheapest honest veto in finance. A fear reading that credit denies and a fear reading that credit confirms are different situations, and the engine should never present the first as the second. Type: RP priced.
9. Leverage gauges. FINRA margin statistics scaled by free credit and by market value (1997, monthly, with the 2010 collection break marked), joined to crypto funding rates as the fast leverage gauge (Coinalyze with the desk key, exchange APIs free, liquid history from about 2019). Why ninth: leverage is the mechanism that converts mood into forced flow, the two series cover its slow and fast forms, and both are free. Type: RP.
10. The attention layer. A FEARS style Google search basket (2004, built with pinned pull parameters so rescaling is documented per pull), GDELT finance tone and volume (GKG form from 2013 and 2015, events from 1979 for context counts only), and Wikipedia pageviews for a maintained crash and recession concept set (2015 API era). Why tenth and not higher: attention is the most publishable and the least calibration grade family (Trends rescaling, GDELT universe drift), so it enters v1 stored and displayed, with its forecast claims last in the queue for ledger grading. It is also the collection that makes ideas 3.1 and 3.3 possible later. Type: AT.

What the shortlist deliberately excludes, and why. Social pulse (family f) is collected from day one where free (StockTwits stream, Reddit API) because uncollected history is unrecoverable, but no social gauge is in the v1 ten: its archive depth is collection start, its contamination is the worst in the catalog, and ranking it above 40 to 70 year series would violate the stated ranking rule. Belief markets (family g) are likewise stored daily from day one (Kalshi keyed free, Polymarket free) for the PredictIt reason (archives die with platforms), and enter the ranked gauges once a year of estate stored history exists. The BofA Fund Manager Survey, Investors Intelligence depth, EPFR flows and RavenPack tone stay named PAID gaps. CNN Fear and Greed stays a context tile, never a gauge of record.

Closing statement of the study. The estate already owns a positioning core, a falsifiable ledger and a grading culture that most sentiment products never build. What it lacks is breadth of mood measurement and depth of honest history. The catalog above shows both are obtainable free: surveys to 1952 and 1965, positioning to 1986, fear prices to 1990, leverage to 1997, attention to 2004. Backfill those inputs with provenance, calibrate the gauges on them, label every reconstruction, write the daily state so it actually accrues, and let the ledger, never the backfill, decide what the Psychology Engine has ever predicted.
