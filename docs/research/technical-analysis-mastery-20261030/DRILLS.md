How to run a drill: open the chart, cover nothing, and work the Volume II order. First the 10 candle ruler and the candle vocabulary (Section E1), then pivots and fitted lines, then the Bulkowski and ChartSchool identification rows (Section E2), then stage, phase, and boundary (Section E3), then write ENTRY, SL, TARGET, invalidation, and the expected volume script (Section E4). Write the answer before reading the key. Grade with the Section E4 rubric: direction, level quality, invalidation discipline, volume script, timing, target honesty, drawing honesty.
