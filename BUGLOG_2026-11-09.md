# BUGLOG 2026-11-09 — coffin_rebuilt landing lane (Midna, Monday 2026-11-09)

## COFFIN-RANKS-UNPACK-20261109 — ranks_at crashed on every flat month-end evaluation
- Severity: high (the new sleeve would have raised ValueError on the
  first month-end it evaluated while flat, i.e. its first real signal
  date, 2026-11-30; caught pre-landing by its own blocker tests,
  never reached the live roster).
- Files: sleeves/coffin_rebuilt.py (ranks_at).
- What broke: the rank loop iterated the stats dict itself
  (`for s, v in vals:`) instead of its items, so unpacking a symbol
  string raised "ValueError: too many values to unpack" whenever a
  month-end entry evaluation ran.
- Fix: iterate `vals.items()`.
- Regression blocker: tests/test_coffin_rebuilt_20261109_blockers.py
  (TestEntryGate drives two month-ends through the ranker; the whole
  file errored 7 tests on this defect before the fix, passes after).

## COFFIN-SESSION-DATE-20261109 — bar session dates derived in ET shifted UTC-midnight stamps a day back
- Severity: medium (latent: with a daily-bar stamp at UTC midnight,
  every session date would shift one day earlier, moving month
  boundaries by one session and corrupting the month-end set; the
  desk's actual stamps are midnight-ET (04:00/05:00Z) or the 09:30 ET
  open, whose ET and UTC dates agree, so the live path was not hit;
  caught pre-landing by the live-chain smoke test, whose fixture
  stamps dates at UTC midnight).
- Files: sleeves/coffin_rebuilt.py (_session_date).
- What broke: session dates were derived by converting the bar
  timestamp to America/New_York; a 2026-11-06T00:00Z stamp became
  2026-11-05. The desk's other daily sleeves derive the session date
  as the bar timestamp's UTC date (the momentum_rotation +4h form),
  which is correct for every stamp form the desk serves.
- Fix: _session_date now returns the timestamp's UTC date, with the
  reasoning recorded in its docstring.
- Regression blocker: tests/test_coffin_rebuilt_20261109_blockers.py
  (TestLiveChainSmoke asserts last_processed 2026-11-06 for a
  2026-11-09 evaluation over bars ending 2026-11-06; it read
  2026-11-05 before the fix).
