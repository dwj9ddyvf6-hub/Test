## Positive evidence
- Lopez-Lira & Tang, JFE 2026 / arXiv 2304.07619v5 https://arxiv.org/html/2304.07619v5 : GPT-4 headline scores, sample Oct 2021-May 2024 POST Sept-2021 cutoff, 159,137 obs/4,123 firms; overnight long-short pre-cost 34bp/day, hit 58%, Sharpe 2.97; intraday 50bp/day Sharpe 2.63; short side Sharpe 2.01 vs long 0.78; stronger small stocks + negative news. Costs/latency caveats (summary https://jun.jahee.shop/article/643).
- Chen, Kelly & Xiu SSRN 4416687 https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4416687 : LLM EMBEDDINGS of news (not verbal verdicts) + supervised return head; incremental beyond reversal/characteristics; meaningful Sharpe after costs; 16 markets/13 languages; "not driven by look-ahead bias" (authors' claim).
- Koijen & Levy (Chicago Booth) 2026-06-30, OPENED PDF https://finm-32800.github.io/_downloads/f8b4cc1124ff7587a678fb69d61bb749/KoijenLevy2026OptimizedAgenticAISystems.pdf : REAL-TIME OOS benchmark, prompts/params frozen BEFORE Q4-2025 earnings; frontier LLMs explain up to 15% of announcement-return variation vs ~5% earnings-surprise lit / 8% Hand et al; GEPA + ACE prompt/context optimization -> ~20% OOS; no single model dominates (model x industry heterogeneity); GEPA learned "good is not news — priced relative to expectations."
- Kim, Muhn & Nikolaev: GPT-4 ~60% vs analysts ~53-57% earnings-direction — PAPER WITHDRAWN 2025-02-20 (co-author found inconsistencies replicating). Chicago Booth page still shows preliminary claims https://www.chicagobooth.edu/research/fama-miller/finance-research/funding/a-demand-system-approach-for-fixed-income/financial-statement-analysis-with-large-language-models — MUST flag withdrawn.
- Didisheim/Kelly et al "pure news" + Chen-Didisheim-Somoza inner-probabilities (slides https://www.antoinedidisheim.com/slides/llms-in-finance-2 ): token-probability confidence filtering raises Sharpe (baseline LLT ~39.6% ann, Sharpe 4.24 in their table); residualizing news on characteristics ("pure news") >2x power, predicts to 18mo. Slides = secondary presentation of working papers.
- Lehner & Lopez-Lira 2026 "ChatGPT as a Time Capsule" https://pdfs.assets.alphaxiv.org/2604.21433v1.pdf : frozen checkpoints' outlook scores predict 1-mo cross-sectional returns t=4.25 after size/quality/value/momentum controls.

## Negative / limits evidence
- FINSABER, KDD 2026, OPENED https://arxiv.org/html/2505.07078v5 : 20 yrs, 100+ symbols; prior LLM advantages deteriorate/vanish; too conservative in bulls, too aggressive in bears.
- Lopez-Lira, Tang & Zhu "Memorization Problem" arXiv 2504.14765 https://arxiv.org/html/2504.14765v2 : GPT-4o perfectly recalls some pre-cutoff S&P levels/prices; cutoff instructions FAIL; masking FAILS (reconstructs entities); post-cutoff no recall; extends to embeddings; in-sample forecast skill non-identified.
- Glasserman & Lin, J Financial Data Science 2024 (doi 10.3905/jfds.2023.1.143): look-ahead + DISTRACTION effect; anonymized headlines OUTPERFORMED in-sample.
- Alpha Arena (Nof1), recomputed https://tradingagentstack.com/alpha-arena-results/ : S1 Oct-Nov 2025, $10k real, crypto perps: Qwen3 Max +20.3%, DeepSeek +6.9%, other 4 -29.9% to -63.4%. S1.5: 5/32 accounts up.
- Multi-agent debate, Stanford, OPENED https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.29701 : reasoning quality vs Sharpe r=0.07 n.s.; SYCOPHANTIC CONVERGENCE failure mode; JSD disagreement-preserving intervention +0.14 Sharpe (p=.028), +0.25 Sortino (p=.026). Historical simulation, preprint — flag.

## Mechanism / countermeasures
- Verbalized Sampling, Zhang et al arXiv 2510.01171 https://arxiv.org/html/2510.01171v4 : RLHF preference data TYPICALITY BIAS -> mode collapse; VS prompting (verbalize distribution) +1.6-2.1x diversity, recovers 66.8% of base-model diversity, no training.
- FunSearch, Nature 625:468-475, doi 10.1038/s41586-023-06924-6: LLM + evaluator + islands evolution; cap set 512 vs 496; evaluator is the system. AlphaEvolve (2025): Gemini ensemble, MAP-Elites+islands, 4x4 matmul 48 vs 49, 0.7% Google fleet compute (firm-reported). Summary https://safeguard.sh/resources/blog/evolutionary-coding-agents-from-funsearch-to-alphaevolve-how-they-work
- AlphaAgent, KDD 2025 https://arxiv.org/html/2502.16789v2/ : Idea->Factor->Eval agents; AST originality vs existing alphas + hypothesis alignment + complexity control vs decay; CSI500 11.0% excess IR 1.49, SP500 8.74% IR 1.05 (authors' backtests — flag leakage/multiple-testing risk).
- Halawi et al NeurIPS 2024 arXiv 2402.18563: retrieval+fine-tuned GPT-4 Brier 0.179 vs crowd 0.149; combined model+crowd 0.146 BEATS either; no retrieval/fine-tune ~0.21-0.25.
- Schoenegger et al Science Advances 2024 https://arxiv.org/html/2402.19379v5/ : 12-LLM ensemble Brier ~0.20 vs human crowd 0.19 (31 Qs, 925 humans) n.s. different.
- Bridgewater AIA Forecaster, arXiv 2511.07678 https://arxiv.org/html/2511.07678v1 (FIRM technical report — flag): agentic search + supervisor reconciling ~10 independent forecasts + Platt scaling vs RLHF hedging to 50%; = superforecasters on ForecastBench (0.0753 vs 0.0740); UNDERPERFORMS market consensus on liquid markets (0.1258 vs 0.1106) BUT blend 67% market/33% AI = 0.106 beats both -> additive divergent info. No search: 0.3609 (worse than coin flip). Also Bridgewater: fine-tuning on expert judgments beat prompting; ~100M-doc time-tagged Vault (via https://automatedalpha.substack.com/p/how-bridgewater-builds-ai-agents — secondary summary of firm publications).
- Bailey & Lopez de Prado DSR (2014): deflate Sharpe by #trials; False Strategy Theorem.
- "Beyond Accuracy" preprint: strongest LLM ~ market accuracy on 822 resolved Polymarket Qs but significantly higher realized returns; edge = losing less when wrong (confidence/divergence sizing).
