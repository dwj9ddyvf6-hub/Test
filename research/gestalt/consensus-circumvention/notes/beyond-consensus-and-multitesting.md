# Added sources (read 2026-11-14 EST, index)

## Multiple testing / generate-and-test arithmetic
- Harvey, Liu & Zhu, RFS 2016: 316 published factors; new factor needs t>3.0; Bonferroni hurdle 1.96->3.78 by 2012, ~4.00 by 2032; FWER@5% t≈3.9, FDR@1% t≈3.0. "Most claimed research findings in financial economics are likely false." Sources: https://foxholm.com/q/research/harvey-liu-zhu-cross-section/ ; audit https://github.com/click6067-ship-it/quant-pramana/blob/HEAD/pramana_research/11_dr3_claude_audit.md
- Hou, Xue & Zhang, RFS 2020 "Replicating Anomalies": 447 anomalies, 286 (64%) insignificant at 5%, 380 (85%) at t>=3; BUT contested: Jensen-Kelly-Pedersen JF 2023 ~84.9% replication (empirical Bayes, 153 factors/93 countries); Chen-Zimmermann ~98% of clearly significant. Present as disputed range. https://antoniomarrazzo.substack.com/p/the-replication-crisis-paper-that ; https://alpha-suite.org/blog/survivorship-bias-investing
- False Strategy Theorem (Bailey, Borwein, Lopez de Prado & Zhu, Notices AMS 2014): E[max SR] ≈ sqrt(V)·[(1-γ)Z⁻¹(1-1/N)+γ Z⁻¹(1-1/(Ne))]. Worked values (unit variance): N=10 -> ~1.57, N=100 -> ~2.53, N=1000 -> 3.26, N=1e6 -> ~4.87. MinBTL: 5y daily data supports ~45 independent trials before expected best null Sharpe ≈1.0; 10y ~780. Primary PDF: https://www.ams.org/notices/201405/rnoti-p458.pdf ; exposition https://www.interactiveml.org/blog/deflated-sharpe-ratio ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deflated_Sharpe_ratio
- DSR worked example: same strategy ann. SR 2.5, T=1250: rejected at realized trial count, passes 95% only if found after N<=46 trials (DSR=0.9505). DSR needs N, V[SR], T, skew, kurtosis — a bare Sharpe is incomputable as evidence.
- PBO (Bailey et al, J Computational Finance 2017): PBO = prob IS-best config ranks below OOS median, via CSCV (S=16 blocks -> C(16,8)=12,870 splits). PBO>=0.5 = selection is coin flip; practitioners want <0.2. Needs ALL trials' returns incl. losers ("keep the graveyard"). https://www.crucible-research.com/probability-of-backtest-overfitting
- Peeking/sequential: 5 unplanned peeks at p<.05 -> true FPR ~14%; 20 looks ~25%+; fixes: O'Brien-Fleming alpha-spending (5 looks: z 4.56,3.23,2.63,2.28,2.04, max sample +2.8%), Pocock, always-valid p-values/mSPRT (Johari et al). https://www.metricuno.com/sequential-testing ; https://wepushers.com/frameworks/sequential-and-bayesian-testing/

## Beyond-consensus research
- Novelty Search: Lehman & Stanley, Evolutionary Computation 2011 "Abandoning Objectives" doi 10.1162/EVCO_a_00025 — reward behavioral novelty vs archive, solves deceptive maze/biped where objective search fails. NS with Local Competition (2011): novelty + local quality. Stanley & Lehman 2015 "Why Greatness Cannot Be Planned" — objectives deceptive, stepping stones don't resemble goal.
- MAP-Elites: Mouret & Clune 2015 arXiv:1504.04909 — archive of best-per-behavioral-niche (illumination, not optimization). Cully et al Nature 2015 "Robots that can adapt like animals." POET (Wang et al 2019) coevolves environments+agents.
- ELM: Lehman et al 2022 arXiv:2206.08896 — LLM fine-tuned on code diffs as INTELLIGENT MUTATION operator inside MAP-Elites; hundreds of thousands of functional Sodarace programs in a domain absent from pretraining; bootstraps new model. LMX (Meyerson et al, ACM TELO 2024): LLM prompting as generic crossover. QDAIF (Bradley et al, ICLR 2024): QD with AI feedback for diversity/quality judgments.
- FunSearch Nature 2023 + AlphaEvolve 2025 (from prior notes): both use island populations; AlphaEvolve explicitly MAP-Elites+islands archive. Diversity maintenance credited for escaping local optima.
- Debate: Du et al ICML 2024 arXiv:2305.14325 — 3 agents x 2 rounds GPT-3.5: arithmetic 67.0->81.8%, GSM8K 77->85.0%, MMLU 63.9->71.1%, biographies 66->73.8%; BUT majority-vote baseline got GSM8K 81.0 of the 85.0 — most gain is ensembling; no compute-matched self-consistency control.
- Liang et al EMNLP 2024 arXiv:2305.19118 (MAD2): debate needs maintained disagreement ("affirmative debate"); LLM judge may be unfair across models.
- Zhang et al 2025 "Stop Overvaluing Multi-Agent Debate" arXiv:2502.08788: 5 MAD methods x 9 benchmarks x 4 models; NO method >20% win rate vs plain CoT; CoT won >80% of 36 configs; conclusion: embrace MODEL HETEROGENEITY.
- Choi, Zhu & Li 2025 "Debate or Vote" arXiv:2508.17536 (NeurIPS 2025 spotlight): Theorem — debate induces MARTINGALE over beliefs in correct answer (expected correctness unchanged by rounds); majority voting accounts for most MAD gains; voting on par/better across 7 benchmarks; more rounds can hurt (Qwen2.5-7B: single .7205, vote .7691, debate T=2 .7377, T=3 .7112). Targeted bias-toward-correction interventions CAN help.
- Huang et al (Stanford) trading debate (prior notes, arXiv 2609.29701): sycophantic convergence; JSD disagreement-preservation +0.14 Sharpe.
- Decoding: Holtzman et al 2020 nucleus sampling / "Curious Case of Neural Text Degeneration" — likelihood-max decoding (greedy/beam) degenerates to bland/repetitive; sampling buys diversity at quality cost. Diverse beam search (Vijayakumar et al 2016 / Li et al 2016): penalized groups, still likelihood-anchored. Verbalized Sampling (prior notes): typicality bias; VS recovers 66.8% of base diversity, 1.6-2.1x. Creativity/novelty measurement literature (secondary summary): RLHF drops Creativity Index ~30.1%; inference tricks give limited gains (Padmakumar & He 2024 line).
- RLVR: Tulu 3 (AI2, Nov 2024, arXiv:2411.15124) coined RLVR; DeepSeek-R1 (Jan 2025, arXiv:2501.12948; Nature 2025): R1-Zero = GRPO on base model, rule-based accuracy+format rewards ONLY, no SFT, no neural reward model (hacking risk) -> emergent long reasoning/self-verification. LIMIT: Yue et al (NeurIPS 2025) pass@k analysis — RLVR beats base at k=1 but base solves MORE at large k: RLVR sharpens/reweights existing distribution, does not expand its support; entropy/diversity narrows. Verifiers gameable (2026 reports of models patching timers/tests) — reward design is the new overfitting surface.
- Finance fine-tuning: BloombergGPT (Wu et al 2023): 50B params, 363B proprietary financial tokens + 345B general, ~$2.7M/1.3M GPU-hrs (secondary figures); SOTA on financial NLP at release, later routinely beaten by frontier general models + RAG; per secondary reporting Bloomberg said (Apr 2026) it was never used in products, stack now = commercial foundation + smaller custom models — FLAG as secondary/unverified. FinGPT (Yang et al 2023): LoRA on open bases, ~$300/run, competitive sentiment — cheap adaptation works for narrow classification, not broad reasoning. RLMF (Reinforcement Learning from Market Feedback) named in secondary survey as 2024-26 direction: tune on realized market outcomes rather than text.
- Dispersion measurements (DIRECT):
  - Persona/SPF study, arXiv:2511.02458 (Nov 2025): GPT-4o replicating ECB Survey of Professional Forecasters, 50 quarters 2013-2025, 2,368 PersonaHub personas, T=1: accuracy ~ human panel (small diffs); persona ablation = NO accuracy gain; AI panel IQR/SD ~ TWO ORDERS OF MAGNITUDE lower than human panel — "diverse prompts produce remarkably homogeneous forecasts compared to human panels."
  - "Same Text, Different Numbers" arXiv:2609.31013: 7 providers score 1,946 S&P500 earnings calls (2024) on 13 constructs: cross-model rank corr avg 0.52; common component only 34% of variation; within-call cross-model SD = 0.75x overall SD; cross-model disagreement does NOT predict analyst/market disagreement -> disagreement is largely model-specific noise, must be validated per-use.
  - Machine Forecast Disagreement (NBER w31583): dispersion across ML model specs as heterogeneous-beliefs proxy; aligns with analyst disagreement but predicts returns MORE strongly; short-high/long-low disagreement decile spread earns 14%/yr value-weighted (attributed to mispricing + short-sale constraints). Traditional ML, not LLM — shows dispersion itself can be a priced signal.
  - St Louis Fed Review 2024 (PaLM inflation forecasts vs SPF): LLM lower MSE most years/horizons in-sample 2019-23; repeated-sampling IQR grows with horizon; temperature controls dispersion mechanically.
