## 1. The vital sign, defined

One number, published at every B5 beside the P and L: the
organism's aggregate Brier score across every mechanism that
emits a probability and has resolved outcomes, decomposed by
organ, with the weighting stated on the face of the report.

Weighting rule (v1, stated so it can be falsified): the aggregate
is the pooled, count-weighted mean of per-call Brier scores
across all included organs. Every resolved probability-bearing
call counts once. No organ is weighted by importance, capital, or
seniority; importance weighting is how organisms lie to
themselves. Organs that emit accuracy only (no probabilities)
are NEVER blended into the Brier; they ride in a separate named
annex. Organs that emit no probabilities at all are named as the
unmeasured surface, with their size, every time the sign prints.

## 3. Publication and divergence rules

The sign prints at B5 beside the P and L, with its n and its
organ decomposition, every time. Divergence is the diagnosis
(Ascension Level 7): profitable but mispredicting is borrowed
time; calibrated but unprofitable is an expression problem. The
two states carry opposite cures and the report must say which
state the organism is in, in those words, or say the sample is
too small to tell (today's state, see VITAL-SIGN-1).

Climatology discipline: every organ prints its own climatology
Brier beside its score wherever a base rate exists. An aggregate
without a same-set climatology is a number without a control.
