# S8(c) DESIGN: LEVEL 10, THE ORPHAN HUNT (our constraints are the edge)

Status: DESIGN, Sat 2026-11-14 (Sunday program). The first scan
is runs/ORPHAN-SCAN-1.md, filed beside this design. Registration
rule: the scan registers candidates as a SCAN. No candidate is
promoted by the scan; registration into the candidate registry is
a later conveyor step with its own falsifier per candidate.

## 1. The hunt, defined

Scan published or observable effects whose stated or estimated
capacity sits below institutional minimums, register them as a
family, and forward-grade the family like everything else: on
resolved results versus SPY and QQQ at B5, net of costs, on fills
not mids. The estate's size (a roughly 100K dollar paper book) is
the claim ticket: effects with a few hundred dollars a day of
true capacity are worthless to a fund and whole to us.

## 2. The gate every candidate passes before any test (Deep Dive Two section 5.1, binding)

- Friction anchor: cost is measured as the signal-weighted
  half-spread on the names the signal actually picks, never the
  universe median. The dive's practitioner anchor: a small-cap
  test where a 14.27bp signal-weighted half-spread against a
  4.08bp breakeven turned a +13.3 percent gross edge into a
  minus 73.5 percent net result. Any candidate whose cost model
  uses universe-median spreads is rejected at the gate.
- Novy-Marx and Velikov gate: turnover above 50 percent per
  month is the death zone absent cost mitigation (buy/hold
  spreads: stricter entry than exit). Every candidate
  pre-registers, before any paper trade: universe, expected
  turnover, and a signal-weighted cost model.
- Capacity honesty: a single capacity number quoted without a
  cost model is marketing (Deep Dive Two section 5.1). Capacity
  is stated as a range with its cost model attached, or as
  UNMEASURED.
- Survival test (Deep Dive Two section 5 falsifiable test): a
  candidate that survives 2 quarters net, on fills, earns a
  capacity study: double paper size 4x and measure impact decay;
  the size at which net Sharpe halves IS its capacity, measured
  on our own book.

## 3. Grading standard

The dive's candidate verdicts are the scan's grading standard
(runs/ORPHAN-SCAN-1.md applies them row by row). The family as a
whole is graded at B5 on the standing yardstick. Individual
candidates carry individual falsifiers at conveyor registration;
the scan itself promotes nothing.

## 4. Falsifier

Ascension Level 10 falsifier, verbatim: "Falsifier: the orphan
family's forward grading at B5, the standing yardstick; two
quarters of flat grading closes the family."
