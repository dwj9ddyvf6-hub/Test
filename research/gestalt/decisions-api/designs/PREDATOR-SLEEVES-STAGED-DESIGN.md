# STAGED DESIGN NOTE: PREDATOR SLEEVES (Invention 5)

Status: STAGED, NOT BUILT, Sat 2026-11-14. Registration:
midna-book/strategy-candidates/2026-11-14-invention-predator-sleeves.md.

## The idea in one paragraph

Breed sleeves whose only job is to find the exact regime
conditions where a champion sleeve dies: adversarial search over
regime space, seeded with failure-mode DNA from the morgue's
torn-down systems (the REA teardown corpus: repaint, future-leak,
and cost-blindness patterns are documented failure genomes).
Inoculate the champion against its predator's findings (a regime
gate, a sizing haircut in the predator's kill zone) before the
market administers the same lesson at full price. GAN logic
applied to a strategy fleet: the predator is rewarded for finding
real, out-of-sample champion drawdowns, never for in-sample
curve-fit ambushes.

## Entry ticket (all required before any build)

1. A champion worth attacking: at least one sleeve at clean
   verdict grade in the attribution ledger (own closes >= 30,
   no NETTED flag). Today that count is zero, which alone stages
   this invention.
2. The research admission exam implemented and passing on a
   pilot predator: DSR >= 0.95 with correlation-clustered
   effective trials, CPCV (N=10, k=2, purge and embargo) run on
   the predator's search, PBO < 0.2 for promotion and > 0.5
   kills the pilot, White/Hansen SPA against SPY and QQQ on the
   champion's gated versus ungated returns.
3. The regime taxonomy pre-registered (research report section
   1.5: lagged observables only, filtered never smoothed labels),
   so the predator searches a fixed space instead of inventing
   regimes after the fact.

## Shape of the build, when the ticket is punched

Predator = a parameterized regime-gate search over the champion:
candidate kill zones are cells of the regime taxonomy; the
predator's fitness is the champion's expectancy inside the cell
(most negative wins), validated by CPCV across episodes, with a
hard requirement of two independent episodes per claimed kill
zone (one episode is an anecdote, per the report). Inoculation =
the champion runs with the validated cell gated or halved, as a
twin against its ungated self, forward, for a quarter.

## Falsifier

Restated from the registration: predator-found weaknesses do not
predict champion drawdowns out of sample. If the gated twin does
not beat the ungated twin on drawdown-adjusted expectancy over
the forward quarter, the predator's map was fiction and the
invention returns to the morgue it borrowed its DNA from.
