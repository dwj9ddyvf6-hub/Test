# THE CONVERSION LEDGER

Standing ledger of the Conversion Engine, opened Sun 2026-11-15.
Filed in the program home and carried as a section of the nightly
resolution harvester from Mon 2026-11-16. It counts, per week:
candidates examined, survivors armed, accruals running, fills
graded, and realized dollars versus SPY and QQQ where they exist.
Zeros are printed as zeros. A count without a source named beside
it does not enter this ledger.

Cadence: the conversion order set the starting cadence at ten
exams a week. The owner's x10 order of 2026-11-15 01:15 ET
superseded the stopping rule during this first run: the conveyor
runs in tranches of ten, in synthesis order, until the honestly
examable queue is exhausted or the night's other lanes force a
yield, with every tranche pre-registered here before it runs and
the queue's remainder named. The exam standard does not soften
as volume grows: PROMOTE requires DSR at or above 0.95, CPCV
PBO below 0.2, SPA p below 0.05 against both SPY and QQQ, and
|t| above 3 for new families; family KILL at PBO above 0.5;
everything else REJECT. Friction basis is bar-based net of 5bp
per traded night unless a row states otherwise.

## Weekly update rule

Every week the harvester section updates five lines from the
records, never from memory: candidates examined this week
(exam verdicts printed, by tranche), survivors armed (accrual
crons read back from the scheduler), accruals running (count
and names), fills graded (journal grading and attribution
counts), realized dollars versus SPY and QQQ (book equity and
week excess, plus any conveyor graduate's own line). A week
with no movement prints its zeros.
