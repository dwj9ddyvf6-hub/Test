the lab count (87) and nothing survives at the program pooled bound

## The random-subset distribution verdict

Forty seeded random subsets (20 of 5 names, 20 of 10, drawn under
the ledger's frozen seed and order) through the same overnight
exam. Gross Sharpe: median 1.53, interquartile range 1.34 to 1.71,
minimum 1.08, maximum 1.99. Every one of the 40 is positive, and
11 of 40 (27.5 percent) post a gross Sharpe at or above the whole
basket's 1.67. Net Sharpe median is 1.06. The best draw, SUB10-16
(AMD, CRWV, EOSE, FCEL, LITE, MU, OKLO, RKLB, SMH, TSM), posts
gross 1.99 and still grades DSR 0.433 at its own family count of
40, 0.212 at the lab count, 0.055 at the pooled bound.

The verdict the distribution forces: the overnight premium is a
property of the complex, broadly shared by its members, not a
property of any selectable handful. A random handful usually
carries most of it, the best handful looks brilliant gross, and
deflation prices that brilliance at nothing. Any future
name-selection refinement of the find (the CARRY-5 form included)
must therefore rest on the friction table and on forward accrual,
never on historical subset Sharpe. Selection effect measured, not
hidden, as the ledger required.

## The illogical trial's fate

ILL-1, The Zodiac Desk: hold overnight, each month, only the Focus
names whose ticker letter-sum mod 12 equals the month index minus
one. The sign map is lumpy (one name in January's sign, five in
July's), so the rule trades 731 of 2,229 nights in seven months of
the year, holding one to five names a night. Its raw numbers are
the largest in the batch: gross +26.67bp per traded night, gross
Sharpe 2.38, net +21.67bp, net Sharpe 1.94, t 3.30. Its gross
Sharpe sits above the entire 40-subset random distribution (max
1.99).

And it fails graduation by a wide margin: DSR 0.581 at the lab
count, 0.396 at the pooled bound. Three honest readings, in order
of importance. First, the letters cannot be the carrier; the sign
groups happen to partition the complex into concentrated
portfolios, and two of the traded signs (the July group: AMD, WDC,
AXTI, CRWV, PLTR; the May group: SMH, TSM, CRDO, ALAB) are stacked
with the complex's high-drift names, so the rule is a monthly
rotating concentrated subset wearing a costume. Second, the right
null for a rotating-membership rule is a distribution of random
rotating schemes, which this batch did not run; the static-subset
distribution above is the nearest available yardstick and the
Zodiac beats it gross, which is a question, not an answer. That
question is batch 2's first specified trial (below). Third, this
is the measurement the ledger promised the trial would make if it
survived anywhere: a rule with no economic content whatsoever,
specified in advance, manufactured the batch's best raw Sharpe.
That is how much apparent signal madness can mint on this tape,
and it is why the counts govern.

## Findings by operator

INVERT. Every inversion loses, significantly: shorting the find
costs 21.46bp a night net (t -6.48), shorting SPY's overnight leg
costs 8.53bp (t -5.26), shorting the basket8 leg costs 18.50bp
(t -4.96). The mirror image confirms the legs' signs are real and
priced; there is no free inversion anywhere in the family.

ABLATE. The find is broad but tail-carried. Removing the five
CARRY names leaves +10.57bp net (Sharpe 1.08, t 3.20); removing
FCEL alone leaves +9.96bp; removing the top three contributors
leaves +9.70bp; removing the whole energy8 sector leaves +9.47bp.
Decapitation wounds it and never kills it. But ABL-6, the ex-post
tail trim, is the batch's most important single number: deleting
the 10 best basket nights of each year (90 nights, 4 percent of
the sample) turns the basket negative net, -3.01bp at Sharpe
-0.34. Roughly 88 percent of the gross premium sits in those 90
nights. The find is a broad, shallow drift plus a thin tail of
very large overnight gaps, and its 60-session forward falsifier
will be decided disproportionately by whether a few such nights
occur in the window. The two cadence halves agree (even nights
+9.83bp, odd nights +13.09bp), so the tail is not a calendar
artifact of one half-sample.

ADD RANDOM. The seeded coin control (RND-5) behaves exactly as a
control should: a random half of the nights keeps +10.90bp per
traded night, confirming the premium is a per-night property of
the tape, not of any identifiable calendar subset. The calendar
gates mostly dilute (day-of-month halves +13.58 and +9.35bp;
prime days +5.35bp at t 0.98). Ticker-length gates split the
basket into a weaker short-ticker half (+8.09bp) and a stronger
four-letter half (+13.62bp), a meaningless split printed because
the ledger promised it. The one real object in the family is
RND-7, the range-rank gate: holding only the prior session's
top-tercile range names concentrates the premium to +20.48bp net
at Sharpe 1.55, t 4.61, on all 2,229 nights. It survives at no
count (0.922 local, 0.214 lab, 0.056 pooled) and is handed on as
a near-miss, but it is the only random-family rule that changes
the per-night economics rather than merely sampling them.

METHOD SCRAMBLE. The estimator is a variable, and the premium is
estimator-fragile in an informative direction: the nightly median
collapses it (+3.89bp, Sharpe 0.42) and the trimmed mean halves
it (+5.50bp), which says the premium lives in the right tail of
each night's cross-section as well as in the tail of nights, the
same fact ABL-6 found from the other side. Dollar-volume weights
reproduce the basket (+11.76bp). The sign filter (hold only names
up on the prior day) concentrates it to +14.24bp at Sharpe 1.48
(DSR 0.879 local, a near-miss). Rank-momentum weights add a
little (+12.79bp). Inverse-vol weights dilute (+8.42bp): the
volatile names are where the premium lives, and down-weighting
them down-weights the effect.

REGIME SCRAMBLE. The premium has a habitat. It vanishes when SPY
closes below its 200-session mean (REG-7: +0.10bp, t 0.01, 434
nights) and concentrates above it (REG-8: +15.27bp, Sharpe 1.64,
t 4.12, 1,595 nights, DSR 0.919 local). It is a calm-tape effect:
low realized-vol nights pay +13.39bp at Sharpe 1.53 against
+6.36bp at Sharpe 0.49 (t 0.68) in the high-vol tercile. High
dispersion pays more per night (+17.16bp) and low dispersion more
per unit of risk (Sharpe 1.69 on 365 nights, DSR 0.766 local).
It exists in both eras and is stronger post-2020 (+12.07bp,
t 2.98) than pre-2020 (+9.35bp, t 1.98). None of these conditioners
survives at the lab count; REG-8 and REG-6 go forward as
near-miss conditioner candidates with their costs attached, and
REG-7's flat zero is the family's most decision-relevant number
for the falsifier window: a downtrend tape is the find's
off-season.

CROSS THE DEAD. The dead stay dead, and the crosses mostly bury
them deeper. Tax-loss population x overnight: +5.90bp per pooled
window night (t 0.52, 69 nights) against +2.79bp on the placebo
shoulders (t 0.22); the overnight leg does not rescue the
population in-window, at this grain, on this universe, whose
populations were thin (7 of 8 seasons produced one, of sizes 1,
14, 2, 14, 2, 4, 4, because a large-cap pull rarely falls 30
percent in a year). Guidance cuts x overnight: -9.98bp over 14
events, the cuts keep falling overnight, directionally consistent
with Wave 4's event-level observation and far too small to grade.
Guidance raises x overnight inverted: -77.44bp mean over 21
events; shorting raises overnight is a way to pay for other
people's good news. Dividend x overnight, the family's cleanest
measurement: across 2,879 ex-date events the overnight slice of
dividend capture nets -0.33bp (gross +4.67bp before the 5bp
charge), and its inversion nets -9.67bp at t -4.03. The overnight
price adjustment for dividends is efficient to within a few basis
points; there is nothing on either side of it. Short interest x
overnight (membership form, the Oct-Nov 2026 snapshot's top
tercile applied backward, carrying the PIT file's backfill label
in full): +1.04bp, t 0.54; x intraday: -3.93bp, t -1.45. Nothing,
in either leg, under the find's own cross. FIT-HI x intraday:
-1.36bp, t -0.30. CRS-10, cuts x loser state, printed 6 events at
+42.17bp mean (t 1.81, DSR 0.728 local): SMALL-N by the ledger's
own rule, an anecdote with a sign, carried as an accrual cell and
nothing more.
