## A-2 THE WORLD'S OVERNIGHT: the premium is global, not local

Construction: the overnight legs of five US-listed trackers
(EWJ Japan, EWY Korea, FXI China, EZU Eurozone, EFA EAFE),
whose own close-to-open gaps price the sessions that run
inside the US night, split the ON_focus29 basket's 2,229
nights. Proxy identity is on the face: these are trackers,
not foreign cash indices, and no futures series is held; the
futures reference route is named unavailable tonight rather
than substituted silently.

The honest reading, stated before any romance: this is the
same night's information measured twice. The trackers' gaps
and the Focus gaps are two expressions of one global overnight
move, so the split is contemporaneous, not leading. It cannot
gate the registered leg, whose entry is the prior close,
before the world has moved. What it buys is anatomy, and the
anatomy matters: the find is not a US-idiosyncratic effect.
The premium is the US expression of a global overnight drift,
present on world-up nights (+71 to +89bp) and negative on
world-down nights (-35 to -64bp), in every name, at every
tracker. Any future story about the find that is told in
US-only terms (US retail flow, US auction mechanics alone)
is contradicted by this table. Wave 8 inherits the question
in its tradeable form: the drift's futures-market expression
over the same window, where the night session is directly
tradeable, is a data build, not a conditioner (see the dive's
Transfer 2A and Wave 8 cell W8-2).

## A-5 DIGIT FORENSICS: our digits are not clean, and it does not predict anything

Diagnostics first, as registered.

Closes: the pooled last-digit (cents) distribution over the
29 Focus names deviates from uniformity at chi-square 942.8
(2018-2021, n 19,167) and 406.3 (2022-2026, n 32,827), modal
digit 0 in both subperiods. Per name the deviation is weak:
only 4 of 17 testable names reject at 0.01 in the first
subperiod and 4 of 25 in the second. Two causes, both named:
genuine round-dime clustering in traded prices, and the
panel's own basis, split-adjusted closes, whose digits are
partly arithmetic artifacts of adjustment factors rather
than traded prints. The pooled deviation is therefore a
data-quality finding about working in adjusted prices, not
a market anomaly: any future digit-level work on this estate
runs on unadjusted prints or not at all (the dive's Transfer
4A reaches the same conclusion independently).

Volumes: first digits of daily share volume reject Benford
in both subperiods (chi-square 609.5 and 77.6), the expected
result for a fixed universe whose volumes live inside a band
rather than spanning orders of magnitude; recorded as a
diagnostic, no claim attached.

Print sizes (from the A-1 corpus aggregates, 68,011,212
prints): the last digit of print size is dominated by
mechanical size conventions. Digit shares: 0 at 38.7 percent,
1 at 21.9 percent, 5 at 8.3 percent, the rest between 3.2
and 7.3 percent; chi-square against uniformity is
81,373,327, a rejection so large it describes the market's
plumbing rather than any anomaly. Round lots (size divisible
by 100) are 12.91 percent of prints; the digit-1 mass is the
modern single-share print, the digit-0 mass is round-lot
convention. No fabrication or corruption signature: the
distribution is the known fingerprint of an odd-lot tape.
One construction consequence, recorded for every future
print study on this estate: size buckets bounded at round
numbers (100, 500, 5,000) interact with exactly this mass,
so bucket shares are conventions before they are signals.

The one registered predictive probe: the stability condition
fired (pooled rejection in both subperiods, same modal digit
0), so the probe ran in its registered form, basket overnight
legs on nights the basket's median close ends in the modal
digit versus all other nights. It is degenerate: exactly 1
of 2,229 nights has a median close ending in 0. The pooled
digit fact does not survive aggregation to the basket's
median, the test has no population, and the cell closes as
a data-quality read with no predictive content. Trial
count charged in full regardless: 4.

## A-7 GEOMAGNETIC: the negative control did its job

Honestly labeled esoterica, one probe, registered with its
own no-sequels clause. Daily Ap (GFZ Potsdam, definitive;
3,240 days pulled) matched to 2,229 sessions; the Ap of the
close date conditions the next session. The literature claim
on the record, read tonight at the source: Krivelyova and
Robotti (Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta Working Paper
2003-5b) report that unusually high geomagnetic activity
depressed the FOLLOWING WEEK's returns across US indices.
Our construction tests the overnight and next-session form,
not their weekly form, and that difference is stated rather
than blurred.

Results: the overnight leg after top-decile Ap days is
+9.7bp against +17.3bp after the rest (t -0.67, p 0.50):
null, and signed against the claim's direction at this
horizon. SPY next-session realized volatility: 0.01222
versus 0.01214 (t 0.12): null. One pooled flag appeared,
basket next-session realized volatility higher after
high-Ap days (0.0439 versus 0.0380, t 4.38), and it
dissolved under its own confound checks, run as diagnostics
on the same statistic: within 2018-2021 the split is t -0.54
(wrong sign), within 2022-2026 it is t 1.48, and the high-Ap
days cluster in the solar-maximum years (73 of 235 in 2025
alone) that coincide with the Focus complex's own high
volatility regime. An era composition artifact, resolved as
such. Verdict: no survivor on any leg, no sequel, per the
registration. The probe's real yield is the one the dive
predicted (its Transfer 5B): geomagnetic activity works as
a negative control, a variable with a published claim and a
flipped out-of-sample record that a healthy pipeline should
reject, and on returns this pipeline rejected it at every
horizon it was offered.

## A-1 TAPE LINGUISTICS: 379 carriers, and the carriers are the window

Corpus, fetched tonight because no held print sequences
existed at depth: the panel's last 30 sessions (2026-10-05
to 2026-11-13), final-30-minute prints for all 31 names
(W2) and opening-30-minute prints for 8 names (W1), tokenized
as sign by size bucket by gap bucket (alphabet 48). Census
verified from the files: 1,170 windows, 68,011,212 prints,
zero corrupt files. Split as registered: first 15 sessions
train, last 15 test, no session moved.

Mining ran exactly as registered: n-grams 3 to 6, at least
40 train occurrences, top 10 per name-window per width by
absolute train forward mean, directions frozen. Selected:
1,560 phrases (40 in every one of the 39 name-windows).
Tested once each on the untouched half, per-occurrence
one-sample t in the frozen direction, carry at matching sign
and |t| at least 2.

The headline the naive pipeline would have shipped:
379 of 1,560 phrases carry (24.3 percent), against a nominal
false-carry expectation of about 36. The carriers are broad
(355 in W2, 24 in W1; spread across names and widths) and
their per-occurrence test statistics reach t of -6.7. It
looks like a discovery. It is a mirage, and the registration
carried the instruments that decompose it:

1. The pooled session-clustered diagnostic (registered):
across the 15 test sessions, the sign-adjusted mean forward
of carrier occurrences is +43.3bp with t 0.48 (p 0.64).
At the level where the forward variable actually varies,
nothing is significant.

2. Presence structure (diagnostic): carriers are not rare
patterns concentrated on a few nights. The median carrier
is present in 14 of the 15 test sessions; none is present
in 5 or fewer. These are near-ubiquitous bursts of ordinary
tape (decoded examples are mixed sign, small-size, fast-gap
runs with no clean pressure story).

3. The regime decomposition (diagnostic, and the decisive
one): 351 of 379 carriers are short phrases, because the
train window was a down regime for the overnight leg and
mining selects the window's sign. A ubiquitous short phrase
earns the test window's average short payoff mechanically;
that average was about +45bp in this second down window.
Measured against its own name-window's test mean, the
average phrase's presence excess is +1.78bp across all
1,560 phrases (28.5 percent positive), and the carriers'
session-level average of +51.3bp decomposes into roughly
+45bp of shared window payoff plus a phrase-specific
remainder inside the pooled diagnostic's noise.

Verdict: at this tokenization, on this corpus, recurring
tape phrases carry no phrase-specific forward information
beyond the regime both windows shared. The phrases are
regime mirrors. No DSR was claimed for any phrase, per the
registration, and none would have survived. The method's
durable yield is the decomposition itself, now the required
instrument for any tape-mining claim on this estate: a
phrase must beat its own window (presence excess, session
level), not merely share its sign. Trial count: 1,560 as
run; the three diagnostics above are analyses of those same
tests, charged no additional trials and labeled diagnostics
wherever they are cited.
