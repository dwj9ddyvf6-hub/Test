# UNLOCK BRAINSTORM: THE DESK, THE SLEEVES, THE RESEARCH, THE MASTERIES
(Sat 2026-11-14, owner's ask. Companion to BRAINSTORM.md on the Decisions API.)

## The honest diagnosis first
Across three censuses (Oct 24, Nov 6, Nov 11) the number of sleeves proven to be bad
strategies is still zero. Nothing on this desk has yet proven edge at verdict grade,
and nothing has been proven worthless either. The estate is not short of ideas,
signals, research, or machinery. It is short of VERDICTS. Every unlock below is
therefore measured by one question: does it produce more graded truth per week
(more fires, more resolutions, more sleeves with verdict-grade attribution, better
percent versus SPY and QQQ)? Anything that does not move one of those is a science
project, however beautiful.

## Lever 1: a capital brain over the sleeves (portfolio of strategies)
Today each sleeve lives or dies alone, sized statically. With Lens T replaying all
8,928 fills, every sleeve has (or can have) a forward equity curve. The unlock:
treat sleeves as assets in a fund of funds. Correlation and regime-conditional
performance across sleeve curves; a meta-allocator that shifts the paper risk
budget toward sleeves earning it and starves sleeves in drawdown, under the
existing risk rails. The desk's return stops being the average of 339 opinions
and becomes a managed portfolio of them. Sleeve-vs-sleeve disagreement on the
same symbol and day also gets recorded as a consensus map: agreement clusters
and lone-wolf fires are both information nobody currently captures.

## Lever 2: turn the research stockpiles into firing inventory
The estate sits on mountains of unexpressed research: the TrendSpider teardown
alone holds 358 indicators with complete source; the morgue holds more; the
candidate queue (ROT-04, C1-relaxed, squeeze redesign, the wave survivors) waits
for artisanal handling. The unlock is a conveyor, not a pile: registered,
backtested, shadowed, paper sleeve, with typed grading at every gate and a
weekly promotion cadence owned at B5. Unlock metric: candidates promoted per
month, counted. Cross-pollination by default: every surviving idea is expressed
in three forms at once (share sleeve, options structure, shadow), because the
Ghost Book already proved expression choice changes the answer.

## Lever 3: grading becomes the fastest machinery we own
The flywheel's missing gear is resolution. The estate generates far more
predictions than resolutions: fires, card theses, forecast rounds, predicates,
calibration hypotheses. The unlock: a resolution harvester that settles
everything settleable against the tape nightly, and a verdict-grade attribution
ledger per sleeve (own closes, own R, versus SPY and QQQ) that updates itself.
Kill, promote, and size decisions become arithmetic read off a leaderboard
instead of arguments in a chat. This is also the Triad's anti-zombie device:
nothing lives forever ungraded, nothing dies ungraded either.

## Lever 4: proprietary data assets hiding in our own exhaust
(a) The 8,928-fill ledger with full context is a dataset: execution research
(slippage by sleeve, hour, spread) turns execution quality into a measured edge
instead of a hope. (b) Archiving the options chains and tape prints daily builds
a private IV and flow history; the condor's 60-observation cold start is one
instance of a general truth, that VRP research (the canon's strongest measured
edge) needs exactly the history we keep throwing away. (c) The typed sentiment
corpus (researcher posts, flow alerts, basket reads) becomes a labeled dataset
for flow-edge studies instead of a pile of screenshots.

## Lever 5: options as the desk's second engine
The book is options-only; the desk is shares-only; the bridge is shadow.
Ghost Book logic estate-wide: every firing sleeve's signals get a shadow
options expression, graded daily. Within weeks the estate owns a map it cannot
buy: which signals are worth more as options than as shares, by regime. That
map feeds the book's menu (Tier-1 utilization is its first draft), feeds VRP
harvesting as a dedicated forward-tested family, and tells us where the desk's
real convexity lives. The options proof sample stops being one spread and
becomes a population.

## Lever 6: the Triad run like a research org
Three frontier nodes plus a typed layer is a staff, if it is managed like one:
standing falsifier-first sprints, each with a named bottleneck target (feeding
this week, grading next), B5 as the promotion board, and sleeve careers: every
sleeve has an owner node, a development state, and a next test on file. No
orphan sleeves, no orphan ideas. Meta-improvement gets a queue and a budget
instead of good intentions: one machinery bottleneck cured per cycle, counted.

## Lever 7: compress time
Proofs serialize on live sessions because we let them. The fixture drives
proved the desk can re-run history through its sleeves. The unlock: a full
historical replay harness, so every question that is actually historical gets
a same-day replay verdict, and live sessions are reserved for genuinely
forward questions. In parallel: shadow books are free; hypothesis families get
their own shadow books (the pod pattern generalized) so forward tests stop
queuing behind one book's card floor.

## Lever 8: mastery as a measured curriculum (the book)
The options craft loop, closed: card, fill, graded journal, typed craft stats
by structure type, and the taxonomy rotation deliberately targets the weakest
structure family next. Mastery stops being a vibe and becomes a curve: which
structures does this trader actually make money in, by regime, improving on a
schedule. The desk's shadow map (Lever 5) supplies the menu; the craft stats
supply the honesty.

## The sequence Midna would run
1. Resolution harvester + verdict-grade attribution ledger (Lever 3): everything
   else reads off it.
2. Ghost Book estate-wide (Lever 5): the highest-information-per-dollar build in
   the building, and it feeds the book directly.
3. The candidate conveyor (Lever 2) with typed grading at the gates.
4. Replay harness (Lever 7) to un-serialize proofs.
5. Meta-allocator (Lever 1) last, because allocating across sleeves before
   their grades are real would be precision theater.

## The falsifier for this whole brainstorm
If, after the harvester and the attribution ledger run for a month, the number
of sleeves with verdict-grade edge (positive, versus SPY and QQQ, on own closes)
is still zero, then the estate's problem is not unlocking. It is that the
signals themselves do not carry edge, and the honest move is to say so and
rebuild the signal layer. The brainstorm is written to make that answer
reachable, whichever way it lands.
