# THE CHAOS FOUNDRY, FIRST CASTING, 2026-11-15

Built under the owner's creation-first order: create something new, eschew training, do the opposite of what seems right, question everything including ourselves. The foundry creates; it does not grade the existing estate. Three castings were poured the night the brainstorm was written, each measured honestly on real data, each counted in the ledger it touches. No em dashes in this report.

## Verdict in one paragraph

Forty-four creations were specified to buildable depth (CHAOS-BRAINSTORM-2026-11-15.md), twelve of them from territories this estate has never entered. The three castings produced one structural finding, one clean rejection with a live residue, and one graveyard with two faint pulses. The Anti-Midna won its pooled read (+268.80bp per position) but beat the naive fade-everything control by nothing (-3.33bp pooled): what it convicted is the night study's structure, whose written directional conclusions are one-signed per night, not yet its per-name skill. The Anti-Focus found the overnight premium alive but weaker among the least-watched names (+4.31bp a night, Sharpe 0.867) and was REJECTED by the family exam at its honest count (DSR 0.257 at 59; 0.074 at the pooled bound of 347). The Graveyard Book resurrected nothing: four obituaries were upheld in numbers (the weekend effect inverted, the January effect precisely zero, Sell in May's harvested differential negative, PEAD inverted on its own burial ground), and two legs kept a pulse under the bar (turn of the month's differential, pairs trading's residue). Creation was free; measurement was not kind. That is the foundry working as designed.
