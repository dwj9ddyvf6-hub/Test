## THE D2 DECISION (verbatim-ready)

W6A D2 DECISION (2026-11-15): No auction-print gross leg is added to either accrual. The registered bar-based leg remains the only gross leg recorded in the ledgers. No print_gross_bp, print_names, or print_reason field exists in the amended files. Print truth is pursued, if at all, by a separate offline capture run outside the accruals, under open row W6A-PRINT-COMPANION below.

Reasoning, on the record:

1. Validation coverage. The print-identification route at keepgoing2/wave5/w5a_carry_capture.py (first trade on exchange Q carrying condition O in the 14:30:00Z to 14:30:03Z window, fallback to 14:30:12Z) was probed and validated on six names only, all Nasdaq listed (SNDK, CRWV, RKLB, NVDA, AMD, MU). It has not been validated for the other 23 focus29 names, whose primary listing venues and cross-print condition conventions were not measured. Run live from session 1, the field would be a partial-coverage mean biased toward the validated subset. A print_names count would disclose the coverage; it would not cure the bias, and a biased diagnostic inside the grading ledger invites exactly the over-trust the Wave 5 capture warned against on per-name prints.
2. No information is lost by deferring. SIP trade records are historical and remain fetchable after the fact; the Wave 5 capture itself computed condition-identified prints for sessions already past (2026-11-02 to 2026-11-13). A companion capture can compute print legs over already-recorded sessions at any later date. Unlike a field that must exist at write time to exist at all, the print leg is backfillable from the same route, so session 1 does not foreclose it.
3. Instrument hygiene on the first morning. This amendment's job is hardening: relabels, ledger markers, a frozen verdict basis, a KeyError guard, and recorded flags that add no network surface beyond the existing bars fetch and one local file read. A trades-endpoint fetch per name per recorded session is a new live failure surface inside the grading instrument, landed in the same change as the hardening, one morning before the falsifier's first lawful session. The minimum sufficient change lands the hardening clean and keeps the instrument to its registered inputs.
4. The gap is material, so deferral is not dismissal. The measured bar-versus-print difference is real: pooled mean gap +5.436bp per leg on the six measured names, CRWV systematic at +28.765bp mean, and the CARRY basket's true-print auction cost at a +5.845bp session median against the bar-based -0.626bp. The typed seat put probability 0.67 on the gap changing the economic reading at the falsifier's session-60 close. That is why the deferral carries a dated companion row (W6A-PRINT-COMPANION) instead of silence.

Typed seat (advisory only, printed beside the decision as charged): one request through the estate typed layer (template keepgoing2/wave5/w5a_typed_seat.py; runner keepgoing2/wave6/w6a_typed_seat.py; record keepgoing2/wave6/w6a_typed_seat.json; bank w6a-d2-print-leg version 1; model gpt-6-luna; 1,538 input tokens; $0.000154). ## W6-3. EARNINGS FLAGS (landed in BOTH accrual files)

Every record now carries "earnings_names" (the names in the session's basket whose earnings event leg falls on that session) and "earnings_count". Recorded fields only; the conditioner is closed and the flags change no computation. The source is the Wave 5 corpus, keepgoing2/wave5/w5c_earnings_edgar.json (673 EDGAR Item 2.02 prints, 24 filers; SMH, SOXX, XLE, ARM, TSM excluded by construction, so those names can never flag). Convention, per WAVE-5 section W5-4 and w5c_analysis.py: a name's event leg is the first overnight leg whose open (09:30 ET on the open date) is strictly after the filing's acceptance timestamp. The accrual implements it as an interval rule on each leg's own start bar: an acceptance in [09:30 ET on the start date, 09:30 ET on the leg date) flags that leg, which is the same mapping on the fetched window and cannot flag a later leg for an event whose true leg predates the window. The corpus loads once per run, fail-soft: a missing corpus yields empty flags plus the state note "missing: earnings flags empty", an unreadable corpus yields "unreadable: earnings flags empty", and neither can crash a run (proven for both modules; the loaded state reads 24 names and 673 events).

## W6-2. WEEKDAY FORWARD CELL (keepgoing2/wave6/w6_weekday_cell.py)

The reader takes the two production ledger paths as arguments (--focus29, --energy8; defaults are the real ledgers), writes nothing, and prints the cell state as JSON per basket: sessions recorded, skipped rows, weekday legs counted, weekend legs, the span-2 holiday class counted and excluded, the first session counted unclassified (it has no predecessor in the ledger), the running weekday mean net5 labeled UNGRADED before session 20, and the scheduled reads at sessions 20, 40, 60 computed over the first 20, 40, or 60 recorded sessions once reached. Classification is by leg span in calendar days between consecutive recorded session dates: span 1 is a weekday leg, span 3 or more is a weekend leg, span 2 is the closed holiday class. Skipped marker rows are not recorded sessions and take no part in spans. Before session 20 the output carries counts and the UNGRADED mean only, no verdict language (asserted on the raw output in the dry run).

Demotion rule, written in the module docstring and restated here: at session 60, if the weekday-leg mean net5 (over the weekday legs among the first 60 recorded sessions) is not positive, cell W5C-WEEKDAY-FWD is DEMOTED; its claim was that weekday legs carry the premium forward. The unconditioned falsifier's verdict stands separately and is not touched by this cell in either direction.
