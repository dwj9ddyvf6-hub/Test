## POSITION-DIALECT-20261030 + CRYPTO-FEE-INKIND-20261030 (found live by the first cured trading cycles, cured same pass)

- Severity: HIGH (the cured desk re-halted itself within one
  cycle of resuming crypto trading).
- What broke: two broker representation facts the flat-book era
  had hidden. (1) Alpaca's POSITIONS endpoint reports crypto
  pairs bare (BTCUSD, LINKUSD) while its ORDERS carry the slash
  form (BTC/USD); reconcile compared raw keys, manufacturing two
  phantom discrepancies per crypto position and attributing the
  broker leg baseline_or_unknown (live proof: the 2026-10-31
  02:58Z cycle reconcile). (2) Alpaca withholds the crypto taker
  fee IN THE ASSET on buys: BTC/USD filled 0.064654274 vs
  position held 0.064492637, LINK/USD 86 vs 85.785 (both exactly

  14.99, equalled the ENTIRE residual cash discrepancy to the

  would re-halt its sleeve on dust and grow the cash gap until
  the desk-wide cash halt fired again.
- Fix (desk3/reconcile.py, desk3/broker_dialect.py is the shared
  translator): reconcile resolves BOTH sides' position keys
  through the one canonical translator before comparing.
  The pre-reconcile sweep additionally MEASURES the in-kind fee:
  for a crypto symbol whose orders are all terminal, dust =
  journal gross position (net of prior fee rows) minus the
  broker's net position; a positive remainder is journaled as
