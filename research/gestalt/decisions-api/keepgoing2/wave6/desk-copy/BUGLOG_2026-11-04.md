## LAUNCH-KILL-LOOP-20261104 — watchdog terminated every slow first cycle; 300.5-minute cycle hole
- Severity: critical (desk-wide, self-exciting supervision failure).
- File: desk3/watchdog.py.
- What broke: the runner's heartbeat (state/runner.json) is written only
  after a completed cycle. A fresh launch was judged launched_no_heartbeat
  at LAUNCH_GRACE_S = 900 s and terminated, but first cycles under load
  (warm phase up to ~440 s + 337 evaluations + serial fill sweep) ran
  longer than 900 s: Tuesday median cycle 861 s. On 2026-11-03 the
  watchdog terminated 32 launches mid-first-cycle on a 15-minute
  metronome from 19:28Z; each kill deepened the backlog, so no cycle

  while the runners were in fact working (journals landing throughout).
  The restart-intensity cap (3 per 600 s) never engaged: one kill per
  900 s is under its rate.
- Fix: activity-conditioned launch hold. Past launch grace, a launched
  runner is HELD (status launch_hold, no terminate, no relaunch) while
  desk activity inside HOLD_WINDOW_S = 1800 s (newest state/<sleeve>/
  journal.jsonl mtime, or state/warm_bars.json landing) proves work is
  in flight: _recent_desk_activity() + the hold branch in check_once().
  A silent launch keeps the old terminate-and-relaunch path exactly;
  test_harden_supervision_blockers.py's pinned past-grace test passes
  unchanged.
- Regression blocker: tests/test_launch_hold_20261104_blockers.py
  (3 tests: fresh journal activity holds; warm product landing holds;
  stale activity still restarts). Fail-first verified: the two hold

  supervision suites (harden, audit-cure, a9 flip) 39/39 green.
- Live proof: watchdog restarted onto the cured code 2026-11-04
  ~05:48Z (old pid 1775 terminated, new pid 28769, status ok), runner
  pid 9772 untouched and cycling.
