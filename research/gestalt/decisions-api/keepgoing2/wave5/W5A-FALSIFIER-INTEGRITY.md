Three claims around the core are wrong or fragile and are named as defects D1 to D7 below; none of them changes the net5 mean as wired, and D2 changes what the gross means relative to the auction-form framing, not relative to the registered bar-return wording.

### What checks out, line by line

The fetch starts at ANCHOR inclusive (line 82), so the 2026-11-13 bar is present as the first previous close. Lines 110 to 112: a session is recorded only if it has at least 10 names. Equal weight is line 113, the plain mean of the leg values times 1e4.
- (b) Record fields. Lines 114 to 124: gross_bp is the mean leg in bp; net5_bp = gross_bp minus REGISTERED_COST_BP (5.0, line 48); crossing_cost_bp and net_crossing_bp come from measured_costs_bp(); auction_cost_bp and net_auction_bp likewise, with net defined as gross minus cost in both cases. All six fields are present in the dry-run row (Part 2).
- (c) Friction keys. measured_costs_bp() (lines 53 to 69) reads per_name[s]["crossing_rt_bp_median"] and basket gross_mean_bp and net_auction_bp_equal_weight. All of those keys exist in keepgoing2/wave4/v1_friction.json as read tonight. The auction value it derives is correct: 18.907241 minus 19.532984 = -0.626bp, matching the stated -0.6bp. The crossing value is NOT the stated one; see D1.

Lines 139 to 143: at n >= 60 the verdict is SURVIVES if mean_net5_bp > 0 else DEMOTED, which matches the registered wording including the exactly-zero case (zero is not positive, so DEMOTED). The state mean is recomputed from the ledger file on every run (lines 128 to 138).

### Defects and silent failure modes, named exactly

- D1. Crossing bound mislabeled in the docstring (lines 22 to 24) and in the Wave 4 report. The docstring calls the crossing bound the mean of the per-name median quoted round trips at +41.2bp. The code (lines 61 to 63) does compute the mean, and the mean is +46.076bp, proven two ways: a direct call of the production module returned measured_costs_bp() = (46.075756, -0.625743), and the dry-run row carries crossing_cost_bp 46.076. The +41.245bp figure in v1_friction.json is basket crossing_rt_bp_median_of_names, the MEDIAN of the per-name medians. Effect: every ledger record's crossing diagnostic leg uses 46.076bp while the docstring and the Wave 4 report describe 41.2bp. The falsifier leg net5_bp does not use this number, so the registered test is unaffected; the diagnostic leg is mislabeled by 4.83bp.
- D2. Docstring claim "the daily bar open and close are the auction prints" (lines 18 to 20) is half false, measured in Part 3. Bar close matched the condition-identified closing auction print exactly in 60 of 60 name-sessions. Bar open matched the opening auction print in only 4 of 60, with a pooled mean signed gap of +5.436bp (bar open above auction open) across the six CARRY names. Effect: the accrual grades a bar-open exit, not a pure opening-auction exit. The registered falsifier is worded as a bar return (previous close to this open), so the script is faithful to the registered wording; it is the auction-form interpretation of the gross that the capture does not support on the open side. The 29-name size of this gap is unmeasured; Part 3 measured six names only.
- D3. No consecutive-session check (lines 98 to 102). Legs pair each bar with the previous FETCHED bar for that name, whatever its date. If a name is missing a bar (halt, feed gap), the next leg silently spans multiple days and is recorded as one overnight leg on the later date. No warning is emitted.
- D4. Sub-10-name sessions vanish silently (lines 110 to 112). A session with fewer than 10 names is skipped and never recorded, with no gap marker in the ledger. The verdict count is recorded sessions only (line 131), so 60 recorded sessions could span more than 60 trading sessions and the ledger alone would not show it.
- D5. Record carries the name count, not the names (line 114). A recorded session's composition cannot be recounted from the ledger; a 10-name session and a 29-name session are indistinguishable in kind, only in the count field.
- D6. No stop at 60 (lines 110 to 127 and 139 to 143). If the cron keeps running after session 60, later sessions keep appending and the verdict line is recomputed over ALL recorded sessions while its text still says "at 60 sessions". A verdict printed at 60 could in principle flip at 61 plus. The cure is to slice the first 60 records for the verdict, or to stop the cron at the verdict; neither is in the code.
- D7. measured_costs_bp() fragility (lines 60 to 63, and 117 to 120). A missing friction file returns (None, None) as documented, but a missing per_name key for any FOCUS name raises KeyError and crashes the whole run, because the dict is accessed directly twice in the comprehension. Separately, the crossing fields use truthiness (if cost_cross) while the auction fields correctly use is not None (lines 121 to 124); a crossing cost of exactly 0.0 would be written as None. Not triggered at the current 46.076bp, named as a latent asymmetry.
- Non-defect observations. Rounding: records are rounded to 3 decimals and the state mean is computed from the rounded records (lines 135 to 138); worst case drift is 0.0005bp per session, immaterial against a 5bp test. The no-explicit-end fetch (lines 81 to 83) means a 09:46 run sees today's bar; today's leg uses only today's open and yesterday's close, both final at that hour, and yesterday's close is re-fetched fresh on the next run before it is used as a previous close, so no provisional close is ever locked into the ledger.
