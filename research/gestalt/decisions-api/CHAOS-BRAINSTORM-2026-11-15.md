# THE CHAOS BRAINSTORM, 2026-11-15

Written under the owner's creation-first order: create something new, eschew training, do the opposite of what seems right, question everything including ourselves. This file CREATES. It does not grade the existing estate; grading machinery appears only as each creation's first falsifier, which is part of its specification. Every creation carries a mechanism, a data contract (a source that exists, or the named build that gets it), why it defies the obvious, and its first falsifier. Vague poetry was refused at the door: if a creation could not name its data, it is not in this file.

Conventions: "shadow" means a paper book with sealed, dated records. Friction language follows dive law: any creation that implies crossing a spread is charged at ask and bid in its falsifier, or the charge is named unmeasured. Counts: every casting built from this file joins the trial ledger it touches at its honest count.

## I. INVERT THE WATCHER: trade what nobody watches

**CB-02 The Silence Premium.** Mechanism: rank liquid names by days since their last news article and last filing of any kind; hold the overnight leg only on names in a live silence streak of 10 or more sessions, exiting the streak the day coverage resumes. Data contract: Alpaca news API timestamps and EDGAR submissions timestamps, both already routed; the streak table is a small nightly build. Defies the obvious: every system on this desk trades on events; this one trades on the absence of events, treating silence as a position the crowd has forgotten to price. **CB-03 Coverage Birthdays.** Mechanism: the first day a name with no options chain gains one, or a name with no news coverage in a year gets its first article, is an attention regime change; measure the 20 sessions after a coverage birth against the 20 before. **CB-04 The Forgotten Filing.** Mechanism: 8-K filings timestamped in the final 30 minutes of the session or after the close on Friday get read by almost nobody before Monday; hold the filing names' overnight leg over the following weekend only, signed by the filing's own first-session reaction direction.
