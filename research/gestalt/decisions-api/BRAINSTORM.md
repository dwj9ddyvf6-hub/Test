## The governing pattern: graduation
Every use starts as a disagreement flag (never a gate, veto, or sizing input, per the
2026-11-12 override). Each use carries a resolvable predicate so the tape grades it.
A use whose measured excess stays positive across a real sample graduates: flag to
advisory to, where the evidence is overwhelming, a checklist gate. A use at zero excess
stays a flag forever and costs almost nothing. This turns the whole estate into a
bench of cheap judges whose trust is earned in public.
