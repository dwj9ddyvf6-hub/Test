### P04. Tail-curve estimator, method class (forecast model, shared): SET

Chosen value, source runfile section 5, restated exactly in substance: the estimand is E[L | I], defined by the identity E[L | I] equals the integral from K1 to K2 of P(ST above k | I) dk.
