# Alpha Hunt Wave 2, Workstream W2-5: Dive Four, The Unentered Territories, Widened 10x

Research date: 2026-11-14. Scope: deep research only. No code was built, no desk file was edited, and no trade is recommended. This estate is paper only. Every use below is a paper research use.

Method: falsifier first for every territory. For each territory this report states the claim, the falsifier that would kill it, the strongest evidence for, the strongest evidence against, realistic friction and latency accounting, and a verdict of ADOPT, TEST, or REFUSE with the breaking point named. ADOPT means adopt as a standard or a context layer, not as a proven profit source, unless the text says otherwise. TEST means a pre-registered paper test is justified and nothing stronger is justified. REFUSE means do not spend further paper capital or research cycles on that form of the claim.

Provenance key used throughout: PRIMARY means a peer reviewed paper, an exchange or regulator publication, or a first party data publisher. REPLICATION means an independent post-publication retest with costs and controls stated. VENDOR means the author sells the data, product, or subscription the claim validates. SINGLE SOURCE means no independent corroboration was found in this dive. MARKETING means a promotional claim with no method that can be checked. Local archive grades use the morgue's own labels: EXTRACTED means a mechanism was reconstructed, VALIDATED means it survived the desk research protocol. In the local archive almost everything cited here is EXTRACTED and not VALIDATED, and this report preserves that distinction.

Binding priors from Dives One to Three, not relitigated here: naive gamma exposure levels failed their best test and may be used only in controls grade designs; VPIN is excluded after a failed replication; the overnight premium is about zero net post-2020 at retail costs; LLM return prediction was refuted under memory controls; post earnings announcement drift is dead in large caps since 2006. Any territory below that touches one of those results must clear the same controls that killed the earlier form, or it is refused on arrival.

## Owner-facing summary

This summary uses plain hyphens only and contains no em dash characters by design.

- The clock still matters for execution and almost not at all for direction. Volatility, volume, and spreads keep a strong U shape, high at the open and close and low at midday. Unconditional first half hour to last half hour momentum is dead post-2020 in the best retests. One narrow conditional form survives as a test: last half hour continuation only when a tape signed dealer book is short gamma at 15:30, plus a cross sectional end of day reversal of about 3 basis points gross that is too small to trade alone.
- Buyback blackouts are real as a calendar and weak as a trade. About 60 percent of programs keep buying through blackouts under pre-set plans, the best index studies find no return difference between blackout and open windows, and daily repurchase data does not exist in usable public form after the 2023 disclosure rule was vacated. Verdict: refuse the directional blackout trade.
- Crypto leads equities by about one minute only on weekends in one 2026 perp study, explaining about 1 percent of the next minute variance. In regular hours neither market leads by a measurable minute. Weekend crypto losses do predict Monday equity weakness in one peer reviewed 2025 study, and weekend crypto volatility predicts Monday variance post-2024. That supports a Monday risk conditioner test, not a directional alpha claim.
- Charm and vanna are real Greeks and unproven as standalone signals. Dealer short vol is proven with holdings data from 1996 to 2001. The causal step from vol falling to dealers buying to price rising has never been proven, and Cboe measures net dealer gamma at 0.04 to 0.17 percent of daily S and P futures liquidity. Naive charm and vanna levels are refused. A controls grade test with VIX and at the money IV controls is the only admissible form.
- Weather moves natural gas and power prices. It does not give a tradable lead for Fluence, Eos, FuelCell, or Bloom equity options. Those four names sell storage, fuel cells, and on site power on contract and data center timelines, not spot weather. The weather to equity link for this basket is refused with reasons stated per name.
- Odd lots split in two. Odd lot quotes inside the quoted spread carry information in peer reviewed work and the November 2025 round lot reform shrinks that hidden market. Odd lot trade counts as a retail sentiment signal are contaminated by algorithmic slicing, fractional shares, and a contested retail identification method whose predictive power weakened in 2016 to 2021. Quotes are a test. Trade counts as sentiment are refused.
- Across every timeframe the estate holds, the same split repeats. At 1 minute, 5 minutes, 15 minutes, 1 hour, and daily bars, volatility and cost seasonality are adoptable for execution timing. Return seasonality at each bar is either dead, tiny, or untested at full rigor. A single pre-registered clock atlas test is justified. A shelf of separate clock strategies is not.
- The overnight and intraday split is a powerful diagnostic and an unproven sizing signal. Momentum earns overnight and beta is priced overnight in the classic samples. Net capture fails on costs in post-2020 trials already binding on this estate. Decomposing each sleeve family is worth one diagnostic test. Resizing or rescheduling sleeves on that split alone is refused until the diagnostic passes.
- Volatility term structure and skew contain one survivor. The Cremers and Weinbaum call minus put IV spread stays significant in every regime of a 2015 to 2026 re-estimation of 12.36 million firm days. The Xing smirk decays to insignificance and reverses. IV rank and IV percentile alone do not predict direction and the high IV rank selling folklore fails in the SPX credit spread test cited here. Test the IV spread. Refuse percentile transitions as a standalone signal.
- Our own archived chains can support surface history and reproducible flow proxies. They cannot support the Pan and Poteshman result, which needs buyer initiated opening trades from a non public Cboe file. Public and archived volume without opening flags predicts for only one to two days in the original paper and is a weaker object. Grade the archive as a surface corpus, not a flow corpus.
- The morgue is a real idea mine and a graveyard of unvalidated extractions. The best predator candidates are named in Territory 11, led by the SqueezeMetrics analogue method, the SpotGamma regime split, the TrendSpider Raindrop VWAP drift rule, and flow scoring from the FlowAlgo and Unusual Whales teardowns, each aimed at a known weakness of our own sleeves. None is validated. All enter as tests, not as beliefs.
- Fleet self interaction is the highest multiple testing risk in this dive. With even 12 sleeve families there are 66 pairs, and with roles and horizons the trial count passes one thousand, which needs a t statistic near or above 4 under Bonferroni and a clustered effective sample size, not the raw trade count. Brute force pair mining is refused. A short pre-registered list of economically motivated pairs is a test.
- The pre-FOMC drift is gone in equities post-2016 in the best retests: minus 0.01 percent per trade since 2016 in one 1994 to 2026 SPY test, and 9.2 basis points in 2016 to 2019 versus 49 basis points in 1994 to 2011 in Kurov and coauthors. Macro dates still matter as volatility and liquidity events. Directional calendar drift is refused. Event volatility scheduling is a test.
- The strongest adopt in this dive is not a signal. It is an execution standard imported from market making: grade every fill with effective spread, realized spread, adverse selection, and implementation shortfall, and treat auctions as designed mechanisms with known imbalance information times. That standard costs nothing in alpha risk and improves every other test in this report.

## Common friction and latency accounting

These numbers recur in every territory, so they are stated once here and applied below.

- Options round trip cost: at least the full bid ask spread on entry plus exit for marketable orders, plus commissions and fees where the paper broker charges them. For Focus 30 single names, spreads on near dated options are routinely several percent of premium. Any gross edge under about 10 basis points on the underlying, or under about 5 percent on a short dated option premium, should be presumed dead until a test with real bid and ask fills proves otherwise. This is the standard that killed the overnight premium and naive GEX in prior dives.
- Equity index futures and SPY or QQQ: a realistic paper to live comparable round trip is 1 to 5 basis points including spread and slippage, per the replication studies cited in Territory 1 (Krueger Algorithms uses about 1 to 5 basis points round trip on index CFDs; Ripbacktest uses about 15.76 dollars per NQ trade in its futures tests). Edges of 1 to 3 basis points gross cannot survive this.
- Latency tiers: co-located high frequency firms act in microseconds to single digit milliseconds between Aurora (CME) and New Jersey equity venues, with microwave transit about 4 milliseconds one way. A retail or paper API workflow acts in hundreds of milliseconds to seconds, plus data delay. Any lead lag measured in milliseconds or in one minute bars is therefore not executable by this estate unless the signal persists for many minutes after it is observable. Persistence, not existence, is the tradability question.
- Auction and close effects: effects measured to the closing auction print cannot be traded at that print after seeing them. Any test must enter before the information is final, or exit in the next session, and must charge the next session gap.
- Multiple testing: Harvey, Liu, and Zhu (2016, PRIMARY) catalogued 316 published factors and argued a new factor needs a t statistic of at least 3.0, with a Bonferroni equivalent near 3.7 at 316 tests. Every territory below that proposes many variants inherits this bar, scaled to its own trial count. The bar is stated per territory.

## Territory 1: Intraday clock and seasonality at full rigor post-2020

**Verdict: TEST (narrow conditional forms only). Unconditional intraday momentum: REFUSE. Volatility U shape as execution timing: ADOPT as context.**

Claim: the time of day predicts returns: first half hour momentum predicts the last half hour (Gao, Han, Li, Zhou 2018, PRIMARY, SPY 1993 to 2013), rest of day predicts the last half hour across 62 futures 1974 to 2020 (Baltussen, Da, Lammers, Martens 2021, PRIMARY, Journal of Financial Economics), lunch is a lull, and overnight versus intraday returns decompose persistently (Lou, Polk, Skouras 2019, PRIMARY).

Falsifier, written first: if, on post-2020 data with real costs, (a) the unconditional first to last half hour slope is zero or negative, (b) the shifted signal control does as well as the real signal, and (c) no pre-registered conditioning variable restores a net effect that beats its control in a sealed holdout, the directional clock claim is dead for this estate.

Evidence for:
- Gao et al. (2018): first half hour return predicts last half hour return on SPY, predictive R squared about 1.6 percent, higher on high volatility days. Baltussen et al. (2021): rest of day to last half hour slope is positive across equity, bond, commodity, and currency futures, and the slope is 6.63 (t 4.78, R squared 3.58 percent) when prior day net gamma exposure is negative versus 0.82 (t 1.03, R squared 0.05 percent) when it is non-negative. That interaction is the strongest single result in this territory because it names a mechanism and a conditioner available before the close.
- End of day reversal, the cross sectional cousin: Baltussen, Da, and Soebhag (2025, PRIMARY working paper, Notre Dame) document intraday losers outperforming winners in the last 30 minutes. A pre-registered post-2019 retest (Vidovich and Zerjal 2026, arXiv 2611.09286, REPLICATION) finds 3.05 basis points per day (t 3.0) in 2023 to 2026 on S and P 500 names excluding about 80 heavily traded names, versus 3.13 basis points (t 7.5) in 2016 to 2019, and 1.69 basis points (t 2.3) from 2020 onward. The authors state no claim net of costs.
- Volatility and volume U shape: Harris (1986) and Wood, McInish, Ord (1985), both PRIMARY, plus every modern replication: volume and volatility are highest at open and close, lowest midday. Practitioner measurement on SPY 5 minute bars (TradeScript, SINGLE SOURCE, practitioner) puts 18 to 22 percent of volume in the first 30 minutes, 15 to 20 percent in the last 30 minutes, and midday 5 minute realized volatility 40 to 50 percent below the first two hours.
- One intraday momentum construction survives a hard retest in one market: the Zarattini, Aziz, Barbon (2024, working paper, VENDOR adjacent because the authors sell research and education) noise area breakout passed all six gates of an independent retest on NQ futures in the New York session only, plus 231,557 dollars per contract over 1,504 trades after costs (Ripbacktest 2026, REPLICATION). It failed on ES, gold, Bitcoin, and in Asian and London sessions.

Evidence against:
- Gao form, post-2020: Ripbacktest (2026, REPLICATION, rules frozen before runs, shifted signal controls) finds the first half hour rule lost money before costs in all 8 versions on ES and NQ futures 2020 to 2026, and on NQ each variant lost from minus 14.31 to minus 1.66 dollars per trade before costs, then more after 15.76 dollars of costs. Signal percentiles versus shifted controls were 28.8 to 43.4, meaning the real signal did worse than its own signals moved to other days.
- Baltussen form, post-2020: Firmtape (2026, REPLICATION, 1,085 SPX sessions, 2022-04-14 to 2026-08-20) finds an unconditional slope of 0.006 (t 0.6), flat in every year. Krueger Algorithms (2026, REPLICATION across 13 indices and FX) finds the Baltussen rule replicates gross at about 40 percent of paper size, plus 1.15 basis points per day (t 3.5), then minus 1.89 basis points per day (t minus 5.7) after costs of 1 to 5 basis points round trip, and reverses in the 2023 to 2026 holdout. The same Krueger set finds Boyarchenko overnight drift about zero since 2017 and minus 5.5 basis points net.
- The conditional exception is fragile across measurement choices: Firmtape finds continuation only on the 14 to 15 percent of days when a tape signed (aggressor signed) dealer book is short at 15:30 (slope 0.036 to 0.038, t about 2.0 to 3.1 depending on specification), and finds no exception on the open interest volume book, where dealers are short on 43 percent of days (slope 0.016, t 0.95). The conditioner therefore depends on a signing construction that public open interest cannot reproduce. That is exactly the handicap that killed naive GEX.
- Day of week: French (1980, PRIMARY) Monday minus 0.17 percent, 1953 to 1977. Post-2000 the effect is gone in every modern count found: Monday plus 0.022 percent and up 54 percent since 2000 in a 1950 to 2026 count (JorgAI, SINGLE SOURCE data post, method stated); Robins and Smith (2016) and Schwert (2003) as summarized in multiple secondary sources; a pre-registered style weekday test through 2026-10-09 (Daily Speculations, Grimes, REPLICATION) finds no weekday differs from the average day after 1980 after Holm correction, and notes that detecting a 0.05 percentage point weekday edge at 80 percent power would need about 57 years. Day of week directional trading is refused outright.
- Lunch effect: the lull is a volatility and volume fact, not a return fact. Allez and Bouchaud (2011, PRIMARY, 126 NYSE stocks) find average return shows no intraday pattern while volatility shows the clean U. Selling lunch breakouts or buying lunch dips as a return rule has no primary support found in this dive.

Friction and latency: last half hour trades pay the full close spread and compete with closing auction flow. A 1 to 3 basis point gross effect (end of day reversal, Baltussen gross) is inside a 1 to 5 basis point round trip before any option spread is considered. Expressed in options, the effect is untradable: a 3 basis point underlying move is noise inside a single name option spread. Any tradable expression must be in SPY, QQQ, ES, or NQ equivalents, at index spreads, and must still beat the shifted control.

Breaking point: the test dies if the tape signed short gamma conditioner cannot be built from data this estate actually receives, or if the conditional slope in the sealed holdout is under 2 basis points gross. Do not substitute the open interest conditioner after seeing that result; Firmtape already shows that substitution fails.

Test design if pursued (paper only): pre-register two arms only. Arm A: SPX or NQ last half hour continuation, conditioned on a 15:30 dealer book signed from trade aggressor data, with VIX and ATM IV as controls, shifted signal control, costs at 2x the desk standard. Arm B: cross sectional end of day reversal on liquid names excluding the most heavily traded names, measured to the last trade before the closing auction, gross and net both reported, with the explicit expectation that net is likely negative and the value is as a veto or exit timing aid for existing sleeves, not as a standalone book.

## Territory 2: Corporate buyback blackout calendars as forced flow

**Verdict: REFUSE (directional trade). ADOPT the calendar only as an earnings season liquidity warning already implied by Territory 13.**

Claim: companies pause discretionary buybacks for roughly two to five weeks before earnings, removing an estimated 2 to 5 billion dollars per day of demand (BigPic Solutions summary, VENDOR adjacent research page, SINGLE SOURCE for the daily dollar figure), so prices sag in blackout and recover when the bid returns, with about 88 percent of S and P 500 names in blackout at the pre-season peak on 30 June in one 2026 count (Quantillions, SINGLE SOURCE, method partly disclosed).

Falsifier: if high buyback firms show no return difference between blackout, earnings, and open windows in a large sample, and if a majority of buyback volume continues through blackouts under pre-set plans, the forced flow premise fails at both steps: no flow removal, and no price pattern.

Evidence for:
- The calendar is real. Blackouts are company policy (not an SEC rule), typically starting late in the quarter and ending a few days after results (Proflex Insights summary of Rule 10b-18 and company policies, secondary). Dittmann, Li, Obernberger, Zheng (2022, PRIMARY, Tinbergen and later journal version) show repurchase and equity compensation timing is largely determined by the corporate calendar through blackout periods and earnings dates.
- Price support in a clean mandatory setting: Keswani, Yang, Young (2007, PRIMARY, UK) find average price declines when repurchasing firms enter London Stock Exchange mandatory non-trading periods, consistent with buybacks providing some support. The authors themselves note significance is moderate and largely confined to periods before interim results, casting doubt on tradable profits. That caveat is part of the evidence.
- Scale: S and P 500 buybacks were a record 942.5 billion dollars in 2024 (BigPic, citing S and P data, SINGLE SOURCE in this dive). The corporate bid is plausibly the largest single net demand source, so its pause is a legitimate structural question, which is why this territory deserved a dive at all.

Evidence against, and it is decisive:
- State Street Global Advisors (Bartolini and Kaplanian, via Alpha Architect, REPLICATION, 136 months, March 1994 to October 2018, S and P 500 Buyback Index of the 100 highest buyback ratio names): no significant return difference in any period measured; the authors reject the blackout decline hypothesis. O Shaughnessy Asset Management count as summarized by BigPic: annualized excess returns plus 3.2 percent in blackout versus plus 3.3 percent in open windows, a 0.1 point gap. Two independent managers, same answer: nothing there.
- The flow does not actually stop. JPMorgan (Kolanovic) estimate quoted in multiple sources: about 60 percent of buyback programs are not affected by blackouts because they run under Rule 10b5-1 plans adopted in advance. Rule mechanics confirm this: a 10b5-1 issuer plan fixes amount, price, and timing in advance and the broker executes through blackout without further company input (SEC Rule 10b5-1, PRIMARY regulation; the 2022 amendments added cooling off periods of 90 days for directors and officers and 30 days for other persons, and those periods expressly do not apply to issuers themselves per the SEC fact sheet summary in Proflex). A forced flow that continues at about 60 percent strength is not forced and is not removed.
- Confounding: the raw dollar correlation between buybacks and the S and P 500 level is 77 percent, but market cap normalized it is 5 percent (State Street). Buybacks and prices respond to the same earnings cycle. Blackout windows are earnings windows by construction, so any return pattern around them is earnings information until proven otherwise, and PEAD in large caps is already a binding dead result on this estate.
- Observability: actual repurchases are disclosed only quarterly in aggregate in 10-Q and 10-K filings. The SEC May 2023 daily disclosure rule was vacated by the Fifth Circuit on 2023-12-19, and reporting reverted to the prior standard (SEC and legal summaries, PRIMARY regulatory history). There is no timely, name level, daily buyback flow series to trade or even to test with. Blackout start dates are company policy and are not filed; any calendar is estimated from earnings dates, which imports look ahead and estimation error into every test.

Friction: even if a 0.1 to 0.3 percent seasonal sag existed, expressing it means shorting high buyback names into earnings, paying borrow, options spreads, and earnings gap risk, to harvest a pattern two large samples say is zero. The trade's dominant risk is the earnings event itself, not the calendar.

Breaking point: already broken. Reopen only if daily issuer repurchase disclosure becomes law and a point in time daily series exists. That is a regulatory event, not a research task.

## Territory 3: Cross-asset lead-lag with honest latency accounting

**Verdict: TEST (weekend crypto as a Monday volatility and downside conditioner only). Intraday lead-lag at retail latency: REFUSE.**

Claim, in three parts: (a) crypto leads equities intraday; (b) weekend crypto moves predict Monday equity opens; (c) futures lead cash by an exploitable margin.

Falsifier: if in regular hours the lead-lag asymmetry at one minute is zero within confidence intervals and flips sign across sample halves, intraday crypto lead is dead at any latency this estate can reach. If weekend effects exist only for variance and downside, and not as a symmetric directional return, the directional weekend claim is dead and only a risk conditioner remains. If futures lead cash by milliseconds, the futures claim is dead for any non co-located participant by physics, not by statistics.

Evidence for:
- Weekend downside asymmetry: Mourey, Shahrour, Soiman (2025, PRIMARY, Finance Research Letters, DOI 10.1016/j.frl.2025.108661) find negative weekend returns in 20 large cryptocurrencies (about 85 percent of market cap) significantly predict Monday equity declines across Nasdaq, Russell 2000, and S and P sector indices, while positive weekend returns have no effect, with a structural break strengthening after the May 2022 LUNA collapse. Song (2026, as cited in the Southeast Asia study below) separates SPY and QQQ opening versus intraday returns and finds the stronger association with overnight Bitcoin losses at the opening.
- Weekend volatility transmission: a 2017 to 2026 HAR study (arXiv 2610.35702, PRIMARY working paper) finds weekend crypto variance predicts Monday variance in the S and P 500, gold, and a Treasury ETF only post-2024: a one standard deviation increase in the weekend crypto factor is associated with about 28 percent higher fitted Monday variance for the S and P 500, 54 percent for gold, 27 percent for Treasuries, across all nine crypto to traditional pairs post-2024, and weak before 2024. Gu et al. (2026, as cited) report overnight crypto returns improve VIX forecasts.
- Weekend perp lead: a Binance 24/7 equity perp study, 2026-04-21 to 2026-11-07, 139 trading days, 21 perps (FollowSM, SINGLE SOURCE, code and public data stated, not peer reviewed) finds that on weekends the stock basket follows BTC by about one minute: correlation of BTC move with the basket move one minute later is 0.108 versus 0.003 the other way, holding in both sample halves. Daily close to close BTC to basket correlation is 0.51, R squared 0.26.
- Futures lead cash: Hasbrouck information share literature (PRIMARY, summarized in multiple sources) places most price discovery in E-mini futures, and the lead is measured in milliseconds in modern samples. Older literature cited leads up to 45 minutes; that era is gone. Electronic trading compressed the lag below bar resolution (Huth and Abergel 2014, as cited in the ultra high frequency lead-lag review).

Evidence against:
- Regular hours crypto lead is zero at one minute in the same perp study: contemporaneous correlation about 0.44 at lag zero, lead-lag asymmetry plus 0.008 with a 95 percent interval of minus 0.006 to plus 0.022, flipping sign between sample halves. Neither leads by a measurable minute when both markets are open. The weekend one minute lead explains about 1 percent of the next minute variance (0.108 squared is about 0.012). One percent of one minute variance, on thin weekend perp books (the basket trades 4 percent of its volume on weekends versus 54 percent in regular hours in the same study), is a measurement, not an edge.
- Southeast Asia check cuts the US story down to size: a 2020 to 2026 study of Vietnam, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia (Research Square preprint, PRIMARY working paper) finds a one standard deviation crypto downside increase associated with only minus 2.3 basis points on the Vietnam opening return and minus 14.9 basis points intraday, with the timing share differing by market. Effects are real, small, and market specific. Extrapolating a US Monday effect size from the Mourey headline without the opening versus intraday split would overstate it.
- MSTR class proxies: no study found in this dive isolates a tradable lead from BTC into MSTR, COIN, or IBIT beyond contemporaneous beta. These names are levered crypto beta at the open; the weekend move is in the Monday gap, which is the untradable part for anyone who cannot trade the weekend instrument itself. Could not verify any MSTR specific lead-lag study; see Could Not Verify.
- Futures to cash at retail latency: Aurora to New Jersey microwave transit is about 4.02 milliseconds one way and fiber about 7.3 to 7.8 milliseconds round trip (BlogBuckett microstructure post, SINGLE SOURCE for the exact figures, consistent with known geography). Co-located firms parse feeds in microseconds. This estate observes futures and cash through APIs with hundreds of milliseconds to seconds of delay. By the time a futures move is visible to this estate, cash has already moved. This is arbitraged away not by opinion but by speed of light plus colocation.

Friction and latency: the only window where this estate is not latency dominated is the weekend to Monday window, because the equity market is closed for about 65 hours and the signal can be read at leisure on Sunday. That is why the weekend conditioner is the sole survivor. Its tradable expression is options positioning on Monday (for example, demanding more premium, widening invalidation, or standing down on new long premium after a large weekend crypto decline), not a Monday open directional bet, because the gap itself impounds the signal at the first print and after hours spreads on the most liquid names run about 60 basis points versus about 5 basis points at the close in one JFE 2025 based count (Quantillions, SINGLE SOURCE).

Breaking point: the conditioner test dies if, controlling for Friday VIX, Friday return, and Sunday futures, weekend crypto downside adds nothing to Monday realized variance or to the Monday opening gap distribution in a sealed holdout. Pre-register the controls; without the Sunday futures control the test will rediscover futures, not crypto.

## Territory 4: Charm and vanna hedging flows, with the controls that killed naive GEX

**Verdict: REFUSE as standalone directional signals. TEST only in the controls grade design specified below. Any charm or vanna level product, dashboard, or wall is refused on arrival.**

Sign conventions, stated as required: vanna is dDelta/dIV; charm is dDelta/dTime (delta decay, sign depends on call versus put and on whether time is measured forward or to expiry, and every source below that does not state its convention is downgraded for it). The vendor convention behind most public numbers: dealers are assumed short puts and long calls on index options, so call gamma counts positive for dealers and put gamma counts negative (SquawkFlow explainer, VENDOR, honest about the assumption). Under that convention, the standard vanna story is: IV falls, put deltas shrink toward zero, dealers buy back underlying they had sold as hedge, price gets an upward tailwind; IV rises, the reverse. The standard charm story is: as expiry approaches, at the money deltas decay, dealers unwind hedges, and price is pulled toward the largest open interest strike into the close.

Claim: those forced flows are large enough to predict intraday direction (the afternoon lift, the closing magnet) and options traders should position with them (HermesGEX docs, VENDOR and MARKETING for the magnitude claims; SharkQuant buyer guide, VENDOR).

Falsifier: if, after controlling for VIX level and change and for ATM IV, a charm or vanna measure adds no incremental prediction of forward returns or realized volatility in a pre-registered test, or if measured net dealer hedging is under about 1 percent of underlying liquidity on typical days, the flow is too small to be the cause claimed and the signal claim is dead. This is the same falsifier structure that killed naive GEX in Dive Three, applied as instructed.

Evidence for:
- The Greeks are real mathematics. Delta does drift with IV and with time. No dispute.
- The positioning premise for index vol is proven once, with holdings data: Garleanu, Pedersen, Poteshman (2009, PRIMARY, Review of Financial Studies) used a unique identified dataset, 1996 to 2001, and showed end users are net long index options, especially out of the money puts (average daily net demand for index puts from non market makers of 124,345 contracts; net call supply of about 21,091 to 45,966 contracts depending on customer class, per the SquawkFlow reading of the same tables, VENDOR secondary). Dealers were therefore net short index vol. This is the one link in the chain shown with holdings rather than inferred from open interest.
- Dealer gamma does condition intraday dynamics in academic work: Barbon and Buraschi, Gamma Fragility (2021, PRIMARY working paper), find dealer gamma imbalance predicts intraday momentum when dealers are short gamma and reversal when long, stronger in less liquid underlyings. Baltussen et al. Territory 1 interaction points the same way. Dim, Eraker, Vilkov (as summarized in Cboe adjacent research roundups, secondary) find market maker net gamma in SPX 0DTE is positive on average and that positive gamma precedes lower intraday volatility and more reversals.
- Pinning is peer reviewed for single stocks: Ni, Pearson, Poteshman (2005, PRIMARY, Journal of Financial Economics) find closing prices cluster at strikes on expiration days, about 8.2 percent of expiration days versus under 6 percent on adjacent days, with an average effect of at least 16.5 basis points, traced to delta hedge rebalancing and proprietary positioning.

Evidence against:
- Size, from the exchange itself: Cboe, Evaluating the Market Impact of SPX 0DTE Options (PRIMARY exchange study, 2023, updated 2025) measures median net market maker gamma at about 170 million to 670 million dollars of delta per 1 percent move, which is 0.04 to 0.17 percent of daily S and P futures liquidity of about 400 billion dollars, with extremes around minus 5 billion to plus 7.7 billion dollars (about 1.3 to 1.9 percent) and no discernible market impact and no uptick in intraday gap moves. Amaya, Garcia-Ares, Pearson, Vasquez (2020 to 2023 Cboe data, as summarized) find market maker gamma adds at most 3.3 percentage points to annualized daily volatility. A flow that is typically one tenth of one percent of liquidity cannot dominate the tape. The JPMorgan stress scenario (30.5 billion dollars of 0DTE trading adding 20 points to a 5 percent five minute crash, March 2023, VENDOR bank research as reported) is a tail scenario, not a typical day claim, and Cboe data for examined selloff days show dealers were often long gamma and damping, not amplifying.
- The causal chain is unproven for vanna specifically: the best honest vendor adjacent summary found (Butterflow notes, VENDOR adjacent but explicitly skeptical) states it plainly: dealers being short vol is confirmed, but no study proves vol falls, therefore dealers buy, therefore the index rises. Correlation of IV declines with afternoon rallies is also the mechanical signature of rallies themselves (spot up, IV down is the leverage effect), so naive vanna regressions rediscover spot returns with an IV costume. That is why the VIX and ATM IV controls are not decoration; they are the test.
- Positioning is inferred, not observed, and the inference breaks for single names: open interest is published once daily and counts contracts, not owners. The short puts and long calls convention has strong support for index puts, weaker support for index calls, and is contradicted for single stocks in the same Garleanu et al. evidence (SquawkFlow audit of the convention, VENDOR, method transparent). Focus 30 names are single names. Applying index vanna logic to NVDA or MU chains imports a sign assumption that the primary evidence does not support there.
- 0DTE has dissolved the classic calendar: SPX 0DTE is about 59 percent of SPX option volume in 2025 and 66.2 percent in July 2026 (Cboe volume releases, PRIMARY). ## Territory 5: Energy and weather sensitivity for the estate energy names

**Verdict: REFUSE for FLNC, EOSE, FCEL, and BE equity and options. No test is commissioned. Weather remains a valid input only for natural gas and power instruments, which this book does not trade for these names.**

Claim: weather drives energy demand, energy demand drives prices, therefore weather forecasts and forecast revisions give a tradable lead on energy equities, in particular the estate names Fluence (FLNC, battery storage), Eos Energy (EOSE, zinc batteries), FuelCell Energy (FCEL, fuel cells), and Bloom Energy (BE, solid oxide fuel cells).

Falsifier: the claim needs three links, each separately falsifiable: (1) weather moves a priced energy commodity with a lead time longer than the market reaction time; (2) that commodity move transmits to these four companies' revenues or margins within a tradable window; (3) the equity does not already impound the public forecast. If link 2 fails by business model, the territory is refused regardless of link 1.

Evidence for link 1 (real, and not enough):
- Electricity cannot be stored at scale and must be generated on demand, so weather driven demand shocks move power and gas prices violently; day ahead versus real time spreads and spark spreads are the professional expression (practitioner explainer, SINGLE SOURCE). EIA Short Term Energy Outlook (PRIMARY, November 2026 edition) assumes heating degree days drive winter gas demand, publishes 10 percent colder and warmer side cases because weather is the largest forecast error source, and cut its November to March Henry Hub average forecast to 2.87 dollars per MMBtu, 16 percent below the prior outlook, on a mild weather assumption. On 2026-11-12 the December NYMEX contract settled up 3.9 percent at 3.340 dollars on colder forecasts against that mild outlook (market report, SINGLE SOURCE). Forecast revisions move gas within the day they publish. Professional gas traders trade the 6 to 10 and 8 to 14 day forecast cycle and the Thursday storage print; the lead time over other gas traders is hours, contested by dedicated weather desks with proprietary models.
- Weather derivatives exist precisely because temperature drives volumetric risk for utilities and retailers (academic review, PRIMARY secondary): over 80 percent of the weather derivative market is temperature linked. That proves hedging demand, not equity alpha.

Evidence against links 2 and 3, per name (the decisive part):
- FLNC (Fluence): revenue is battery storage systems, services, and software sold on project contracts (about 649.8 million dollars in Q3 2026 in one summary, SINGLE SOURCE financial press). Storage value rises with power price volatility over years of contracted deployments, not with next week's heating degree days. A cold snap does not change Fluence orders, backlog conversion, or margins inside any options horizon. No weather beta study for FLNC was found; see Could Not Verify.
- EOSE (Eos): a pre-scale zinc battery manufacturer whose price is driven by financing, manufacturing ramp, and order announcements. Weather is not a named revenue driver in any source found. Its volatility is idiosyncratic and financing led; any weather correlation in a short sample would be a beta costume.
- FCEL (FuelCell): revenue is project and service revenue on fuel cell platforms, driven by backlog, policy incentives, and execution. Fuel input is natural gas over contract terms, hedged and passed through in service agreements, not marked to daily Henry Hub inside a tradable window.
- BE (Bloom): product sales dominate (1.074 billion dollars of 1.236 billion dollars total revenue in Q3 2026, per a research summary, SINGLE SOURCE), driven by data center time to power demand, with grid interconnection queues of four to seven years cited as the catalyst. Bloom servers run on natural gas, biogas, or hydrogen as a continuous on site supply; the investment case is deployment speed against grid queues, the opposite of a spot weather trade. Severe weather is listed as a risk for utility comparators like NextEra (hurricane exposure in Florida), which is an operational disruption risk, not a forecast lead.
- The mood weather literature does not rescue the claim: Hirshleifer and Shumway (2003, PRIMARY, Journal of Finance) found morning sunshine associated with higher returns across 26 exchanges, 1982 to 1997 (annualized 24.8 percent on perfectly sunny New York days versus 8.7 percent on perfectly cloudy days). A 1950 to 2026 retest on the S and P 500 with within month ranking (Stochastic Parrot run 535, REPLICATION) finds a sunny minus cloudy gap of plus 2.73 basis points per day with a 95 percent interval of minus 0.71 to plus 6.17 (p 0.120), and plus 1.43 basis points since 2003 (p 0.69). Even the market wide mood effect cannot be confirmed on modern data. A four name energy subset claim starts far behind that.

Friction: these are among the widest spread, highest idiosyncratic volatility names in the Focus 30. Options spreads of 5 to 15 percent of premium are common in such names. A weather signal would need to predict multi percent equity moves to clear friction, and no mechanism above predicts even the sign of next month revenue impact, let alone next week price.

Breaking point: already broken at link 2. Reopen only with company disclosed, quantified weather sensitivity (for example, a utility with regulated volumetric revenue and a published degree day earnings bridge). None of the four names publishes one, because their models do not work that way.

## Territory 6: Retail odd-lot microstructure from real prints

**Verdict: TEST (odd lot quotes as a microstructure feature). REFUSE (odd lot trade counts as a retail sentiment or informed flow signal at this estate's size and data access).**

Claim, in two forms that the literature keeps separate and vendors keep merging: (a) odd lot quotes, orders under a round lot that sit inside the NBBO, contain information; (b) odd lot trades identify retail (or, in the older claim, informed) traders whose imbalance predicts returns.

Falsifier: for (a), if post-reform data show odd lot quote information is already in the consolidated feed and no longer predicts short horizon prices out of sample, the feature is dead. For (b), if odd lot marketable orders are not more informed than round or mixed lot orders once resting order artifacts are removed, and if retail identification accuracy is low against ground truth accounts, the signal claim is dead.

Evidence for (a):
- Bartlett, McCrary, O'Hara (2021 working paper; 2025 Review of Financial Studies version, PRIMARY): using virtually every message on 16 US exchanges, January to March 2021 (the MIDAS class data), they show a large inside market: for Amazon, a better odd lot bid existed 60 percent of the time (54 percent for the other high priced example in the excerpt). An XGBoost model using odd lot quotes predicts future prices and a simple strategy using them is profitable in sample. The SEC round lot redefinition reduces but does not eliminate the inside market.
- SEC DERA study (2026-08-12, PRIMARY regulator working paper): on 2025-11-03 the round lot fell from 100 to 40 shares for stocks priced above 250 dollars. The reduction produced minor market quality improvements for already liquid stocks and greater improvements for less liquid stocks and off exchange trades. This is a clean regression discontinuity style evaluation and it confirms the inside market was real enough for a rule change to shrink it.
- The rule path matters for any test start date: Market Data Infrastructure rules (December 2020, PRIMARY) set tiered round lots (100 shares at 250 dollars or less, 40 shares from 250.01 to 1,000 dollars, 10 shares from 1,000.01 to 10,000 dollars, 1 share above 10,000 dollars); the September 2024 Regulation NMS amendments accelerated round lot compliance to 2025-11-03 and odd lot information (including best odd lot orders, BOLO) dissemination to May 2026, with SIP operators requesting a two year exemption to May 2028 for depth of book odd lot quotes beyond the best level. Any odd lot study that pools data across November 2025 without a regime split is misspecified on its face.

Evidence for and against (b):
- The original informed claim: O'Hara, Yao, Ye (2014, PRIMARY, Journal of Finance) found odd lots were a median 24 percent of trades (60 percent or more in some stocks) and contributed 35 percent of price discovery, consistent with informed traders hiding in odd lots when odd lots were not reported to the tape. That reporting exemption ended in December 2013, removing the hiding motive that powered the result.
- The rebuttal: Upson and Johnson (2015, PRIMARY working paper, SSRN 2708881) show Nasdaq reports trades by resting limit order size, so about 50 percent of odd lot trades are fragments of larger marketable orders, and odd lot marketable orders are not more informed than round or mixed lot marketable orders. The informed odd lot was substantially a measurement artifact.
- Retail identification: Boehmer, Jones, Zhang, Zhang (2021, PRIMARY, Journal of Finance) identify marketable retail trades from subpenny price improvement in TAQ and find weekly retail imbalance predicts returns (about 10 basis points over the following week for net bought versus net sold names), with about half attributed to persistence and liquidity provision and the rest suggestively to information. The 2026 revisit (Ardia, Aymard, Cenesizoglu, Financial Markets and Portfolio Management, REPLICATION) finds predictive power weakens considerably in 2016 to 2021: large cap weekly prediction is gone and the long short strategy is no longer profitable under the original algorithm, with method sensitivity to the quote midpoint alternative. Barber, Lin, Odean (2024, PRIMARY) add ground truth: against 85,000 trades from six retail accounts (December 2021 to June 2022), the BJZZ approach accurately identifies only about 35 percent of trades and incorrectly signs about 28 percent of those identified, and they reconcile the paradox: retail imbalance positively predicts equal weighted returns but retail trades lose money, because purchases concentrate in attention grabbing stocks that underperform; extreme imbalance long short returns are minus 14.8 percent annualized among heavy retail stocks and plus 6.6 percent among other stocks.
- A conflicting post pandemic study (Jones, Tan, Zhang, Zhang 2026 version, PRIMARY working paper) finds retail flow predictability increased from pre pandemic (3.81 basis points per interquartile daily move) to post pandemic (6.69 basis points, May 2021 to December 2023). The honest summary is method and period sensitivity, not a stable effect: two competent teams, overlapping data, opposite trend conclusions, both hinging on an identification algorithm with 35 percent ground truth accuracy.
- Attention link: Kupfer and Schmidt (2021, PRIMARY) find abnormal Google search volume predicts odd lot trading share (plus 10 basis points of odd lot share per standard deviation at a 78 dollar mean price, with the sign flipping below 11 dollars). Odd lot share measures attention and wealth constraints at least as much as information.
- Fractional shares (Da, Fang, Lin 2025, PRIMARY, as summarized in the RFS big data review): brokers offering penny increment fractional trading since 2019 changed who trades high priced names in tiny sizes. An odd lot in NVDA in 2026 can be a fractional retail order, an algorithmic child order, or a resting order fragment. Three different traders, one print size.

Friction and size accounting: this estate trades options on Focus 30 names, in paper size that does not need odd lot equity prints for execution. The only use is as a feature. Quote level odd lot data requires proprietary direct feeds; the consolidated tape historically excluded odd lot quotes, and full BOLO depth is exempted to 2028 per the SIP request above. With SIP level data only, form (a) cannot be tested at the fidelity Bartlett et al. used. That access gap must be stated in any test registration, and a SIP only version that fails does not kill the proprietary version, and a SIP only version that passes is the only version this estate may use.

Breaking point: form (b) is already broken by Upson and Johnson plus the ground truth accuracy numbers. Form (a) dies for this estate if a SIP only BOLO feature adds nothing to short horizon price prediction on Focus 30 names in a post November 2025 sample. Do not pool across the reform date.

## Territory 7: Intraday clock effects across every timeframe the estate holds

**Verdict: TEST (one pre-registered clock atlas). ADOPT volatility and cost seasonality for execution scheduling at every timeframe. REFUSE a shelf of per timeframe return strategies.**

Claim: clock effects exist not only in the classic first and last half hour windows, but at every bar the estate holds: 1 minute, 5 minute, 15 minute, 1 hour, and daily, including within hour periodicities at 5, 15, and 60 minute boundaries.

Falsifier: an atlas that tests every bar position at every timeframe with a single multiple testing correction will produce apparent winners by construction. The claim survives only if specific bar positions, named in advance with a mechanism, beat the atlas wide correction and a time shifted placebo in a sealed holdout. Winners discovered by scanning the atlas do not count; they are the placebo distribution's upper tail.

Evidence:
- Within hour periodicity is documented in crypto and, by mechanism, plausible in equities: Andersen and Bollerslev tradition plus a 2026 arXiv periodicity study (PRIMARY working paper) on BTC and ETH across Coinbase, Binance, and Uniswap finds systematic volatility and volume patterns by day of week, hour of day, and within the hour, with discontinuities at the first second of each hour and at 15, 5, and (lesser) 1 minute boundaries, attributed to algorithmic scheduling and futures funding times, growing stronger over the years. In equities, the analogous schedulers are VWAP and TWAP engines, options expiry timestamps, index calculations, and the 09:30, 10:00, 14:00, 15:30, 15:45, and 16:00 institutional clock. The mechanism (machines trade on round clocks) is timeframe general.
- At 5 minutes: SPY volume concentration and the lunch trough (Territory 1) are 5 minute bar facts. Opening range facts are timeframe sensitive in the Zarattini and Aziz work: 5 and 15 minute opening ranges outperform 30 minute ranges in their sample, which is itself a warning that window choice is a fitted parameter.
- At 15 minutes and 1 hour: no primary study found in this dive isolates a return effect at 15 minute or hourly bar positions in US equities post-2020 that survives costs. European 5 minute work (Harju and Hussain 2006, PRIMARY) finds volatility seasonalities shift with US macro announcements at 14:30 CET and the US open at 15:30 CET, which is an event clock, not a free standing hourly return effect. This is a genuine evidence gap, recorded as such, not filled with practitioner lore.
- At daily bars: day of week is dead (Territory 1). Turn of month is the one calendar survivor mentioned in multiple secondary counts (first trading day strength persisting since 2000 in the JorgAI count, SINGLE SOURCE), but this dive found no post-2020 primary test of turn of month with costs, so it enters the atlas as a named candidate, not as a fact.
- Volatility seasonality at every timeframe is the robust layer: U shape within the day, higher volatility at bar boundaries, overnight variance dominating (about 75 percent of daily S and P 500 volatility occurs in the 6.5 trading hours in Hansen et al. as cited; overnight moves themselves are large relative to any 5 minute bar). For an options estate, this layer prices theta against realized movement by clock position: a long premium position opened at 09:30 faces a different realized path than the same position opened at 12:15, when 5 minute realized volatility runs 40 to 50 percent lower. That is an execution and holding cost fact, usable without any directional claim.

Friction accounting by timeframe: 1 minute bars carry the worst spread to signal ratio and the highest data error sensitivity; a 1 minute return seasonality under about 2 basis points is unmeasurable at this estate's data fidelity. 5 and 15 minute bars are the cheapest honest resolution. Hourly bars have only 6.5 observations per day, so an hourly atlas needs years to reach power and will be dominated by the open and close hours that Territory 1 already covers. Daily bars have the multiple testing problem in calendar costume (weekday, turn of month, day of month, pre holiday, post holiday, expiry week: each is a separate hypothesis family).

Trial count for the atlas, stated honestly: bar positions (78 five minute bars, or 26 fifteen minute bars, or 390 one minute bars) times 5 timeframes times long and short times a small set of conditioners quickly exceeds 1,000 hypotheses. At 1,000 tests the Bonferroni t bar is about 4.0. Any atlas result under t 4.0 is a lead for a named follow up registration, not a finding.

Breaking point: if the atlas, run once with the correction above, yields no pre-named bar position above the bar in the holdout, the clock return program closes at every timeframe together. Piecemeal resurrection timeframe by timeframe after a joint null is exactly the data mining this design exists to prevent.

## Territory 8: Overnight and intraday decomposition per sleeve family

**Verdict: TEST (one diagnostic decomposition of the estate's own ledger). REFUSE sizing or scheduling changes based on the decomposition until the diagnostic passes its falsifier.**

Claim: sleeve families differ systematically in where their returns accrue: momentum families earn overnight, mean reversion and other characteristic families earn intraday, beta earns overnight, and knowing a family's split lets the estate size it, schedule it, or express it in the session where its edge lives.

Falsifier: compute, from the estate's own paper ledger, each family's overnight share (close to open) and intraday share (open to close) of total return, with the split measured on the instruments actually held (including options, marked honestly). If family splits are unstable across halves of the sample, or if the split does not predict which session a family's forward returns arrive in, the decomposition is accounting, not a signal, and no sizing or scheduling use follows.

Evidence for the phenomenon (market level, primary):
- Lou, Polk, Skouras (2019, PRIMARY, Journal of Financial Economics, US 1993 to 2013 plus nine international markets): past overnight returns forecast future overnight returns and past intraday forecast future intraday, with persistence measurable at lags up to five years; momentum's abnormal return is almost entirely overnight (overnight three factor alpha 0.95 percent versus intraday 0.11 percent, as summarized by Alpha Architect), while the other characteristic strategies they examine earn primarily intraday. Their mechanism is clientele tug of war: open oriented and intraday oriented traders lean against each other persistently. Note their measurement subtlety, flagged in a careful reading note (trading-autoresearch note, secondary): overnight return is imputed as a residual from close to close and open to close, not measured directly, which matters for names with noisy opens.
- Hendershott, Livdan, Rosch (2020, PRIMARY, Journal of Financial Economics, US 1992 to 2016, international 1990 to 2014): the security market line is upward sloping overnight and downward sloping intraday; beta is priced at night and anti priced by day, in the US, internationally, and for industry, book to market, cash flow beta, and discount rate beta portfolios, with Treasury futures showing the same day night split. The flat 24 hour CAPM is two strong opposite relations cancelling.
- Cliff, Cooper, Gulen (2008, PRIMARY): index and ETF returns differ systematically between trading and non trading hours, the original night and day result.

Evidence against tradability (binding on this estate):
- Capture fails on costs, again: the Delphic Alpha trial (REPLICATION, 33 futures markets, 2008 to October 2026, settings frozen on pre-2020 data) finds an overnight only book earned 4.12 basis points per day before costs and paid 3.85 basis points in costs, leaving plus 0.2 basis points in 2020 to 2026; only 8 of 33 markets cleared their own breakeven cost, and the ten market book lost money in 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026. In stocks, the top momentum decile held overnight earned 12.5 basis points per day gross post-2020 against 10 basis points per day in costs at 5 basis points per side: a 2.5 basis point gross to net sliver that assumes institutional equity execution, before options spreads. This estate expresses momentum largely in options, where the overnight leg also carries the widest spreads of the day at both ends (close and open) and gap risk through earnings and macro prints.
- Options specific problem: an option's overnight return is not the underlying's overnight return. It bundles underlying gap, IV change (overnight IV typically falls into the open except on event nights, when it rises), and theta. A family that looks overnight earning in underlying terms may be earning IV crush or losing theta in option terms. The decomposition must be run on option marks, and prior dives' binding result (overnight premium about zero net) was measured on tradable instruments, which outranks any underlying only decomposition.
- No study found decomposes returns by anything resembling this estate's sleeve families (Saty derived, GPT sleeves, Tensor, earnings, premium selling, and the rest). The family level claim is therefore an extrapolation from characteristic sorted stock portfolios to a heterogeneous paper fleet. Extrapolation is a hypothesis, not evidence. That is precisely why the verdict is a diagnostic test on our own ledger and nothing more.

What the diagnostic is worth even if the signal use fails: it is a risk instrument. A family earning all its return overnight is short gap insurance by construction; a family earning intraday and losing overnight is paying for overnight holding it does not need, and in options terms is donating theta. The diagnostic also detects ledger errors (a family whose split flips when opens are measured directly versus imputed has a data problem, per the Lou et al. measurement note). Those uses justify one careful computation. They do not justify resizing.

Breaking point: if the split by family is not stable across sample halves at a rank correlation the registration names in advance, or if options mark decomposition cannot be computed without look ahead IV, stop. Report the decomposition as descriptive accounting with its instability stated, and take no scheduling action.


## Territory 9: Volatility term structure and skew dynamics as signals

**Verdict: TEST (Cremers and Weinbaum IV spread, cross sectional, weekly). REFUSE (IV percentile or rank transitions as a standalone signal; VIX backwardation as an automatic buy signal; skew level as a directional forecast).**

Claim, split into its four distinct sub claims, because the literature grades them differently and vendors merge them: (a) term slope: VIX to VIX3M ratio and single name term slope changes predict returns or regime; (b) skew dynamics: steepening or flattening of the 25 delta risk reversal predicts direction over 3 to 10 days; (c) IV spread: call IV minus matched put IV predicts the underlying cross sectionally; (d) IV percentile transitions: crossing into high or low percentile bands times premium selling or buying.

Falsifiers: (a) dies if backwardation episodes, traded as buys, do not beat matched non backwardation days net of costs out of sample, given backwardation coincides with drawdowns already in progress. (b) dies if skew changes lose significance once spot return and ATM IV change are controlled, because skew steepens mechanically when spot falls. (c) dies if the IV spread result does not survive the 2015 to 2026 regime split. (d) dies if high percentile entries do not outperform low percentile entries for the same defined risk structure net of bid and ask.

Evidence:
- (c) is the survivor. Cremers and Weinbaum (2010, PRIMARY, JFQA): stocks with relatively expensive calls versus matched puts earn about 50 basis points per week abnormal (0.51 percent weekly in the 1996 to 2005 hedge portfolio summary). The authors themselves note predictability decreases over their sample. The decisive modern evidence: a unified re-estimation on 12.36 million firm days, 10,026 underlyings, 2015 to 2026 (arXiv 2608.26115, PRIMARY working paper, REPLICATION of the canonical set) finds the Cremers and Weinbaum IV spread and a Bakshi style risk neutral skewness remain significant in every regime and specification (low vol 2015 to 2019, high vol transition 2020 to 2022, AI and mega cap concentration 2023 to 2026), while the Xing et al. (2010) smirk coefficient decays monotonically from minus 0.023 (t minus 5.5) to minus 0.006 (t minus 1.5, insignificant) and reverses at the three month horizon (plus 0.016, t plus 2.1), losing significance everywhere in the multivariate specification. In the same study, a boosted tree model beats linear signals only in the most recent regime (out of sample R squared plus 1.29 percent versus plus 0.07 percent) and none of the canonical hand built signals ranks in the top five features in any regime. Honest reading: one linear signal survives, magnitudes are not restated in tradable net terms, and feature importance has moved elsewhere.
- (b) skew as signal is mostly positioning description. The 25 delta risk reversal is persistently negative for equities since 1987 (Bates 2003, PRIMARY, jump risk pricing). Vendor claims that steepening leads downside by 3 to 10 trading days (Strike Watch, VENDOR, MARKETING for the lead claim) come with no sample, no controls, and no costs. The honest vendor adjacent line in the same literature: risk reversal levels have modest predictive content for short horizon returns, stronger for realized variance asymmetry, regime dependent, more reliable as a positioning gauge than a directional forecast (Options Analysis Suite documentation, VENDOR, unusually candid). Skew steepening into drawdowns in October 2008, March 2020, and August 2024 is contemporaneous with those drawdowns in every account found; a coincident gauge cannot be graded as a leading signal without a lead lag test controlling for spot, which no source in this dive performs.
- (a) term structure is a regime descriptor with a known trap. Contango is the state about 80 to 85 percent of the time since 2010 (Cboe based counts, PRIMARY exchange data as summarized). VIX to VIX3M above 1.0 coincides with major drawdowns (2008, 2015, 2018, 2020). Coincidence with drawdowns in progress is not prediction of their end; the same sources warn backwardation produced many false positives in multi week drawdowns and is not an automatic buy. Single name term slope has an event confound the index does not: a kink at the earnings expiry is the earnings implied move, already the subject of Dive Three, and must be stripped before any slope signal is computed.
- (d) percentile folklore fails its own test. A 35 year VIX study with an SPX put credit spread model (726 trades, 2009 to 2026, ETFap, SINGLE SOURCE, method stated, model backtest) finds the variance risk premium exists at every IV rank level (VIX above next month realized by about 3.4 to 4.1 points on average) but does not grow with rank, and the best credit spread results came at low to middle IV rank, with IV rank 50 to 75 trades averaging minus 107 dollars per spread in the excerpted table against better results at lower ranks. A quant transcript source (SINGLE SOURCE) reports the same inversion: selling in the top two IV percentile deciles was the only consistently negative segment. Tastytrade's own educational material (VENDOR) states IV rank does not predict direction or future volatility. When the vendor that popularized the gauge disclaims the signal claim, the signal claim is marketing residue.

Friction: cross sectional IV spread trading means weekly rebalancing across many single names in options or in stock against an options signal. The original studies do not charge options spreads; the tradable expression for this estate is stock direction conditioned on the spread, or defined risk options on the extreme deciles only, with earnings expiries stripped and with the spread computed from matched strike and expiry pairs, which requires clean chain data (Territory 10 grades what our archive can support).

Breaking point: the IV spread test dies if its weekly long short, computed on our archived chains with point in time discipline (open interest is next morning data; volume is same day data; do not mix the clocks), does not survive the 2023 to 2026 regime split that the re-estimation study used. A result that lives only pre-2020 is a museum piece under this estate's binding priors.

## Territory 10: Options flow mined from the estate's own archived chains

**Verdict: TEST (reproducible surface and volume variants only, honestly labelled). REFUSE any claim to replicate Pan and Poteshman signed opening flow from our archive. Grade of the archive: B for surface history, D for flow evidence.**

Claim: the estate's archived option chains are a historical flow corpus: volume, open interest changes, and surface shapes over time can be mined for the informed flow effects documented in the literature.

Falsifier and grading standard, written first: a flow corpus must identify, per trade, whether it opened or closed a position and which side initiated it. If the archive lacks opening trade flags and aggressor signing, then by the primary literature's own partition, the archive contains only the publicly observable component, whose predictive life the primary paper measures at one to two days, and any longer horizon flow claim built on it is refused without a test.

What the primary literature actually requires:
- Pan and Poteshman (2006, PRIMARY, Review of Financial Studies, Cboe data 1990 to 2001): put call ratios built from buyer initiated volume to open new positions predict returns: lowest quintile beats highest by over 40 basis points the next day and over 1 percent over the next week. Partitioning the signal into publicly and non publicly observable components, they find the predictability comes from the non public component (informed traders' private information), and publicly observable signals predict for only the next one or two trading days. The dataset is a unique Cboe file with investor class and open buy classification that standard OPRA and NBBO feeds cannot reproduce (confirmed in multiple secondary audits, including a 2026 pre-earnings research report that states the construction is NOT reproducible from NBBO and open interest and registers only reproducible variants).
- Johnson and So (2012, PRIMARY, Journal of Financial Economics): options to stock volume ratio predicts earnings news, low O/S beating high by about 0.34 percent per week. This variant needs only volumes, not signing, and is therefore reproducible from an archive with equity and options volume.
- Cremers and Weinbaum IV spread (Territory 9) needs only matched call and put IVs. Reproducible from chains with quotes.
- Xing smirk needs the IV surface. Reproducible, and already graded decaying in Territory 9.

What our archive can and cannot support, graded honestly:
- CAN: daily chain snapshots with NBBO, open interest, volume, and greeks support (i) IV surface history (term structure, skew, smirk, IV spread) with point in time discipline, (ii) volume based ratios (put call volume ratio, O/S ratio, volume to open interest spikes), (iii) open interest change proxies for opening activity (delta OI is a net, next morning quantity: it cannot separate opening from closing within the day, and a day with large opening and large closing volume shows a small delta OI; every delta OI proxy must carry that label), (iv) the SqueezeMetrics style convexity share G and the SpotGamma style open interest signed GEX, both already extracted in the morgue as measurements, not flow.
- CANNOT: buyer initiated opening volume (the Pan and Poteshman object), customer versus firm versus market maker origin (the Cboe Open-Close object), aggressor signing at trade level unless the archive stored trade prints with quote at trade time (the Firmtape construction in Territories 1 and 4 requires prints plus contemporaneous quotes; a chain snapshot archive does not contain it), and intraday flow sequencing (snapshots at hourly or daily cadence cannot order trades within the interval).
- Vendor flow products do not close this gap: Unusual Whales and FlowAlgo class alerts are broadcast versions of the public component, and no peer reviewed study of broadcast flow alerts was found in this dive or in the options strategy evidence review found in search (BranBer research notes, secondary, states NOT FOUND for peer reviewed broadcast alert studies). By the time an alert is public, the Pan and Poteshman partition says the remaining life is the shortest segment.
- Archive integrity caveats from house records: the estate's own AGENTS note records that the IV snapshot ledger has no duplicate guard and a prior double execution left duplicate date, symbol, strike keys, deduped by keeping the last occurrence. Any mining run must dedupe on that key and log it, and must treat snapshot cadence changes over the archive's life as regime breaks, because a flow proxy computed on hourly snapshots and the same proxy on daily snapshots are different statistics wearing one name.

Friction: none at mining time; all of it at expression time. The reproducible variants predict underlying returns, so expression is equity or defined risk options with Territory 9 friction. ## Territory 12: Cross-sectional interactions between sleeve families

**Verdict: TEST (a short, pre-registered, economically motivated pair list only). REFUSE brute force pair mining across the fleet.**

Claim: the fleet's own family signals can serve as each other's features: one family's signal confirms, vetoes, or sizes another family's trade, and the interaction carries information neither family has alone.

Falsifier and the multiple testing burden, stated honestly as instructed: enumerate the trial count before looking at any interaction result. Let F be the number of sleeve families. Pairs are F times (F minus 1) divided by 2. At F equals 12, pairs equal 66. At F equals 15, pairs equal 105. At F equals 20, pairs equal 190. Each pair can be tested in at least 3 roles (confirmation, veto, sizing conditioner), at 4 horizons, with 2 directions, and on at least 2 samples: 66 pairs times 3 times 4 times 2 equals 1,584 tests before any parameter is chosen. At 1,584 tests, the Bonferroni per test alpha is 0.05 divided by 1,584, about 3.2e-5, requiring a t statistic of about 4.2. Harvey, Liu, Zhu (2016, PRIMARY) set the profession wide bar at t 3.0 for 316 factors; a fleet internal search of this size sets its own bar higher, not lower. Hou, Xue, Zhang (2020, PRIMARY) replicated 452 anomalies under one consistent method and found the majority insignificant at 5 percent. The fleet is not exempt from the factor zoo because the factors wear sleeve names.

The clustered N problem, as instructed: sleeve family trades are not independent observations. Families share the same Focus 30 underlyings, the same sessions, and often the same macro days, so the effective sample size is the number of independent clusters (roughly, distinct session by regime blocks, or distinct underlying by week blocks), not the number of trades. A pair interaction computed over 5,000 trades that occurred on 400 sessions in 30 names has an effective N closer to the session or name count than to 5,000, and standard errors must be clustered at least by session and by underlying (two way clustering), with the admission exam's clustered N applied. Any interaction result reported with unclustered trade level standard errors is graded as unproven on arrival, however large its t statistic looks.

Evidence for interactions in principle:
- Conditioning genuinely works in the primary literature when the conditioner is a mechanism, not a search winner: Baltussen's intraday momentum exists only in the short gamma state (Territory 1); Barbon and Buraschi's gamma effect flips sign with dealer gamma sign (Territory 4); Barber, Lin, Odean's retail effect flips sign between heavy retail and other stocks (Territory 6). Sign flipping conditioners are real. They were each found from a theory of who is trading, then tested, in that order.
- Within this estate, the cheapest true interaction is definitional, not statistical: premium selling families and long premium families hold opposite vega and theta. One family's realized environment (realized versus implied, by name and week) is the other family's P and L driver. Using the fleet's own realized versus implied ledger as a shared conditioner is accounting reuse, not data mining, and needs no pair search.

Evidence against the brute force form:
- Every additional pair tested on the same ledger that produced the families' own track records is an in sample reuse: the families were themselves selected, calibrated, and kept alive using overlapping history, so pair interactions on that history inherit two layers of selection. The honest sample for pair tests is forward paper trading after registration, plus a sealed historical holdout that neither the families nor the pair search has touched. If no such holdout exists for a pair, the pair can only be tested forward, and its expected time to verdict is months, which must be stated at registration, not discovered later.
- Interaction effects are the first thing overfitting finds and the last thing that survives: a veto that improves a family's Sharpe in sample by removing its worst decile of trades will, out of sample, remove a random decile unless the veto variable has a mechanism. The registration must name the mechanism in one sentence (who is on the other side, what constraint binds, why the interaction sign should be what it is) or the pair is not admitted to the list.

Admitted pair list (the whole list; additions require a new registration): (1) premium selling families vetoed or sized by the fleet realized versus implied ledger in the same name (mechanism: same underlying, opposite vol exposure); (2) breakout and momentum families vetoed by scanner extremity at entry (Territory 11, Candidate 4; mechanism: late flow); (3) all directional families conditioned by the Territory 3 Monday crypto risk state (mechanism: weekend information arrival); (4) intraday families conditioned by the Territory 4 tape signed gamma state (mechanism: dealer hedging direction); (5) earnings families conditioned by the Territory 9 IV spread (mechanism: informed options positioning before events). Five pairs, three roles collapsed to the one role named per pair, one horizon per pair named at registration: 5 tests, Bonferroni bar t about 2.8, clustered as above. That is a testable program. The other 61 to 185 pairs are refused until these five report.

Breaking point: if the five admitted pairs produce zero survivors at the stated bar in forward plus holdout evidence, the interaction program closes rather than expanding the pair list. Expanding the search after a null on the motivated list is the definition of data mining.

**Verdict: REFUSE directional pre-event drift trading (FOMC drift is dead post-2016 by two independent routes). TEST event volatility scheduling only: sizing, entry timing, and premium posture around FOMC, CPI, and auctions as a risk calendar, with no directional claim.**

Falsifier: for drift, the claim dies if the post-2016 effect is zero or negative net of costs against matched non event control days. For vol scheduling, the claim is weaker and different: it dies only if event day realized vol and spread behaviour do not differ measurably from non event days, which no source in this dive asserts.

Evidence on FOMC drift, the flagship claim:
- Lucca and Moench (2015, PRIMARY, Journal of Finance): plus 49 basis points in the 24 hours before FOMC announcements, 1994 to 2011, about 80 percent of the annual equity premium, robust across countries and assets.
- Kurov, Wolfe, Gilbert (2021, PRIMARY, Finance Research Letters): the drift fell from 44.5 basis points (1994 to 2015) to 9.2 basis points (2016 to 2019), and is effectively zero on announcement days without a press conference; a Wilcoxon test rejects equality of the two regimes at 1 percent.
- Ripbacktest full rigor replication (REPLICATION, SPY 1994 to 2026, costs charged, matched random control days on month and weekday): plus 0.20 percent per trade overall after costs, but plus 0.457 percent in 2006 to 2015 and minus 0.010 percent since 2016 (85 trades, 2016 to October 2026). - Conflicting variant, flagged honestly: a Substack futures study (Delphic Alpha, SINGLE SOURCE) reports the 16:00 day before to 14:00 announcement day ES rule at plus 25.8 basis points per trade after 2 basis points costs for 2008 to 2021 (111 meetings, t 3.4) and plus 19.9 basis points from 2022 onward (39 meetings, t 2.2), but minus 15 basis points average over the last 12 months (8 meetings). The two routes disagree on the post-2022 sign. The resolution is window mechanics, not mystery: the Ripbacktest window includes the announcement hour and the close, where the modern loss sits; the Substack window exits before the announcement. A rule whose sign depends on exiting at 14:00 sharp, with 8 meetings in the last year averaging negative, is not an estate grade edge either way. Both sources agree the 1994 to 2015 form is gone.
- The announcement premium is real but it is compensation, not a calendar gift: Hu, Pan, Wang, Zhu (2022, PRIMARY) measure pre release overnight drift across releases (NFP plus 10.1 basis points, GDP plus 9.6, ISM plus 9.14, 1997 to 2019), and Ai, Bansal, Guo (2024, PRIMARY) estimate about 44 announcement days per year carry over 71 percent of aggregate equity risk compensation (1961 to 2023). No primary post-2020 CPI drift study was found in this dive. CPI day is a vol event for this book (8:30 release, pre open, gaps the cash open) and belongs in the scheduling layer. Evidence on treasury auctions: Krueger Algorithms pre-registered test (REPLICATION, 2014 to 2026 auction calendar, 914 auction days in the 2015 to 2022 search period): auctions do move yields in the expected direction in the first minutes (12:58 to 13:05 move plus 0.50 basis points, t 1.4), but the primary equity rule (long maturity auction, weakness z at least 1, exit at close) earned minus 2.94 basis points per trade (139 days, t minus 0.66) and did no better than randomly swapped directions (p 0.47). Evidence on OPEX and quad witching: single stock futures no longer trade in the US, so quad witching is now effectively triple witching (third Friday of March, June, September, December), with index futures, index options, and stock options expiring together. ## Territory 14: Adjacent field transfers, one level further

**Verdict: ADOPT market making accounting as the estate's execution standard (measurement, not a trade). TEST closing auction imbalance awareness for exits. REFUSE information cascades as a standalone signal; adopt herding extremity only as the Territory 11 and 12 veto candidate already registered.**

### 14a. Market making: inventory and adverse selection accounting as our execution standard

Transfer claim: a market maker's P and L decomposition is the correct accounting for any liquidity taking strategy: gross edge minus adverse selection minus inventory cost minus fees. Our sleeves currently grade on entry signal and exit price; the transfer grades every fill on what the market did immediately after the fill.

The canon, with provenance:
- Glosten and Milgrom (1985, PRIMARY): with competitive market makers and some informed traders, the bid ask spread exists purely from adverse selection; quotes are conditional expectations given order direction. The spread is the price of trading against someone who might know more.
- Kyle (1985, PRIMARY): price impact lambda, price equals prior value plus lambda times net order flow; informed traders camouflage in noise flow. Lambda is the measurable form of adverse selection for size.
- Avellaneda and Stoikov (2008, PRIMARY): optimal quoting balances inventory risk against spread capture around a reservation price adjusted for inventory and volatility. The transferable piece for a taker is the reservation price idea: every sleeve should know the price at which its own signal is worthless, and refuse fills beyond it, instead of chasing.
- Spread decomposition (Stoll 1978; Huang and Stoll 1997, PRIMARY): adverse selection is roughly 30 to 60 percent of the spread, inventory 10 to 30 percent, order processing 10 to 30 percent (educational summaries of the estimates, ranges vary by stock and era). The operational measures: effective spread (what we pay versus midpoint), realized spread (what remains after the midpoint moves, the liquidity provider's gross), and the gap between them, which is our adverse selection cost, measurable per fill from data we already record if we store midpoint at fill and midpoint 5 to 30 minutes later.
- Implementation shortfall (Perold 1988, PRIMARY concept): total execution cost is decision price versus final average execution, including delay and opportunity cost, not spread alone. For an options estate the honest unit is shortfall per contract against the arrival midpoint, reported per sleeve per month.
- Square root impact law (empirical consensus across the impact literature, PRIMARY reviews): impact scales with the square root of participation; at this estate's paper size impact is negligible in Focus 30 names, which is itself a finding: our execution problem is spread and adverse selection, not impact, and any sleeve whose backtest charges impact but not adverse selection has the costs backwards.

Why ADOPT and not TEST: this is a measurement standard, not a return claim; it cannot be falsified by a backtest because it makes no prediction. It is adopted the way double entry bookkeeping is adopted. Its first deliverable is a per sleeve execution grade: median effective spread paid, median adverse selection at 10 minutes, share of fills beyond the sleeve's reservation price. Sleeves with good signals and bad grades get execution fixes; sleeves whose signal only exists inside the spread get honest verdicts. The Jane Street style research note found in search (secondary, rivers-research opentrader note) makes the same point from the practitioner side: the transferable parts of a trading firm are the control layers (realized vol forecasting, imbalance features, session split execution, inventory style position management), not the edge.

### 14b. Auction theory: opening and closing auction design and our fills

Transfer claim: US equities concentrate price discovery into two designed auctions. The opening cross and closing cross publish imbalance information (NYSE and Nasdaq Net Order Imbalance Indicator and equivalent feeds) before the print, and the closing auction now carries index, ETF, and rebalance flow at a scale that moves closing prices, which are also option settlement and mark prices.

Evidence:
- Imbalance feeds are real, public by subscription, and published in the minutes before each cross (exchange documentation, PRIMARY). They are the one place in US market structure where order flow is officially previewed.
- Closing auction share of volume has grown structurally with passive and rebalance flow; month end pension rebalancing is estimated at 5 to 10 billion dollars of predictable closing flow (Etula, Rinne, Suominen, Vaittinen 2020, PRIMARY, as cited in secondary summaries). Predictable flow into a single print is the textbook setup for closing price pressure and next morning partial reversal, documented for index additions, rebalances, and expiry days in the index effect literature.
- Opening auction overshoot: one microstructure study found in this dive (Substack research post, SINGLE SOURCE, magnitudes unverified) reports gap downs of 1 percent or more opening a further 0.28 percent lower on average with partial reversal within 15 to 30 minutes, and gap ups fading 0.24 percent. The direction pattern (continuation at the print, partial reversal after) is consistent with auction mechanics on overnight information, but the magnitudes are single source and are not adopted; they define the test's expected shape only.
- Options relevance is direct: index option settlement and the marks our ledger carries are closing prices. A sleeve whose exit rule fires at 15:55 is trading into the most flow distorted print of the day on rebalance and expiry dates, and its fills and marks inherit that distortion.

TEST design: for exits only, compare realized exit quality (fill versus 16:00 print, and versus next session 10:00 price) for exits executed in the last 15 minutes on high imbalance days (month end, expiry, rebalance dates) versus matched normal days, using imbalance direction as the conditioner. If our exits systematically sell into buy imbalances or buy into sell imbalances at worse prices, the fix is scheduling (exit before 15:45 or into the cross deliberately), which is an execution rule, not a signal. Directional trading on imbalance itself is refused at this stage: the imbalance feed is the most watched data in the market at 15:50, and any naive directional edge there is competed by every closing desk.

### 14c. Information cascades and herding signatures

Transfer claim: cascade theory (Banerjee 1992; Bikhchandani, Hirshleifer, Welch 1992, PRIMARY, Journal of Political Economy) says agents rationally ignore private signals and imitate predecessors once public history dominates, producing herds that are strong and fragile: they form on little information and break on little information. The tradable signature is not the herd's direction but its extremity and fragility.

Evidence:
- Barber, Odean, Huang, Schwarz (2020, PRIMARY working paper, Robintrack data May 2018 to August 2020): extreme herding episodes in retail crowded names are followed by minus 4.7 percent abnormal returns over the following 20 days. The herd is a fade signal at its extreme, at a 20 day horizon, in the names retail crowds into.
- Barber, Lin, Odean (2024, PRIMARY): the same extremity logic with ground truth accounts (Territory 6): extreme imbalance long short earns minus 14.8 percent annualized in heavy retail names. Two independent routes, same sign: extremity fades.
- Baltussen, Da, Soebhag (2025, PRIMARY working paper): part of the end of day cross sectional reversal is attributed to attention driven retail buy the dip behaviour near the close and short seller risk management, which is cascade behaviour with named actors, already carried as a candidate in Territory 1.
- Fragility is the part with no tradable test found: cascade breaks (sudden reversals of crowded consensus on small news) are identifiable after the fact in every account and predictable in none found in this dive. A signal that requires recognizing the break in real time is a latency and judgment product, not a backtestable rule at this estate's fidelity.

REFUSE rationale: as a standalone signal, herding requires real time retail flow identification, which Territory 6 grades at 35 percent accuracy against ground truth. As a veto and conditioner on our own entries (Territories 11 and 12), extremity needs only daily proxies (volume, attention, scanner style concentration) and a 20 day fade prior from two primary sources. The transfer therefore lands as a conditioner, not a strategy, and it is already registered there; it does not get a second, standalone registration here.

Breaking point for the territory: the execution accounting adoption is permanent unless a better standard replaces it. The auction test dies if exit quality shows no imbalance conditioning over one quarter of paper exits. The cascade refusal stands until retail flow identification accuracy against ground truth improves materially in a published audit.
