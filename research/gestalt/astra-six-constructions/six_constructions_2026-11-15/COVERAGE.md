# Save scope and evidence boundaries

The enumerated local six-construction source set contains **14 files**. All are copied byte-for-byte under `original/`; `provenance/source_inventory.json` records their names, sizes and SHA256 values. This includes the original concise and detailed reports, original prototypes/tests, initial and amended configurations/results, event paths, logs, fractional comparison and conditional payoff examples.

The build additions include all six implementation specifications, a machine-readable registry, data dictionary, research/decision records, a portable historical runner, standard-library reference functions, tests, worked fixtures, exact runtime pins and verification utilities. The available historical inputs include **799 files** archived into four size-bounded parts, with an inner file manifest and retained source retrieval manifests. Missing months are represented in those source manifests, not silently described as present.

The source snapshots include the equity evidence archive, four historical research dependencies, the selected parent event file, the earlier recovery report and earlier payload manifest. They preserve the provenance needed for this handoff. Earlier corpus inventories, research registers, Decisions requests/results and other work remain in the immutable prior research commits linked in PRIOR_RESEARCH.md and in the new branches' ancestry.

This save's claim is completeness of the enumerated six-construction work and its implementation handoff. Prior lifetime archives and original-host backlog have their own recorded coverage boundaries. This package does not relabel them as fully inspected or reconstruct unavailable historical conversations. The original reports and the decision record preserve the user-facing analysis and rationale available for this work.

No secrets, credentials, sealed material or unrelated personal account screenshots are included. The manifest enumerates exactly what was published, so 'saved' can be checked against bytes rather than a narrative count. Original local copies remain available after publishing.

All economic evidence stages are explicit. C1 is selected historical exploration. C5 is a conditional finite model with supplied facts/prices. C2/C3/C4/C6 remain designs with reference calculations. The handoff's new software tests and replay do not elevate those evidence stages.
