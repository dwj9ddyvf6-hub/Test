## Headlines

Territory 1 is resolved. Both earnings primaries were found. The 9,622 event straddle study is not a lost academic paper. It is the IVolatility post itself, a vendor primary with full method disclosed and real bid and ask pricing, whose short side is far more robust than its long side. The gated calendar study is not a paper at all. It is a YouTube video. Its exact gates are now pinned, and the second summary that disagreed is traced to a GitHub wiki paraphrase that loosened both thresholds.

Territory 2 finds no new free lunch. The best post 2020 numbers are small, gross, or both: index closing auction dislocations of 9 to 30 basis points, an end of day reversal of about 3 basis points gross, and a capacity framework in which more than 60 percent of anomalies earn under 1,000 dollars a day. The borrow fee anomaly largely disappears when shorts are charged the marginal fee a new borrower actually pays.

Territory 3 finds four transfers worth building and four breaking points worth respecting. Closing line value as a grading standard, extremized aggregation with an overlap check, node locked opponent modeling, and queue aware passive execution all transfer. Each breaks in a named place, detailed below.

Territory 4 grades the shelf. Gamma exposure survives only as a volatility regime descriptor and fails as an independent signal once VIX and ATM implied vol are controlled. LLM return prediction fails memory controlled tests. The overnight premium is real gross and approximately zero net at retail costs in two independent post 2020 trials. Retail flow and term structure carry trades survive only in narrow, conditional, defined risk forms already adopted in Dive One.

## Territory 1: The Earnings Calendar Primary Hunt

### 1.1 The 9,622 event straddle result: primary found, and it is the vendor post itself

Claim as carried from Dive One. Across 9,622 S and P 500 earnings events, 2021 to 2026, long at the money straddles entered 9 days to expiry and exited the next session lost 18.5 percent per trade on average with a 22 percent win rate. Sorted by a synthetic edge proxy, implied move minus realized absolute move, the most underpriced quintile earned plus 36.7 percent with an 80 percent win rate and the most overpriced quintile lost 44.5 percent with a 0.3 percent win rate. Spearman correlation between proxy and real profit and loss was minus 0.73.

Evidence from the primary, opened in full. Source: IVolatility, Does IVolAI Earnings Edge Actually Pay, at ivolatility.com/news/3147, read 2026-11-14, PRIMARY OPENED, VENDOR flag: IVolatility sells the API and the Earnings Calendar dashboard the study validates. Sample: 497 symbols, 9,651 synthetic events and 9,622 successful real round trips, 2021-02-24 to 2026-02-23. Method is fully disclosed, which raises it above most vendor work: entry cost is call ask plus put ask, exit value is call bid plus put bid, on the real chain fetched per event, strike nearest spot, expiry nearest 9 DTE within a 4 to 14 DTE window widened if needed. No commissions or market impact slippage are added. The short side profit and loss is stated as approximately minus long profit and loss minus 5 to 10 percentage points of extra slippage, not measured directly. Headline numbers confirm exactly as carried: mean long profit and loss minus 18.47 percent, median minus 26.45 percent, win rate 22.0 percent, mean IV crush plus 15.64 points, median crush plus 9.89 points.

Three findings inside the primary change how the headline should be used, and none was in the Dive One summary.

First, the two sides are not symmetric. Split at edge equal to zero on 9,551 joined events: edge positive, N 5,674, mean long profit and loss minus 38.97 percent, long win rate 0.9 percent. Edge negative, N 3,877, mean long profit and loss plus 11.67 percent, long win rate 53.0 percent. At quintile level the asymmetry is starker. Q1 most underpriced, median edge minus 4.83 points, mean long plus 36.68 percent, win rate 80.0 percent. Q2, median edge minus 1.11 points, mean long already minus 12.28 percent, win rate 27.1 percent. Q3, median edge plus 0.83 points, mean long minus 31.71 percent, win rate 2.7 percent. Q4 mean minus 40.34 percent, win rate 0.2 percent. Q5 most overpriced, median edge plus 3.82 points, mean minus 44.46 percent, win rate 0.3 percent. In plain terms: only the extreme underpriced quintile is longable. Everything from Q2 upward is a short vol ranking. The dashboard FAIR band of plus or minus 1 point is, by the vendor own numbers, mislabeled: events inside it still lose about 30 percent as long straddles. The vendor itself recommends narrowing FAIR to plus or minus 0.3 points and requiring edge at or below minus 1 point for any long label.

Second, the long side fails at symbol level in a way the quintile hides. Of the 15 most underpriced symbols by synthetic edge, only 9 confirm with positive real long profit and loss. The 6 failures, AKAM, CIEN, GEN, CVNA, DECK, LULU, all carry mean IV crush of plus 20 to plus 57 points. The proxy was right that realized moves were large; the crush ate the return anyway. The vendor own reconciliation rule follows: an underpriced flag with historical mean crush above plus 30 points should be graded AVOID for long straddles, not LONG. The short side has no such failure in the sample: all 15 most overpriced symbols confirm with negative long profit and loss, and the 15 name zero win cluster, mostly regulated utilities, REITs, and media and data names including UDR, REG, LNT, NI, ATO, EVRG, FRT, ES, NWS, NWSA, CPT, FOX, TECH, JKHY, VLTO, shows 0 percent long win rate over 20 or more events each, with mean long losses of minus 47 to minus 72 percent. Note the caveat inside that strength: a near 100 percent historical win rate on the short side is a statement about long losses at bid and ask. It is not a measured short profit and loss net of assignment, borrow of attention, and the 5 to 10 point slippage the vendor estimates but does not model. One tail event outside the sample can repay many small wins. Defined risk form is still required.

Third, the edge decayed and then partly returned, which matters for any forward gate. Synthetic edge by year: 2021 plus 1.26 points with 68.3 percent overpriced, 2022 plus 1.35 points, 2023 plus 0.59 points, 2024 plus 0.16 points with only 51.5 percent overpriced, 2025 plus 0.87 points, 2026 Q1 only plus 0.39 points on 408 events. Real long profit and loss by year: 2021 minus 21.24 percent, 2022 minus 18.53 percent, 2023 minus 12.65 percent, the best year, 2024 minus 16.18 percent, 2025 minus 24.00 percent, the worst year, with mean crush rising to plus 19.30 points. The vendor attributes the 2025 re widening to the April 2025 tariff shock regime and flags it as unexplained. Also new: AMC events lose more than BMO events in real profit and loss, minus 19.68 percent versus minus 17.55 percent, because overnight crush is larger, plus 18.54 points versus plus 13.43 points, even though synthetic edge is identical at about plus 0.84 points. Sizing, not direction, should differ by timing. One data artifact is disclosed and must be excluded from any reuse: FISV at median edge minus 23.98 points reflects the Fiserv to FI rebrand and corporate actions, not a tradeable pattern.

Provenance and flags. Single vendor primary, no independent replication of this exact 9,622 event run was found. Method disclosure is unusually complete for vendor work, including the 1.4 percent of events that failed to produce a clean chain, the TNS timing default to AMC affecting about 0.4 percent of cases, and the absence of commissions. Confidence grade: HIGH for the ranking claim, that the synthetic edge sorts long straddle outcomes, because it is measured on real bid and ask with a monotone quintile ladder and a stated mechanism. MODERATE for the short side magnitude, because short profit and loss was not directly simulated. LOW for the long side beyond Q1, because Q2 is already negative and symbol level confirmation is only 9 of 15 even inside Q1 names. Usable confidence for this estate: use the proxy as a short vol screener and as a veto on long straddles, not as a long straddle entry signal except in the extreme quintile with a crush filter.

Falsifier or forward test. Forward over two earnings seasons, log synthetic edge, quintile, historical mean crush, BMO or AMC, and real bid and ask round trip for every S and P 500 and Focus 30 earnings event, with no trading required for the test itself. The claim is falsified forward if the Q5 minus Q1 spread in long profit and loss compresses below 20 points, if Q1 win rate falls below 60 percent, or if the short side defined risk twin, iron condor or put and call credit spreads at equal max risk, fails to beat the middle quintiles net of full spread cost. The crush filter is falsified if underpriced names with crush above 30 points perform no worse than underpriced names below that line.

Transfer verdict. ADOPT as a screener, with the vendor own corrections applied: FAIR band narrowed, long label only at edge at or below minus 2.3 points approximately, the Q1 boundary in sample, plus historical crush at or below 30 points, short candidates drawn from Q4 and Q5 in defined risk form only, AMC sized with the larger crush in mind. Do not import the per symbol shortlist as permanent. It is a 2021 to 2026 sample property and must re earn its place forward.

### 1.2 The gated long calendar result: primary found, and it is a YouTube video

Claim as carried from Dive One. A study of about 72,500 earnings events across about 4,500 stocks, 2007 to 2024, found unfiltered short straddle and long calendar returns near zero, and filtered returns of short straddle plus 9.0 percent per trade at 64 percent win rate and long calendar plus 7.3 percent at 66 percent win rate, behind three gates: term structure slope as the top filter, an IV to RV gate, and a volume gate. Two secondary summaries disagreed on the gate constants.

Evidence: the primary is a video, not a paper. Source chain, all read 2026-11-14. The origin is the Volatility Vibes YouTube video titled This Option Strategy Turned 10k Into 1 Million In One Year. The most faithful codification is the GitHub specification in walkerhughes tastytrade-mcp, docs earnings-calendar-strategy.md, PRIMARY OPENED, which states plainly that it is a faithful specification from the video public auto captions, not an endorsement of its return claims, and audits the linked community implementation. Timestamped claims from the video as codified: about 72,500 events across 4,500 stocks since 2007 at 06:08; candidate predictors term slope and ratio, 30 day average volume, IV30 RV30 at 06:26; final model uses three factors at 09:54; filter leaves about 10 percent of calendar events and raises mean calendar return to 7.3 percent with 28 percent standard deviation at 10:53; full Kelly presented as 60 percent for calendars and explicitly rejected as ruinous at 12:05, with the presenter using one tenth of it, a 6 percent debit allocation, as an illustration at 15:40; decision rule at 17:39, all three filters passing is recommended, term slope plus exactly one other is consider, all else avoid. Trade form: sell the first expiry after earnings, buy the same strike about 30 days farther out, enter about 15 minutes before the close before the announcement, exit about 15 minutes after the next open. A next close exit was tested and rejected in the video because post earnings drift worsened results. Short straddle filtered mean is reported in secondary coverage as plus 9.0 percent with 48 percent standard deviation; the specification itself centers the calendar numbers.

Exact gates, resolved. From the specification published defaults, corroborated independently by two code implementations found in this dive, the Acelogic Earnings Volatility Calculator on GitHub and the QuantConnect implementation credited to Volatility Vibes, both INDEX:

- Minimum average volume: 1,500,000 shares per day, mean over the latest 30 sessions.
- Minimum IV30 RV30: 1.25, where IV30 is at the money IV interpolated at 30 DTE and RV30 is annualized 30 session Yang Zhang volatility.
- Maximum term structure slope: minus 0.00406, in decimal IV per calendar day, defined as slope equals IV45 minus IV front, divided by 45 minus DTE front. A more negative slope means stronger front backwardation. IV45 must be interpolated from bracketing expirations.

The disagreement is resolved, and its source is identified. The second summary, Version B in Dive One, with volume at or above 500k and IV RV above 1.5, is the GitHub wiki chapter IV and Corporate Events Reporting in WilliamXXu quantitative_finance, cli_agent IVwiki chapter 12, PRIMARY OPENED in this dive. That chapter reports the same 72,500 events, the same filtered means, 9.0 percent and 7.3 percent, and the same win rates, 64 and 66 percent, but lists the filters in looser paraphrase: term slope described as front month IV minus back month IV with a note that negative values indicate favorable conditions, which reverses the sign convention of the specification formula while pointing at the same economic condition; IV RV values above 1.5 indicate overpriced volatility; volume minimum 500k shares per day. No source for the 1.5 or 500k constants is given in the chapter, and no other implementation uses them. Every code artifact that actually implements the screen, the specification audit, the Acelogic calculator, the QuantConnect algorithm with thresholds volume 1500000, IV RV ratio 1.25, term slope minus 0.00406, and a further scanner implementation in franciscoa19 stonkmonitor with the identical triple, uses Version A. Verdict: Version A is the video model. Version B is a lossy paraphrase, likely rounded from decile discussion in the video into hard thresholds. Anyone who backtested Version B tested a different, looser strategy that admits roughly three times the volume universe and demands a materially richer IV RV reading.

Limits of the evidence, stated by the specification itself and adopted here. The video publishes no event level dataset, no train test split, no delisting treatment, no exact slippage model, no threshold selection procedure, and no out of sample results. Its mean returns, Kelly fraction, and growth path, including the title claim of 10k to 1 million in one year, cannot be independently reproduced from the materials provided. The specification also documents two implementation traps in the linked community resource that would corrupt a naive replication: IV45 was fetched only from expirations below 45 DTE and then silently clamped to the last point, distorting slope; and realized vol labeled close to close variance as its open to close Yang Zhang component, while the 1.25 threshold was published without data to recalibrate after correcting that error. That combination remains unvalidated even in the faithful implementation.

Independent and negative evidence found in this dive. Three items, all INDEX unless noted.

First, an independent QuantConnect backtest of this exact strategy, author u shock_and_awful on Reddit, creator Volatility Vibes, using the Version A thresholds verbatim, over 2024-06-01 to 2024-11-03, produced 77 orders, average win 0.21 percent, average loss minus 0.25 percent, expectancy minus 0.165, compounding annual return minus 1.275 percent, net profit minus 0.544 percent on a 1,000,000 dollar paper account, estimated capacity 260,000 dollars, lowest capacity asset PG. The window is short, five months, and one implementation, so it does not refute a 17 year claim. It is, however, the only independent executable replication located, and it is negative. It belongs in the record at full weight as a warning about implementation sensitivity.

Second, ORATS published a genuinely independent earnings backtest, orats.com blog Earnings Options Strategies Backtest, published 2021-10-12, INDEX, VENDOR flag, ORATS sells options data. Sample: 5,217 earnings announcements, 20,868 trades, four structures, buy and sell straddles and buy and sell calendars, entered the day before and exited the day after, selected by a forecast theoretical value ratio rather than the Vibes gates. In sample January 2020 to July 2021, out of sample July 2021 to October 2021. Out of sample, the long calendar was the best performing strategy, which directionally corroborates the calendar over the straddle. But the magnitudes are not comparable and must not be blended: ORATS reports returns on capital at risk, buy calendar 0.90 percent in sample on 205 trades and 0.91 percent out of sample on only 54 trades, sell straddle 1.18 percent in sample on 214 trades and 0.65 percent out of sample on 54 trades, buy straddle 0.40 percent in sample and 0.65 percent out of sample on only 13 trades. The Vibes 7.3 percent is a mean return on calendar debit. Different denominator, different selection rule, different window. The honest joint statement is narrow: two independent vendor exercises both rank the long calendar first among the four naive structures in their windows. Neither establishes 7.3 percent per trade as an expectation.

Third, the academic anchor cuts both ways and is routinely misquoted. Gao, Xing, and Zhang, Anticipating Uncertainty: Straddles Around Earnings Announcements, Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis 2018, working paper November 2017, sample 1996 to 2013, INDEX via the Tsinghua hosted full text excerpt and multiple secondary briefs. The famous plus 3.34 percent is the return on delta neutral at the money straddles from three days before the announcement to the announcement date. It is a pre earnings run up result: uncertainty is underpriced before the print. It is not a through earnings hold result, and the paper entries are T minus 3 and T minus 1 only, never T minus 5 to T minus 7. A Bocconi Students Investment Club replication on the S and P 500, 2011 to 2021, INDEX, finds straddling into and through earnings delivers consistently negative returns, in contrast to the earlier literature, consistent with the IVolatility aggregate above. A small practitioner replication located in this dive, 117 SEC dated prints on 12 names, 2024 to 2026, real expired option bars, GitHub Murathanx12 investing-bot-test, INDEX, reports a pre print IV ramp long straddle variant refuted hard, T minus 5 mean minus 19.5 percent, t minus 12.9, T minus 3 mean minus 10.1 percent, and an event calendar variant, short front through the print and long back at 21 or more days, at n 76, mean plus 5.5 percent of debit, median minus 7.4 percent, hit rate 49 percent, t 0.77, with the steep term structure tercile at plus 14.3 percent versus flat at minus 8.9 percent. Sample is tiny and single source, flagged as such, but its shape matches the gated hypothesis exactly: ungated calendar near zero with a negative median, gated by slope materially better. Separately, de Silva, So, and Smith, as summarized in a GitHub evidence review, INDEX, find short at the money straddles earn 7 percent in low expected announcement vol names to 18 percent in high names on announcement day at mid prices, with an 11 point high minus low gap at t 19.4. Mid prices, not bid and ask. The direction supports filtered short vol; the magnitude will not survive spread crossing intact, and Dive One cost rule applies.

A distinct claim that must not be conflated. A second video transcript located in this dive, The Earnings IV Ramp Is a Lie, 21,000 plus straddle backtest, INDEX, tests a different trade: buy the straddle about 14 days before earnings and exit before the announcement, never holding through the gap, on liquid names from 2009, about 21,500 trades, with commissions and slippage modeled. Unfiltered median minus 6.3 percent, mean plus 0.3 percent, win rate about 37 percent. A four signal regression filter is claimed to turn it profitable out of sample, with coefficients paywalled into a vendor product, Oakquans spelling varies in transcript. Grade: the unfiltered numbers are usable as a base rate for the pre earnings hold. The filtered claim is UNTESTABLE as published because the model is the product.

Provenance and confidence grade for the calendar claim. Primary is a monetized YouTube video with an extraordinary title claim, no dataset, and no out of sample split. That is the weakest primary form this estate accepts as a lead, and it caps confidence regardless of how many GitHub repositories repeat the numbers, because repetition of a paraphrase is not replication. Confidence: MODERATE for the gate idea, that term slope first, then IV RV, then liquidity, sorts earnings short vol outcomes, because it is corroborated in direction by ORATS ranking, by the tiny calendar replication slope tercile, and by the IVolatility term structure logic. LOW for the constants as precise optima, because they were selected on the same sample they are reported on, by an undisclosed procedure. LOW for 7.3 percent and 9.0 percent as forward expectations. Treat them as in sample filtered means on an unknown cost model.

Falsifier or forward test. Pre register Version A exactly as specified above, Yang Zhang RV, bracketed IV interpolation, no clamping, entry 15 minutes before the prior close, exit 15 minutes after the next open, long calendar with about a 30 day gap and a defined risk short straddle twin replaced in this estate by an iron condor twin at equal max risk. Run gated and ungated twins together for two full earnings seasons, minimum 100 gated events. The gate hypothesis is falsified if gated expectancy net of entry at ask and exit at bid on every leg is at or below the ungated twin, or if the slope tercile ordering inverts. The constants are falsified as optima if a pre registered neighborhood, volume 1.0M to 2.0M, IV RV 1.15 to 1.40, slope minus 0.003 to minus 0.005, shows a spike only at the exact published triple, the plateau rule from Dive One. Log the Version B triple as a third arm for one season only, to settle empirically whether the looser wiki gates dilute or improve the result, then retire the loser.

Transfer verdict. TEST, do not adopt. Build the gated calendar as a twin tested sleeve with Version A gates, defined risk only, sized as an experiment. The gate concept earns a build. The video return claims earn nothing until the forward twin pays for them.

## Territory 2: Capacity Orphan Effects With Friction Accounting

Grading rule for this territory, from Dive Two and applied without exception: an effect is quoted gross and net separately, capacity is stated as dollars per day or per event where a source supports it, and turnover above 50 percent per month is presumed dead net of costs until proven otherwise on signal weighted spreads, meaning spreads measured on the names the signal actually picks.

### 2.1 The capacity ceiling itself: most anomalies are worth under 1,000 dollars a day

Claim. Statistical predictability does not imply dollar profitability, because predictability concentrates in illiquid names with little tradable volume.

Evidence. Bottom Up Capacity Constraints and the Limits of Anomaly Profitability, conference paper, INDEX, read 2026-11-14. Framework: trade size modeled from each stock average daily volume, profitability measured in dollar terms, applied to 126 cross sectional predictors. Findings: capacity constraints alone, at zero trading costs, materially reduce profitability. More than 60 percent of single anomaly strategies generate less than 1,000 dollars in daily dollar return out of sample. Only 24 of 126 predictors are significant out of sample once capacity constrained. Fewer than half are significant even in their original in sample periods under the framework. Costs reduce returns further, but capacity is the primary limiter, ahead of costs. This updates and hardens the Dive Two summary of the same work.

Provenance and flags. Single unrefereed conference paper, method dependent, flagged. Its qualitative result is corroborated by Hou, Xue, and Zhang majority failure under one consistent method and by the Novy Marx and Velikov turnover gate, both carried from prior dives.

Falsifier or forward test. For any anomaly this estate papers, report dollar profit at a stated participation cap, for example 1 percent and 5 percent of ADV per name, alongside percent return. A sleeve whose dollar profit at 5 percent participation is under 1,000 dollars a day at target size is not a sleeve, it is a study. Kill it or merge it as a filter.

Transfer verdict. ADOPT as accounting. Every capacity orphan below is graded against this ceiling first.

### 2.2 Index addition and deletion: the run up is real, the close dislocation is 9 to 30 basis points

Claim. Additions rise and deletions fall into reconstitution, then reverse. The harvestable question is which segment, front running, the closing auction dislocation, or post event reversal, survives costs at small size.

Evidence. Dimensional, Measuring the Costs of Index Reconstitution: A Global Perspective, published 2025-03-19, updated 2026, INDEX, read 2026-11-14, VENDOR adjacent flag: Dimensional sells flexible index implementation, so its incentive is to show rigid indexing is costly, but the data work is index provider scale and the numbers are checkable. Sample: 10 US indices plus 5 international, 2014 to 2023. Average excess return to additions and deletions combined: plus 3.9 percent over the 20 days leading into reconstitution, reversal of 4.4 percent over the 20 days after. That 20 day segment is the crowded segment, competed by every front runner. The intraday segment is new in this dive and much smaller. Using high frequency data 2019 to 2023 on US reconstitution days: additions rise on average 9 basis points from the end of continuous trading at 4 pm into the closing auction relative to non rebalanced stocks, and reverse by 13 basis points by the next open. Deletions fall 30 basis points into the close and reverse by 63 basis points by the next open. A companion Dimensional small cap study, April 2026, INDEX, puts total reconstitution price impact at 5 to 30 basis points per year across the Russell 2000, S and P SmallCap 600, and CRSP US Small Cap indices, affecting about 25 percent of each index per year, with reconstitution day volume multiples of 21.3 times for Russell 2000 additions and deletions and closing auction volume concentration of 119 times the prior month average in the final window for Russell names. BMLL market insight on the June 2024 Russell reconstitution, INDEX, VENDOR, data vendor, confirms the scale: about 275 billion dollars traded on NYSE and 103 billion on Nasdaq in the final moments on June 28, 2024, with over 34 percent of daily notional in major additions executed in the closing auction, and dislocation between final mid and auction price spiking on the day. A theory paper, Tasitsiomi, On the Hidden Costs of Passive Investing, arXiv June 2025, INDEX, models the liquidity provider who builds a small inventory after announcement and provides at the event as earning several hundred basis points under its game theoretic assumptions. Model output, not measurement. Do not quote it as a return.

Net assessment. The 20 day run up and reversal, about 4 percent, is gross, pre cost, and the most competed trade in the calendar. The closing auction dislocation itself, the part a small fast book might imagine harvesting, is 9 basis points on additions, inside one spread on most small names, and 30 basis points on deletions, with an overnight reversal of 63 basis points on deletions that is the largest clean number in the set. Deletions are the interesting side: forced selling into the close, larger dislocation, larger reversal, and fewer willing buyers because the name is leaving the index. Costs: crossing the spread twice on a deletion name on its highest volume day, plus overnight gap risk. At 63 basis points gross reversal, a small book paying 10 to 20 basis points all in has a plausible net remainder. At 13 basis points on additions, it does not.

Falsifier or forward test. Paper the deletion side only for the next two Russell and S and P reconstitutions: buy deletions in the closing auction, exit at the next open and at plus 5 days as two arms, with quoted spread logged at entry. Minimum 30 deletion events. Falsified if next open reversal net of spread is at or below zero, or if the plus 5 day arm shows the reversal was already complete overnight and the auction entry adds nothing versus a next open entry control.

Transfer verdict. TEST the deletion reversal as an episodic liquidity provision sleeve, June and December concentrated. REJECT front running the 20 day run up at this estate speed, and REJECT the additions close dislocation as sub spread noise.

### 2.3 Closing auction and end of day reversal: about 3 basis points gross, regime fragile

Claim. Stocks that fell into the late afternoon outperform winners in the last half hour and into the next day, because closing flows are price pressure, not information.

Evidence. Baltussen, Da, and Soebhag end of day reversal, sample ending December 2019, as carried in Dive Two. New in this dive: a pre registered post sample test, Medium summary with paper and Zenodo replication archive, INDEX, read 2026-11-14, SINGLE SOURCE for the replication numbers, method unusually transparent for a blog summary. Design: point in time S and P 500 members, 2016 to October 2026, with about 80 large heavily traded names deliberately excluded because the testers had examined them in earlier unpublished work, a disclosed sample surgery that cuts both ways. Results: dollar volume weighted reversal measured to the last trade before the closing auction is 3.13 basis points per day in 2016 to 2019 and 3.05 basis points per day in 2023 to 2026 in the proxy sample, and the registered no weakening verdict holds on that sample. But over all sessions from 2020 it is 1.69 basis points, 46 percent below the earlier period, with 2020 to 2022 indistinguishable from zero. On the full index the reversal falls from 3.40 to 0.74 basis points. Equal weights or dropping the five largest names per day restores it, so the weakness concentrates in the mega caps where dollar volume weight now sits. The closing auction itself carries a reversal in every period, and it grew. Four pre registered mechanism tests, market component, idiosyncratic component, 0DTE weekday effect, single stock leveraged ETF launches, none passes after Holm correction. A separate working paper, The Execution Gap of Trading Mutual Funds, draft November 2026, INDEX, finds 97 percent of flow driven abnormal returns occur before the closing auction itself, 74 percent before the final half hour, and partially revert over the next several days, costing the 13.7 trillion dollar domestic equity fund complex an estimated 0.26 to 0.60 basis points per year. That reframes the close: by the time the auction prints, most of the pressure has already traded.

Net assessment. Three basis points gross per day, daily turnover, is the Novy Marx death zone in its purest form. Even at 1 basis point per side in mega caps the remainder is about 1 basis point with mega cap concentration risk, and in the small names where the effect is stronger the spread is wider than the effect. The only form with a coherent net story is as an execution overlay: if the book already needs to trade near the close, trade with the reversal, sell intraday winners and buy intraday losers for planned orders, and donate nothing. As a standalone strategy it fails its own arithmetic.

Falsifier or forward test. No standalone build to falsify. As an overlay, measure implementation shortfall on close orders with and without the reversal tilt over one quarter. Keep the tilt only if shortfall improves by more than 1 basis point net.

Transfer verdict. REJECT as a strategy. ADOPT as an execution timing note for orders already planned near the close.

### 2.4 Odd lot and retail imbalance: predicts returns, loses money where it is strongest

Claim. Retail order imbalance, identified from subpenny internalized trades by the Boehmer, Jones, Zhang, and Zhang algorithm, positively predicts next day and next week returns. The tradeable version is much weaker than the predictive version.

Evidence. Resolving a Paradox: Retail Trades Positively Predict Returns but Are Not Profitable, Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis 2024, INDEX via publisher PDF excerpts, read 2026-11-14. Sample: TAQ identified retail trades 2010 to 2019. Resolution of the paradox: imbalance tests equal weight stocks, but retail purchases concentrate in attention grabbing stocks that subsequently underperform. Long short strategies on extreme retail imbalance quintiles earn minus 14.8 percent annualized among stocks with heavy retail trading, and plus 6.6 percent among other stocks. Smaller retail trades concentrate more in attention names and perform worse. A second JFQA paper, Institutional Liquidity Costs, Internalized Retail Trade Imbalances, and the Cross Section of Stock Returns, 2025, INDEX, builds an institutional liquidity cost measure from average absolute retail imbalances and finds an associated premium of 2.7 to 3.2 percent post 2010 even excluding microcaps, interpreted as compensation for resorting to wholesaler liquidity, not as a retail following signal. Dive Two carried the Jones et al update of the raw predictive effect: interquartile imbalance worth plus 3.81 basis points next day pre pandemic, plus 5.91 basis points pandemic, plus 6.69 basis points post pandemic, gross. Put the two together and the picture is consistent: a 4 to 7 basis point gross next day effect, daily turnover, concentrated in exactly the attention names where the long short implementation loses 14.8 percent a year. Odd lot specific evidence remains thin: no source in this dive or Dive One isolates odd lot share itself, as distinct from subpenny identified retail imbalance, with a net of cost result. Odd lots are a large share of prints in high priced names and are on the consolidated tape, but a falsifiable odd lot rule still does not exist in the located literature.

Falsifier or forward test. Use imbalance only as a filter on trades the book already wants, never as a standalone daily book, per Dive Two. Forward test of the filter form: existing sleeves log BJZZ style imbalance at signal time where data allows. Falsified as a filter if taken trades in the favorable imbalance tercile do not beat taken trades in the unfavorable tercile over two quarters.

Transfer verdict. FILTER ONLY. The standalone form is a documented net loser in its strongest segment, which is the cleanest negative result in this territory.

### 2.5 Borrow constrained names: the fee anomaly is largely an average fee illusion

Claim. High lending fee stocks underperform, and shorting them looks profitable even net of fees. The new evidence says the profit depends on charging the short the average fee on outstanding loans instead of the marginal fee on a new loan.

Evidence. The Marginal Cost of Borrowing Stock, draft August 2026, INDEX via full text excerpts, read 2026-11-14, SINGLE SOURCE for the headline magnitudes, data from FIS Astec Analytics daily US equity lending. Mechanism: lending is a search market with dispersed fees. When new loan fees fall, outstanding fees absorb 67 percent of the decrease within a month as borrowers replace expensive loans. When new loan fees rise, outstanding fees absorb only 30 percent of the increase, because incumbents keep cheap legacy loans. Result: a persistent wedge between the average fee and the marginal fee, widest where it matters most, at utilization above 90 percent the interquartile range of new loan spreads is about 32 basis points monthly and 5 percent of stocks show spreads above 4 percentage points. Asset pricing consequence: valuing short positions at the marginal fee reduces returns to shorting high fee stocks by roughly one third on a value weighted basis and eliminates them on an equal weighted basis. The paper replicates the classic result at average fees first, Drechsler and Drechsler and Engelberg et al fee portfolios beating 102 anomalies, both carried in Dive Two, so the correction is internal to the literature, not an outside attack. Corroboration on the friction trend: Inefficiencies in the Securities Lending Market, working paper October 2025, INDEX, finds borrow costs have risen dramatically over decades against the general trend of falling trading frictions, far more names are hard to borrow, by July 2022 some 43 percent of names in its sample carry fees above 1 percent versus 9 percent in D Avolio 2002, and estimated inefficiency since 2020 exceeds 300 million dollars per day. The long side evidence from Dive Two stands and is strengthened: holders of high fee names lose heavily in high fee episodes, fee at or above 25 percent, 2005 to 2023, average loss about 20 percent per episode, and squeezes cluster exactly when fees and utilization are high, forcing closures that erase apparent short profits.

Net assessment. There are three different trades often confused. Short the high fee name directly: largely eliminated at marginal fees, plus squeeze and recall risk, plus the fee itself can reprice against a new borrower fastest exactly when the thesis is working. Avoid or underweight the high fee name on the long side: supported, costs nothing in borrow, and the episode loss numbers are large. Use fee plus quantity rising together, the Cohen, Diether, and Malloy demand shift signature, as a veto on new longs and as a bearish conditioner: supported and cheap. Only the first trade died in this dive. The other two survive because they do not pay the marginal fee.

Falsifier or forward test. Any short sleeve in this estate must be charged the quoted new loan fee at entry, re marked weekly, plus a squeeze exit rule, in paper accounting from now on. A short idea that only profits at the average fee from a database field is falsified by construction and should not reach paper.

Transfer verdict. ADOPT the veto and the long avoidance. REJECT direct shorting of high fee names as a systematic sleeve at this size, where one squeeze is a portfolio event.

## Territory 3: Adjacent Field Transfers

Each transfer is stated as a mechanism this book can implement this quarter, the evidence from its home field, and its breaking point, the condition under which the transfer stops working and must be switched off.

### 3.1 Poker: GTO baseline plus measured deviation, never deviation alone

Mechanism. Solvers compute an unexploitable baseline, a Nash equilibrium strategy in which bluff to value ratios and defense frequencies make an opponent indifferent. Profit does not come from playing the baseline. It comes from measuring how a specific opponent or pool deviates from it and deviating in the opposite direction by a computed amount. The tooling names transfer directly. Node locking: fix the opponent strategy at one decision node to its measured frequency, re solve, and read the maximally exploitative response. Mass Data Analysis, MDA: aggregate millions of hands for a defined pool, by site, stake, and player profile, so every node carries a real frequency and a sample size, and refuse to publish an exploit for any node whose sample is too small. A current exploitative solver product, GTOkiller, INDEX, vendor page read 2026-11-14, VENDOR flag, describes exactly this pipeline: about 500,000 nodes per tree, rolling two year window, full tree solved at once rather than one street at a time, with a solid base retained wherever the pool deviation is marginal or counter adaptation would be easy. The one node at a time limit of mainstream tools, GTO Wizard solves one street and assumes perfect play later, is documented in the same source and matters for the transfer.

Transfer to this book. The baseline is the fleet admission exam and the conditional gates from Dives One and Two: what an unexploitable, no view book does. The opponent is not a person, it is a measurable population: earnings short vol counterparties, index rebalancers, retail attention flow, dealers hedging. Node locking becomes scenario locking: fix one counterparty behavior to its measured value, for example deletions will be sold at the close regardless of price, or the overpriced earnings quintile will crush by its historical mean, re price the structure, and size only the deviation the measurement supports. MDA becomes the estate own hand history: every sleeve firing logged with the population frequencies that surrounded it, segmented by regime, name, and event type, with a minimum sample rule below which no exploit is sized. The poker discipline that transfers hardest is the refusal rule: if a node does not have enough sample, the solver does not force a solution. Most trading research forces a solution on 12 events.

Bankroll practice, the second poker transfer. Fractional Kelly with explicit risk of ruin accounting, staking pools that diversify across many positive expectancy but high variance players and profit from selection and pooling rather than coaching, and the norm that results over a few hundred trials say little, win rate standard deviation per 100 hands is large even for winners, and 50 to 100 buy ins is a normal professional buffer at a 5 percent ruin tolerance. Dive One already set fleet sizing at one quarter to one half Kelly on deflated expectancy, solved jointly. Poker adds the ruin framing: state for each sleeve cluster the probability of ever halving its allocation at the chosen fraction, one half at full Kelly, one eighth at half Kelly in the canonical example, and treat that number, not the Sharpe, as the sizing argument presented for approval.

Breaking point. Poker opponents are stationary within a session and the game rules never change mid hand. Market populations adapt across quarters, the pool that overfolded last year gets a solver too, and the equivalent of a rule change, a new expiry cycle, a fee change, a borrow regime shift, invalidates the measured frequencies without announcing itself. Exploits therefore expire. Every population frequency used for sizing needs a window, a refresh date, and a decay check, exactly as the exploitative solver discards hands older than two years. Second breaking point: in poker the deviation is observed directly in hand histories. In markets the counterparty deviation is inferred from prices and flow with classification error, the open close problem from Dive One. Size the exploit by the classification confidence, not just the measured deviation.

Falsifier or forward test. Pick one population exploit already in the book, the earnings overpriced quintile short, and run it in two arms for two seasons: baseline size versus MDA sized, where size scales with the logged sample count and measured deviation for that name and regime cell, floored at baseline when the cell count is under 20. The transfer is falsified if MDA sizing does not improve return per unit of max drawdown.

Verdict. ADOPT the method: baseline, measured deviation, full tree re solve, minimum sample refusal, expiring frequencies. It is the opponent modeling section of Dive Two with better tooling discipline.

### 3.2 Sports microstructure: closing line value as the grading standard, steam as a warning

Mechanism one, CLV. Closing line value is the difference between the price a bettor obtained and the closing price, the market most informed price, expressed in probability terms with vig removed. Practitioner and bookmaker consensus, multiple sources INDEX read 2026-11-14, with academic anchors Levitt 2004 on closing line efficiency and Kaunitz, Zhong, and Kreiner 2017: consistent positive CLV is the strongest single indicator of long term edge, it correlates with eventual profit at far lower variance than win loss records, every bet receives a grade within hours instead of at season end, and books themselves use CLV, not win rate, to identify and limit sharp accounts. The transfer was sketched in Dive Two and is stated as a build here. Every sleeve and every forecaster in this estate gets a closing benchmark: for an overnight hold, entry price versus the close and versus the next open; for an earnings structure, entry implied move and IV versus the last pre print mark; for a probabilistic forecast, the forecaster stated probability versus the final internal aggregate on resolution eve and versus the realized outcome scored by Brier and log. CLV resolves in hundreds of trials what profit and loss needs thousands to show, because it strips out the outcome lottery and keeps the pricing error, which is the part the process controls.

The critique that must ship with it. On the Bet the Process podcast as reported by bet105, INDEX, professional bettors Rufus Peabody and Rob Pizzola, with Jeff Ma, argue CLV is a diagnostic, not the objective: an originator with genuinely orthogonal information can hold a positive expectancy bet that does not beat the close, because the close does not contain their information; steam followers manufacture cosmetic CLV by drafting behind a move they did not cause; and optimizing for CL from the same signals everyone follows guarantees convergence to the market, not an edge over it. All three points transfer intact. In this estate, a sleeve that originates from a private signal, for example the EDGAR cluster buy pipeline, should be graded on outcome scores first and CLV second. A sleeve that follows public flow should be graded on CLV first, because if it cannot beat the close it has no other claim. And CLV must be measured against a benchmark the sleeve did not move and did not copy, or it is circular.

Mechanism two, syndicate information flow and steam. A steam move is a rapid, coordinated repricing across many books within minutes, triggered by sharp or syndicate action at originator books, followed by copycat adjustment at follower books that have taken no bet at all, partly automated. Sources: multiple practitioner explainers, INDEX, read 2026-11-14, consistent on mechanics. The lessons that transfer are about information cascades in any market. One, originators capture the value by hitting many venues simultaneously before adjustment; followers who react to the visible cascade are buying the corrected price, and the edge is gone in minutes, with free alerts delayed 5 to 15 minutes arriving after it is over. Two, cascade size overstates information content: one group model finding half a point of value can produce a market wide lurch once automated followers pile in, and late steam chasers add a further overmove that can create value on the other side. Three, the stale venue is punished: the last book to move absorbs concentrated one sided flow at a wrong price. Translated: in options and equities, a sudden correlated repricing across names or venues, an IV spike cascading across a sector, a gamma driven chase, is information that a repricing happened, not an instruction to join it at the new price. The estate version of steam chasing, entering after a flow alert, a scanner spike, or a peer AI enthusiasm cascade in the Triad room, has the same expected value as the sports version. The profitable seats are originator, with a model that disagrees before the move, or liquidity provider to the cascade at the stale price with a reversal thesis and a size cap. There is no third seat.

Breaking point. Sports closes are clean: one event, one closing line, a binary or spread outcome hours later. Market closes are continuous and the benchmark itself trades. CLV in markets must therefore use a pre declared benchmark timestamp and a no vig equivalent, mid at the benchmark time adjusted for spread, or grades will drift with definition choices. Second breaking point: in sports the close is exogenous to a small bettor. A sleeve large in a thin name moves its own close. CLV is only honest in names where the book is a price taker.

Falsifier or forward test. Implement CLV logging for three sleeve families for one quarter, pre declaring the benchmark per family. Test the core claim directly: does first 100 trade CLV predict next 100 trade profit and loss better than first 100 trade profit and loss predicts itself? If not, the close in that family is not the efficient benchmark the sports market provides, and CLV grading should be dropped for that family rather than ritualized.

Verdict. ADOPT CLV as the early grading standard with the originator exception and the circularity guard. ADOPT the steam lesson as a standing rule: no entry whose thesis is that a cascade is happening. Entries are originated or they are liquidity provision against a cascade, labeled as such.

### 3.3 Forecasting tournaments: extremize only what does not overlap, update in small steps, keep score in public

Mechanism. The Good Judgment Project won the IARPA ACE tournament by 35 to 72 percent over rival teams across seasons, with superforecasters about 30 percent better than intelligence analysts holding classified access, per the sources carried in Dive Two and refreshed in this dive, INDEX, read 2026-11-14. The recipe has four separable parts, and the tournament literature is unusually clear about their relative size: about one hour of probabilistic reasoning training, teaming with structured discussion after independent first forecasts, weighted aggregation favoring recent and accurate forecasters, and extremizing, pushing the aggregate away from 50 percent in log odds space, which was the largest single lever at about 35 percent of the accuracy gain in the Dive One synthesis. Update cadence is a measured behavior, not a slogan: the best forecasters updated frequently, in small increments, as evidence arrived, rather than rarely and largely, and aggregation weighted frequent updaters more, partly as an effort proxy. Metaculus community practice converged on the same shape: recency weighted median, with performance weighting and extremization in its former algorithmic forecast.

Transfer to this book, concretely for the three AI research team and for any probabilistic sleeve. One, every material forecast is a number with a resolution date and a scoring rule fixed in advance, logged before discussion, Delphi round one, so herding is measurable. Two, aggregation is a formula: weights from domain matched Brier and log scores with temporal decay, shrunk toward equal weights by the Dive Two rule, weight equals equal plus kappa times optimal minus equal, kappa equals n over n plus 50, and no routing or upweighting at all in domains with under 50 resolved questions. Three, extremizing factor is estimated from measured information overlap, residual correlation of member errors, and collapses to 1, no extremizing, when members demonstrably read the same sources, which for three language models sharing a research note is the default assumption until residuals prove otherwise. Dive Two KalshiBench warning stands and is part of this transfer: frontier language models tested on post cutoff questions were systematically overconfident and reasoning enhanced variants were worse calibrated, expected calibration error 0.395 versus 0.120 for the best model. An AI team that extremizes its own already overconfident aggregate without an overlap correction is manufacturing confidence. Four, update cadence becomes a sleeve rule as well as a forecaster rule: positions and forecasts are revised in small pre declared steps on new evidence, earnings, borrow fee changes, flow regime breaks, not in binary flips, and the revision log is itself scored.

Breaking point. Tournament questions resolve cleanly and forecasters cannot move the outcome. Market forecasts are reflexive: a published internal forecast that sizes a position changes the position P and L that grades it, and many market questions, will this sleeve beat SPY next quarter, resolve on a path dependent, cost dependent number the forecasters themselves influence. Keep tournament scoring for genuinely exogenous questions, earnings move versus implied, event outcomes, peer claims verification, and use CLV plus outcome P and L, Section 3.2, for endogenous ones. Second breaking point: extremizing assumes forecasters hold different information. When the Triad shares one memory pool by design, overlap is structural. The honest default extremizing factor for this team is near 1, rising only in cells where residual correlation is measured low.

Falsifier or forward test. Run 50 pre registered internal questions in parallel under three aggregators, simple median, weighted, and weighted plus extremized with the overlap correction. Predictions from Dive Two stand: weighted beats median by at least 10 percent on Brier, extremized beats weighted only in low overlap cells, and ensemble error in the bottom diversity quartile is at least 1.5 times its average error. Grade and publish internally at resolution 50.

Verdict. ADOPT, with extremizing demoted from headline trick to a conditional, overlap gated adjustment. The scoring, weighting, shrinkage, and update discipline are the transfer. Extremizing is the garnish that poisons the dish when overlap is high.

### 3.4 Market making: price the fill you actually get, skew by inventory, respect the queue

Mechanisms, three models that together price passive execution honestly.

Glosten and Milgrom 1985: the spread is adverse selection insurance. With value either high or low, gap Delta, and informed fraction mu, a competitive maker sets the ask at expected value given a buy arrived, and the spread equals mu times Delta. Each uninformed trade earns the half spread; each informed trade loses. When Delta jumps, the morning of earnings, the same name must trade wide or the maker must step away. Avellaneda and Stoikov 2008: inventory moves the center, not just the width. Reservation price r equals mid s minus q times gamma times sigma squared times time remaining T minus t, where q is inventory, gamma risk aversion, sigma volatility. A long maker shades both quotes down, making the ask easier to hit and the bid harder, so flow flattens the position. A simulation synthesis located in this dive, INDEX, reports inventory control halving the volatility of a market making book at unchanged expected profit, and a hard toxicity limit: once informed flow reaches about a third of fills, no inventory policy keeps the book alive, only width or absence does. Moallemi and Yuan 2017, Management Science line, Columbia working paper PRIMARY excerpt read 2026-11-14, INDEX: queue position itself has a value with two components, a static component from the spread versus adverse selection tradeoff, where adverse selection worsens with queue position because a back of queue order fills mainly when a large informed order sweeps the whole queue, and a dynamic component, the optionality of improving toward the front as orders ahead cancel or fill. For large tick size stocks, front of queue value can be the same order of magnitude as the spread itself. A 2026 counterpoint preprint, Ayyar, Queue Priority as Adverse Selection Exposure, INDEX, proves the sign is conditional: moving one rank forward adds exactly one marginal execution state, so priority is valuable when that marginal fill is benign and costly when it is toxic, and depth ahead can act as a shield rather than a delay. Both results are needed. Front is usually better. Front is not always better, and in toxic flow the queue ahead is insurance.

Transfer to this book, which does not make markets but constantly decides between crossing and posting, and whose paper fills flatter passive execution. One, every passive order gets a Glosten Milgrom sanity price: expected value given that this order fills, not unconditional mid. A limit order inside a wide earnings spread that fills instantly has told you something, and the fill price is not the mid you modeled. Paper accounting must charge passive fills an adverse selection haircut estimated from post fill markouts, price 1, 5, and 30 minutes after fill versus mid at fill, logged per sleeve. Two, Avellaneda Stoikov skew becomes position management for multi leg options books: as a cluster accumulates delta or vega in one direction, new entry quotes and exit urgency shift by a pre declared function of inventory, vol, and time to expiry or event, instead of each trade being priced as if the book were flat. Three, queue value disciplines limit order tactics in large tick names and in options with penny wide but deep queues: joining the back of a long queue to save half a spread is buying the worst adverse selection in the line. Either pay for priority, improve price by a tick where the economics allow, post early when the intent is firm, or cross. The saving is often negative.

Breaking point. These models assume the maker can requote or cancel freely and observes the book. A small book on a retail broker route has cancel latency, no co location, and options queues where displayed size is a fraction of true interest. Queue tactics that require winning a speed race are not available. What transfers is the valuation, knowing what a queue position and a passive fill are worth, not the racing. Second breaking point: options market making adds hedging flow and multi leg netting that the single asset models omit. Use the models for haircuts and skew direction, not as literal quote engines.

Falsifier or forward test. For one quarter, log markouts on every passive fill in paper and any live small orders, by name, spread width, and queue proxy. Pre register the prediction: passive fills in the widest spread tercile and in earnings week names will show negative 5 minute markouts exceeding half the spread saved. If markouts are benign, the haircut can be retired with evidence. If they confirm, the haircut becomes a permanent paper accounting rule and several wide spread sleeves from Territory 2 and 4 will re grade downward, as they should.

Verdict. ADOPT as accounting and tactics discipline. This is the mechanism that makes the rest of the report honest, because it prices the fills the backtests assume.

### 4.2 Gamma exposure products, GEX levels, gamma walls, and the flip

Claim as sold. Dealer gamma exposure computed from open interest predicts support and resistance levels, pinning, squeeze risk, and in strong marketing form, direction. Intraday updates every 15 seconds to 1 minute are sold at premium tiers as a faster view of market maker positioning, including products that argue daily open interest based rivals are guessing while their own feed is direct.

Evidence for, the honest core. Dealer hedging is a real flow and gamma describes its sign. The best evidence uses actual market maker positions, not open interest inference: Amaya, Garcia Ares, Pearson, and Vasquez 2025, S and P 500 index options, July 2020 to June 2023, as summarized in a Tradytics evidence review, INDEX, read 2026-11-14, VENDOR author flag, the review sells a competing exposure product, but it reports against its own interest in places and cites the primary. Findings: dealer gamma was usually positive, positive gamma reduced volatility by about 0.2 percentage points on average, negative gamma raised volatility with a maximum effect of 3.3 points of annualized daily volatility. Baltussen et al 2021 tied end of day momentum to short gamma hedging across more than 60 futures markets. Ni, Pearson, and Poteshman 2005 established that optionable stock price behavior responds to hedging related stock trading. CBOE analysis cited in the same review found customer 0DTE SPX flow extremely balanced, leaving net maker hedging at most about 0.2 percent of SPX daily liquidity, a scale check that cuts against cascade marketing.

Evidence against, and it is decisive for the marketed form. First, a vendor self backtest of unusual quality, located in this dive: a dealer exposure API seller backtested his own GEX, DEX, VEX, and CHEX products on 1,972 SPY trading days, 2018-04-16 to 2026-04-02, with pre registered hypotheses, INDEX via dev.to full summary, read 2026-11-14, SINGLE SOURCE but against interest, which raises its credibility. Raw result looks strong: GEX versus next day realized vol, Spearman rho minus 0.36, top minus bottom quintile minus 10.63 vol points, t minus 13.00. After controlling for VIX alone, rho falls to minus 0.14 and the quintile spread to minus 3.15 points. After controlling for VIX plus ATM implied vol, rho is minus 0.03, p 0.18, spread minus 0.99 points, p 0.25. Not significant. DEX, VEX, and CHEX carry no robust independent information in residualized tests. DEX versus next day return is dead on arrival. The author conclusion, adopted here: GEX is not fake, the raw effect is real, the incremental information after VIX and ATM IV is the part that fails. Useful regime descriptor is not independent forecasting edge. In the top VIX quintile, where an extra signal would matter most, the double sort shows no clean edge. Second, the construction flaws are admitted across the spectrum, including by sellers: open interest updates once daily and cannot see this morning positions, in a market where about two thirds of SPX volume on some measures expires the same day; the dealer side is an assumption, dealers long calls and short puts, that reverses exactly when customers pile into calls; and no located study supports direction prediction, gamma walls as tested support or resistance, or the flip level as a tradable trigger. A worked counterexample in the Tradytics review: October 30, 2026, QQQ ended in positive gamma and fell 3.16 percent, twice the SPY drop, while SPY sat in negative gamma. One day proves nothing alone, but it is the correct shape of counterexample for a level based marketing claim. Third, Dive Two verdict stands unreversed: GEX is a regime descriptor built on an unobserved positioning assumption, not a directional forecast, and precise levels are untestable without trade signed data.

What would settle it. A pre registered test on trade signed or actual position data, not open interest inference, showing incremental prediction of next day realized vol or intraday range after VIX and ATM IV controls, out of sample, plus a separate pre registered hit rate test for wall and flip levels versus placebo levels at matched distances. The vendor self backtest above is the template. Any product that will not run it on its own history should be graded by the result of the one seller who did.

Grade: CONDITIONAL as a volatility regime descriptor, where it adds a modest amount over VIX alone and nothing proven over VIX plus ATM IV. UNSUPPORTED for levels, walls, flips, pinning calls, and any directional use. The intraday speed premium is UNSUPPORTED: updating an assumption driven estimate more often does not cure the assumption.

Falsifier or forward test for this estate. If GEX is displayed anywhere in the stack, run the residual test on the estate own logged history each quarter: does the GEX bucket predict next day realized vol after regressing out VIX and ATM IV? Two consecutive quarters at p above 0.10 removes it from decision surfaces, leaving it, at most, as chart decoration labeled as such.

Transfer verdict. DO NOT SIZE ON GEX. A binary regime note, dealers likely long or short gamma by the standard assumption, may sit beside VIX term structure as context for the short premium gates from Dive One. Nothing more earns its place.

### 4.3 AI and LLM trading claims

Claim as sold. Large language models and agent frameworks predict returns, read news into tradable direction, and in backtest report large excess returns and Sharpes. Retail experiments and agent arenas are marketed as proof of arrival.

Evidence for, stated fairly. Lopez Lira and Tang, forthcoming Journal of Financial Economics, revised October 2025, as summarized in two independent reviews located in this dive, INDEX: GPT 4 predicted the direction of the initial market reaction to news headlines with high portfolio level hit rates in a deliberately post cutoff window, October 2021 to May 2024. The same summaries state the limits in the same breath: the initial reaction portfolio is explicitly non tradable, per headline accuracy is about 51 percent, the tradable drift is thin and concentrated in small caps, and returns decay as adoption rises. Language models are also genuinely useful in this estate proven roles: filing summarization, extraction, classification at scale with the Luna routing rule, research assistance, and behavioral process discipline. None of that is return prediction.

Evidence against. Three layers, all INDEX read 2026-11-14. One, memorization: Can LLM Based Financial Investing Strategies Outperform the Market, arXiv 2505.07078, 2025, shows LLM backtests are inflated because a model whose training covers the test period recalls tickers, dates, and moves rather than forecasting them, and performance degrades past the cutoff. A 2026 memory controlled benchmark, arXiv 2605.28359, confirms that controlling for memorized history sharply reduces apparent skill. A mitigation paper, FinCAD, arXiv August 2026, reports in sample return corrections as large as minus 67.1 percent from removing parametric look ahead, which is a measure of how large the contamination was. Two, field audit: a 2026 audit of 77 LLM trading studies, arXiv 2605.19337, concludes evidence for durable alpha remains thin and locates practical value in execution, monitoring, and research assistance. A 2024 survey, arXiv 2408.06361, flags short windows and small universes across the field. Three, live money: the best documented retail case, the ChatGPT Micro Cap Experiment with open logs, ended December 29, 2025 at 82.88 dollars from 100 dollars, down about 17 percent while the S and P 500 grew to 111.68 dollars, annualized alpha minus 34.31 percent. In nof1 Alpha Arena, across 32 tracked results models finished profitable 6 times and the aggregate portfolio lost about a third of its capital. Add the fabrication base rate from the same reviews: financial fact fabrication up to 41 percent on unsafeguarded questions for even robust reasoning models, and numeric accuracy collapse on multi step calculations. A predictor that invents inputs cannot be sized.

Relation to Dive One foundation models. Consistent and mutually reinforcing. Chronos and TimesFM zero shot return R squared was negative, minus 1.37 to minus 2.80 percent, directional accuracy about 50 to 51 percent, in the 94 country study. Kronos rank IC was 0.0267 in sample from an unreleased large model. Text models memorizing history and time series models failing zero shot are the same finding from two directions: off the shelf foundation models do not forecast daily returns. The defensible uses remain features, extraction, and process, forward tested on post cutoff data only.

What would settle it. Any LLM strategy evaluated only on data after the specific model version training cutoff, with the version and cutoff stated, pre registered prompts and rules, bid and ask honest accounting, and a matched non LLM baseline, a momentum rank or a headline dictionary, beaten net of API and spread costs over at least six forward months. Backtests overlapping the training window are disqualified by construction, not discounted.

### 4.4 Overnight and session effects

Claim. Most of the equity premium accrues overnight, close to open, while intraday returns aggregate near zero, Lou, Polk, and Skouras tug of war, 2019, with cross sectional confirmation in Bogousslavsky 2021. Therefore a book should hold overnight and stand aside intraday, or trade the overnight segment directly.

Evidence for. The pricing fact is robust in the original samples, 1993 to 2013 and extensions: overnight about 0.55 percent per month versus intraday about 0.38 percent per month for the market portfolio in the Dive Two synthesis, all past return strategies earning overnight, and the clientele mechanism, retail concentrated near the open, institutions via market on close, is coherent and independently described. As an allocation fact it has survived many replications gross.

Evidence against the tradable form, new and strong in this dive. Two independent post sample trials, both INDEX read 2026-11-14. First, DelphicAlpha, Theory on Trial: Can Anyone Capture the Overnight Premium, published 2026-11-02, SINGLE SOURCE practitioner research with full method disclosed. Design: 30 minute futures across 33 markets, 2008 to October 2026, plus the Lou Polk Skouras stock rule, top momentum decile held only overnight, every setting chosen on data to 2019 and run unchanged on 2020 to 2026, 5 basis points per side for stocks. Results: a ten market overnight futures book Sharpe 1.96 gross on 2008 to 2019 and 1.41 gross on 2020 to 2026, falling after costs to 0.29 and minus 0.20. The book earned 4.12 basis points a day before costs and paid 3.85 basis points a day in costs, leaving plus 0.2 basis points in the earlier period framing and a losing book after 2020, negative in 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026. Only 8 of 33 markets cleared their own breakeven cost. The stock rule did better and still barely: top decile overnight earned 9.3 basis points a day gross before 2020 and 12.5 after, against 10 basis points a day in costs, Sharpe minus 0.15 before 2020 and 0.31 after, positive overnight in 24 of 26 years while the intraday hold of the same stocks lost money even before costs. Author conclusion, adopted: the premium is real, and the cost of collecting it is almost exactly as large. It can only be harvested if cost per side sits well below breakeven. Second, Krueger Algorithms replication of seven published anomalies on minute CFD data, INDEX: Lou Polk Skouras trend earns only overnight did not replicate in its form, overnight plus 0.9 basis points, t 0.8, intraday minus 1.8 basis points, and net of costs at minus 4.6 basis points, and across all seven papers the summary is gross at 40 to 80 percent of published size, none survives costs, with tradable effects of 1 to 3 basis points against round trip costs of 1 to 5 basis points. CFD data and one implementation, flagged, but the direction matches the futures trial. A conditional variant circulating in GitHub research repos, hold SPY overnight only after a negative intraday day, with a 200 day filter, INDEX, is presented as a hypothesis file with regime tables and no audited net result located. Grade it a hypothesis, not evidence.

What would settle it. A forward paper run at this estate actual costs, MOC or 15:55 entry and open exit with quoted spreads logged, on SPY and QQQ and on the momentum decile form, for six months. The breakeven arithmetic predicts the index form nets near zero and the decile form nets small positive only if per side cost holds at or below about 5 basis points. That is a measurable, killable prediction.

Grade: SUPPORTED as a pricing and allocation fact. REFUTED as a daily round trip strategy at ordinary costs. CONDITIONAL as a holding preference: exposure the book wants anyway should be held overnight rather than intraday, which harvests the split without paying the second spread, exactly the Dive Two verdict, now with post 2020 net numbers behind it.

Transfer verdict. NO standalone overnight sleeve. YES to the timing preference for existing exposure, and to counting overnight versus intraday attribution per sleeve in grading, so a sleeve that earns intraday against the tide gets credit for it.

### 4.5 Volatility term structure trades

Claim in its strong form. The VIX futures curve sits in contango about four days in five, so systematically selling the front of the curve, short VIX futures, inverse ETPs, or calendarized short vol, harvests a structural roll yield. In its weak form, term structure shape is a regime gate for short premium.

Evidence. Curve facts, multiple sources INDEX read 2026-11-14, consistent: contango on about 79 percent of trading days 2013 to 2026 in one CBOE via FRED reconstruction, about 80 to 84 percent in Dive One sources. Roll arithmetic, worked in full in an HMA Quant note, INDEX: with spot VIX 15.00, front future 16.50 at 20 days, second month 17.80 at 50 days, a constant maturity 30 day position loses 1.30 points, 7.68 percent, over a month if the curve is frozen, compounding to about minus 61.7 percent over a frozen year. Real curves are not frozen, but the direction explains long vol ETP decay without invoking fees: VXX lost about 60 percent a year over 2009 to 2018 in one reconstruction while inverse XIV gained about 50 percent a year until February 2018, when it lost about 97 percent in a session and terminated. Backwardation is a state, not a signal: total backwardation has mostly appeared inside an S and P decline of at least 5 percent already underway and seldom ahead of a further 5 percent fall, and VIX closed above VIX3M on 48 of 438 sessions between August 2024 and April 2026, so the recent regime inverts more often than the long run average suggests. Premium compression evidence from Dive One governs the strong form: Dew Becker and Giglio, Chicago Fed WP 2025-17, traded index option alphas indistinguishable from zero over the past 15 years as frictions fell and vol selling crowded. A July 2026 VRP review located in this dive, INDEX, VENDOR adjacent, reaches the same conditional endorsement: implied exceeds realized about 85 to 90 percent of periods historically, the premium is real, and it is compensation for insurance underwriting now competed down, to be harvested only in out of the money constructions with a disciplined implied versus forecast realized spread. Retail earnings option losses add a micro confirmation, de Silva et al as cited there: retail overpays versus realized, trades through half spreads near 8 percent, and holds through predictable post announcement decay. The spread, again, is where the retail version of the premium goes.

What would settle the strong form. Nothing further is needed at index level: the Chicago Fed result plus the XIV terminal event plus the frozen curve arithmetic settle it. Short vol as an unconditional, leveraged, or ETP based standing book is refuted as an alpha claim and disqualified as a risk claim. What remains open, and testable, is only the conditional form.

Grade: REFUTED in the strong unconditional form. CONDITIONAL in the gated, defined risk form from Dive One, Section 3.1: enter only with implied minus trailing realized at or above 3 vol points, term structure in contango, no major event in the first half of the trade, IV percentile at or above 50, exit at 50 percent of credit or 21 DTE, hard stop at twice credit, stress sized to a 30 percent gap.

Falsifier or forward test. The Dive One twin test governs: conditional sleeve versus its own unconditional twin, 50 or more trades forward, net of bid and ask. If the gates do not beat the twin forward, conditional VRP is dead for this cost structure and the term structure survives only as a no new short premium gate in backwardation, its best evidenced use.

Transfer verdict. KEEP term structure as a gate and a regime variable everywhere. NO standalone roll harvesting book, no inverse ETP holding, no naked short futures. The curve pays insurers who survive the inversion. This estate will collect it only in defined risk, gated, stress sized form, or not at all.
