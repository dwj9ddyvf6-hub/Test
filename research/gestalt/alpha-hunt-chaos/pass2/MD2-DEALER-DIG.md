## PART 2: THE DEALER EXTENSION, RUN

**What existed on disk.** The lane's script (`pass2/work_taskd/dealer_extension.py`), its log showing the verification gate passed (33 daily rows recomputed against the scalar pipeline, 0 mismatches), and no output files: the extended table and results the script declares were never written. Process inspection found the reason: the lane's script was still running as an orphaned process, roughly 14 minutes elapsed and 4 minutes 34 seconds of CPU spent in its step 2 census, unfinished. The scalar static-GEX computation costs roughly 2 to 6 seconds per snapshot in pure Python (101 grid points times three IV legs over about 3,900 counted contracts), so 333 captures price at 11 to 33 minutes of CPU on an uncontended core, and this box is contended. The finisher stopped the orphan and changed route, below.

**Archive depth, measured first.** The morgue chain store holds daily snapshots for exactly SPY, QQQ and IWM, 11 sessions each, 2026-10-29 to 2026-11-13: the window and universe of the original table are the archive's whole extent, and no wider daily extension exists to build. The morning-map session history behind A12 holds the same 11 sessions. What the archive holds beyond the daily rows is intraday captures: SPY 111, QQQ 113, IWM 109, 333 files in all, timestamps from 2026-10-29T223941Z to 2026-11-13. The extension therefore runs on the intraday surface, exactly as the lane designed it: A13 states per capture, morning-state rows from each symbol-day's first capture. A12 has no intraday record and no deeper daily record, so the fused A12xA13 state cannot be extended; that limit is a property of the held records, proven by the census above, not a route not tried.

**Verification gate, run twice by two routes.** The scalar route's gate stands in the lane's log: 33 of 33 daily rows recomputed, 0 mismatches. The finisher's vectorized route (the identical convention computed with numpy over contracts, `work_taskd/dealer_extension_fast.py`) first failed its gate with 32 of 33 rows mismatching on net GEX only, every flip exact; the cause was in this finisher's replication, not in the table: the scalar module rounds each strike's net from its unrounded call and put sums, and the first vectorized version rounded the legs before summing, a difference of cents per strike across hundreds of strikes. Corrected to the module's own order and re-run, the vectorized gate passes at the table's printed precision: 33 of 33 flips reproduced at 4 decimal places, 33 of 33 net GEX figures at 2 decimal places, 0 mismatches. The extension below is computed on the gate-verified vectorized route and ran in 92 seconds.

**The daily fused census, from the original table's own rows.** Fused state: agree_spot_below_both_flips on 30 of 33 rows; null on the 3 rows of 2026-10-29, where A12 is absent because the morning-map history starts 2026-10-30. The disagreement cell never populates on daily rows, and with A12 daily-only, it can only ever populate on the nightly accrual from Mon 2026-11-16, where both conventions print per session.
