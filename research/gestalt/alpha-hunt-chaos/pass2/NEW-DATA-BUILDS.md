# NEW DATA BUILDS: The Three Ungraded Objects, Built and Read

Three candidates from the Pass 2 hunt were ungradable for one reason each: a data series that did not exist. This program built each series from the routes the estate actually holds, printed the coverage and the route limits honestly, and took each candidate's first read on the built series. Build 1 persisted the dealer-state table. Build 2 proved the closing-imbalance route's limit with evidence and built the executed-volume series the route does yield. Build 3 pulled the futures overnight series on a public route after the disk route came up empty.

## POTENTIAL EDGE FOUND

**Lead 1, the dealer regime object (Build 1): the dealer family is one regime signal, and it is now a persisted, forward-accruing series.** The fused dealer-state candidate (gamma flip x static-GEX flip x trigger basis, the seat's strongest one-object triple at p 0.64) could not grade because no per-name-night dealer series existed. The built table (SPY, QQQ, IWM x 11 sessions, 33 rows, 30 graded legs) shows the two flip conventions agree on which side of spot the flip sits in 30 of 30 name-nights where both exist, and never straddle spot once. The covered window is a single regime: spot sat below both flips on every covered name-night, and that populated cell carried +41.98bp mean overnight (median +45.97bp, win 0.741, t 2.679, n 27) against the window's unconditional +23.49bp (t 1.301, n 30). Stated at its true strength: the cell's elevation over the unconditional mean is arithmetic, not a state effect, because the cell excludes exactly one night (2026-10-29, the night with no modelled flip on record, -143.0bp over its 3 legs) and the state never varies inside the window. The potential edge is the object itself: a dealer-regime series whose discriminating cells (split, agree-above) are empty because the window is one regime, not because the candidate failed. **Cheapest forward check that arms it:** accrue the table nightly from Mon 2026-11-16 (the collector and the static-GEX module already run daily; the 2026-11-13 rows take their outcome at Monday's open), and grade the first 10 name-nights that populate the split or agree-above cells against the agree-below cell on the same table. Cost: one collector run per night, no new machinery.

**Lead 2, the index futures overnight drift as its own weak object (Build 3a).** On 1h bars over two years (2024-11-15 to 2026-11-13, 16:00 to 09:00 ET approximation, roll weeks flagged), the futures overnight segment runs ES +3.44bp (median +7.34bp, win 0.576, t 1.186, n 488) and NQ +5.35bp (median +9.20bp, win 0.564, t 1.308, n 486); excluding roll weeks, +2.00bp and +2.68bp. A real sign, a positive median, and no significance: a weak object, printed as one. **Cheapest forward check:** accrue one futures overnight field per night beside the falsifier tracker (two chart pulls per night) and re-read at 60 forward nights.

**Lead 3, the early Globex segment (Build 3b).** The futures move from the 16:00 ET open to the 18:00 ET open, known at 18:00 ET while the night still has 15.5 hours to run, splits the Focus basket's overnight legs by +192.41bp on ES (up segment nights +45.63bp, n 21, t 0.741; down segment nights -146.77bp, n 18, t -1.878) and +110.41bp on NQ, over the 39 of 48 window nights carrying both bars. No cell separates. Its timing is its limit and its use: it postdates the 16:00 cash close, so it can never gate the cash basket's entry; it could only drive a futures-side overlay or hedge timing on nights the basket is already held. **Cheapest forward check:** the same nightly accrual as Lead 2, read at 60 forward nights.

**Lead 4, KG-10's conditioner stays armed through its proxy, and its true variable now has a named source (Build 2).** The exchange-print route cannot produce imbalance (proven below, with the field-level evidence), so the executed closing-cross series built here (84 name-sessions) grades null and does not touch KG-10's conditioner, whose signal is the imbalance's sign. The true source is named as an external data item: the Nasdaq Order Imbalance Indicator (NOII) for Nasdaq-listed names. **Cheapest forward check:** the one already wired, SVS30 and intraday-sign fields on the falsifier tracker grading the down-day cross at 60 sessions from Mon 2026-11-16; if a NOII-class feed is ever held, the executed series and the KG-10 legs are already joined and waiting for it.

**What did not survive, stated plainly:** the futures expression of the Focus premium. The premium does not live in the index futures (Build 3, below): the futures carry the night's index share only, about +3 to +5bp and insignificant, while the basket's premium is name-level. No cost or capacity relief for this premium is available through ES or NQ on this evidence.

Extending the collector to the Focus names is the named route to a Focus table; nothing about the historical window can be backfilled, because the snapshots were never taken.

**Field limits, named on every row.** A12 is missing for 2026-10-29 (3 rows): the morning-map session history starts 2026-10-30, so those rows carry the null with its reason. A26 trigger basis is a named null on all 33 rows: trigger basis is a property of a forecast card (close-confirmed vs intraday), not of a chain snapshot, and no card population exists for these index nights in the window; the fused grade runs on the A12xA13 agreement state and A26 is carried unpopulated rather than fabricated. The 2026-11-13 rows (3) carry no outcome yet: their next session is Mon 2026-11-16. IWM outcomes come from the Alpaca SIP daily-bars route, named on the row, because the desk cache carries IWM hour bars only, ending 2026-10-31; SPY and QQQ outcomes come from the desk cache daily bars.

**First read of the fused candidate.** 30 graded legs. Unconditional overnight: +23.49bp (median +37.93bp, win 0.667, t 1.301). ## BUILD 2: TRUE CLOSING IMBALANCE

**The route test.** The falsifier tracker's exchange-print route (Alpaca SIP trades; closing cross = first Q-exchange trade with condition '6' in 15:59:59 to 16:00:04 ET) was pulled for KG-10's 6 names (NVDA, AVGO, AMD, AMAT, MU, PLTR) x the 14 sessions of KG-10's span (2026-10-21 to 2026-11-12), plus a pre-cross control window (15:55:00 to 15:59:58 ET) on every name-session. The closing-cross print was found on 84 of 84 name-sessions, exactly one print each.

**The route's limit, with the evidence.** Every trade record the route returns, in the cross window and in the control window alike, carries exactly the field set {c, i, p, s, t, x, z}: conditions, id, price, size, timestamp, exchange, tape. A sample record stands in ndb2_results.json. There is no indicative matched quantity, no imbalance quantity, and no imbalance message type anywhere in the route, and the pre-cross window returns ordinary trades with the same fields. SIP trade prints are executions: the closing cross prints after the imbalance has already been published and acted on, and the print records what executed, never what was imbalanced. A true closing-imbalance series cannot be constructed from this route, by construction. **The actual source, named as an external data item:** the exchange imbalance feeds. For Nasdaq-listed names (all six KG-10 names) that is the Nasdaq Order Imbalance Indicator (NOII), disseminated on Nasdaq TotalView-ITCH and as a standalone imbalance product; for NYSE-listed names, the NYSE order imbalance feed (imbalance messages in NYSE OpenBook and the Integrated Feed). The estate holds neither. Nothing was fabricated in their place.

**The series the route does yield, and KG-10's cross graded on it.** Executed closing-cross volume share of the session's total volume: 84 name-sessions, mean share 13.32 percent, median 11.63 percent. Joined to all 72 KG-10 legs. KG-10's down-day row, split at the down-day median share (0.1419): high-share legs +76.37bp (n 22, t 0.969) against low-share legs -18.07bp (n 21, t -0.18), spread +94.43bp. All legs, split at the overall median: spread +86.83bp, both cells' |t| below 1.0. No split separates, and the reason is structural, printed rather than buried: executed cross volume is unsigned, and the imbalance conditioner's signal is the sign. This null grades the executed series, not KG-10's conditioner, which remains exactly where KG-10 left it: a down-day ordering seen under two signed proxies, awaiting its registered forward check on the falsifier tracker's 60 sessions and, for the true variable, a NOII-class feed.

**Seat lens:** seven-lens mean p 0.487 on the claim as stated in NDB2-REGISTER; adversarial p(artifact) 0.70; action read top band 20-40 at 0.58. No grade was set by a seat.

## BUILD 3: FUTURES EXPRESSION OF THE OVERNIGHT DRIFT

**Routes tried, in order.** (1) CME or futures data on disk: an estate-wide find returned no futures series (the CME-named files are the CME Group equity's own desk records, not futures data). Route failure, recorded. (2) Stooq daily CSV for the continuous contracts: bot-walled (a JavaScript verify page, no data). Route failure, recorded. (3) Yahoo Finance chart API, ES=F and NQ=F continuous front-month: answered, and is the route used. **Contract-roll handling, stated:** Yahoo's continuous series splices the front contract at quarterly expiry (third Friday of March, June, September, December); roll dates are not separately observable inside this feed, so overnight windows falling in roll weeks are flagged on every row and every headline read is printed with and without them. No roll gap is silently counted as drift.

**Read (a): the futures overnight drift itself.** Long span, 1h bars, 2024-11-15 to 2026-11-13, overnight segment approximated as the 16:00 ET bar open to the next session's 09:00 ET bar open (the approximation is the label; the exact window needs the 15m bars below): ES +3.44bp (median +7.34bp, win 0.576, t 1.186, n 488; excluding 46 roll-week nights +2.00bp); NQ +5.35bp (median +9.20bp, win 0.564, t 1.308, n 486; excluding roll weeks +2.68bp). Exact span, 15m bars, the cash window exactly (16:00 ET open to next session 09:30 ET open), 48 nights, 2026-09-08 to 2026-11-13: ES +3.77bp (t 0.495); NQ -5.73bp (t -0.412). The index overnight drift is a small positive-median object that reaches significance on neither span nor either contract.

**Read (b): the basket's legs conditioned on the futures overnight sign.** Same 48 nights; the basket is the 17 of 29 Focus names carrying bars in the desk cache for this window (AAOI, ALAB, AMAT, AMD, ARM, AVGO, AXTI, CRDO, CRWV, FCEL, LITE, MU, NVDA, OKLO, PLTR, SNDK, WDC; the other 12 names have no bars in this window and are named in ndb3_results.json). The window is the premium's violent cold stretch: basket unconditional -31.96bp (t -0.713), and single nights run to -619bp and +689bp in the cache's own bars. Split by ES overnight sign: up nights +151.48bp (n 27, t 3.317) against down nights -267.81bp (n 21, t -5.522), spread +419.29bp, night correlation 0.683. Split by NQ sign: +191.27bp (n 24, t 4.254) against -255.19bp (n 24, t -5.936), spread +446.46bp, correlation 0.918. The split survives on the five mega-cap names alone (ES spread +345.99bp, NQ +370.98bp), so it is not a small-name artifact. It is also not a conditioner: the futures overnight return and the basket's overnight leg complete at the same 09:30 moment and are the same information read twice, which is what correlations of 0.68 and 0.92 say. **Verdict on the expression question:** the Focus premium has no futures-market expression with different cost or capacity on this evidence. What the futures would capture is the index share of the night, about +3 to +5bp and insignificant; the premium's remainder is name-level and stays in the names. Abyss A-2's named tradeable form is therefore answered in the negative on its first read, on data, not on argument.

**Seat lens:** seven-lens mean p 0.584 on the claim as stated in NDB3-REGISTER; adversarial p(artifact) 0.74; action read top band 20-40 at 0.54. No grade was set by a seat.
