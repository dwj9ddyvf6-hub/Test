## THE PROXY SERIES BUILT

DH1-CROSS-SERIES.jsonl holds 84 rows, one per name session for NVDA, AVGO, AMD, AMAT, MU, PLTR x the 14 KG-10 sessions, with no dropped rows. The closing cross print was found on 84 of 84 name sessions (first Q exchange trade with condition 6 in 15:59:59 to 16:00:04 ET on the Alpaca SIP trades route), and the Alpaca auctions endpoint (R8) reproduced the same cross price and size on 84 of 84, so the cross identification is verified by two independent Alpaca surfaces. Each row carries, all labeled PROXY where derived: the cross price and size; the cross volume share of the session total volume (desk 1Min cache RTH sum basis); the last non cross trade price before the cross window (last trade in 15:55:00 to 15:59:59 ET); the signed PROXY cross price impact in bp (cross price against that pre cross price); the session total volume; the intraday sign and intraday bp from the 1Min cache; PROXY SVS30 recomputed by KG-10's exact definition; and the overnight return on KG-10's bar basis (last RTH 1Min close to next session first RTH open), null only on the 2026-11-12 rows, whose next session is outside the KG-10 span, with the reason named on the row. The 2026-10-23 rows carry their hole spanning overnight value flagged hole_spanning_leg true and primary_span_included false, exactly as KG-10 treated that leg.

**PROXY cross volume share distribution (84 name sessions).** Mean 0.16988, median 0.14112, p25 0.10527, p75 0.21476, min 0.0313, max 0.42681. Basis note, printed so the numbers are never conflated: this share divides by the session's RTH volume summed from the 1Min cache, while NEW-DATA-BUILDS Build 2 divided by 1Day bar volume and printed mean 13.32 percent and median 11.63 percent. The executed cross is a large object either way, roughly one seventh of the session at the median on this basis. It is also unsigned, which remains the structural limit on every executed volume read.

**PROXY cross price impact distribution (84 name sessions).** Mean minus 0.23bp, median 0.0bp, min minus 34.993bp, max plus 36.432bp; 40 positive, 44 nonpositive, 3 exactly zero. The median cross printed exactly at the last pre cross trade price. The impact sign leg is therefore a weak PROXY by construction: most crosses clear at or near the prevailing price, and its sign is the direction the clearing price moved in the final seconds, not an imbalance side. The label mapping used in the read mirrors KG-10's SVS30 split at zero: impact above zero is the buy pressure PROXY, impact at or below zero is the sell pressure PROXY. Under the inventory pricing mechanism, a sell imbalance would be expected to push the cross price down (nonpositive impact) and then revert upward overnight, which is the ordering the read tests.

## METHOD AUTOPSY LINE

What produced the honest read here: the routes ledger was closed before the PROXY was built, so no PROXY result could quietly stand in for the true variable; the build verified its own cross identification on a second independent surface (R8, 84 of 84) before any read was taken; and the comparison cross was required to reproduce KG-10 cell for cell before the primary cross was believed, so the vanishing is attributable to the leg, not to a span or method drift.
