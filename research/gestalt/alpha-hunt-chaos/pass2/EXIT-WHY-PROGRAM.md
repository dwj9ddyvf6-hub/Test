## WHAT WAS BUILT

**The spec** (`EXIT-WHY-SPEC.md`, 12,442 bytes): population definition (stamped exit fills only; unstamped counted, never graded; lot-closure rows by dispatcher FIFO; broker-pool lots excluded forward, with the residual share printed), Vein 3's measurements carried unchanged (dir-signed post1/5/10 excess vs QQQ, exit-date clustered t, cut share, phase and segment definitions), the grade floor (post5 n at least 20 across at least 5 exit dates), verdicts (EARLY, WELL-TIMED, NO-SEPARATION, with the horizon sign-conflict demotion), a standing claim and falsifier per reason class fixed in advance, the five lead counters with their registered falsifiers, the delay log's fields, first-read dates with their estimation basis printed (census Fri 2026-11-21; pooled_close and unknown first grades Fri 2026-12-05; the strategy classes Fri 2026-12-19; L3 Fri 2026-12-05; L5 Fri 2026-11-28; L1, L2, L4 interim Fri 2026-12-19 with counts governing), and the book stream's arming condition (book fills join when they carry an exit_reason field of their own; until then the book's exits stay graded by their journal L-07 record).

**The grader** (`exit_why_grader.py`): recomputes the full stamped population from `state/dispatcher.jsonl*` on every run (no incremental state is trusted), builds lot closures by FIFO against each sleeve's own dispatcher entry lots, prices post-exit paths and delay shadows from the desk bar cache with the widened cache as fallback, appends newly priceable rows to `~/workspace/midna-book/exit-why-log.jsonl` and `~/workspace/midna-book/exit-delay-log.jsonl` (dedupe keys verified: a second identical run adds zero rows), rewrites `~/workspace/midna-book/exit-why-state.json`, and prints the close-review block. Crypto and option-contract rows are censused and logged, never graded (KG-4 quarantine).

**The shadow delay log**: one row per stamped equity exit fill per horizon (delay0 = exit session close, Vein 3's registered L3 shadow in close-print form; delay1 and delay3 = the +1 and +3 session closes the owner order specified), each carrying the dir-signed raw and excess move from the exit price to the shadow close. L3's falsifier applies to delay0 at 30 open-drive exits, as registered.

**The retrospective companion** (`exit_why_retro.py`): implements the inference rule above and writes `~/workspace/midna-book/exit-why-retro-read.json` (18 profile rows carried: 2 inferred, 16 bounded, every row basis-labeled). The forward grader never reads it; the two populations share no table without the basis printed on the row.

**Wiring**: the close review job `personal-quant-close-review` gained an EXIT-WHY GRADING section by cron.view, cron.update with the complete body, and projection verification: the regenerated projection is 17,553 bytes (was 16,201), every pre-existing section anchor present exactly once (ORIGINATED ITEM, EXECUTION GRADE FIRST, MANDATE METRICS, TA TOOLBOX, X STRATEGIES REPORT, G2 SHADOW RESOLUTION, G19 CALIBRATION DRILL), the new section present exactly once at the end, and four distinctive mid-body phrases each counted exactly once. From Mon 2026-11-16 the review runs the grader and carries its block, with grader errors recorded by exact error text, never skipped silently.
