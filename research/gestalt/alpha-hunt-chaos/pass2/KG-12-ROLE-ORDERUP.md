UNMAPPED is a residue class, not a role, and forms no feature.

Machinery: KG-1's, verbatim in discipline. Cells are pairs (order 2) and triples (order 3, three base features, or two base features plus FAMILY, as in KG-1) of (feature, class) assignments. A cell is TESTED at >=3 sleeves in and >=3 out. Statistic: sleeve-level mean outcome in minus out. Null: permutation over sleeve outcomes, cached by cell size (4,000 draws, seed 20261115; p counted by bisect over the sorted null, an identical count to KG-1's linear scan). Deflation: Benjamini-Hochberg q within each (outcome, order) family of tested cells. Verdicts: LEAD q <= 0.10; NOMINAL p <= 0.05 with q > 0.10; else NULL.

The central limit, printed before the results: replay outcomes are computed over shared bar windows and shared symbols, so the 65 replay sleeves' outcomes are not independent draws the way live fills are; the permutation null treats sleeves as exchangeable and therefore overstates separation for replay-heavy cells. Every object below prints its live/replay split, and the two poles that matter most (L1, L3) are majority-live or all-live sets. Read every replay-heavy diff as an upper bound on its forward form.
