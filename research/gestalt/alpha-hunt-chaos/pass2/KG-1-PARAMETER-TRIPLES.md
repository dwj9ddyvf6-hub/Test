## METHOD AND DATA BASES

Outcomes and tiers are Lane C's, computed by its run_variations.py definitions (fwd5 mean excess vs QQQ over each sleeve's priceable recorded fills; SCORED bar >=10 fills on >=3 dates). Features are semantic roles mined from pass2/work/param_maps.json (10,447 parameters, 339 sleeves) by the printed name rules in kg1_analysis.py: HOLD (hold/horizon constants, weeks x5), STOP (stop-named fractions in (0.0005, 0.6)), TARGET, LOOKBACK (max period/channel/window/SMA/EMA constant), RISK, NOTIONAL, MAXPOS, CADENCE, CAPITAL; carrier counts in the 21-sleeve population run 3 (TARGET) to 21 (CADENCE, CAPITAL), which is itself the cross-sleeve finding: the estate has no shared parameter namespace, so most constants cannot form cross-sleeve classes at all. Classes: carrier median split LOW/HIGH plus ABSENT. Statistic: sleeve-level difference of mean outcomes, permutation null cached by cell size (4,000 draws, seed 20261115), BH q within each (outcome, order) family. Fill-level companions were recomputed on the bar cache as widened by Unblock Lane 1 during this run (151 symbols vs Lane C's 51), which is why companion fill counts exceed Lane C's priceable counts for the same sleeves (e.g. beatendown 17 vs 2); the sleeve-level outcomes under test are Lane C's and are unaffected.
