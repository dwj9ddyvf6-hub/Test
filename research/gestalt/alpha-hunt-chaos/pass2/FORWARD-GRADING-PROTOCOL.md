## Standing mechanics (all classes)

1. **Evidence classes never merge silently.** Forward rows grade against forward rows. Any print combining forward rows with the retrospective IN-SAMPLE cohorts names both classes and both counts (the tag schema's rule, GESTALT-AXIS-TAGS.md).
2. **The grading rule, uniform:** within a class sitting, cells group by joint state; a cell reports n, hits, hit rate, excess versus the class cohort's base rate, z, and a BH q computed across all cells of the same order inside the class at that sitting. A cell separates at n >= 20 AND BH q <= 0.10. On panel-class cells (bp outcomes), separation additionally requires |night-cluster t| >= 3 over at least 20 distinct nights, the squeeze's panel rule carried forward unchanged.
3. **When grades print:** at the Friday B5 of the week a floor is crossed. A cell is graded at first crossing and re-graded at each doubling of its n. A class whose accrual act was skipped in a week reports the skip in that week's print, never a silent zero.
4. **A missing tag is a defect, not a zero.** Under the tag schema's rule 2, an omitted tag on a graded call is a named defect in the grading entry with the same standing as a missing anti-Midna control line; ns (not stated) is a value, omission is not.
5. **Dates are arithmetic.** Night counts run on trading nights from Mon 2026-11-16 at five nights a week on a plain calendar (holidays ignored, so each date is the Friday of the week the count lands, read at that week's B5): night 5 = Fri 2026-11-20; night 10 = Fri 2026-11-27; night 15 = Fri 2026-12-04; night 20 = Fri 2026-12-11; night 25 = Fri 2026-12-18; night 40 = Fri 2027-01-08; night 60 = Fri 2027-02-05; night 80 = Fri 2027-03-05. Lane 8's measured accrual rates are the inputs: the night study produced 40 graded calls over 8 nights (5.0 per night) and the full log 201 over 9 days; the dates below use the night-study rate unless the class names a faster source.

## What this protocol does not do

It does not grade any combination on seat output, on synthetic rows, or on pooled shrinkage (Lane 8's pooling rule failed its falsifier and stays unadopted; the interim standard of raw rate, n and Wilson interval governs every print under this protocol). It does not promote any lead: a separating forward cell enters the estate's normal falsifier machinery as a candidate, with its own registered forward check, exactly as Lane B's leads did. And it does not retire the retrospective grades: the IN-SAMPLE reads stand as hypothesis-generation evidence at their printed strength, and the forward grades print beside them, never averaged into them.
