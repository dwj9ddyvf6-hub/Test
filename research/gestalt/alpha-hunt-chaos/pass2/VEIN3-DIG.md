# VEIN 3 DIG: THE FLEET'S EXITS, DECOMPOSED AS EVENTS

Vein 3 of the Edge-Find Method Ledger's vein map (the exit-and-phase vein: finds 8, 11, 13). KG-4 decomposed the fleet's entries and held phases, KG-7 and KG-13 tested exit rules on replay streams, but the fleet's ACTUAL exits had never been decomposed as events. This dig does that over the desk's live records: dispatcher fill history, the broker order history (8,886 filled orders of the 8,961 pulled), the pooled FIFO reconstruction of Unblock Lane 1, and the midna-book ledger. Register: `VEIN3-REGISTER.jsonl`, 6,274 rows, one row per exit event (a lot closure), plus the book's 4 realised trades. Build: `work/vein3_build.py`; aggregates: `work/vein3_analyze.py` and `work/vein3_cells.json`; seats: `work/vein3_seats.py`, runs in `work/vein3_seat_runs.json`.

Bases used throughout, printed wherever they bind. FILL level: the 1,526 dispatcher exit fills; exit-side facts (segment, sleeve, family) and post-exit paths live here. LOT level: the 6,270 dispatcher register rows; entry-dependent facts (phase, realized bp, profile consistency) live here and are POOLED-BASIS wherever attribution is POOLED_LOT, meaning the entry is the pooled broker account's FIFO lot, not the exiting sleeve's own recorded entry. Post-exit paths are dir-signed returns from the exit print to the close 1, 5, and 10 sessions after the exit date on the symbol's own bar calendar, excess vs QQQ, priced on the desk bar cache with the widened cache as fallback. Bars end 2026-11-14, so post5 is priceable only for exits on or before about 2026-11-06 and post10 only for exits on or before about 2026-10-29; every cell prints its priceable n.

## POTENTIAL EDGE FOUND

**The separation rule, fixed before results were read, returns ZERO leads.** A cell had to show n of at least 20, at least 5 distinct exit dates, a clustered t (cluster = exit date) of at least 2.0 in absolute value on the 5-session post-exit excess, and the 1-session and 10-session mean excesses carrying the same sign. No phase, segment, trigger, family, or sleeve cell meets it. Per the KG-10 precedent, the strongest sub-threshold cells are printed below at their true strength, each with the cheapest forward check that would arm it. The seats agreed with the arithmetic: on all five objects the seven-lens panels put the probability the claim is a standing property at 0.15 to 0.28, the adversarial seat put the probability of a pure artifact at 0.67 to 0.80, and the actionability score still landed in the 40 to 60 band (material: worth a registered forward check) on all five, because the checks cost almost nothing. Seats never set grades; the grades below are the record's.

**L1. aster_saty_research exits precede large rises (the strongest cell in the dig, and a concentrated one).** Fill level, equity: 35 exit fills priceable at every horizon, realized mean on the pooled basis only -119.9bp, then the names rose after the exits: post1 excess +634.9bp (clustered t 1.36), post5 +2,818.9bp (t 4.06), post10 +2,303.8bp (t 17.04), and 34 of 35 exits were followed by a 5-session rise (cut share 0.971). It fails the rule on clusters: the 35 fills sit on only 3 exit dates, 2026-10-29 and 2026-10-30 carrying nearly all of them, in small and mid cap names (VSAT, CORZ, TWST, TSEM, SOXL, AXTI, TTMI, FFIV) that rallied broadly in early November. True strength: a real, large, two-session event; unproven as a standing property of the sleeve. Cheapest forward check: a forward exit-timing log on this sleeve (one extra record per exit in the nightly grading: post1, post5, post10 excess as bars accrue). Falsifier: over the next 20 priceable forward exits, mean post5 excess at or below 0, or cut share at or below 0.50, retires it. Seats: lens mean p(real) 0.154, adversarial p(artifact) 0.73, actionability band 40 to 60 with 0.64 of the mass.

**L2. Exits that close a 1-night-old lot precede rises (the same event from the lot side; the framing is what is on trial).** Lot level, pooled basis: 91 rows whose closed lot was aged exactly 1 night; 69 priceable at post5: post1 +1,063.0bp (n 85), post5 +3,320.7bp (clustered t 0.41 on 5 clusters), post10 +2,064.7bp (n 64), cut share 0.971. Composition is the warning: 63 of the 91 rows are L1's sleeve exiting, 64 of 91 exit on 2026-10-29/30, and the closed lots were entered mostly by aster_trader_donchian55 (33 rows) and tensor_swing (19). The t of 0.41 says the cluster means disagree wildly even while the pooled mean is huge. Cheapest forward check: the same exit log keyed by lot age at closure for every sleeve; the framing earns its own life only if NON-saty 1-night closures also precede rises. Falsifier: the non-saty 1-night subset's mean post5 excess at or below 0 over its next 20 priceable closures retires the framing and leaves L1 as a sleeve observation. Seats: lens mean 0.164, adversarial 0.80, actionability 40 to 60 (0.63 of the mass).

**L3. Open-drive exits lean early; midday and close-hour exits do not.** Fill level, equity: exits executed 09:30 to 10:30 ET (307 fills, 171 priceable at post5) are followed by post1 +165.2bp (n 274), post5 +450.3bp (clustered t 1.68, 9 exit dates), post10 +520.2bp (n 47), cut share 0.532. The same fleet's midday exits (821 fills, 275 priceable) sit at post5 -3.3bp (t 0.25) and its close-hour exits at -68.9bp (n 82, t 0.63). The sign pattern is consistent across all three horizons only for the open drive, and it fails the rule on the clustered t. Composition caveat printed on the object: open-drive exits are different sleeves and names than midday exits, so this may be a who-exits-when fact rather than a when fact. Cheapest forward check: a shadow delay log: for each forward open-drive exit, record the same day's midday and close prints as shadow exit prices alongside the actual; falsifier: shadow minus actual at or below 0 over the next 30 open-drive exits retires it. Seats: lens mean 0.193, adversarial 0.67, actionability 40 to 60 (0.79 of the mass, the widest agreement of the five).

**L4. confluence exits avoid further loss at 5 sessions (the avoidance side).** Fill level: 52 exit fills, 27 priceable at post5 over 3 exit dates: post1 +287.3bp (n 32, a bounce against the exits), post5 -275.0bp (clustered t -2.95, with the exits), post10 unpriceable (n 0; the exits are too recent for the bar window), cut share 0.333, realized mean -95.2bp (n 30). It fails the rule on clusters (3) and on the missing post10 leg. True strength: the strongest clustered t in the dig on the avoidance side, on a thin date base. Cheapest forward check: the forward exit log on this one sleeve; falsifier: mean post5 excess at or above 0 over the next 20 priceable exits retires it. Seats: lens mean 0.280 (the highest of the five), adversarial 0.68, actionability 40 to 60 (0.90 of the mass).

**L5. Same-session closures bounce for one session, then fade hard (a shape, not a trade).** Lot level, pooled basis: 5,717 rows closing a lot opened the same session, realized mean -72.3bp (n 5,549). Post-exit: post1 +110.3bp (n 5,537), post5 -154.4bp (n 3,450, clustered t 0.86, 8 dates), post10 -1,047.6bp (n 1,823), cut share 0.429. The shape says the fleet's intraday exits are directionally right over 10 sessions and roughly one session early against a capitulation bounce. It fails the rule on the clustered t and carries a concentration warning: 1,810 of the 5,717 rows are dated 2026-10-27, an import-heavy day. Cheapest forward check: none beyond the standing exit log (the post1 vs post10 shape is a free read off L1 to L4's logs); falsifier: post10 mean excess at or above 0 over the next 500 same-session closures retires the shape. Seats: lens mean 0.271, adversarial 0.69, actionability 40 to 60 (0.84 of the mass).

**Structural leads (the record itself; these are the dig's most actionable output).**

- **S1. No exit in the fleet's records states why it happened.** Across 1,526 dispatcher exit fills, 339 per-sleeve journals, and 8,886 broker orders, a strategy reason (stop, target, horizon, signal flip, manual) appears nowhere. The dispatcher's reason strings are operational (attempts exhausted, market closed hold, reconcile halt). The drained_terminal class, the one operational class that carries a reason onto an exit, matches ZERO exit fills. Consequence: the WHY half of this dig's question is ungradable from the desk's own records, for every sleeve, until an exit-reason field exists on exit intents. That is the same class of cure as the intent-log fields landed on 2026-11-15, and it is the cheapest high-value instrumentation the desk does not have.
- **S2. Entry fills exist for only 42 of 339 sleeves, so exit attribution is forced onto the pooled broker book.** Dispatcher entry fills: 6,708, held almost entirely by 11 sleeves (tensor_swing alone 3,633). The remaining sleeves' entries were never recorded as fills, while their exits were (imported from the broker from 2026-10-27). Of 270,075.61 units of exit-fill quantity, own-record lots cover 9,263.00 (3.4 percent, 4 register rows); the pooled FIFO covers 187,688.01 (69.5 percent); 73,124.59 (27.1 percent) cannot be attributed at all. The desk's own virtual-book dual-read events say the same thing from inside (kind: no_sleeve_position against nonzero broker quantity). Until entry fills are recorded for all sleeves, every sleeve-level hold-phase claim about exits rests on a pooled accounting convention.
- **S3. Registered exit profiles exist for 61 of 339 sleeves, and for almost none of the sleeves that actually exit.** Lane C registered a stop, target, or horizon for 61 sleeves; the sleeves producing exit rows carry one in only 3 cases (red_day_reversal 13 rows, rule_1w_cyan_mom_adx 3, bta_onebar_qqq 2). Of 6,270 dispatcher exit rows, 6,252 (99.7 percent) are NO_REGISTERED_PROFILE; 16 are WITHIN_PROFILE; 2 are CONSISTENT_WITH_STOP; zero are CONSISTENT_WITH_TARGET or CONSISTENT_WITH_HORIZON. The KG-7 question (are actual stop exits early or well-timed) therefore has a 2-row answer on the live record: ungradable as registered. The parameters exist in sleeve code (the KG-11 namespace work found them); the register extraction read only sleeves.json fields. That gap, not the exits, is what blocks the question.

## WHAT THE DIG ANSWERS, AND WHAT IT CANNOT

The question was whether there is potential edge in WHEN and WHY the fleet exits, separable from entry edge, including any trigger class systematically early or late. The answer on this record: WHEN shows two sub-threshold separable patterns (L3's open-drive earliness across 9 exit dates, the broadest base in the dig; L1/L2's saty and 1-night concentration on 3 dates) and one avoidance-side pattern (L4), none proven, all cheaply checkable forward, and the seats concur (probably artifact, worth the near-free check). WHY cannot be answered at all: no exit states a reason (S1), the one stated closure class (pooled-lot closure, 5,802 of 6,270 rows) is an accounting event rather than a decision, and no trigger class can be graded early or late until exit reasons are recorded. The fleet's own hold phases at exit are likewise unrecoverable for 99.9 percent of exits (S2). The dig's verdict on its own vein: the exit side of the fleet is running uninstrumented; the potential edge visible in timing (L1 to L5) is smaller than the edge being lost to not being able to see exits at all (S1 to S3).

## METHOD, SEATS, AND VERIFICATION

Attribution order per exit fill, against one unified lot book so no entry lot is counted twice: first the sleeve's own dispatcher lots FIFO (the desk's virtual-book view), then the fill's broker FIFO trips (the pooled fallback), then a residual UNATTRIBUTED row with its reason printed on the row. Broker-only lot consumption (1,870 trips with no dispatcher record) is not tracked against the lot book; that approximation can move pooled rows' entry attribution and never moves the exit census. Profile consistency is an INFERRED field, computed only where a registered profile exists, labeled as inference in the register and in S3. Separation rule and clustering as stated at the top. Seats: 5 objects, 7 lens predicates plus 1 adversarial predicate plus 1 actionability score each, 45 questions in 5 requests on gpt-6-luna via the Decisions harness, spend $0.001861 total; no 429 was hit; checkpoint `work/vein3_seat_runs.json`. Seats set no grades.

Verification on the final files: register recount 6,274 rows (6,270 dispatcher + 4 book); fill-level regrouping returns exactly 1,526; quantity reconciliation closes to the cent (270,075.61); every census count in this document was recomputed from the final register, not from the build's intermediate output.
