# PASS 2, LANE D: RESEARCH PAIR CHECKS, EXECUTED

Checks executed on 98 of 153 pairs by recomputation from the two studies' own records (register five-field reads plus extraction digests, both derived from the primary sources), with outcomes: 29 corroborated by recomputation, 0 contradicted, 69 indeterminate (check executed, no identical labeled quantity printed in both records for the test to bind to), and 55 not executed, each with its exact missing input named in its row.

## Method

Pass 1 seated the 153 pairs and printed a deciding check for each, then left the 123 mechanical pairs' recomputation checks unrun. Pass 2 runs them. The recomputation standard is the checks' own: the shared quantity, recomputed from both primary records on one denominator, must agree in sign and rough magnitude (ratio within 1.67, or exact). A corroboration is credited only when the SAME labeled quantity is printed in both records and the values agree; a contradiction only when that same quantity disagrees in sign or by an order of magnitude. Seat scores were never used as evidence; they are printed beside each verdict only to show where the seat and the recomputation diverge. Seven first-pass candidate disagreements were adjudicated by hand and every one proved to be two different quantities sharing a unit word, so the contradicted count is zero by recomputation, not by threshold. Where a pair's check demands an input the records do not contain (a dataset, a forward window, a live artifact), the row is NOT EXECUTED and the missing input is named exactly.
