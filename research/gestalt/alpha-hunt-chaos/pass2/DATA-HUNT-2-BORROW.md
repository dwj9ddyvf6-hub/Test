## POTENTIAL EDGE FOUND

**The QLD monthly inversion SURVIVES borrow pricing at both horizons, on both streams, by a wide margin.** The landed borrow fee for QLD is 0.7690 percent per year (Interactive Brokers, snapshot 2026-11-15, 1,500,000 shares available). At that fee the carry cost of a short is 1.53bp over a 5-session hold and 3.05bp over a 10-session hold, against gross inverted excess of +239.9bp and +401.8bp. Borrow is a rounding error in this trade, not its risk.

Reprice table, QLD fee basis (fee 0.7690 percent per year; carry equals fee divided by 252, times hold sessions). Basis label, printed once and binding on every number in this section: the fee is a 2026-11-15 snapshot applied to the October to November 2026 graded windows. It is a carry estimate, not a historical series. No lawful free route to a historical daily fee series for those windows exists; the routes ledger below is the evidence.

That is a 96x to 157x rise. The dig's other cost references sit in the same small range (a 6bp grader exit haircut, a measured 5.8bp auction friction), so on the cost side the lead is not fragile. What remains fragile is unchanged from the dig: BH q 0.267 across the 35-stream family, so this is a carry check passed on a WATCH-grade lead, not a promotion. The forward shadow grade named in the dig is still the deciding test.

## 2. ROUTES LEDGER (every route actually attempted, 2026-11-15)

**(a1) Interactive Brokers public shortstock file, direct.** IB publishes its whole stock loan file free, pipe delimited, header SYM, CUR, NAME, CON, ISIN, REBATERATE, FEERATE, AVAILABLE, at ftp2.interactivebrokers.com (and ftp3), file usa.txt, public user shortstock with a blank password, per IBKB article 2024. Attempted three ways from this host. FAILED on this host, exact failures: direct curl FTP timed out (curl 28, Operation timed out after 20003 milliseconds with 0 bytes received); a raw TCP connect to port 21 on both ftp2 and ftp3 raised TimeoutError; through the host egress proxy both hosts returned curl 52, Empty reply from server; an HTTPS request to ftp2 also returned curl 52 with 0 bytes. This is a route failure on this host's network, not evidence about the file, and it matches a prior independent probe of the same feed that found ftp3 silent and ftp2 serving. The mirror leg below is the genuinely different route to the same file, and it landed.

**(a2) The same IB file, mirror leg. LANDED.** ChartExchange and CompaniesMarketCap both republish the IB file and state on the page that the figures come from Interactive Brokers. Fresh fetches on 2026-11-15 (both pages crawled within a minute of the fetch, 23:44 to 23:45 UTC) gave: QLD 0.7690 percent per year, 1,500,000 shares available (CompaniesMarketCap; a 49-day-old search snippet of the same page showed 0.5080 percent and 3,800,000 shares and is superseded by the fresh fetch). QQQ 0.26 percent per year, 10,000,000 shares available, headline as of 2026-11-15 18:31:11 EST with the latest dated reading 2026-11-13 18:36:27 EST at 0.26 percent, 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-15 being weekend days (ChartExchange), cross-checked the same evening at 0.2646 percent and more than 10,000,000 shares on CompaniesMarketCap. The IB file's own date stamp is therefore 2026-11-13, the last trading day, carried in a file republished on 2026-11-15. DH2-BORROW-SERIES.jsonl is built from this leg.

**(b) FINRA short interest. NOT A FEE SOURCE.** Free, Rule 4560, twice a month. Its published layout is Settlement Date, Symbol, Market Class, Current Shares Short, Previous Shares Short, Avg Daily Share Volume, Days to Cover. That is position data, shares sold short and not yet covered. No borrow fee, rebate, utilization or availability field exists in it.

**(c) SEC fails-to-deliver. NOT A FEE SOURCE.** Free. The SEC page, read in full, lists the file fields as SETTLEMENT DATE, CUSIP, SYMBOL, QUANTITY (FAILS), DESCRIPTION, PRICE, and states that fails can occur on long and short sales and are not necessarily the result of short selling. It is a settlement fail balance. No fee field exists. Latest file listed at fetch time: October 2026, first half.

**(d) Alpaca. FLAGS ONLY, NO FEE.** Live API calls on 2026-11-15 through the estate credentials pattern (desk3.run_live.credential_env from muse-desk-v3, vault surrogate route, values never printed), GET /v2/assets/{symbol} on the paper base. QLD: shortable true, easy_to_borrow true, borrow_status easy_to_borrow, marginable true. QQQ: identical flags. The full asset object key list contains no fee or rate field of any kind. Alpaca's own documentation, as quoted in the research read for this route, states it does not currently provide hard-to-borrow rates via its API; its public pages state zero borrow fees on easy-to-borrow names, which is an Alpaca account term, not a market fee series. Useful as an availability confirmation for both names, useless as a fee source.

**(e) TastyTrade. NO PUBLISHED SURFACE.** Its support page, Hard-to-Borrow (HTB) Fees and Share Availability, read in full, states the HTB rate is displayed only during order entry in the platform after clicking Review and Send, as an annualized rate, and that the history of HTB fees paid appears only in an account's History tab. No public file, page or API field publishing per symbol borrow rates was found.

**(f) The paid walls, printed as their pages state them. None purchased, no accounts created.** Ortex, on its own pricing page: Basic 49 dollars per month with delayed short interest, cost to borrow and days to cover; Advanced 149 dollars per month with real-time short interest and cost to borrow; history and API access sit behind those tiers. S3 Partners, on its AWS Marketplace listing: Institutional pricing variable based on the number of users within your enterprise, retail users directed to contact the vendor; the listing names Offer Rate, Bid Rate and Last Rate financing fields and coverage since 2015, with no public price and no free tier. IHS Markit, now S&P Global Market Intelligence Securities Finance: a contracted subscription dataset; partner pages state contracting procedures with S&P Global are required and give a contact address instead of any price or free access.

**Historical fee series, stated explicitly.** No lawful free route to a historical daily borrow fee series for the graded windows (October to November 2026) exists among the routes attempted. The IB file is a snapshot and retains no history. The free mirrors keep only days: IBorrowDesk free pages show the past 7 days of readings and state that full history is a paid Supporter feature, and the ChartExchange QQQ table fetched for this hunt lists readings back to 2026-11-12 only. FINRA and SEC carry no fee at any date. Alpaca exposes no rate at any date. The vendors that do hold history (S3 states coverage since 2015, Ortex history sits in its paid tiers, S&P Global Securities Finance is contracted) are all behind the paid walls above. A historical series can be built lawfully from here only two ways: start archiving the free IB snapshot daily from today, or pay a vendor. Neither was done in this hunt; the reprice therefore carries the current-fee basis label.
