# Q07 Graveyard Retests and the s_wait Execution Policies

Fire-stream program, Sunday 2026-11-15. Tier A standard: extreme rigor, numbers at true strength, nothing promoted below the separation bar.

## Method

Scope. The 66 Q07 graveyard ids are the rows of pass2/GRAVEYARD-REGISTER.jsonl with record_class RECORD-RETESTABLE, cheapest_forward_check starting with "rebuild the external fire stream", and id starting with s_q07 (66 ids: s_q07_ins2, s_q07_wiki, and 64 s_q07w ids). The six s_wait ids are graded under their recorded shared entry policy from the md2 lz_engine config preserved in research_notes/sleeve-census-20261029/crosswalk.json.

Id grammar (engine truth, muse-desk-v3/desk3/q07_engine.py). s_q07w_{fam}{horizon}_{band}_{filters}: fam c is cyan, g is green, s is swing; horizon 52 or 78 weeks (the sleeve exit horizon; it does not enter the fwd5/fwd10 grading); band mi is micro (mcap under $300M), sm is small ($300M to $2B), ml is midlarge (over $2B). Filters use the engine _filters_pass semantics: ins2 is insider_buys >= 2 (flat fire field, then tags); cheap5 is the last close as of the signal date < $5.00; deep70 is close <= 0.70 x the maximum high of the trailing 252 sessions (engine minimum 20 bars); mom6 is close > close 126 sessions ago; vix20 is VIX >= 20 on the signal date; tierA is tags.tier_a true or tags.tier equal to A (absent tier evidence fails closed, as in the engine). The two non-weekly ids carry no family, horizon, or band token: s_q07_ins2 is read as the single filter ins2 over the whole stream with no band check, and s_q07_wiki is read as wiki coverage (wcov: tags.wiki_cov true or wiki_views > 0, fail closed when no wiki evidence is stamped) over the whole stream with no band check. Both readings are named here because the ids themselves record nothing more.

Fire stream. state/ingest/fires.json (source desk-fires-composer-v1 reconstruction) plus every archive fires.json under state/ingest/archive/2026-11-1{2,3,4,5}/ were pooled (138 raw rows) and deduplicated on (sleeve, symbol, signal_date), giving the consolidated stream of 46 fires written to q07_fires.jsonl with all stamped fields preserved. The archive copies dated 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-15 are exact re-publications of the same 46. The 2026-11-12 and 2026-11-13 archive folders hold no fires.json; they hold rule_fires.json (43 rule fires from a different producer), which is not part of the composer stream and was not merged. Coverage against the composer itself: the composer file carries 46 fires and the consolidated stream carries 46, so fires found equal the composer's own count, with zero additional fires in the archives and zero stamped rows lost (the file's not_stamped list is empty).

Candidates. Rows sharing (family, symbol, signal_date) differ only in the addressee sleeve (verified field by field: zero differences in any stamped field), so each variant's candidate pool collapses those rows to one candidate per (family, symbol, signal_date). The 46 stream rows are 10 such candidates and 9 distinct (symbol, signal_date) events. A variant's pool is the candidates of its family; the band check then applies to the fire's stamped band, normalized midlarge to ml and small to sm. Every fire in the stream carries a stamped band, so no band check was ungradable.

Bars and VIX. Price filters and all entry and forward grading use the daily bars in pass2/work/unblock2_bars.pkl (the widened cache pickle), sliced as of the signal date for filters; bars after the signal date never enter a filter. The pickle's daily QQQ is byte-identical in closes to the desk cache QQQ 1Day file on all 515 dates, and the session calendar is the QQQ bar calendar (last session 2026-11-13). The pickle's secondary QQQ agrees within rounding except 2026-11-12 and 2026-11-13 (2026-11-13: 684.70 in daily and cache vs 679.89 in secondary); no entry or graded horizon lands on those dates, so the choice does not move any number below. VIX is the desk cache cache/bars/VIX/1Day.jsonl close series (1,262 rows, 2021-10-25 to 2026-10-30). VIX on 2026-10-30 is 19.42. There is no VIX close on this box for 2026-11-06 or 2026-11-13, so vix20 is UNGRADABLE on those fires, never assumed. Bar coverage for the 8 fire symbols: TSLA 515 daily bars (2024-10-25 to 2026-11-13) and CRML 53 (2026-09-01 to 2026-11-13) exist in the pickle; ABT, MNDY, TLN, TMDX, WMT, and XNCR have no daily bars in the pickle or in the desk cache. A price filter that needs a missing bar series is scored UNGRADABLE for that fire with the datum named, separately from a true fail; the engine itself fails closed in both cases, and this retest keeps the two apart so a data gap is never reported as a signal result. mom6 with 126 or fewer bars sliced as of the signal is likewise UNGRADABLE (the close 126 sessions before the signal does not exist in the source), which binds CRML (43 bars as of 2026-10-30).

Entry and grading (Q07). Per the family rule: entry is the first session strictly after the signal date, within 5 sessions, on which QQQ closes down; entry price is that session's close of the symbol. A passing candidate whose window has not fully elapsed in the data (all 2026-11-13 signals, whose windows start after the last bar on 2026-11-13) is counted as undetermined, not as a policy no-entry. Each entry is graded by fwd5 and fwd10 excess vs QQQ in bp from the entry close over the same sessions, computed only where both series reach the horizon. Date-clustered t treats each entry date's mean excess as one observation (t is undefined, printed n/a, with fewer than 2 entry dates). Verdicts under the hunt's fixed rules: LEAD requires date-clustered t >= 2.0 at fwd5 or fwd10 with n >= 10 entries on >= 3 dates; WATCH requires n >= 5 entries; NO-FIRE is 0 entries with the binding filter named (the filter that killed the most candidates; ties break by id token order and are named in the text); UNGRADABLE-BLOCKED is an empty family/band pool or a pool whose grading is blocked by a named missing datum. The fixed rules name no tier for 1 to 4 entries, so those rows print their numbers at true strength under the label THIN (below WATCH). Benjamini-Hochberg q is printed across the 66-variant family for any variant reaching nominal t >= 2: no variant produced a computable clustered t at all (every graded variant has entries on a single date or none), so no nominal t >= 2 exists and no q is printed; the BH family is empty on this stream, which is itself the finding.
