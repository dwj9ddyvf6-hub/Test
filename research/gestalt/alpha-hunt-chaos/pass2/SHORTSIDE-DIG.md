# THE SHORT SIDE OF EVERYTHING: PER-STREAM INVERSION, AVOIDANCE CELLS, AND THE NEGATIVE-SPACE CENSUS

Register: SHORTSIDE-REGISTER.jsonl, 409 rows (315 INVERSION rows, one per graded stream; 94 NEGATIVE-OBJECT rows, one per dispositioned negative object). Compute: work/shortside_compute.py, work/shortside_build.py; seat lens: work/shortside_seats.py, runs in work/shortside_seat_runs.json.

SCOPE: every graded sleeve stream in the Unblock registers (41 live-record, 274 replay), inverted honestly; every graded avoidance cell named so far (SW-10475, the KG-12 DMAC pole, LS5, the LINK absorption stream) given its tradeable form; and a census of every significantly negative object in the registers. Method fixed before results were read: per event, inverted selection equals the negation of base excess versus QQQ (the inverted stream's universe term is the sign-matched benchmark, exactly Lane 9's anti-Midna decomposition, where the anti-portfolio's +268.80bp raw decomposed into a +272.13bp universe term and a selection term of -3.34bp, t -0.08). The Unblock 2 grader's stored inversion subtracts the long benchmark from flipped returns, which double-counts the universe term for a short stream; it is not used anywhere here. Separation is judged on date-clustered statistics at fwd5 (events on one date are one observation), with the bar at n >= 10 events, >= 3 dates, clustered t >= 2.0, and Benjamini-Hochberg q printed across the 35 streams that meet the testability bar. Integrity: the live recompute reproduces Unblock 1's hold profiles exactly on every stamped sleeve (0 mismatches); on replay, 19 Tensor batch consumers whose single entries were priced through Unblock 2's fallback bundle route are not reproducible from the stored daily book and are carried by exact negation of the register's own base stats, flagged in-row (21 rows carry the pricing note). Basis limit, printed once: sleeve inversions are computed on the close-to-close hold profile, the same basis the lanes graded the long side on; inverting a sleeve's exit-rule path properly requires flipping its barrier logic, which no lane has built, so an inversion lead here is a claim about the entry stream, not about the sleeve's exit-sim P&L.

## THE BURST FINDING (the dig's structural result)

Every giant inversion in this estate is a burst. The five streams with fwd5 event t >= 2.5 on inversion that are neither leads nor near leads (verdict BURST-ONLY in the register) all collapse under date clustering: s_mn_hourly_1w (event t 89.38, clustered t 0.83, 3 dates; and its fwd1 inversion is -694.5bp because the long earns its night-1 spike, so the object is a hold-length mismatch, already Unblock 1's lead, not a short), the three DMAC variants (event t about 10.5, clustered t 0.83 to 0.84, 3 dates, contract basis), and red_day_reversal (event t 5.66, clustered t 0.21, 3 dates; its fwd10 inversion is -2,632.0bp, so even its sign is horizon-unstable). The horizon scan tells the same story at fwd1: donchian's event-level separation (+797.5bp, t 4.68) is 13 events on 2 dates (clustered t 0.35), capitulation's is 14 events on 2 dates (t 0.20), ts_momentum_decile's is 14 events on a single date. The seat panel's strongest substantive agreement after DMAC is with this framing (lens mean 0.806 on the claim that the estate's negative streams are date-burst phenomena whose inversions cannot be graded as streams on the existing record). Practical consequence: the short side of this estate will be built from forward date accrual (tensor_swing first, the QLD pair's forward fires second), not from inverting the loudest in-sample losers.
