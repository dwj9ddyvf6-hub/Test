### Look A, first-hour survival conditioning: registration, stated before the numbers

Question: is the signal's edge concentrated in entries that survive their first hour without touching the stop, which would make the expression an entry-timing strategy rather than a dead one? Method: one uniform walk for all 21 fires, 1Min bars from the fire's fired_at stamp through ET date 2026-11-13, first touch of the stamped stop or target wins (a bar touching both would be reported ambiguous; none occurred), exit at spread intrinsic at the touched level, R on the retired resolver's denominator. Split: SURVIVED if no bar in (fired_at, fired_at + 60 minutes] touches the stop, NOT-SURVIVED otherwise. Outcomes graded per side: realized intraday R, and MFE_FRAC, the maximum over bars strictly before the resolving bar of (bar high minus entry) divided by (target minus entry), the fraction of the target distance the underlying covered before the position resolved. Pre-set separation margins: a side separates only if its mean realized R exceeds the pooled mean by at least 0.25R, or its mean MFE_FRAC exceeds the pooled mean by at least 0.25, with side n at least 5. Kill condition: if neither side separates on either outcome, the entry-timing form of the rescue is closed, the expression stays resolved-against, and the mirage's remaining routes are the already-dated settlement reruns and the premium arm's registered forward evaluation only. Cross-checks fixed in advance: the covered 10's first touches must reproduce conv_expression_intraday_results.json, and the uncovered 11's holding-window first touches must reproduce conv43_uncovered_results.json.
