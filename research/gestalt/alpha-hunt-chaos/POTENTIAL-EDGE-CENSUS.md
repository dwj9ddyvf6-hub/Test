## THE MEASURE (stated first, because it governs everything below)

Success in this program is POTENTIAL EDGE found and armed, not proven trades. Proof requires a forward window and a time machine; nobody honest can produce it today. The hunt's product is candidates whose evidence survives honest deflation at their true trial count, whose mechanism is named, whose falsifier is registered, and whose forward accrual is wired so the window can decide. Every object below is graded by that measure and no other. A candidate that fails deflation is not hidden and not killed: it is printed as a lead or a resolved negative, with its numbers.

## RUBRIC (mechanical; seat output never enters the arithmetic)

Each scored candidate gets five fields, taken ONLY from numbers printed in its own source file:

- **E, deflated evidence (0-3):** 0 = no numbers; 1 = external or vendor numbers only, or own numbers that fail their own printed deflation; 2 = own in-sample numbers with deflation printed and survived at lead strength; 3 = own in-sample exam passed at the true trial count (DSR at effective N, SPA, PBO printed).
- **M, mechanism (0-2):** 0 = none claimed; 1 = named; 2 = named and specific (who pays, why, and through what pipe).
- **D, consensus distance (Morillo lens, 0-2):** 0 = consensus trade restated; 1 = named baseline with a plausible difference source; 2 = difference conditioned on private data only this estate has.
- **A, accrual (0-2):** 0 = no forward route; 1 = logging or a dated one-shot grade; 2 = live forward falsifier running or armed with a registered rule.
- **S, speed (0-2):** 0 = first forward read beyond six months; 1 = months; 2 = days to weeks.

Total /11. Rank is by total, ties broken by E, then D. The Decisions API seats (Section 4) are lens and generation only.
