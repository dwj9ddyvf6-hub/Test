## 1. THE OVERNIGHT FOCUS-NAMES EDGE

**THE FIND:** Holding the Focus basket from close to open earns +16.46bp a night over 2,229 sessions back to 2018 (t 4.97, all 29 names positive, +12.24bp at t 5.24 with every gap night removed, against SPY +3.53bp and QQQ +4.85bp on the same nights; discovery exam DSR 0.99994, SPA 0.001 vs SPY and 0.002 vs QQQ, PBO 0.067, gross Sharpe 1.67), while the intraday leg of the same names is worthless (Sharpe 0.01); the tradeable form is the auctions, with true-print auction cost about 5.8bp on the carry names against a 15bp death line, and a 60-session forward falsifier on auction prints from Mon 2026-11-16.

**THE FRAMEWORK:** Segment decomposition of the trading day, then widening, then anatomy, then a cost-basis audit. The basket's close-to-close return was treated as a bundle of separable legs (overnight, open drive, midday, close hour, intraday whole), each leg graded alone; the surviving leg was then widened in universe and in time, split by an exogenous world state, and finally re-measured at true auction prints instead of bar proxies (Wave 5: bar close is the closing auction print, exact in 60 of 60 name-sessions; bar open is not the opening print, exact in only 4 of 60, CRWV off by +28.765bp mean).

**THE METHODS AND TECHNIQUES, IN ORDER:** (1) Decompose the day and grade each segment separately; the worthless intraday leg is what isolates the overnight leg as the carrier. (2) Widen: Lane 9 re-ran the effect on the full Focus complex (29 of 30 names, SIVE has no bars) over the full 2,229 nights, with split halves (+14.88bp t 3.61, +18.03bp t 3.48) and a gap-night removal sensitivity that costs the effect a quarter of its mean and leaves it standing. (3) Anatomy: Abyss A-2 split the basket's nights by five foreign trackers' own overnight signs; the premium is the world's night move in US names (sign-split diffs +106.4 to +152.7bp, t 17.1 to 26.1, per-name breadth 1.00), contemporaneous, not leading, which kills every US-only story about the find. (4) Cost audit at the true prints: crossing quoted spreads destroys the leg (measured crossing cost 46.076bp on the W5A audit night); closing-auction entry and opening-auction exit are the only viable form.

**THE MINDSET:** A basket return is a bundle, not a fact. Refuse to grade the aggregate; find which leg carries it, then ask what the leg is made of and what it costs to actually touch, as three separate questions with three separate answers.

**WHAT I DID DIFFERENTLY:** The default behaviour, from today's record: grade the basket's aggregate return as one object, on the proof measure, with cost read off the daily bar's open as if it were the auction print; that default had the overnight premium diluted inside a close-to-close number and its cost mismeasured by up to +28.765bp a name. The deviation: I decomposed the aggregate into its legs and graded the worthless leg too, widened the survivor in universe and time, and re-audited its cost at condition-identified auction prints. The find exists only on the far side of all three deviations.

**THE REUSABLE RULE:** Decompose any aggregate effect into its natural time segments and grade each segment alone before believing the aggregate; the edge, if one exists, lives in a segment, and the segment determines the tradeable form.

**WHERE IT APPLIES NEXT:** The desk fleet's own daily results have never been decomposed this way: fleet and book returns split into entry-day, hold, and exit-day phases, and sleeve fills attributed by segment, which is also the grading Lane 3's hypothesis H-D is owed from Mon 2026-11-16.

## 9. THE SEGMENT SHAPE ITSELF

**THE FIND:** Decomposed into segments, the trading day has a shape nobody had separated: on 294 symbol-sessions (21 symbols, 14 sessions, 2026-10-20 to 2026-11-13), overnight +15.6bp, open drive +11.7bp, midday −28.9bp at a 43 percent win rate, close hour −7.8bp, with midday the only persistently negative segment; Lane 9's replication under a different completeness filter reads the same shape (overnight +12.89, open drive +11.83, midday −33.69, close hour −9.37).

**THE METHODS AND TECHNIQUES, IN ORDER:** (1) Cut an aggregated series at its natural joints (the session's own structure: overnight, open drive, midday, close hour) instead of at round hours. (2) Compute every segment on the same rows so the segments are comparable and sum to the whole. (3) Replicate under a different completeness filter and report both reads with the filter difference named (294 rows against 273). (4) Cluster by the independent unit (sessions, not symbol-sessions) before believing any segment, and let the clustering demote what it demotes, in print. **THE MINDSET:** The day is not one observation. And the kill-happy correction applies in both directions: a shape that survives re-filtering but not clustering is neither promoted nor buried; it is registered forward with its falsifier and graded by accrual, because the binding constraint is nights, and nights only accrue.

**WHAT I DID DIFFERENTLY:** The default behaviour, from today's record: treat the day as one observation, and, inside a decomposition, trust pooled symbol-session statistics. Pooled, midday's −28.9bp over 294 symbol-sessions reads like a find; the pooled default would have promoted it, the way earlier passes promoted shapes that later died on the independent unit. The deviation: I cut the day at its own joints rather than at round hours, then clustered by sessions, the independent unit, and let the clustering demote midday in print from find to registered hypothesis (H-D) with its falsifier and grading date attached. The shape was found by the decomposition; its honest status was produced by refusing the pooled read.

**THE REUSABLE RULE:** Decompose any time-aggregated series into its natural segments, grade each segment on statistics clustered by the independent unit, and register whatever survives direction but not clustering as a forward hypothesis with a named falsifier.

**WHERE IT APPLIES NEXT:** Sleeve-level segment attribution: which sleeves' fills and intended entries land in which segment is computable from Mon 2026-11-16 forward under the cured intent records, and H-D's grading at 20 resolved shadow fires per family is the first scheduled application.
