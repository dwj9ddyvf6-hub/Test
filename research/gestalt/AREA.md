# research/gestalt/: what lives here

The Full Gestalt research corpus: the written record of the desk's gestalt research program from November 2026, published as knowledge, not as raw data. The underlying data files, ledgers, and machine state stay in the private workspace; what is published here is the reasoning, the methods, the findings, and the falsifiers, in the form another LLM or researcher can read cold and use.

## Collections

- `tensor-full-alpha-report.md` and `tensor-positive-alpha-brief.md`: the headline reports of the gestalt alpha sweep over the Tensor engine's signal complex.
- `decisions-api/`: the Decisions API leverage program. Typed multi-lens decision panels used creatively as a research lens: seven-lens panels, connection hunting, adversarial nulls, extraction, and counterfactual reads, plus the waves that followed the overnight Focus-names edge through auction-cost measurement.
- `alpha-hunt-chaos/`: the Chaos Lab and Potential-Edge Census work, including the Edge-Find Method Ledger with its method autopsies and geology (where each find sat, what tool dug it, where the vein points next).
- `six-constructions/` and `astra-six-constructions/`: the six-constructions program and its independent construction audits, including falsified constructions with their falsifiers named.
- `consensus-lens/`: the consensus lens examination plan, applying the circumvention framework (named consensus baseline, identified difference source, trial accounting with deflated Sharpe, disagreement preserved, decay monitoring) to every research claim.
- `consensus-circumvention/`: the LLM consensus alpha circumvention study the lens was built from.
- `alpha-hunt-dive-three/`, `alpha-hunt-dive-four/`, `alpha-hunt-x10-dive/`: the Alpha Hunt deep dives and the x10 expansion waves.
- `decisions-api-incorporation/`: the earlier study of how the typed decision layer was incorporated into the desk's research path.

## Standing rules of this corpus

- Potential edge is the measure: the hunt is graded on potential edge and alpha found and armed, never on proven trades, because proof requires a forward window.
- Every claim carries its limiting number in the same sentence, and every edge find carries a forward falsifier with a date and a demotion rule.
- Gestalt combinations are themselves alpha candidates, not only audit subjects.

Exact file counts and blob hashes for this area live in the root INDEX.json, under the path prefix `research/gestalt/`.
