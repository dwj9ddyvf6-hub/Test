# Gestalt research: section index

This folder is the public evidence record of the desk's November 2026 edge-hunt
program: the two-day alpha hunt of 14 and 15 November 2026, the grading machinery
that examined what it found, and the follow-on studies (the consensus lens, the
Decisions API panels, a visiting researcher's constructions). The **method** these
studies produced lives in the companion repository
[Edge](https://github.com/dwj9ddyvf6-hub/Edge), in
`skills/engineer-trading-edge/`; this folder is the evidence the method rests on.
Nothing here is a claim of profitability: the two surviving objects are
prospective, and their forward falsifiers decide them.

## The record in one paragraph

The hunt wrote 25 method autopsies (the ledger below): every find with its
framework, its methods in order, what was done differently, and the reusable rule.
The potential-edge census registered 198 objects: 2 exam-passed, 18 live
candidates, 66 leads, 59 watch, 22 avoidance, 31 resolved-against. Of the first
17 graded conversion exams, 1 was promoted, 4 killed and 12 rejected. The two
survivors are the overnight Focus-names edge (DSR 0.99994 at the Wave 3 family
count; the reprint at the pre-registered count of 10 is DSR 0.99910; SPA 0.001
against SPY and 0.002 against QQQ; PBO 0.067; t 4.97; gross Sharpe 1.67 over
2,229 sessions, 2018 to 2026; the intraday leg is worthless at Sharpe 0.01; the
premium is tradeable only through the auctions, and five names carry it after
true auction costs) and RND-7 (promoted at DSR 0.989 under clustered trial
accounting, PBO 0.022, SPA 0.000 against both benchmarks, t 4.61, +20.48 basis
points a night; under raw-count accounting the same object prints DSR 0.007, and
the tension is recorded, not smoothed). Both run 60-session forward falsifiers
from 16 November 2026. The failures are kept in the same record: the options
expression of the overnight edge (rejected on real chains), ON_fit_lo14 as
stamped (look-ahead; its point-in-time rebuild stands FAIL, family PBO 0.222
against a 0.2 bar), the insider-cluster build (rejected, DSR 0.579 against a
0.95 bar), and FX-01 (rejected on paper, forward watch only).

## How to read the hunt

1. Start with the ledger's introduction and its first autopsy (the overnight
   Focus-names edge): it teaches the autopsy form every other entry follows.
2. Read the wave reports in [decisions-api/](decisions-api/) in date order for
   the chronology: what was tried, in what order, and what each wave closed.
3. Read [FAILURE-AUDIT-2026-11-15.md](alpha-hunt-chaos/FAILURE-AUDIT-2026-11-15.md)
   and the ledger's negative ledger before the survivors: the kill rate is the
   method's honesty proof.
4. For any object, find its exam in the conversion records and its falsifier
   before quoting its numbers. A number without its trial count and its
   falsifier is not a result this library stands behind.

## Status vocabulary used here

- **Exam-passed / promoted:** cleared the fleet admission exam at its registered
  trial count. Still prospective until its forward falsifier resolves.
- **Live candidate / lead / watch:** registered objects at earlier grades.
- **Avoidance / resolved-against:** examined and decided against, with the
  deciding evidence in the same record.
- **Killed:** its own registered falsifier fired on its own resolved sample.
