# Forecasting / calibration science — key numbers
Read 2026-11-10, index unless noted.

- Tian et al EMNLP 2023 (arXiv:2305.14975): RLHF verbalized confidence better calibrated than conditional token probs, ECE reduced ~50% rel. on TriviaQA/SciQ/TruthfulQA, GPT-4/Claude etc; synth notes ECE ~0.03-0.04 GPT-4/Claude-2 with 2-step/top-k on factoid QA; still systematically overconfident; RLHF worsens token-prob calibration.
- Kadavath et al 2022 (arXiv:2207.05221): larger models well-calibrated on MC/TF in right format; P(True) self-eval encouraging calibration/scaling; P(IK) partial generalization, struggles calibration on new tasks; format-dependent.
- Proper scoring: Brier 1950 BS=mean(p-o)^2; log score -log p(outcome) penalizes confident-wrong unboundedly; both strictly proper (Gneiting & Raftery 2007 JASA); goal maximize sharpness subject to calibration (Gneiting et al 2007). Murphy 1973: BS = Reliability - Resolution + Uncertainty; uncertainty = climatology BS = base*(1-base); BSS = 1 - BS/BS_ref.
- Whelan 2026 calibration benchmark (henrywhelan.com): 8 frontier models, 15 entries, 143 post-cutoff prediction-market questions, base rate 36.4%, climatology Brier 0.2314; 0/15 significantly beat climatology bare; AUC 0.61-0.72 all; resolution ≈ reliability loss (over-extreme probabilities); 1-param shrinkage toward base rate fitted on 205 disjoint markets improves all 15 by mean 0.0223 Brier, 11/15 then significantly beat OOS climatology.
- Halawi et al NeurIPS 2024: retrieval-augmented GPT-4 system Brier 0.179 on 914 test questions vs human crowd 0.149, no-retrieval GPT-4 0.208; RMS calib err 0.42 vs crowd 0.38 (units as reported in lit review); 4:1 crowd:system weighted avg 0.146 beats both alone; models rarely output <0.1/>0.9 (attenuation).
- Schoenegger et al Sci Adv 2024: 12 LLMs, 31 binary questions, 925 humans; LLM crowd median Brier 0.20 vs human crowd 0.19, p=0.850, equivalence bound 0.081 wide (constant 50% would qualify per Metaculus critique); acquiescence bias mean predictions significantly >50% despite ~even resolutions; exposing GPT-4/Claude2 to human median improved 17-28% but simple averaging human+machine beat updating.
- Schoenegger et al 2025 prompt study (via Metaculus/playbook synth): 38 prompts x 4 frontier models x 100 questions; no prompt significant after Benjamini-Hochberg; frequency reasoning -0.014 Brier, base-rate-first -0.011 point estimates; Bayesian prompts harmful +0.005..+0.015.
- Combination: Bates & Granger 1969; forecast combination puzzle Stock & Watson 2004, Smith & Wallis 2009 — simple average often beats estimated optimal weights due to finite-sample weight-estimation error (MSFE gains small ~<=4% but consistent).
- Extremizing: Baron et al Decision Analysis 2014 (two reasons: end-of-scale compression + judges discounting others' info); Satopaa et al IJF 2014 logit aggregator, 1300+ experts/69 geopolitical events, superior to widely used aggregators, single parameter robust vs overfitting; practitioner synth: extremizing factor 1.2-1.5 regular teams, ~1.0 superforecaster teams already sharing info; caveat requires diverse/independent info — correlated LLM judges sharing priors should NOT be extremized naively.
- Prophet Arena (arXiv:2510.17638): 1,367 events / 72,136 markets from Kalshi; GPT-5[R] Brier 0.184 ECE 0.042 avg market return 0.943 vs market baseline 0.187 / 0.069 / 0.899; top models Brier 0.17-0.24 band, ECE <=0.05 strong vs 0.05-0.2 weak; LLMs systematically conservative vs market at extremes; prompt variation changes Brier <0.01, self-consistency generally worse; even top model return <1 (not break-even).
- ForecastBench (Karger et al ICLR 2025, arXiv:2409.19839): original superforecasters 0.093, public 0.107, best LLM 0.111; Feb 2025 update difficulty-adjusted super 0.081 vs GPT-4.5 0.101, naive 50/50 = 0.25, parity extrapolated late 2026 (95% CI Dec 2025-Jan 2028); May 2026 leaderboard (parallect): super Brier Index 70.2 (~Brier 0.089) vs best AI DeepMind green tree 67.9 (~0.103), AI wins dataset subset trails market subset; July 2026 FRI claims several systems indistinguishable from super level; GJI critique: human baseline frozen single July 2024 solo elicitation, no teaming/updating. Brier Index = (1-sqrt(Brier))*100.
- Vaticinus 2026 beyond-Brier preprint: 25 ForecastBench rounds 2024-26, 370,972 resolved market forecasts, 342 configs; frontier LLMs add ~0.001 nats incremental log-likelihood beyond liquid market price, trend non-positive with scale; human superforecasters ~0.09 nats on the one released human round (~75x, single-round caveat).
- Metaculus AI forecasting 2026 synthesis (11 analyses Oct2024-May2026): Pros beat bots every quarterly tournament, peer-score margins 11.3 (Q3 2024, p=.036), 17.7 (Q1 2025, p=.001), 20.03 (p=.00001); 86% of Fall 2025 winners ensemble; Platt/logistic recalibration improved bot Brier 0.016 binary / 0.005 MC (p<0.001); Phan et al Platt 0.0999->0.0934; capping extremes strongest within-winner differentiator r=+0.48 p=0.005.
- AIA Forecaster Bridgewater arXiv:2511.07678: agentic search+supervisor+Platt, ForecastBench Brier 0.108 vs super difference n.s. p=0.15 (one secondary says market subset 0.0753 vs human 0.0740); without search 0.361 worse than random on its live benchmark per playbook synth.
- Merger-arb LLM (ICML/PMLR 2026 pdf): 404 deals 42 countries held-out; finetuned system class-balanced Brier 0.151 vs Platt market-implied 0.199 (24% below), XGBoost 0.186, frontier models 0.201-0.259; finetuning cut calibration error 0.089->0.039; correlation with market 0.36 vs XGBoost 0.75.
- Prediction markets: Dickerson et al PNAS 2026 (doi 10.1073/pnas.2615627123): 1,014,122 forecasts, Polymarket/Kalshi/Manifold/Metaculus, 12 domains, Oct2015-Nov2025, overall highly calibrated (p% events occur ~p%), fine-grained systematic over/under in consequential domains (conflict/geopolitics overestimate per TAU summary); calibration strong already at 25% of forecast period, accuracy gains over time mainly resolution. PredScope (non-peer-reviewed, selection-biased sample): 542 markets Jan2025-Mar2026, avg calib error 3.2pp, 1.8pp for $1M+ volume, median Brier 0.17 vol-weighted, Kalshi Fed markets Brier 0.09 error 1.1pp; markets Brier elections 0.14/econ 0.11 vs superforecasters 0.13/0.14 on its subsets. Le preprint via playbook: calibration slope 0.99 within 1h of resolution -> 1.32 beyond 1 month (underconfidence far from resolution), politics strongest.
- Power: detecting 1-3 Brier-point (0.01-0.03) edge needs hundreds to low-thousands resolved per category (playbook synth).
