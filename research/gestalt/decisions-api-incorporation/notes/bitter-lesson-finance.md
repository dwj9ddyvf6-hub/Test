# Bitter lesson + finance pitfalls + engineering
Read 2026-11-10, index.

Bitter lesson:
- Sutton Mar 13 2019 incompleteideas.net: "general methods that leverage computation are ultimately the most effective, and by a large margin"; Moore's law generalization; human-knowledge helps short term, plateaus/inhibits, breakthroughs from scaling search+learning (the two scalable methods); examples chess (Deep Blue 1997 massive deep search vs grandmaster knowledge), Go (AlphaGo/Zero self-play, Zero removed human data), speech (statistical/HMM -> deep learning vs hand rules), vision (learned vs hand-crafted features).
- AlphaGo detail: policy/value nets + MCTS, beat other programs 99.8%, Fan Hui 5-0, Lee Sedol 4-1 2016; Zero tabula rasa self-play surpassed human-trained version in days/hours scale. Boundary noted in wiki synth: template depends on closed rules, cheap simulation, verifiable outcomes — markets have none of those three cleanly.
- Weather: GraphCast Science 2023 beat ECMWF HRES on >90% of 1,380 targets, 10-day forecast <1 min on 1 TPU; GenCast Nature 2024 beat ENS on 97.2% of 1,320 targets; AIFS operational Feb 25 2025, gains up to 20%, ~1000x less energy; BUT trained on ERA5 reanalysis produced by physics NWP, operational centres run AI+physics side-by-side and blend, AI can miss extreme OOD events.
- Sutton 2025 Dwarkesh interview (secondary transcripts): LLMs are a "dead end" in his view — they predict what a person would say, not what will happen; no goals/ground-truth learning from experience. Implication: an LLM judge is human-knowledge compression, not bitter-lesson learning from outcomes, UNLESS operation closes loop with resolved-outcome learning (its ledger).
- Counter: Chollet 2019 On the Measure of Intelligence — intelligence = skill-acquisition efficiency wrt priors+experience+generalization difficulty; ARC tasks solvable from 3-5 examples, humans >95% in original framing, scaling buys skill not necessarily generalization; smoothness hypothesis working paper: in nonsmooth domains scaling curve flattens early, structure/inductive bias/search are prerequisites, "lesson not equally bitter for all domains"; Wikipedia bitter lesson note: mid-2020s complexity theorists argue diminishing thermodynamic returns to brute scaling, architectural/memory efficiency matters.

Finance pitfalls:
- Harvey, Liu & Zhu RFS 2016: 316 factors published to 2012, most likely false; new factor needs t >= 3.0 (Bonferroni for ~300 tests at 5% ≈ 3.78); Harvey & Liu 2015 haircut Sharpe — rule of thumb halving backtested Sharpe, cut grows with #tests.
- Bailey & Lopez de Prado 2014 Deflated Sharpe Ratio: P(true SR>0) adjusting #trials, sample length, skew/kurtosis; significant only if DSR > 0.95 (convention in summaries); Bailey et al 2014 Pseudo-Mathematics: best of 100 worthless strategies expected annualized Sharpe ~1.26 (submillisecond glossary synth).
- White 2000 Reality Check; Sullivan, Timmermann & White JF 1999: expanded Brock et al 26 rules to 7,846 rules, 100y DJIA; best rules survived snooping adjustment in 1897-1986 sample but performance did not persist in 1987-96 out-of-sample per follow-ups; Hsu et al re-exam 39,832 rules: significant profitable rules for NASDAQ/Russell 2000, NOT DJIA/S&P500.
- Lopez de Prado AFML 2018 themes via synth: non-stationarity, regime shifts, low SNR (daily return R2 ~0.01-0.02), only ~4-5 independent business cycles in 20y daily data, alpha decay/adversarial adaptation, purged+embargo CV needed, fractional differencing stationarity-memory tradeoff (d≈0.4 examples).
- Automated mining: WorldQuant BRAIN mines hundreds of thousands to billions of formulaic alphas combined into mega-alpha (Kakushadze framing); Renaissance right ~50.75% of trades across 150k-300k executions/day (secondary quant-factor-mining compilation — treat as illustrative, not audited). Genetic-algorithm rule mining case (Hedonist Research, killed): best in-sample Sharpe 4.1 -> OOS -0.3 after 200 generations, "industrial-scale data snooping". Contrast: scale works when bets are many, independent-ish, cheap, feedback fast; fails when selection is on same history used for validation.

Engineering patterns:
- AICassindra Decisions GA guide (Oct 26 2026, synthetic example, no provider call): two-band thresholds fitted on labelled sample + cost model: synthetic 200 duplicate-charge cases, reliability top bin mean 0.962 vs 0.894 actual, bottom 0.039 vs 0.093; 0.5 cut cost 1125 (20 wrong approve/13 wrong deny at costs 40/25/3 review) vs band deny<=0.10 approve>=0.90 cost 412 reviewing 54; fitted on first 100 -> holdout cost 244 vs 720 for 0.5; score handling: levels probs 0.1/0.3/0.5/0.1 score 1.6, but P(level>=high)=0.6 is threshold quantity, do NOT round score; choice gate on top-value probability (0.71 -> review); questions sharing evidence are NOT independent (misleading evidence -> all wrong together); keep instructions stable or thresholds invalidated; refusals -> review, never default to 0/no; track review-band share + agreement with later human outcomes for drift.
- LLM judge discipline (prasad.tech; aiagentic handbook; jiamingqu handbook ch7): judge = instrument; deterministic gates first, model judges remainder; cache verdict keyed on (case, rubric version, model version), store provenance (stage, rubric/model version, time) = audit trail; calibration set 200-300 human-labelled items, require judge-human kappa >= human-human; pin judge snapshot+prompt+decoding, store on every score; fixed ~200-item canary re-judged weekly, >2-point shift with no input change = drift; ~100-item monthly human audit; judge upgrade = migration: dual-run old/new on calibration+baseline, re-score baseline, never mix versions in one trend line; biases: position (Wang 2023 swap flips), verbosity (AlpacaEval 2.0 length control), self-preference (Zheng 2023; GPT-4 judge >80% agreement with humans on MT-Bench general chat, not domain); five drift types: input distribution, judge, evaluation standard/criteria drift (Shankar et al), annotator, concept; anchored signals = re-run fixed anchor set on schedule.
- Version drift evidence: Chen, Zaharia & Zou 2023 (arXiv:2307.09009, HDSR 2024): GPT-4 March vs June 2023 same tasks: prime/composite accuracy 84%->51% (revised balanced set; original 97.6->2.4 was primes-only artifact flagged by Narayanan & Kapoor), code directly-executable 52%->10% largely markdown wrapping (70% after stripping) — lesson: behaviour/formatting drift breaks pipelines even where capability debate continues.
- OpenAI prompt caching guide (official): min cacheable 1024 tokens for GPT-5.6+, writes 1.25x input for 30m lifetime, reads 0.1x on most models; write-once+read-once costs 1.35x vs 2x uncached; cache stores KV tensors, exact prefix match.
- Commercial typed judges: Jev (TypeSafe, System One, launched Sep 15 2026): $0.042/1M input, output free, 70-500ms vendor-reported, RLCD (Reinforcement Learning for Calibrated Decisions) explicit calibration claim, confidence = concentration of distribution NOT chance correct (Braintrust guide), choice up to 255, score 2-10, one workflow eval 67.8% label agreement at $0.0004/case 0.4s (lumadock secondary); jaggedness/prompt injection caveats: does not treat state as hostile by default.
