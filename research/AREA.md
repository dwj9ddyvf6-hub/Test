# research/: what lives here

Standalone research works: the gestalt research corpus (the November 2026 edge-hunt record), the chart-reading mastery collection, the technical analysis study and practice archive, and the master trader study.

Exact file count, byte total and captured revision: [the generated repository index](../INDEX.md). The matching entries in [INDEX.json](../INDEX.json) retain every path and blob hash.

## Collections

- `gestalt/` (344 files): the public evidence record of the desk's November 2026 edge-hunt program: the alpha hunt and its 25 method autopsies, the grading and conversion exams, the consensus lens examination, the Decisions API panel studies, and a visiting researcher's six constructions, pre-registered and graded. First read: [gestalt/INDEX.md](gestalt/INDEX.md), the section index that states the record with its limiting numbers. The method distilled from this corpus lives in the companion repository [Edge](https://github.com/dwj9ddyvf6-hub/Edge); this collection is its evidence.
- `chart-reading/` (32 files): the chart-reading masteries: the desk's complete written method for reading a chart, as code plus written mastery (the chart-read and stock-analysis skills, the mastery program, and the setup evidence studies). First read: [chart-reading/AREA.md](chart-reading/AREA.md).
- `technical-analysis-study-2026-10-30/` (6 files): the atlas in Markdown, Word and PDF, the archive ZIP and its checksums.
- `master-trader-study-2026-10-29/` (3 files): study.md, README.md and the Word form.

A separate research area, docs/research/, holds independent additional studies; INDEX.md enumerates every captured collection and ROADMAP.md gives the source-reading routes.
