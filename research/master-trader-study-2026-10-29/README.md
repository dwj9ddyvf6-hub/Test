## Public edition

Historical research and source-access qualifications are preserved. User-specific timing-authority names, terminal links, deployment versions and connection-test observations have been removed or generalized. Coverage examples are illustrative, not claims about a current connected account. Unneeded document metadata and preview/custom-XML residue were removed.

The Markdown preserves the study's paragraph and table content and external research links. Word layout, automatic numbering, page references and internal navigation are best viewed in the DOCX. The original research date and access limitations are retained; this publication does not assert that sources or market observations have been refreshed.

Schedules are proposals. The study does not activate monitoring, authorize orders or establish profitability.
