A stock qualifies only when the method's own gates all pass: enough data behind the score, at least five of eight checks agreeing, and a high enough score and confidence. The full list shows every refusal and its reason — nothing is quietly dropped.
How to read this page: the Map tells you the terrain — whether dealer positioning is likely to amplify moves or absorb them, and the two price levels where the largest positions sit. The Board tells you which individual stocks earned a full pass through an eight-check confluence method (relative strength, price action, volume, trend, support and resistance, sector strength, institutional positioning, market context). Support is the line the idea is wrong below; the first target is the next significant level above. Every figure on this page is written to a permanent public record before it is shown, and yesterday's figures are graded against what the market actually did — see the record above. Method note: the score is our open implementation of a published method; the transforms are ours and are labeled as such wherever the record is kept.
