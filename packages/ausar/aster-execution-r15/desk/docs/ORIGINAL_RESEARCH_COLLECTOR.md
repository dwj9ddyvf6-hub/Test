# Prospective original-method research collection

`backend.original_research_runtime` collects TRACE, CRD, UIR and TCFT inputs in its own unfunded SQLite journal. It cannot submit orders, install trading sleeves, fund a book, execute a financial simulation or promote alpha. Constructing the main application does none of those things. `read_status(db_path)` uses SQLite read-only mode and does not create a missing database or parent directory.

Run from the repository root with the desk environment:

```sh
.venv/bin/python -m backend.original_research_runtime status
.venv/bin/python -m backend.original_research_runtime once --symbols AAPL,MSFT
.venv/bin/python -m backend.original_research_runtime serve --symbols AAPL,MSFT --interval 300
```

The default journal is `data/original-research.sqlite`; `--db` or `ASTER_ORIGINAL_RESEARCH_DB_PATH` selects another research journal. `--operating-db` selects the source ledger and is read-only. The collector rejects the operating ledger, symlink/hardlink aliases and databases containing another ledger as its own destination. `--no-public` skips public HTTP sources and SPY acquisition. An explicit `once` registers research; it does not start a persistent worker. The coordinating desk service starts `serve` only after review.

Each serving collector holds an exclusive flock beside its own journal. `once` takes the same lease, so it cannot overlap an active CLI worker. Heartbeats bind the real PID and Linux process birth, interval and lease. Projection verifies PID birth, the existing OS lease and receipt age, with expiry `max(2 × interval + 75 seconds, 120 seconds)`. At a five-minute interval this is 675 seconds, covering the bounded acquisition window. Stop or crash releases the OS lease; an old heartbeat cannot make a stopped collector appear active. `serve` prints compact cycle summaries, and records failures without erasing prior receipts. This mechanism does not claim recovery after a host or container reset; external process supervision supplies that separately.

## Preserved reference lineage

Ten exact specification/kernel/Anna reference files are materialized under `strategies/original_research/lineage`. `SOURCE_BINDING.json` records canonical archive origins, local filenames, byte counts and SHA-256 values. The runtime compares each materialization with its original hardcoded pin and records the chosen local path. These references make a container independent of old `/workspace` paths. They do not provide all dependencies, original fitted models or a complete executable implementation of any of the four original strategies. No source archive is modified or executed by the collector.

The control registry freezes the named original-method comparisons before prospective collection. A changed registry, missing source or mismatched source blocks collection/series admission. Current-only and persistence remain distinct controls; original source semantics are preserved.

## What is collected

**TRACE:** Read-only snapshots retain exact compressed operating H/D/W BLOBs, source revision and first-observation clocks, limited requested enrollment and actual selection/decision/census schemas. AAPL/MSFT are a small reproducible initial sample, not an equity-selection rule. SPY and XLK are requested explicitly; missing panels are reported. SPY is additionally acquired through three actual public Yahoo GETs: hourly `60m/1mo`, daily `1d/2y`, weekly `1wk/2y`. The existing provider's Monday boundary, OHLC normalization and exact native exchange-calendar finality are used in a disposable isolated Store. No provider tables are added to Aster. Exact raw bodies, selected response headers and SHA-256 identities are retained; original raw first arrival governs finality on reread. SPY is an ETF market factor, not an ordinary-share candidate. Availability reports 252 daily/72 weekly training history and 28 hourly/20 daily/12 weekly feature windows. Adequate counts still leave canonical cross-frame adjustments, issuer/action/sector lineage, complete enrollment, B/H projection and the fitted original continuation/failure/expiry model blocked. A Tensor selection or prediction is not an original CRD forecast.

**CRD:** No original forecast emitter was found in the operating schema. Explicit `ingest-forecast --forecast-json path.json` can preserve a genuinely emitted future series. Receipt-series admission requires original CRD source/control pins, emission after collector registration, receipt within five minutes, finite signed residual/rank-only forecasts and nonnegative uncertainty, integer original support counts of at least 100 calibration dates and 30 state origins, and already-received source inputs whose bytes match their SHA. Pre-registration or late archives remain context and are never aged into prospective slow memory. IDs reject conflicting reuse; identical replay/restart preserves the first receipt. Admission qualifies chronology/source binding only. It does not verify coefficients, matured labels, an authenticated model emitter or full canonical financial calibration. The original eleven-state model, joint scenarios and complete held-book context remain blocked. This collector does not decay, synthesize, interpolate or back-age predictions.

**UIR:** Actual SEC submissions and the issuer release are preserved with receipt clocks. SEC accession identity is deduplicated and its explicit `acceptanceDateTime` is the separate publication clock. Filings published before registration are previous-filings context; current submissions do not recreate old opportunities. The submissions receipt does not claim to include filing document bodies. Issuer date-only text is recorded as a date, never converted into an invented midnight publication instant. Missing signed pre-event operating relationships, residual surprise model and receiver absorption prevent original UIR qualification.

**TCFT:** The actual LSEG page binds the announced upcoming Russell schedule: November 13 preliminary lists, December 11 implementation after close, December 14 effective open. The collector verifies the date text and preserves its date-only publication precision. It invents no additions/deletions, original announced target weights, private inventory bounds or forced market orders. Those missing inputs and the signed physical-return model block strategy qualification.

Every public acquisition has an absolute cancellable ten-second deadline across connection, headers and streamed body, and a two-MiB body cap. HTTP failures are retained as failures; a 404/error body is not relabeled as a successful primary document. Total immutable receipt bodies are limited to 25 MiB. Budget exhaustion preserves prior evidence and records a blocker; it does not delete history or grant fresh information. Raw vendor observations acquired today are context received today, even when they contain years of bars.

## Verification and first actual capture

Author tests exercise read-only and noncreating projections, alias rejection, source/control integrity, duplicate/conflicting IDs, concurrent ingestion, missing/later inputs, future and malformed clocks, strict finality, compressed-size bounds, real OS leases/PID birth and a local slow-stream HTTP deadline. Synthetic fixtures are mechanics checks, not financial evidence.

The R15 evidence folder `original-research/` retains a small actual capture, including the first real fetch-driver failure and its repair. The initial Aster read-only capture contains nine AAPL/MSFT/XLK native panels. Actual SPY acquisitions add completed hourly/daily/weekly factor panels. Actual SEC/issuer/LSEG responses are separate from the test fixtures. The pilot journal is under the evidence folder; the default operating companion journal remains unstarted until the coordinator activates it. No original strategy profits, original model qualification, QQQ/SPY outperformance, broker cash or live execution are claimed by these receipts.
