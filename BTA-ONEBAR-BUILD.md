## What was built

Shared rules (base core, frozen): 30-minute bars, completed bars only. Channel = highest high and lowest low of the prior 50 completed bars, excluding the evaluated bar. Long entry when the evaluated bar trades at or above the channel high (buy stop fill at the channel high, or at the bar open on a gap past it); short entry mirrored at the channel low. If one bar spans both extremes, no entry (OHLC cannot order the touches). One position at a time per symbol. Exit at the open of the next bar after the entry bar. Protective stop 0.20 percent from entry (the translated $1,000: 50 NQ points at the frozen 25,000 reference level; long stop = entry x 0.998, short = entry x 1.002), carried as ExitRule data for the dispatcher. Sizing: qty = floor(slice equity / entry), so the $10,000 slice is the notional cap and the binding constraint; modelled stop risk is then 0.20 percent of the slice, the same fraction the original risks on one NQ contract's $500,000 notional. Max 2 entries per day per symbol by the entry bar's ET date. No re-entry in the same bar after an exit. Sizing equity falls back to the configured $10,000 slice when the live snapshot carries no slice key (the dmac F1 lesson).

bta_onebar_qqq: QQQ, every completed 30-minute bar in the desk warm product (Alpaca SIP, cash session centred; the futures 24-hour session cannot be reproduced from ETF bars and is not faked).
bta_onebar_basket: SPY, QQQ, MDY, DIA with identical rules and parameters per symbol, per-symbol plans, per-symbol daily caps, one position per symbol at a time. This sleeve is the post's portability claim run as a live experiment.
bta_onebar_rth: QQQ, RTH bars only (bar starts 09:30 to before 16:00 ET) for channel and entries, entries only during RTH, and any position open at 15:55 ET exits at market instead of holding past the close. Config flat_by_close stays false with flatten_time_et null: the 15:55 exit is the sleeve's own rule, so no second flatten path exists (grok and teddy precedent).

Desk execution note: the core models the intrabar stop fill; the live dispatcher fills entry intents at the next available price after the entry bar completes, so live entries generally land near the following bar's open and exits near the bar after that. The one bar holding period is preserved. Named in ANALYSIS.md so the paper record is read honestly.
